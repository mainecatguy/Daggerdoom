# Doomerfall player manual

## Health

Max HP is 50 + Endurance, plus Endurance / 15 for each level after the first, always using your current Endurance.
Your class adds a bonus to both your Endurance and its maximum (Barbarian +17, Warrior +12, Mage -2), which is the
only way past 100.

| Endurance | Level 1 | Level 10 | Level 20 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 80 | 98 | 118 |
| 50 | 100 | 130 | 163 |
| 75 | 125 | 170 | 220 |
| 100 | 150 | 210 | 277 |
