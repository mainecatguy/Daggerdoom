hostile Doom bot, the same bot template as the guards. The quest survey (`work/notes/20261124-104520-quest-types.md`)
counts 38 quests with a foe in a town building and 100 with `create foe`. Proposed quest cards 02 and 07 must use this
instead of their single-monster stopgap. Assumed until the owner says otherwise: one bot for each foe Daggerfall
would create.
