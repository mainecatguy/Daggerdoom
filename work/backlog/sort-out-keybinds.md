# Sort out keybinds

**Waiting on:** the late development and polish phase.

Resolve keybind conflicts across Doom, Daggerfall, and Doomerfall. For example, Doom binds Ctrl to fire and Alt to strafe by default, which conflicts with the planned Ctrl+number and Alt+number weapon hotkeys. F9 is quicksave in Daggerfall but quickload in Doom. The owner chose to leave quick keys and quit behavior alone for now (2026-11-23).
