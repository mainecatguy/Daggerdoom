# Marksmanship

**Waiting on:** owner design. Backlog for now (owner, 2026-11-25).

A new skill for single-shot rifles. Archery stays a separate Daggerfall skill (backlog, [original weapon
skills](original-weapon-skills.md)). Owner draft: damage +0% to +100% at 100; at 25, a rifle hit that kills carries
its remaining damage through to the next enemy with no limit until the damage runs out (skip if too hard to code).

Rifles to govern: stock Doom has none. DRLA 1.1.5 and DRLAX tag about 12 weapons with DRLA's `SingleShotRifle` token
(`actors/base/ArsenalTokens.txt`), including the Marksman Rifle, High Power Marksman Rifle, Elephant Gun, Anti-Materiel
Rifle, Gauss Rifle, Railgun, Steel Beast and some assemblies. Rifles and Pistols already covers semi-automatic
ballistic rifles, so the split between the two skills needs settling.
