# Review native Doom monster drops

**Waiting on:** later development, once Daggerfall loot drops from mooks are working and can be play-tested beside native drops.

**Current rule (owner, 2026-11-23):** Doom monsters keep their native drops for now. Daggerfall still rolls its own loot.

These monsters drop items natively:

- **Colourful Hell monsters** drop health and armor bundles, ammo, backpacks, CH weapons such as `CH_Chainsaw`, and DRLA weapon and armor spawners when DRLA is loaded. Colourful Hell has settings that turn these off: `CH_HealthDrops`, `CH_AmmoDrops`, `CH_ExtraDrops`, and `CH_BackPackDrops`.
- **DRLA monsters** have their own drops.
- **Stock Doom monsters** drop clips, shotguns, and chainguns.

Later, decide whether native drops should stay, be reduced, be turned off, or be routed through Daggerfall's loot rules. Consider how they affect the ammo economy, the area loot identity, and Daggerfall's control of loot.
