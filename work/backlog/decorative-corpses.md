# Decorative corpses

**Waiting on:** the encounter module ("Mook packs" in `work/todo.md`) landing.

Owner idea (2026-11-24). A rare gene in the encounter genome places decorative monster corpses in some
dungeons. Corpses cost 0 points. For example, a Civil War that already ended leaves corpses of the losing
side. Keep it a rare easter egg, not common.

Design: `work/notes/20261123-205402-fractal-encounter-variety.md`. Corpses should come from the same
army and colour rules, so they look like they belong. They are scenery: Daggerfall never counts them, and
they hold no loot.
