# Doom weapon prices

**Waiting on:** owner approval of the multipliers below, *DRLA rarity tiers*, and *Doom weapons have a Daggerfall material*. Related open question: how Doom items are priced and which shops buy them (`work/questions.md`, 20261124-002144).

Owner direction (2026-11-24): DRLA weapon prices are similar to Daggerfall's base weapon prices, then modified by
material and rarity. The owner's starting rarity multipliers: common x1, uncommon x2, rare x3, very rare x6,
legendary x20.

## Proposal

- **Base price:** the Daggerfall weapon closest in role, for example a pistol like a dagger and a rocket launcher like
  a two-handed weapon. A table maps each DRLA weapon class to a Daggerfall weapon template.
- **Material:** Daggerfall's own material price rule for weapons, so an Ebony Doom weapon costs what an Ebony
  Daggerfall weapon of that base would.
- **Rarity:** x1, x2, x4, x8, x20. Roughly doubling per tier is easier to read and closer to how rarity grows, with
  legendary as a jackpot. The owner's x1, x2, x3, x6, x20 also works.
- Check the top end: a Daedric legendary multiplies Daggerfall's Daedric price by 20. If that is beyond any shop's
  gold or any reasonable sale, cap it and report the numbers.
