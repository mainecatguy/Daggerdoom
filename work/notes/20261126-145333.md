## Fixes in this run

**Distant windows lost their glow (hw_dfmesh.cpp, one line).** The window pass alpha-tested the mask at 0.5. Daggerfall's
window panes are lattices; once mipmapping averages lit and unlit texels, most of the window falls under 0.5 and is
dropped. At the paired run's 320x200, only 3 lit pixels were left on a house; at normal resolution the same happens to
houses farther away. The mask is white with alpha 0 or 1 and drawn additive (colour x alpha), so the alpha test is now
off: far away the glow averages down the way Unity's emission map does. After: 14 lit pixels on the same house, whole
windows visible, and the second house's windows show too (`zoom-night2.png`). Near windows look the same.

**Interiors no longer get a winter cache file (dd_towncache.cpp, CityExport.cs).** In winter every place, interiors
included, was cached as `T<id>W.ddt` and converted again with `--winter`. Daggerfall draws interiors in summer all year
(`DaggerfallInterior.climateSeason`) and dungeon blocks never swap (`RDBLayout`). Interior sets were not in the winter
list, so the output was already identical (`T8325716.ddt` and `T8325716W.ddt` both 71,483 bytes). The only effect was
a second conversion per interior per season. Now only outdoor groups (ids below 1,000,000) are seasonal, and the
converter ignores `--winter` for space ids.

## Findings

- Daggerfall's interior light is warmer than Doom's: in the interior sheet Daggerfall's plaster and floor look cream and
  green, Doom's white and grey. Same textures (I first took this for a winter texture bug; the cache sizes disproved it).
  That's interior lighting, which is outside this goal. It's on the play-test list.
- When the world look test calls `SetWeather` inside a building, Daggerfall resets its fog to the outdoor density
  (`fog 1 2 0.0005 0 0 0 0 0 0` after entering). That is Daggerfall's own behaviour under the test, and Doom follows it.
  In normal play the weather doesn't change through this call while the player is inside.
- Heavy fog by day looks thicker in Daggerfall's camera than in Doom's (houses more washed out), and close at night.
  The density sent is the same (0.05/m, Doom 42.67). Doom's wider field of view makes houses look farther away, which
  makes the sheet hard to judge. On the play-test list.
- Each light_shots run converted the summer town once at startup before the `dd_sky` winter line ran (`DDTCONV group=7154`
  before `DDTCACHE season winter`). Known gap from the winter run: the first place loads before the season is known.
