## What Daggerfall Unity actually does outdoors (read from the code)

- Outdoor water is the **water ground tile** (tilemap index 0) of the terrain, towns included (the location's RMB
  ground is merged into the terrain tilemap, `StreamingWorld.UpdatePlayerTerrainTileIndex`). Shallow-water tiles
  (5, 6, 8, 20, 21, 23, 30, 31, 33-36, 49) are walked on, not swum (`PlayerMotor.OnShallowWaterTile`).
- Standing on the terrain over tile 0 without Water Walking is `OnExteriorWaterMethod.Swimming`
  (`PlayerMotor.GetOnExteriorWaterMethod`; not on static geometry such as a ship or dock, not levitating). Then:
  - the controller sinks to 0.30 m, so the eye goes from 1.71 m to 0.21 m (`PlayerHeightChanger.DoSinking`), and
    `IsPlayerSwimming` is set;
  - speed is `base * Swimming / 200 + base / 4` (`PlayerSpeedChanger.GetSwimSpeed`, `PlayerMotor.UpdateSpeed`);
  - jumping is refused (`AcrobatMotor.HandleJumpInput`); landing there does no falling damage (already in Doom);
  - each game minute `PlayerEntity` tallies one Swimming use and, on a failed Swimming roll (never for Argonians),
    charges swimming fatigue instead of the normal loss; resting is refused.
- **Breath and drowning never happen outdoors in Daggerfall Unity.** `PlayerEnterExit.Update` only computes
  `IsPlayerSubmerged` inside dungeons ("Underwater swimming logic should only be processed in dungeons at this time"),
  and breath only runs down while submerged (`PlayerEntity.FixedUpdate`). Outdoor water is a surface. So "runs out of
  breath as in Daggerfall Unity" outdoors means: it does not. I implemented that faithfully and asked the owner (see
  Questions) in case they want diving and drowning outdoors, which would go beyond Daggerfall.
- **Monsters:** Daggerfall Unity's enemies ignore outdoor water tiles; `EnemyMotor`'s water logic (aquatic enemies,
  the water line) is dungeon-only. Doom's own dungeon water does not change monster movement either. So "monsters treat
  it as water" as in Daggerfall means no change, and nothing was changed for monsters. Asked the owner.

## Plan (as done)

1. Bridge: `DFB_PLF_WATER_WALKING` (DF to UZ, in `player_flags` next to the existing `DFB_PLF_OUTDOOR_WATER`) and
   `dfb_uz_state.player_motion` with `DFB_UZM_SWIMMING` (UZ to DF). Bindings regenerated.
2. Daggerfall owns the shoreline: Doom swims only where Daggerfall's own tile under the player is water
   (`DoomerfallFallState.cs` already published it for the falling exemption). That covers the streamed terrain and town
   maps by construction, since Daggerfall's tilemap includes the towns' ground.
3. UZDoom `dd_bridge.cpp` `DD_BridgeOutdoorSwimming()`: exterior, water flag, no Water Walking, on the floor, not
   flying, not on an actor, and on the **open ground**: within 12 Doom units of the terrain height at that point
   (`DDTreadmill_HorizonFrame/HorizonHeight`, same triangle split as the treadmill grid). This is Daggerfall's "on the
   terrain, not on static geometry": a dock or roof over a water tile is not swimming. Exposed to ZScript as
   `Actor.DD_OutdoorSwimming()`, published as `player_motion`, and `OnGround()` (rest and transport checks) counts
   swimming as not on the ground, as its comment always said.
4. Mod `DoomerfallSwim.zs`: while swimming, speed factor `0.25 + Swimming / 200` (an Inventory `GetSpeedFactor`, which
   multiplies with the Speed attribute, as Daggerfall's swim speed multiplies its base speed), eye lowered to
   0.21 / 1.71 of the standing eye over about 0.1 s, jump button cleared. Collision height is unchanged on purpose:
   shrinking the hitbox would change how monsters hit the player (combat). Given only with a Daggerfall character.
5. Daggerfall `DoomerfallSwimState.cs` (one-line hook in `DoomerfallBridge.FinishFrame`): sets
   `PlayerEnterExit.IsPlayerSwimming` from Doom's report (the hidden player's motor is off, so `PlayerHeightChanger`
   never would). Daggerfall's own per-minute code then tallies Swimming and charges swimming fatigue unchanged.
