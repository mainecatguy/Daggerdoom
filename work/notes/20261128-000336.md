# Exterior collision: finite heights and fidelity (2026-11-28 overnight)

Goal: no exterior collider reaches the sky; every exterior object's collision stops at its Daggerfall model's top; a
metric of collision vs model with a baseline and a reduction.

## What was wrong (Part 1 finding)

Town and dungeon-exterior collision is one pipeline (`tools/dungeon_converter/CityExport.cs`, `City.Run`, used both by
`--city` and by the running game's `--group` conversions). It rasterises Daggerfall's models on an 8-unit grid into a
heightfield. Each cell is either open floor, a building roof (`CityRoofs.cs`), or *solid*. Solid cells that were not
inside a building's sides (city walls, gate pillars, fountains, statues, fences, wells, rocks, props, roofless ruins,
graveyard walls) and unreachable open ground (walled yards, wall walks) had **no sector at all**. Their edges became
one-sided linedefs, which Doom treats as walls of unlimited height. That was the only way an exterior collider got a
sky-high top:

- no sector ceilings are low (every sector's ceiling is the sky, 2048 above the highest point),
- nature (trees, rocks, plants in the countryside) is `+NOBLOCKMAP` billboards with no collision, as in Daggerfall Unity,
- there are no 3D floors in exteriors.

The only remaining one-sided lines are the outer edge of the converted map (the walkable margin, 24 tiles beyond the
location), which is the map boundary, not an object.

Baseline on the test set: **682,427 raster cells (8×8 units each) open to the sky**, plus 1,057 roof cells whose roof
plane stood above everything in the cell (worst 308 units: a roof plane carried past its triangle onto a wall cell, or a
flat fallback at the building's highest point).

## What changed

- `CityTops.cs` (new): after roofs are built, every cell still without a sector gets one:
  - unreachable open ground keeps its own floor (walled yard, wall walk),
  - a solid cell gets a floor at the **top of its solid stack**: from the street floor, surfaces closer together than
    the player's height merge (the player cannot stand between them); a higher overhang with room below is left out.
  - a roofed cell whose roof plane stands more than 4 units above every model surface in the cell at any corner takes a
    flat top at the highest surface it actually holds.
- `CityFidelity.cs` (new): the finite-height check (always run; a `--city` conversion exits 1 on failure; the runtime
  `--group` conversion records it in the stats and never refuses entry), the fidelity metric (`--fidelity`), the
  flight-test samples (`<slug>.tops.txt`), and `--find-group <name>`.
- `--legacy-void` reproduces the old open-topped cells, for measuring the baseline only.
- `exterior_fidelity.py`: the fixed test set; `exterior_flight.py`: the in-engine flight test.

## The metric

The dungeon metric (`collisionmetric` 01) has not landed, so this is a standalone exterior version on the converter's
own code. It samples Daggerfall's collider triangles on a fixed 4-unit grid (twice as fine as the raster) and compares
the true top with the collision top Doom gets at each fine cell:

- true top: inside a building's sides and not reachable from the street, the highest surface (the roof seen from
  outside); where model surfaces block the player's body, the top of that solid stack; otherwise the walkable floor,
- error = collision top − true top, capped at ±512; a cell open to the sky counts as +512,
- weight 1 within 4 raster cells (32 units) of ground reachable from the start, 0.25 elsewhere,
- reported: weighted mean |error|, excess (collision above model), missing (below), counts of too-tall cells (> 8),
  phantom walls (too tall where the model does not block and is not a building), too-low cells (< −24).

Honest limits: the true-top rule uses the same stack logic as the fix, so the metric does not judge that rule itself;
it does judge the raster's 8-unit footprint, roofs, quantisation and open-to-sky cells against a finer independent
sample. It is a 2.5D heightfield measure: it cannot see gaps under overhangs lower than a body height.

## Known gaps

- **Phantom walls left (92,554 fine cells ≈ 1,480 m² over the set)**: the 8-unit raster makes a cell solid if any face
  touches it, so collision reaches up to one cell past a thin wall or a diagonal edge. The fix is the dungeon side's
  diagonal edges (`dungeonedges` 01) or the grid-shape work (`collisionshape` 01, not landed). Both are larger changes
  that need the door and reach checks run again.
- **Too low (12,416 fine cells)**: mostly building cells whose roof plane is below a chimney, parapet, or tower edge in
  the same cell. The player can only meet these by levitating; the roof is still finite and below the model. A few are
  open street cells where the fine sample sees a blocking face the 8-unit cell missed, for example Privateer's Hold
  `--city` map at 1918,-14978.
- A gap under an overhang lower than a body height (a beam at 40 units) is solid up to the overhang's top: a heightfield
  cannot hold a gap. Only a 3D floor could.
- **Town cache**: the game keeps converted towns in its cache with no version check (`dd_towncache.cpp`), so towns
  converted before this change keep their old collision until the cache is cleared or evicts them.
- The standalone `--city` mesh package's script no longer compiles against the current engine (line 75 and 80 errors).
  This is the same before and after, and the runtime `--group` path is unaffected.
- The `collisionmetric` 01 dungeon metric has not landed. This exterior metric is standalone and should be merged into it
  when it lands.

## How to rerun

```
dotnet build tools/dungeon_converter -c Release
python tools/dungeon_converter/exterior_fidelity.py --out <scratch dir> [--legacy-void]   # exits 1 if any height check fails
python tools/dungeon_converter/exterior_fidelity.py --find "<location name>"              # group ids
DungeonConverter.exe --city "Privateer's Hold" --region Daggerfall --out <dir> --fidelity   # exits 1 if the height check fails
python tools/dungeon_converter/exterior_flight.py <dir> --slug privateershold_city
python tools/dungeon_converter/city_walk.py <dir> --slug privateershold_city --no-mesh
python tools/dungeon_converter/roof_check.py <dir> --slug privateershold_city --no-mesh --timeout 75
DungeonConverter.exe --group 7154 --ddt <dir>\g.ddt --out <dir> --fidelity [--legacy-void]
python tools/dungeon_converter/exterior_doors.py <dir> --slug privateershold_city --map DDT07154
```

## Play-test list

0. Clear the town cache first (the play folder's `data\cache`), or old towns keep their old collision.

1. In the City of Daggerfall, levitate onto the city wall and walk along it. Does it feel like the wall's real top?
2. Jump or levitate over fences, low walls, and fountains in a village. Are there any invisible walls above them?
3. At Privateer's Hold's exterior, fly over the ruin walls. Any invisible walls?
4. Shoot over a low wall at a monster behind it. Do shots pass above the wall top?
5. Statues, signposts, and wells now have solid tops at their model's top. Do any feel too big?
