# Encounters 1: the monster pool and its data file (2026-11-24)

Card 1 of 7 in `work/queue/encounters`. Design: `work/notes/20261123-205402-fractal-encounter-variety.md`, sections 5, 6 and 8.

## What was built

- `mods/doomerfall/src/zscript/EncounterPool.zs`
  - `DoomerfallEncounterData` parses the data file.
  - `DoomerfallEncounterPool` builds the pool from `AllActorClasses` the first time something asks for it. It builds lazily so that skill `ReplaceActor` rules are already in force.
  - `DoomerfallMonster` is one pool entry: class, kind, tier, group, traits, points, health, radius, height, capped, Civil War safe, and the reason a monster is unsafe.
  - Later cards call `DoomerfallEncounterPool.Get()`, which is play scope.
- `mods/doomerfall/src/doomerfall_encounters.txt` is the data file. It holds:
  - the 17 kinds, with base points (section 8.3) and default traits
  - the kind-by-name and kind-by-ancestor tables
  - excludes and includes
  - the colour and family multipliers (section 8.2) and the tier rules
  - the tier 8 "own value" rules
  - extra traits, including the summoner list
  - caps
  - the Civil War unsafe list
  - the area level curve (section 6.1)
  - titan chances (6.3)
  - boss counts and strength (6.4)
  - army shares, Civil War, split, squad, and gene numbers
  - the theme table (4.1, all 19 Daggerfall dungeon types)

  It contains only class names and our own numbers. Nothing in it is copied from the mods.
- `mods/doomerfall/src/zscript/EncounterPoolTest.zs` is the test hook. `+set dd_encounter_pooldump 1` (declared in `CVARINFO.txt`, not saved to the ini) prints every pool entry, skipped class, per-kind count, per-tier count, curve and titan sample, and a summary as `ENCPOOL` lines.
- `tools/encounter_pool/run_test.py` builds the PK3 and runs the dump in each setup. It runs minimized with a private config and a 45 s limit per run, stops at `ENCPOOL done` or on any doomerfall script or fatal error, and checks the results.

### How a class gets into the pool (in order)

1. It must be a hostile monster (`ISMONSTER`, not `FRIENDLY`) with health above 0 and a See state. Colourful Hell's wrapper stubs, such as `BrownImp`, are not flagged as monsters, so this drops them automatically.
2. It must count as a kill, or match an `include` rule. Most summoned helpers do not count as kills: Pain Elemental skulls, Colourful Hell ghosts and eggs. DRLA's Lost Souls, Hell Mine, and Armageddon Imp, Lost Soul and Baron also do not count as kills, so they are included by name.
3. It must not match an `exclude` rule. Excluded: `RLCC*`, `RLDRPG*`, `*Reborn`, `RLAdaptive*` (random variant selectors), Chex and beta classes, `ID24*`, ZDoom's stock `Stealth*`, a DRLA debug class, and 20 Colourful Hell summon or phase classes.
4. No other loaded class may replace it. This is checked with `GetReplacement`, which includes DRLA's skill `ReplaceActor`, so stock monsters drop out when DRLA is loaded.
5. Its sprite frames must exist in the loaded data. The engine defines Heretic, Hexen, Strife and ID24 monsters in every game, and this check removes them.
6. Kind comes first from a class-name pattern. Colourful Hell's real classes mostly have no stock parent, so the name decides. Otherwise the nearest ancestor that is a stock class or a `kindclass` entry decides, such as DRLA's `RL*Base` classes. A class with no kind is skipped and listed as `no-kind`.
7. Tier and points:
   - An `own` rule makes the class tier 8.
   - Otherwise the first matching tier rule gives a colour or family group, and points = kind points × the group multiplier.
   - With no match, the group is Stock, tier 1, ×1.
8. Traits are:
   - the kind's defaults
   - `flies` when the class has `FLOAT` or `NOGRAVITY`
   - `stealthy` when it has `SHADOW` or `STEALTH`, or uses fuzzy rendering
   - any `trait` rules in the data file
9. Civil War safety. A class is unsafe when it is an Arch-vile (revives), has the `summons` trait, or matches an `unsafe` rule.

### Where the mod-specific lists came from

A throwaway script read the extracted DECORATE in `GameMods/ExtractedData` (DRLA Monsters and Colourful Hell). It is not committed and lives in the session scratchpad. The script listed:

- **Unfiltered `A_RadiusGive`:** calls that give to monsters or corpses with no class or species filter, mapped to the owning monster. Helper projectiles are mapped to the monster that spawns them.
- **`Thing_ChangeTID`:** `MonsterBoss.txt:21` is `RLMonsterpackArtifactGuardian`, and `ArmageddonRevenant.txt:631` is `RLRevenihilatorDrone`, which `RLArmageddonRevenant` spawns.
- **Summoners:** monsters that spawn other monster classes. Spawns in death, pain, raise, "Tickles" and "Grow" states were ignored. In those states Colourful Hell turns a monster into another colour, as a reaction to the Abyss Pain Elemental or an Arch-vile's grow aura. That is not summoning.

The results were written by hand into the data file as class-name lists.

## Did not fit the tags cleanly

- **Black and White monsters that are not EX titans** (`BlackImp1`, `WhiteImp2`, `BlackCybie2` and so on; 2,500 to 21,000 health). The design's ladder has no place for them. They are tier 8 with a placeholder price: kind points × √(health ÷ stock health) × 2. That gives very uneven numbers, from 21 (BlackImp1) to 206 (Whitemind2). This is a question for the owner.
- **DRLA bosses** (`RLBruiserBrother`, `RLAgonyElemental`, `RLTerminator`, `RLAbominant`, `RLSuperTechvile`, `RLFinalTechvile`). The design says "own value" but gives none. They are tier 8 with a placeholder: kind points × √(health ÷ stock health). Bruiser Brother is 13.9, which is cheaper than a Nightmare Baron at 15.4, so this is clearly too low. This is a question for the owner.
- **Wildcard kinds** are engineering picks by the closest stock look: Bruiser Brother as Baron, Agony Elemental as Pain Elemental, Terminator as Cyberdemon, and Abominant as Mancubus.
- **Former Captain** (`RLFormerCaptainPlasmaRifle`, `RLEliteCaptainTristarBlaster`) descends from DRLA's chaingunner base, so its kind is ChaingunGuy. Appendix C lists Captain as its own row, but it is not one of the 17 kinds.
- **DRLA weapon variants** of one species and family share one price. For example, every `RLEliteHuman*` costs 1.75. Hitscan and projectile are taken from the kind, because they cannot be read from the class at runtime.
- **Summoners are counted conservatively.**
  - Colourful Hell's Brown monsters spawn shield helpers flagged as monsters, such as `BrownImpShieldMini`, so `BrownImp2` and others are tagged `summons` and Civil War unsafe.
  - `RLLostSoul` is unsafe under the design's literal rule: it gives `RLAgonyElementalCounterAttack` to every monster within 128 units.
  - `RLSpiderMastermindBFG`'s BFG ball gives a damage effect to monsters, which is probably harmless in a war, but it is still unsafe under the literal rule.
- **Not in the pool (`no-kind`):**
  - DRLAX `DRLAX_PitGuardian`, `MolochWraith`, `RLIscream*`, `RLamv2k9Spider` and `RLBruiserBrother2`
  - Colourful Hell helpers such as `BlackRevEx2`/`3`, `WHOLETTHEDOGSOUT`, `SlimyWorm`, `DeepTentacle` and `MiniSentinelSpider`

  Each could get a `kindclass` line if it should be spawnable.
- **`RLAgitatingRevenant`** is in the pool as a Standard Revenant. Its definition is not in DRLA Monsters' actors, so it presumably comes from DRLAX. Not checked.
- **`RLFinalTechvile`** may be a phase of `RLSuperTechvile` rather than a spawn candidate. Not checked.
- **`capped_share`** (0.10) is a placeholder. Section 5.3 says a capped class gets "a maximum share" but gives no number.
- **Titan chance at level 20** is set to 0.10, a straight line extended back from the 25 and 30 anchors. Below 20 it is 0.

## Failed approaches

- A "must count as a kill" rule on its own dropped real DRLA monsters, such as the Armageddon Imp and every DRLA Lost Soul. The `include` list was added for them.
- Kind by ancestry alone missed every Colourful Hell real class, because most have no parent. The name patterns come first for that reason.
- The first summoner scan counted Colourful Hell's colour changes on death and on conversion. That flagged nearly every CH monster, so those states are now skipped.
