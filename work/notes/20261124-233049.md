# monstersqueeze: monsters narrow only against level geometry (run 20261124-233049)

Continues `work/notes/20261124-212938.md`. That run gave monsters a narrower movement width everywhere on Daggerfall maps,
so they also stood closer to the player, to each other and to walls than their sprites suggest. Owner (2026-11-24): the
narrow width does one thing only, letting monsters squeeze through gaps in the level that are too narrow for their stock
width. Everywhere else they keep stock width. Door leaves (`DFDoorBlocker`) count as level geometry. Rule: squeeze only
when stuck.

## Plan

1. Monsters move at stock width. When a move fails against level geometry or a door leaf, retry at the narrow width.
2. Do not let "walked into a wall" count as stuck, or open-room wall spacing changes (see Design: the gap test).
3. Bodies block each other at stock radii (only a squeezing monster meets a door leaf at its narrow width).
4. Prove: same doorways as last run, stock monster-monster, monster-player and open-wall spacing, hit tests, stock maps.

## Design

- `AActor::SqueezeRadius()` (`actor.h`): the old rule, `radius × DD_MonsterMoveScale` for monsters, `radius` otherwise.
  `AActor::MoveRadius()` now returns `radius`, except for the actor in `DD_SqueezeActor` while it is being retried.
  Off Daggerfall maps the scale is 1, so `SqueezeRadius == radius` and `P_TryMove` makes exactly one stock attempt.
- `P_TryMove` (`p_map.cpp`): the original body is now `static P_TryMoveOnce`. The wrapper tries stock; if that fails, the
  blocker is a line or a door leaf (not another actor), and `DD_InGap` says yes, it sets `DD_SqueezeActor` and tries again.
- Movement sites that read `MoveRadius()` are now only those used inside that check: the line box and line iterator in
  `P_CheckPosition`, and the portal line box. `DD_MoveBlockDist` (in `PIT_CheckThing` and `P_TestMobjZ`) is the stock
  radius sum, except squeezer vs. door leaf. Everything the previous run changed elsewhere (teleport, player-start stomp,
  wall sliding, the intersector searches, `P_FindFloorCeiling`, the dropoff check in `P_NewChaseDir`, the thing iterator
  radius) is back to stock code, so the vendored diff is smaller than before.
- The gap test, `DD_InGap` (`dd_monsters.cpp`). Squeeze only if both hold:
  - (a) the narrow body could carry on forward by `radius − squeeze width`. Walking into a wall or a corner fails this,
    so the monster stops at its stock distance.
  - (b) no sideways step of up to that distance would let the stock body fit. Hugging a wall at a shallow angle fails
    this.
  - A monster already squeezed where it stands (its stock body overlaps geometry) needs only (a), so it can carry on out
    of a doorway past an open leaf, where only the leaf is beside it.
  - Standing still (the placer): the stock body fits nowhere within that distance in 8 directions.
  - The first version tested "blockers on opposite sides closer than the stock width". That failed at doorway mouths,
    where the blockers are the two wall faces ahead: `monster_doors` went to 1 of 46. Then (a)+(b) alone gave 27 of 46
    (blocked on the far side beside the open leaf). Adding the already-squeezed rule gave 45 of 46.
- The door leaf class is looked up once per map (`PClass::FindActor("DFDoorBlocker")`), so maps without door packages
  pay nothing.
- `DD_MoveRadius()` (ZScript) now returns the squeeze width. Its users still want that width: the door script's `Clear()`
  (leaves are geometry, so a door waits only for a body's squeeze box), the door check, and the width harness.
- Encounter placer: bodies are stock size (spacing between monsters is stock). `Check` squeezes by the same rule as
  `P_TryMove`, in standing mode, for leaders on their exact markers and the fit measurement. Spread mooks take only spots
  their stock body fits, so they never start clipped into a wall. `fitRadius` is now a stock radius, and the planner's
  `Fits` compares `m.radius <= fitRadius`.
- Test-only changes: `dd_encounter_test.cpp` now counts a monster overlapping at stock width as stuck only if it cannot
  take a short step in any of 8 directions by `P_TryMove`'s rule. The door check's `Stand` lets a monster stand at its
  squeeze width when its stock body does not fit there.

## Known gaps

- Placement at area level 30 takes about 2 s (was 1.1 s). Stock-size bodies fill the map sooner (1,142 crowded
  monsters vs 474). Removing the squeeze from mook spots did not change this, so the cost is the search, not the gap test.
- The gap test is a heuristic. In a corridor only slightly wider than a monster, it may squeeze a few units nearer a wall
  than stock. A monster leaving a gap may hug the wall beside it until it steps clear.
- While a monster stands squeezed and something calls `P_FindFloorCeiling` or `P_ChangeSector` on it (a moving sector),
  the stock box is used. Daggerfall doors are actors, not sectors, so this should be rare.
- On a squeeze retry, a monster that bumps a push-activated line could trigger it twice. Converted maps use no
  monster push lines that I know of.
- Polyobjects still use the stock radius (unchanged).
