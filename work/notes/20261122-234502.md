# Main quest dungeon traversal check

Branch `agent/dungeon-20261122-234502`. Nothing was fixed, and no quest, trigger, or game-content system was touched.

## Result

All 14 main quest dungeons and 5 random-seed dungeons convert, load in UZDoom with their mesh, door and action packages, and can be walked. **None is shown to be fully traversable.** In 12 of the 19, a 21-wide, 56-tall body walking from the entrance reaches 41 to 67% of the converted floor positions. The other 7 start in a closed-off pocket: Sentinel, Woodborne Hall, Mantellan Crux and the two 5-block graveyards reach 6 to 12%, Lysandus' Tomb 15%, and Scourg Barrow 0.7% on foot (72% once it can jump out of the coffins).

What the other 33 to 40% is in each dungeon was **not established**. It is either gated by a system Doom lacks, sealed in Daggerfall's own data, or lost by the converter. I could not tell these apart by script, so that part is `Unknown` unless a gate is named below.

## The main quest dungeon list

`--list-main` in `tools/dungeon_converter/Program.cs` prints it. The 14 map ids are copied from `DaggerfallDungeon.IsMainStoryDungeon` in DFU, because that class needs Unity. All 14 have a dungeon.

Scourg Barrow, Direnni Tower, Shedungent, Castle Necromoghan, Privateer's Hold, Daggerfall (castle), Tristore Laboratory, Sentinel (castle), Woodborne Hall, Wayrest (castle), Orsinium, Mantellan Crux, Lysandus' Tomb, Castle Llugwych.

"Castle Wayrest" in the feedback is the Wayrest entry. Names repeat across regions, so the converter now accepts `"#<map id>"`.

## How it was checked

All numbers come from these scripts. Nobody played any of it.

- `reach_all.py` converts a dungeon, then runs a walker **inside UZDoom** (`tools/dungeon_converter/reach/zscript.zs`). The walker is a 21-wide, 56-tall actor that does a flood fill from the entrance over a 12-unit grid, using the engine's own `TryMove`. It does not use the converter's links. Stage 1 is walking (step up at most 24, fall off any ledge). Stage 2 adds crouching (28 tall). Stage 3 adds jumping, modelled as a step of up to 40 (Doom's default jump rises about 40 units).
- Each converter node counts as reached if a searched state sits on it at about its height. The script then groups the unreached nodes and labels each group `sealed` (no reached floor beside it), `height-gap` (a reached floor is more than 24 units away vertically) or `step-ok-but-unreached` (it touches reached floor at a walkable height, so suspect Broken).
- Doors count as open, because they are separate objects and not in the map file. The earlier door check passes 46 of 46 two-sided doors in Privateer's Hold.
- Locks, lifts, switches, spells, riddles, teleporters and moving walls are **not modelled**. The moving action models are removed from the map file, so a closed portcullis or moving wall counts as open, and the lifts and coffin lids that give vertical access are missing.
- Water is non-solid in the check, so swimming is not exercised.
- `smoke_all.py` exports each dungeon's mesh, door and action packages and loads them in the mesh build for 12 seconds. All 19 start, spawn every door, and load every action record with no script error (for example Wayrest: 224 doors, 153 records, 32 door links).
- Timings are wall clock with the window minimized, and they are not performance numbers. The walker needs 3 to 16 s per dungeon in total, including engine start and the map load (2 to 4 s to the first script line).

The earlier `check_map.py` is out of date and was not used for results. It still uses a 32-wide probe and the old door lines, and it reported 3% reachable on Privateer's Hold. I did adjust it (21-wide probe, per-edge height error logged), but did not rely on it. `frontier.py`, `refused.py`, `seams.py`, `seam_summary.py`, `seam_nodes.py` and `block_edges.py` are small diagnostics used to read the results.

### What the sealed 5-block dungeons show

The B'ast Graveyard and the Greenham Vaults have 5 blocks each and **no link at all between blocks** in the converter's graph. The start block (M0000004) is 848 positions, and its east-edge corridor sits at floor 0 with a ceiling of 96, while the neighbouring block's floors at the same seam are 384 and 960. Daggerfall Unity also places every block at height 0 (`DaggerfallDungeon.cs` line 319), so this may simply be how those dungeons are built: a closed tomb with filler blocks. I did not confirm that in Daggerfall. If a human knows these two dungeons, they can settle it in a minute.

## Broken problems, ranked by impact

None of these is proven to block a real route. They are the places where the check found the conversion disagreeing with itself.

1. **The engine refuses links the converter drew (every dungeon, about 5 to 8% of positions).** The converter's graph reaches more than the engine walker does. Examples: Privateer's Hold 844 links in 335 places (34 through doors), Scourg Barrow 3,416, Wayrest 1,650. Most are level (694 of 844 have no height change, 77 more change by 24 or less) and most targets (523) have a 96-high opening. They look like walls the converter did not draw or that the collision fit moved, consistent with the 3 to 4% false walls already measured in the parity work. Not yet traced to a cause.
2. **Closed-off entrance areas (6 dungeons).** Sentinel (8.3%), Woodborne Hall (5.8%), Lysandus' Tomb (15.5%), Mantellan Crux (11.6%), and the two graveyards. Woodborne Hall is the strongest Broken suspect: 24,402 nodes touch reached floor at a walkable height.
3. **Height gaps of 192 and 576 units between reached floors and the next area (at least 14 dungeons).** Typical gaps: -192 (Direnni, Daggerfall, Orsinium, Castle Llugwych), -576 (Woodsley Manor, Orsinium, Tower of T'uzun, Lysandus), -288 (Daggerfall, Orsinium), +1344 (Tristore and T'uzun upper levels, near rotate/spell/move records). These need a ramp, stairs, a lift, climbing or levitation. I could not tell which. Where an action record is near, a lift is plausible.
4. **Large "sealed" remainders (about 30 to 40% of every dungeon).** Cause unknown, see above.
5. **Tooling only:** `check_map.py` is obsolete for dungeons with door objects and fat for the 21-wide body. Replace it with `reach_all.py`.

**Climbing or levitation:** the walker has no climb or levitate stage, so every `height-gap` group in the per-dungeon `reach.txt` is where one would be needed unless a lift, stair or ramp exists that the converter missed. The biggest are Wayrest (9,722 nodes, 264 down), Daggerfall castle (6,029 nodes, 288 down), Wayrest again (5,415 nodes, 192 down), Orsinium (1,431 nodes, 576 down), Direnni (1,431 nodes, 192 down) and Castle Llugwych (1,681 nodes, 192 down). The jump stage changed the result in only two dungeons: Scourg Barrow (+71.6%, the coffin exit) and Woodsley Manor (+4.9%). That means very little in these dungeons is gated by a 40-unit jump.

## Failed approaches

- The first run used `check_map.py` as is. It reported 3.4% reachable on Privateer's Hold, because its probe is 32 wide (the world is 0.75 scale with a 21-wide body) and it expects door lines that no longer exist. Fixing the probe took it to 3.8% only, because the check follows the converter's own node list, where a few doorway nodes fail. The in-engine walker replaced it.
- Running the batch with `run_in_background` stalled on the second dungeon three times, with no log. Foreground runs worked, so the batches here were run in the foreground. I did not find why.
- The walker's step size means narrow gaps less than about 21 wide are missed. Doorways were checked by the earlier door test, not by this one.
