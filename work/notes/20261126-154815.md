Goal (`skillcore` 04): add Rifles the way card 01 added Shotguns, Automatics and Explosives and card 03 added
Pistols. Skill only, no perks. Done when Rifles shows on a new character's sheet, Doom receives its value, a save and
load keeps it, and an old save loads. Do not change class lists.

## Step 2: verifier finding (Precision is not a rifle list)
I had written that every non-cell, non-rocket gun with DRLA's single-shot rifle token is a ballistic rifle. **That was
wrong.** The Assault Rifle Chaingun (`AssaultRifleChaingun.txt:133`), the Assault Rifle Minigun
(`AssaultRifleMinigun.txt:170`) and the Autocannon (`master/misc/Autocannon.txt:68`) also give that token and reload
from `Clip`. The owner's card (`work/backlog/rifles-skill.md` in the main checkout) says these three are Automatics,
not rifles. The rifles research had warned that the token is not a clean list. Mapping Precision to slot 8 would
have put them in Rifles.
Fix: `SkillSlot(Precision)` is back to -1, so no category has the Rifles slot until `rifletag` 01 decides which guns
are rifles. I chose not to move Automatic ahead of Precision, because that would reorder card 01's precedence, which
is another card's decision. The three guns are in the weapon test.
Rerun: **RESULT PASS** (49 checks). `WEAPCAT RLAssaultRifleChaingun precision -1`, `RLAssaultRifleMinigun precision -1`,
`RLAutocannon precision -1`, `RLMarksmanRifle precision -1`. The new check "Assault Rifle Chaingun/Minigun and
Autocannon do not have the Rifles slot" passes. Only zscript changed, so the C# builds and the sheet test results
above still stand.
