# Jumping perks and falling damage (skillperks 01) — 2026-11-26 night run

## Plan

1. Prerequisite: falling damage in Doom on Daggerfall maps, by Daggerfall Unity's own rule.
2. Jumping skill effects: height, double jump at 50, falling immunity at 75, triple jump at 100.
3. Perk window: mark the four Jumping perks as working.
4. Paired test (test hook outside game source), stock E1M1 checks, demo folder, manual.

## Findings

- **Daggerfall's rule exists and is usable.** `AcrobatMotor.CheckFallingDamage` + `PlayerHealth.ApplyPlayerFallDamage`:
  the fall is measured from `fallStartLevel`, set on the first frame the player is not grounded (so from the takeoff
  point of a jump, not the apex), to the landing point; damage = `(int)(5 * (metres - 5))` past a 5 m threshold, taken
  straight off HP (no armor). No damage when landing in outdoor water; regaining a climbing hold or slow fall resets the
  start. `EnemyMotor.ApplyFallDamage` uses the same numbers.
- Because the fall starts where the player left the ground, **jumping in place can never hurt** under this rule, at any
  height. That is exactly the owner's "must never hurt jumping in place" condition, with no special case.
- The hidden Daggerfall pawn takes no falling damage: its motor is disabled while the sync is engaged
  (`DoomerfallPositionSync` calls `ClearFallingDamage`). So there is no double damage.
- Scale: Doom maps are Daggerfall's layout at 75%, and Daggerfall has 40 units per metre, so **30 Doom units = 1 m**
  (`DoomerfallPositionSync.cs` header). The comment at `dd_bridge.cpp:802` ("75 units per metre") looks wrong but is
  not used here.
- Player HP: a Doom hit on the bridged player is sent to Daggerfall as an HP event (`DD_BridgeHpHit` in
  `P_DamageMobj`), and Doom HP = Daggerfall HP 1:1. So falling damage is a normal `DamageMobj` of type `'Falling'`
  with no armor, damage factor or protection; Daggerfall applies it to its own HP.
- Perks follow the **base** skill value until the owner answers (skillcore 01). Doom only had the live value
  (`Actor.DD_SheetSkill`), so I added `Actor.DD_SheetSkillBase` (3 small engine additions, inactive without a sheet).

## What was built

- `mods/doomerfall/src/zscript/DoomerfallJump.zs` (registered in MAPINFO/ZSCRIPT). Active only with `dfi_rules:1`
  (generated Daggerfall map in a bridge session) and a Daggerfall character; otherwise it restores the default JumpZ
  and does nothing.
  - Falling damage: last ground height → landing height, Daggerfall's numbers. Resets on teleports/window shifts
    (moved farther in one tic than its speed allows), flight, noclip, climbing hold (no gravity), water, death.
  - Height: JumpZ = default × sqrt(height factor), height factor = 1 + s/100 + 0.25 from 25 on (height goes with the
    square of jump velocity, so the card's "height" is applied literally: ×2.25 height at 100 = ×1.5 velocity).
    Skill clamped to 0..100.
  - Mid-air jumps: 1 at 50, 2 at 100, refilled on landing, same strength as a ground jump (vertical velocity set, not
    added). Not from a climb's hold, not while crouched or swimming, not where the map forbids jumping.
  - Immunity at 75. Below 75 no reduction (curve is open).
- Engine: `DD_BridgeSkillBase` (dd_bridge.cpp), `DD_SheetSkillBase` thunk (vmthunks_actors.cpp), declaration in
  actor.zs.
- `DoomerfallPerks.cs`: the four Jumping perks are marked built (the window no longer says "does not work yet").
- Test: `tools/jumping/jump_probe.zs` + `tools/jumping/run_jump_test.py` (its own pk3; the only hook in game source is
  the `testSkill` field and the one-line `Skill()` override).
- `manual.md`: "Falling and jumping".

## Engineering calls (owner may want to look)

- Mid-air jumps are also allowed after walking off an edge (no ground jump used first). Standard double-jump behavior;
  the perk text "jump once more in mid-air" reads that way too.
- An air jump replaces the vertical velocity, so it can stop a fall for a moment, but the fall damage still counts from
  where you left the ground (Daggerfall's rule), so it does not dodge falling damage.
- Jumping is not trained by jumping in Doom (no skill tally). Out of this card; noted in the manual.
