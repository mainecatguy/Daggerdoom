# Status key (location, time and health) in Doom — 2026-11-27 run 103722

Goal: Daggerfall's Status key (where you are, the time, your health) works in Doom, indoors and outdoors, in a paired run.

## Plan

Daggerfall's Status key (`InputManager.Actions.Status`, I by default) posts `dfuiStatusInfo`, and
`DaggerfallUI.DisplayStatusInfo` shows two chained message boxes: text record 22 (location, date and time) and then
`CreateHealthStatusBox` ("You are healthy." or the diseases and poisons). These are Daggerfall windows, so the key
follows the route the character sheet and spellbook keys already use: a Doom control sends a net event, the bridge asks
Daggerfall to open a menu, and Doom shows Daggerfall's rendered menu while Doom is paused.

- `bridge/doomerfall_bridge.h`: `DFB_UI_STATUS 13u` (bindings regenerated with `bridge/generate_bindings.py`).
- `UZDoom/src/dd_bridge.cpp`: `netevent df_status` opens `DFB_UI_STATUS` with Doom's world context. In Daggerfall the
  Status key itself pages from the first box to the second and then closes (`ExtraProceedBinding`), so while the status
  boxes are open Doom's Status key is sent to Daggerfall as Enter, the boxes' proceed key (`StatusKeyAsEnter`), instead
  of closing the whole menu as the other menu keys do. Escape and clicks are Daggerfall's own.
- `DoomerfallUi.cs`: `DFB_UI_STATUS` posts `dfuiStatusInfo`. Logs Daggerfall's area, Doom's area and the time.
- Mod: "Status (location, time, health)" in Doomerfall Controls (`MENUDEF.txt`), and the event is in the list the mod
  leaves to the engine (`Doomerfall.zs`).
- Key: **L** in the play installer and the demo. Daggerfall's own default, I, is Doomerfall's inventory key. L is not
  bound by Doom, DRLA or the other Doomerfall keys. Ordinary engineering call; rebindable.
- `manual.md`: a Status section.
- Test: `tools/status_key/statuskey.py` with `harness.zs`, and the Daggerfall-side logger
  `DoomerfallStatusTest.cs` (`-dfstatustest <file>`, its own file, no hook in game classes).

Indoors: Daggerfall follows Doom through doors (`DoomerfallTransition` calls `TransitionInterior`), so Daggerfall's
location text is its own and matches the place. Daggerfall owns the location, time and health; Doom only shows them.

## Play-test list
