# QA bot 3: high-level fight and explore commands

Read D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall\AGENTS.md first. Design: `work/notes/20261124-120622-qa-bot.md` sections 6 to 8. Builds on
QA bot card 1.

**Overnight only** for any run longer than a short check (2AM to 7AM).

## Goal
Cut the tool calls of a dungeon run by an order of magnitude, so long places are affordable.

## What to build
- `attack <ref>`: honest aiming (view angle and pitch as input) and firing (`BT_ATTACK`) until the target dies, is
  lost from sight, or a time budget ends.
- `clear_room [radius]`: honest fighting until no hostile is in sight; reports kills, damage taken, ammo used.
- `explore_until <condition> [budget]`: frontier search over unvisited walk-graph nodes, stopping on a condition
  (an actor kind or class in sight, a Daggerfall id, a window opened, health below a threshold, the exit seen).
- A local re-planner: when stuck, probe the engine around the player (dry-run moves) and route around.
- `actors` summary mode (counts by kind plus the nearest 10) as the default.

## How to prove it
- Privateer's Hold start to exit with these commands: compare calls and game time with card 1's run.
- One dungeon that needs levitation, once Doomerfall has levitation (see the owner question in the design note).
