# Fixed drops: Civil War must be seeded

Branch `agent/fixeddrops-20261125-082544`, continuing `work/notes/20261125-081147.md`. Goal: a Civil War dungeon
plays out the same in every game, whenever and however the player arrives, and each monster drops the same items
whether the player or the other army kills it.

## Plan

1. Find where the war's randomness comes from. Drops were already seeded per monster from its Daggerfall id at death
   (`dd_lootrng.cpp`, previous runs), independent of the killer. What was not seeded: the fight itself. Every living
   monster, its shots and their damage rolls drew from Doom's shared random streams, whose state depends on everything
   else in the game (the player's own ticks, the arrival, other actors). So which monsters died, where and when, differed
   between games.
2. Give the fight its own fixed randomness without changing any odds: each Daggerfall-placed monster ticks under its own
   random state seeded from its Daggerfall id, and each shot or monster it spawns gets a state drawn from it.
3. Test with a new paired harness that lets the war run unfrozen (the old one froze it) and compares fresh games, a
   save and reload mid-war, a run where the player walks and other code burns shared random numbers, and a run where the
   player kills exactly the monsters the other army killed.

## Design

Engine (`UZDoom/src/dd_lootrng.cpp`, small hooks elsewhere, all `[Doomerfall]`):
- `AActor::ddAiRng`: the live random state. `DThinker::CallTick` runs every thinker's tick under it when it is set
  (`DD_AiStateFor`, one line in `dthinker.cpp`). Outside a bridge session `DD_AiStateFor` returns null at once, so stock
  games and plain WADs never use it.
- A living monster with a Daggerfall id gets it the first time it ticks, seeded from the id alone (salt `SALT_LIVE`).
  Saved with the actor (`ddairng`).
- A missile or monster spawned while such a state runs (a shot, a Lost Soul) gets its own state, one step of the
  running one (`DD_LootInherit`). So a shot's flight, its hit and its damage roll are fixed too. Puffs, blood and other
  helpers already got a loot state there, unchanged.
- When a monster starts dying (`DD_LootDied`) the live state is cleared, so its corpse ticks as before and nothing it did
  while alive reaches its loot, which keeps coming from its id alone. `Revive` clears both.
- The odds, the AI and every random call are untouched; only the stream each draw comes from changes.

War time: the war starts when Daggerfall's facts reach Doom and the mooks are spawned, so it runs relative to that tic,
not to Daggerfall's clock or the player's arrival. Doom reads no Daggerfall time for a dungeon fight.

## Test

`tools/fixed_drops/civilwar.zs` (harness, test only) and `civilwar.py` (runner, reuses `paired.py`'s launcher and window
driver). Play folder `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\review\civilwar-play`, a copy of `fixed-drops-play` with this branch's
uzdoom.exe. Nothing else changed: no bridge, C# or mod change. Area level 10, DRLA set, real New Game arrival in
Privateer's Hold, which rolls Civil War. The player is out of the war: god mode, `CF_NOTARGET`.

War time counts from the tic the Civil War thinker first exists (the mooks are spawned when Daggerfall's facts arrive),
so it does not depend on when the player arrived. Runs:
- `fresh`, `fresh2`: watch; snapshot every Civil War monster (health, position) at war time 6 s and 12 s, log every
  death (war tic, monster, corpse seed), then stop the fighting and list the new loose items.
- `save`: the same, bundled autosave at war time 3 s. `load`: fresh copies of both games load it and watch on.
- `freshnoise`: the player paces about the start (not solid, not shootable) and the harness burns a varying amount of
  numbers from Doom's shared streams every tic (default, Chase, TryWalk, NewChaseDir, LookForPlayers and others).
- `freshkill`: at war time 2 s the player kills, with a plain (non-overkill) blow, exactly the monsters the other army
  killed in `fresh`; the rest are made dormant; the drops are listed.

Command: `python D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall\scripts\overnight\quiet_run.py --timeout 2600 -- python
tools\fixed_drops\civilwar.py D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\review\civilwar-play series fresh save load freshnoise freshkill`,
then the same with `fresh2` alone, then `civilwar.py <play> compare fresh save load freshnoise fresh2 freshkill`.

## Findings

1. **Before (old build; `review\civilwar-paired-baseline-oldbuild`, `review\civilwar-baseline.txt`), two identical
   fresh games diverged at once.** By war time 12 s: 28 deaths against 34; at 6 s, 55 of 231 monsters at different
   health or places; different drops. `CIVIL WAR FIXED DROPS FAIL`.
2. **After: `CIVIL WAR FIXED DROPS PASS` (`review\civilwar-paired\result-*.txt`).** fresh, fresh2, save, load and
   freshnoise all have the same 24 deaths at the same war tics with the same seeds, the same 207 survivors at the same
   health and exact position at 6 s and 12 s, and the same 42 drops (class and seed). The load resumed at war 107 and
   matched every later death. The war started at map time 11 to 12 in this series and 12 to 20 in earlier ones, so the
   arrival offset varied while the war did not.
3. **The killer does not matter.** In freshkill the player killed 22 of the 24. The other 2 had already been killed by
   the other army before war time 2 s, as in fresh. Its 42 drops are identical to fresh's (class and seed).
4. **Other code drawing from Doom's shared streams no longer changes the war** (freshnoise).
5. **A player who is present still changes the war, as expected.** With only the actor's `NOTARGET` flag (the old
   harness's setting), monsters near the start still targeted the player, so the walking-player run differed near the
   start (`review\civilwar-paired-run1`). With the player cheat `CF_NOTARGET` it matched. In real play monsters see and
   hunt the player, so once the player is seen, shoots or stands in the way, the war goes its own way from there. Each
   monster's drops stay fixed regardless.
6. **DRLAX gizmos are not seeded (outside this goal, reported as an oversight).** `DRLAX_GizmoSpawner` (35 tics after
   every map load) and the `STAT_STATIC` `DRLAX_GizmoHandler` place random machines and shrines from Doom's shared
   streams, with a counter that carries across levels (`Gizmos.zscript`). A Skull Shrine spawns a random skull: that is
   the source of the unexplained unseeded `RLBloodSkull` in `20261125-061121.md`, and of an `RLFireSkull` seen here. In
   noise runs a `DRLAX_DimensionalBox` or `DRLAX_AppraiserObject` appeared where fresh runs had none. The harness
   removes gizmos as they spawn and reports them, so the runs compare the war alone.

## Known gaps

- Drop seeds of a death that spawns missiles changed from before (a missile now takes one step of the running loot
  state). Still fixed per monster, which the goal allows. The old `paired.py` series was not rerun with this build.
- A monster summoned during play (Lost Souls) now has fixed live randomness, but its drops are still random (open
  question 20261125-081147).
- Arrival by another route into Privateer's Hold (travel from a town instead of New Game) was not run. Doom takes no
  input from Daggerfall's clock for the fight (the placer uses time only for timing logs), and the arrival tic varied.
- Helper actors that are neither monsters nor missiles tick under the shared streams outside their state actions (their
  actions use their own loot state, as before). None changed the result here.
- The war is not already partly over on arrival; it starts when the player arrives. The owner allows either.
