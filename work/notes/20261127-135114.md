## Design as built
- `DoomerfallShopIdentity` (new): a pure function of location + building key, so each shop is the same in every game.
  - **Breadth**: arcsine (U-shaped) in [0,1]. Kinds the shop does not favour weigh 0.03 (specialist) to 1 (generalist).
  - **Lean**: 1-5 favoured kinds (specialists fewer), from the shop type's classic table, in proportion to its group
    chances, rarity <= quality.
  - **Rhythm**: 50% trickle every 2/3/4 days, 50% big delivery every 7 (60%), 14 (25%) or 30 (15%) days on a fixed day.
    Nobody delivers every 5-6 days.
  - **Suppliers**: two or three regulars share 85% of deliveries; 15% go to a Pitman-Yor stick-breaking tail
    (discount 0.5, concentration 1). Draws are i.i.d. from the shop's fixed supplier weights (the de Finetti form of
    Pitman-Yor), seeded by (location, building, day), so no history is stored and every shelf agrees on the day's
    supplier. Each supplier favours one kind (x3). Newcomers keep appearing; they do not graduate to regulars (that would
    need the sequential form and stored history). Names and visible labels are card E.
- `DoomerfallShopLedger`: Doom ammo daily on the first shelf as before. Goods arrive only on the shop's delivery days and
  **refill the shelf to its share of the owner's stock target (15 + 2 x level)**, so a trickle shop gets small deliveries
  and a big-delivery shop thins out between deliveries ("sparse stock between", the card). Goods are drawn from classic
  delivery rolls by kind weight (lean x supplier), never by material or value.
- **First visit**: a never-stocked shelf starts at the shop's most recent delivery day (at most one interval, 30 days,
  back) and runs the sell-off since, with one day's Doom ammo (as before). Before, it was a single day; with a rhythm a
  single day would leave a big-delivery shop empty on most first visits. Remaining gap: stock on a day can still differ a
  little with the date of the first visit (goods from deliveries before that window).

## Verifier round 1 (fixed)
1-2. Removed the material budget I had added (it cut all weapon and armor supply to reach the Daedric target, an
     unapproved economy change, and gave general stores a per-building Daedric budget). The trickle cap of
     share x interval/7 is gone too: every delivery refills to the stock target.
3. Stock-size check has a real lower bound again: on the shop's delivery days, after the delivery, stocked goods must
   be at least 3/4 of the target (the original bound), plus never above the cap and new goods keep arriving. Between
   deliveries big-delivery shops thin out by design, so the bound is applied where the owner's design says the shop is
   stocked.
4. The first-visit catch-up was 120 days with daily ammo build-up; now it is only back to the latest delivery, with one
   day's ammo.

## Why the Daedric target is blocked
Mean Daedric pieces in stock = Daedric arrivals x mean stay. The approved odds (5% per weapon at level 30) and the
approved sell-off (Daedric at 1-3% a day, so stays of 30-100 days) are fixed. Refilling to the kept stock numbers
(target 75) brings about 7 new Daedric pieces a month to a top smith, so 7-8 are in stock. Rhythm only spreads the same
refills over fewer days: big-delivery smiths hold 7.3 instead of about 9. Reaching "about one" needs roughly 8x fewer
Daedric arrivals. With unchanged odds that means about 8x fewer weapons and plate arriving at top smiths. I measured it
in round 0: a budget of 0.4 Daedric-capable pieces a day gives 1.09 Daedric pieces, but a top smith then holds about 7-10
goods instead of the 75 the owner kept. The verifier rejected that as an unapproved economy change, rightly. Only the
owner can choose which rule gives way; see the questions in the report.
