# Light Carbine (`lightcarbine` 01), 2026-11-26 overnight

## Plan

1. Rebuild DRLA's Battle Rifle (`standard/BattleRifle.txt`, unmodded path) as a standalone ZScript weapon in the
   Doomerfall mod, so it needs no DRLA class: `DoomerfallLightCarbine` + magazine `DoomerfallLightCarbineClip`.
2. Copy its sprites, icon and sounds into the mod under new names (owner's exception), credit them in `art/README.md`.
3. Semi-auto with `+WEAPON.NOAUTOFIRE` and no `A_ReFire`; damage 6 x 1d3 (Battle Rifle 5 x 1d3, +20%).
4. Drop swap for the Battle Rifle monsters when DRLA Monsters is loaded.
5. Rifle tag; Daggerfall item path; test; demo.

## What was built

- `mods/doomerfall/src/zscript/DoomerfallLightCarbine.zs`
  - `DoomerfallLightCarbine : Weapon`. Same as the Battle Rifle with no mods: `A_FireBullets(2, 0.5, -1, dmg)`, 20-round
    magazine loaded from `Clip`, `AMMO_OPTIONAL`, slot 4, selection order 25, kickback 40, Alpha bob, the same frames
    and tics for fire (4 tics: Y Z then V B / W A), empty and partial reload, and the DRLA recoil nudges done as
    `A_SetPitch` in the frames. Damage 6 instead of 5. `+WEAPON.NOAUTOFIRE`, no `A_ReFire`.
  - Puff is `BulletPuff`; DRLA replaces it with `RLBulletPuff`, so with DRLA it hits exactly like the Battle Rifle.
  - The gun is its own world pickup (like stock guns). `DoomerfallGuns.PickupFor` and `dd_items.cpp IsGun/WeaponOf`
    already handle that, and the magazine is named `<weapon>Clip`, so hotkey swaps and drops carry it.
  - Left out (they need DRLA): mods, assembly, scavenging, perks, DRLA's weapon tokens and weapon limit.
- `DoomerfallLightCarbineDrops` (same file, in MAPINFO's handler list): on `WorldThingDied` of a monster whose drop list
  holds `RLFormerHumanBattleRifleDrop`, `RLFormerCommandoBattleRifleDrop` or `RLEliteHumanBattleRifleDrop` (Former
  Human, Former Commando, Elite Former Human, Former Cyborg and their CC/DRPG variants), the corpse is watched for 10 s.
  A `RLBattleRiflePickup` (exact class, so not the Modded one) that appears within 16 units of a watched corpse is
  that monster's drop; a coin decides, and the next tic it is replaced by a carbine with the same position, angle and
  toss. One rifle per death. Coin: the drop's fixed loot seed (`DD_LootSeed`) when it has one, so a Daggerfall-map
  drop is the same on every visit and no random number is consumed; else `random[DoomerfallCarbine]`.
- `DoomerfallWeaponCategory.IsRifle(w)`: the rifle tag. Only the carbine for now; `rifletag` 01 adds the DRLA rifles.
  The carbine has no skill category (reads "other"), like every rifle until the Rifles skill exists.
- Assets in `mods/doomerfall/src/sprites/lightcarbine/`, `graphics/DFLCICON.png`, `sounds/lightcarbine/`, `SNDINFO.txt`.
  Renamed `UACR*` -> `DFLC*` so they can never clash with DRLA's lumps.
- Test hook `DoomerfallLightCarbineTest.zs` (CVar `dd_carbine_test`), runner `tools/light_carbine/run_carbine_test.py`.
- `manual.md`: "Light Carbine" section. `art/README.md`: provenance of the copied DRLA files.

## Results (`python tools/light_carbine/run_carbine_test.py D:/dev/DoomDaggerfall-build/uzdoom-location/Release/uzdoom.exe D:/dev/DoomDaggerfall-build/carbine-test`)

- stock (DOOM.WAD + doomerfall.pk3 only): no script errors; carbine given, tagged rifle; a pull on the empty
  magazine reloads (20 loaded, 80 left) without firing; trigger held 40 tics = 1 shot; taps every 2 tics -> shots 8
  tics apart (taps every other tic gave 6; the Battle Rifle is 4); reload key fills the magazine; every hit 6/12/18.
- drla (DRLA + Monsters + DRLAX + mod): same checks pass; DRLA's Battle Rifle held 40 tics fires every 4 tics for
  5/10/15. Damage unit 6 vs 5 = +20%.
- drops: 200 kills (50 of each Battle Rifle monster): 200 Battle Rifle drops, 106 became carbines (53%), every swapped
  rifle gone; 100 loose Battle Rifles stay Battle Rifles. Earlier runs gave 46% and 38% (the 38% run was before the
  16-unit tolerance, when 2 of 200 drops were off the corpse spot by more than 1 unit and stayed rifles).
- stock Doom still starts: the stock run is DOOM.WAD E1M1 with the mod; no engine change was made.

## Failed approaches

- Pressing buttons from ZScript (`p.cmd.buttons |= BT_ATTACK` in `WorldTick`): useless, `G_Ticker` copies the net
  command over `player.cmd` before `P_PlayerThink`, and `WorldTick` runs after `P_PlayerThink`. The runner now drives
  the trigger with one `+exec`'d console line (`+attack; wait N; -attack; ...`). Waits on separate cfg lines do not
  serialize, aliases defined by a startup exec are not found, and `+wait` on the command line passes before the map.
  1-tic taps collapse under DRLA load on the private desktop, so taps are 2 down / 2 up.
- Watching DRLA's drop helper (`RLEnemyWeaponDrop`): it drops the rifle while it is being spawned, before
  `WorldThingSpawned` reports it, and a `ThinkerIterator` for it found nothing at that moment. Matching the dying
  monster's corpse works.

## Not verified / known gaps

- Daggerfall side not run: no Daggerfall code was needed (Doom guns are generic: name from the Tag, "<material>
  Light Carbine"; hotkey from Doom slot 4; magazine carried as gun state), but no paired game was launched. Play-test.
- No DRLA weapon tokens: DRLA perks that key on `RLStandardWeaponToken`/`RLRapidfireTypeWeapon` ignore the carbine.

## Verifier fixes (second pass)

- Reload frames were swapped: DRLA plays C-J when rounds are left and N-U when empty. Fixed (`ReloadPartial`).
- The drop swap lost the rifle's Daggerfall loot identity (the carbine was spawned outside any loot roll, so its
  material would not be fixed). New engine native `Actor.DD_TakeLootId(from)` (dd_lootrng.cpp, 6 lines; does nothing
  unless the source has an id, which only Daggerfall maps give) copies `ddLootId`/`ddLootRng` to the carbine.
  Not verified on a Daggerfall map (no paired run): play-test item 4.
- Pickup ammo now matches DRLA: the first carbine gives only the gun, another one gives 20 bullets (`HandlePickup`).
- Raise sound: DRLA's `weapons/weaponholster` (DSSWISH) copied as `DFLCUP`, set as `Weapon.UpSound`.
