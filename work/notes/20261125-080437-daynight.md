# Day/night, time-of-day light and weather in Doom

Branch `claude/hopeful-benz-984e6a`. Status: built and proven in Doom-only and paired runs. Owner answers (chat,
2026-11-25): weather is **visual only**; **Daggerfall's weather and ambient sounds are in** this task; **no lightning
flash** (as Daggerfall Unity).

## Result

Daggerfall owns time and weather; Doom only draws them. Exteriors (overworld `DDOVER01` and town maps) follow
Daggerfall's hour, season, climate sky and weather. Building interiors get Daggerfall's slight night tint and no
sky, daylight or rain. Dungeons are unchanged, as in Daggerfall (no time of day there).

| Piece | How |
|---|---|
| Bridge | `DoomerfallWorldLook.cs` sends text `sky <index> <frame> <stars> <night> <ambient> <weather> <loop> <sun> <elevation>` on change and every 5 s, read from Daggerfall's own hidden `DaggerfallSky`, `PlayerAmbientLight` formula, `SunlightManager` sun light, `PlayerWeather` and `AmbientEffectsPlayer.Presets`. No curve or table is ported. No bridge header change. |
| Sky | `dd_weather.cpp` rewrites the `DFSKY` texture from `SKYnn.DAT` (frame, afternoon mirror) or `NITEnnI0.IMG` with random stars, read at run time from `-ddarena2`. Nothing written to disk. About 1 ms per rewrite, only when Daggerfall's sky frame changes. |
| Light | Daylight = ambient + 0.5 (`dd_sun_share`) x sun x sin(sun elevation), Unity's gamma lighting on flat ground; a low sun lights the ground less. The town mesh (`hw_dfmesh.cpp`, which includes the town ground) takes it linearly; Doom sectors (overworld terrain, sprites) take `70 + 122 x daylight` (`dd_daylight_min`, `dd_daylight_max`) because Doom's sector light also darkens with distance. Both fitted to Daggerfall's own camera (below). |
| Fog | Daggerfall's exponential weather fog (rain 0.003/m, snow 0.005/m, fog 0.05/m grey) as sector fog and on the mesh. Fog colour = sky's lower left pixel, as `SetSkyFogColor`. |
| Rain, snow | Particles around the player, outdoors only. Rain uses a generated streak texture. Unlit, at daylight brightness. `dd_rain_drops` 24, `dd_snow_flakes` 10 per tic. |
| Sounds | Rain and cricket loops follow Daggerfall's presets; every ambient one-shot (birds, thunder, dungeon drips, cemetery, water) is forwarded as `amb <clip>` by a two-line hook in `AmbientEffectsPlayer.cs`. Doom loads the clip from `DAGGER.SND` into its sound system on first use (WAV wrapped in memory). Storm clips are non-positional; others are played 160 units from the player in a random direction. `dd_ambient_volume`. |
| Switch | `dd_weather 0` turns all of it off. Without Daggerfall nothing arrives and maps keep their own sky and light. `dd_sky "<line>"` feeds a line by hand. |

### Brightness fitted to Daggerfall's camera (owner's suggestion)

`paired.py run <name> --draw` keeps Daggerfall's 3D world drawn, and `-dfworldlookshots` saves its main camera at
the end of each step. Doom's screenshot from the same moment, at the same place and facing, is compared by
`brightness.py`: mean luma of the ground band below the horizon. Two limits:
- Daggerfall's main camera draws no sky (its sky has its own camera), so only the ground counts.
- The save is winter, so Daggerfall's ground is snow while Doom's is grass. Setting the month to summer
  (`hour:weather:month`) changes Daggerfall's sky but did not swap its terrain textures within 8 s.

So absolute levels can't be compared, but light multiplies texture in both engines, so each hour relative to
Daggerfall's brightest (summer 10:00) can. Summer noon stays Doom's full brightness, the old fixed look.

Graphics settings `gl_bloom 1; gl_light_shadowmap 1; gl_ssao 2; gl_lens 1` (architecture.md, Graphics) were on
for the final runs (`bright4`, `owcurve2`).

| Daggerfall step (ambient, sun, elevation) | Daggerfall ground | Daylight now | Doom town ground | Doom overworld now | Overworld before the fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| night (50, 0, below) | 0.20 | 0.21 | 0.24 | 0.21 | about 0.27 (sqrt curve) |
| 07:00 (83, 33, 15) | 0.37 | 0.39 | 0.41 | 0.38 | |
| 08:00 (119, 69, 30) | 0.66 | 0.67 | 0.68 | 0.66 | 0.82 |
| 15:00 (134, 84, 45) | 0.85 | 0.86 | 0.86 | 0.86 | 0.95 |
| winter noon (91, 62, 90) | 0.70 | 0.70 | 0.71 | 0.69 | 0.67 |
| summer 10:00 (142, 92, 60) | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 |

How it was fitted:
- Runs `run/bright1` (winter) and `bright2` to `bright4` (summer month), plus the overworld sweeps `owcurve` and
  `owcurve2` (`dd_daylight_min` 50, 70 and 90).
- `dd_sun_share` 0.5 fits best among 0.4 to 0.7.
- Weighting the sun by the sine of its height (sent by Daggerfall as `elevation`) removed the low-sun error: 08:00
  and 15:00 were 0.1 to 0.15 too bright.
- Overworld sectors take `70 + 122 x daylight`. A minimum of 50 is too dark at night (0.11), and 90 is too bright
  (0.33).
- Town ground is DFMesh, lit linearly, so the sector curve does not affect it.

## Frame cost

Measured before the graphics settings (bloom, shadow maps, SSAO, lens) were made standard; not re-measured with them.

- Sky rewrite: 0.8 to 1.0 ms, once per Daggerfall sky frame change (a few times per game hour).
- Rain/snow particles, fog, light: within run-to-run noise. Rain on 7.6 to 17.5 ms/frame vs off 8.7 to 12.5 ms over
  two alternating runs of three views (`cost-*`); other lanes were using the machine. No consistent cost was visible.
  Rain keeps about 580 particles alive, snow about 1,600 (of `r_maxparticles` 4000).

## Untested paths

- Sound was never heard: `quiet_run` adds `-nosound`. `DAGGER.SND` reads 459 clips and the rain loop path ran without
  error; the `amb` one-shots were not observed in a run.
- Dungeons: by code, Doom changes nothing there (place 3); no run entered one.
- Save and load: light and sky are reapplied on the next map tic and the next line (5 s), not tested.
- Fast travel and resting at high time scale: Daggerfall's sky stops updating above `MaxTimeScale`, so Doom keeps the last frame until it slows, as Daggerfall does. Not run.
- The overworld town LOD mesh at night next to sector-lit terrain.

## Play-test list (looks only a human can judge)

1. Noon, dusk and night in a town and on the overworld: does the brightness feel like Daggerfall? Fitted by pixel average to within 0.04 (above); tune `dd_sun_share` (0.5), `dd_daylight_min` (70) and `dd_daylight_max` (192) by eye.
2. Night overworld terrain: too dark or too bright at distance compared with the town mesh?
3. Rain streaks and snow flakes: size, speed, density (`dd_rain_drops`, `dd_snow_flakes`), and their brightness at night.
4. Fog weather: density on the mesh vs the terrain sectors.
5. Sky frames across dawn and dusk, and the afternoon mirror: any visible seam or jump?
6. Sounds: rain loop, crickets at night, birds by day, thunder; volume (`dd_ambient_volume`) and whether rain should be heard indoors (Daggerfall decides).
7. Tavern at night: is the slight purple tint noticeable or distracting?
