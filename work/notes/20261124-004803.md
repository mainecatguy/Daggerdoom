# Townspeople inside their homes (run 20261124-004803)

Branch `agent/townhomes-20261124-004803`. Demo: `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\review\townhomes\play.bat` (2:00 at night,
facing a lived-in house in Gothway; press Use on the door). `play-day.bat` is the same spot at noon.

## What was built

- **Who is at home.** `DoomerfallTownspeople.AtHome`: the person's schedule has no step at this hour (asleep between
  days; every daily plan ends by walking home), or the step is "inside" (kind 0) at their own home site. Dead ids are
  skipped. It is the same ledger as outdoors, so the dead stay dead indoors and out.
- **Daggerfall sends them.** Inside a building of a populated town, Daggerfall sends a townsfolk list (kinds 64 to 66)
  every half second. The list is marked `space = DFB_TOWNSFOLK_INTERIOR`, and its rows are already in the interior
  map's Doom units. The conversion is the same as the verified `-dfnpcreport` one:
  `space.InverseTransformPoint(p) * 0.75 / GlobalScale`. Each row also carries the spot's height in the new `z`
  field, so the right storey is found.
- **Bridge.** `dfb_townsfolk.pad` became `space`, and `dfb_townsperson.pad` became `z`. The layout and size are
  unchanged, so the protocol version is not bumped. New constants are `DFB_TOWNSFOLK_STREETS` and
  `DFB_TOWNSFOLK_INTERIOR`. The bindings were regenerated.
- **Doom** (`UZDoom/src/dd_townsfolk.cpp`, small change): it asks for a list indoors as well as outdoors. Interior rows
  are placed directly, with no treadmill, and the floor search starts at the spot's height. A list for the other side
  of a door (a street list arriving in a house, or the reverse) is dropped.
- **Spots.**
  - Spots come from Daggerfall's Rest markers, where it places quest people in a building. If a building has none, the
    Enter markers are used. All Gothway houses tried had only Enter markers (1 to 4 each).
  - The marker Daggerfall lands the player on is left out. That is the Enter marker nearest the street door used,
    `interior.EntryDoor`, the same rule as `PlayerEnterExit`.
  - Every resident of the house gets a fixed spot whether they are alive, dead, home or out: seed mod spot count, and
    a contested spot goes to the next free one in roster order. So nobody moves when a housemate is out or dead.
  - When there are more people than spots, a person stands one or two steps (28 Doom units each) around a marker. The
    seeded direction is tried first, then the other seven. Daggerfall's interior colliders must show nothing in the
    way and floor on the same level. If no direction is clear, the person shares the marker itself.
  - Facing comes from the seed.
- **Test hooks.** `-dftownreport` now also logs `townhome <ordinal> <key> <residents>` for each lived-in house. The
  ordinal counts building records in the converter's `--list-buildings` order, so the harness can find the door. This
  is in `DoomerfallTownspeopleTest.cs` only.
- **Tool.** `tools/townhomes/townhomes.py` (`assemble`, `run NAME [--hour H] [--home N|-1] [--kill] [--save|--load]`,
  `check`) and `harness.zs`. The route stands outside the door and lists the street, goes in and lists who is home,
  goes out and lists the street again, then goes back in and lists again.

## Failed approaches

- Assigning spots only among the people present: housemates shifted spots when one was dead or out. Spots are now fixed
  over the whole household.
- Leaving out the spot nearest the hidden player: this depended on where the player stood when the list was first
  built, so it would differ after a load.
- Leaving out the spot nearest each door: the interior's door list holds 20 doors, inner doors included, so nearly
  every spot was removed. The fix is Daggerfall's own landing rule, from `EntryDoor`.

## Known limits and design calls to review

- Sleepers stand upright with the idle sprite, because there is no lying-down pose (question for the owner).
- Only homes are populated. Workers who are "inside" at a workplace or tavern are not drawn there. Shops and taverns
  already have their stationary NPCs.
- A house with two street doors could leave out a different spot depending on the door used.
- Home occupants are as passive as outdoor townspeople (+NOINTERACTION in the converter's DFTownsperson). They have no
  talk and take no damage, the same as outdoors.
- `-dftownhour` is used by the demo to set the time. It is a test hook.
