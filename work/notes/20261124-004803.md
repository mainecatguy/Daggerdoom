# Townspeople inside their homes (run 20261124-004803)

## Plan
1. Daggerfall decides who is at home: no schedule step (asleep between days), or an "inside" step (kind 0) at their
   home site. Dead ids are skipped (same ledger as outdoors, so the dead stay dead indoors).
2. Inside a building of a town, Daggerfall sends a townsfolk list (kinds 64-66) marked `space = DFB_TOWNSFOLK_INTERIOR`,
   with rows already in the interior map's Doom units (same conversion as the verified `-dfnpcreport`:
   `space.InverseTransformPoint(p) * 0.75 / GlobalScale`), plus the spot's height in the new `z` field.
3. Spots: Daggerfall's Rest markers in the interior (where it places quest people), falling back to Enter markers.
   Spot = person seed mod spot count; collisions go to the next free spot in roster order; if more people than spots,
   offset 28 Doom units in a seeded direction. Facing comes from the seed. So the same person is in the same place each
   visit (unless an earlier-roster housemate already holds their spot).
4. Doom (`dd_townsfolk.cpp`) requests lists indoors too, places interior rows directly (no treadmill), starts the floor
   search from the spot's height, and drops lists for the other side of a door (stale street list in a house, or the reverse).
5. Test, then demo in `review/townhomes`.
