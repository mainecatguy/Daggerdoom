# Stationary NPCs in Doom

## Result

Daggerfall's layout NPCs now appear in generated Doom maps, using their real sprites at the 75% world size. They are passive, block nothing, and take no damage. Each has a stable ID that both games compute independently.

Demo: `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\review\stationary-npcs\play.bat`. It starts in Gothway facing a tavern door. Press Use to go in.

## What appears

| Location type | Daggerfall source | Doom actor | Live test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Building interiors | `BlockPeopleRecords` | `DFNpc` | Gothway tavern, 11 of 11 matched |
| Closed building | Same, switched off by Daggerfall | `DFNpc`, hidden | Gothway general store at 23:00, 1 hidden |
| Town exteriors | Flats with a nonzero faction | `DFNpc` | Daggerfall Coven, 2 of 2 matched |
| Dungeons | Flats in `RDBLayout.NPCFlatArchives` | `DFDungeonNpc` | Direnni Tower, 1 of 1 matched |

- NPC types are whatever those records name: residents, shopkeepers, guild staff, and fixed individuals.
- Outdoor NPCs are rare. Wayrest, Sentinel, and Gothway have none. Daggerfall Coven has 2.
- Privateer's Hold, the packaged dungeon, has no NPCs. Direnni Tower was converted only for its test run, so **the demo shows no dungeon NPC**.
- Wanderers (Daggerfall's mobile townspeople) are **not included**. Fixed NPCs were finished first, as the goal allowed.

## What wanderers need

`PopulationManager` keeps a pool of `MobilePersonNPC` objects around the player, randomizes them, and recycles any that get more than 150 world units away. They have no layout record, so the same person does not exist on a later visit, and the ID method above cannot apply.

Showing them needs:

1. An owner decision on identity: temporary IDs that last while the person is in the pool, or persistent townspeople generated per location.
2. A live Daggerfall-to-Doom list of pool members with position and appearance. Their motor must be stopped so they stand still.
3. Export of the mobile billboard archives, which are directional and are not part of the flat package.
4. Rules for recycling, since a person who vanishes in view would look wrong in Doom.

## How IDs match

`id = tag 6 (3 bits) | hash (61 bits)` of map ID, scope, block coordinates, building record, and the record's file offset.

- The converter computes it in `tools/dungeon_converter/NpcIdentity.cs`.
- Daggerfall computes it from the live `StaticNPC` in `DoomerfallNpcs.IdOf`. The file offset is recovered from `nameSeed`.
- Doom stores it in `args[0]` and `args[1]`, which saves already keep.
- `DoomerfallNpcs.FindNpc(id)` returns the live NPC for later talk and death work.

**Limit for later tasks:** outdoor and dungeon NPCs beyond Daggerfall's culling range lose their block parent, so `FindNpc` only finds them nearby. Interior NPCs are not affected.

## Presence

Inside buildings, Daggerfall sends the list of who is present (bridge kinds 60 to 63). Doom keeps indoor NPCs hidden until the list arrives. This keeps closed shops and owned houses empty.

## Verification

Run with `python tools/stationary_npcs/stationary_npcs.py`.

- Tavern, with and without DRLA: 11 of 11 IDs matched, position difference at most 0.01 units.
- Outdoors: 2 of 2 matched, with one constant offset between the two games' coordinates.
- Dungeon: 1 of 1 matched, position difference 0.
- No NPC was solid or shootable in any route.
- Stock `DOOM.WAD` E1M1: no errors and no bridge activity.
- DRLA E1M1: the same 3 script errors and 763 unknown-class lines as the previous build.
- Plain Daggerfall launch: no bridge activity and no exceptions.
- Registry hash unchanged across the test runs.
- Bridge runs log one `DaggerfallUI.SetupSingleton` null reference. The previous demo's log has it too.

## Not tested

- NPCs spawned while walking into a town from the overworld. This uses a separate spawn path in `dd_treadmill.cpp`.
- Indoor NPC visibility after loading a save.
- Whether idle animation frames play. They are packaged, but no automated test watches them.
- An owned house, and a guild hall's access rules. These use the same Daggerfall flag as the closed shop.

## Failed approaches

- Presence from active objects under the interior. Daggerfall's distance culling moves NPCs under an inactive parent, so far-away people in large buildings would vanish. It now uses each NPC's own active flag.
- Outdoor block coordinates from local position. The streaming world offsets every block by the location's origin, so the IDs did not match. It now counts from the first block.

## Other notes

- The town cache tag changed from `DDT2` to `DDT3`, so old caches rebuild themselves once.
- A Unity build modifies `addressables_content_state.bin`. That change is not part of this work and was left uncommitted.

1. Run `play.bat`. Are the tavern's people in sensible places, standing on the floor?
2. Do they look the right size next to furniture and doors?
3. Do they play their idle animations?
4. Can you walk and shoot through them with no effect?
5. Walk out of the town into the wilderness and back. Does anything duplicate or vanish?
