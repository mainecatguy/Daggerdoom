# Research: Skill XP from monsters, and how skills level (2026-11-26)

Research and proposal only. No game code was changed. Owner direction is in the goal card and
`work/backlog/skills-in-doom.md`. Scripts: `tools/skillxp/advancement_tables.py` (Daggerfall tables) and
`tools/skillxp/encounter_hp.py` (real encounter HP from the planner).

## Plan (as run)

1. Read Daggerfall's real advancement code and the preset class data. Done.
2. Measure real Doom monster HP per encounter placement and per dungeon, stock and Colourful Hell + DRLA. Done, by
   running the existing encounter budget test hook.
3. Build the anchor (30 Doom kills = one Daggerfall fight of 6 to 7 hits) into a damage formula and check it
   against the measured HP. Done.
4. Translate Daggerfall's 25-50, 50-75, 75-100 melee times into Skill XP and Doom kills. Done.
5. Work out use-based gain, level-ups, and write cards. Done (cards in `work/notes/20261126-010200-cards.md`, local copies in `work/queue/skillxp/proposed/`).

## 1. Daggerfall today (verified in code)

- Uses needed for the next point (`FormulaHelper.cs:319`):
  `floor(skill x skillMult x classMult x 1.04^level x 2/5) + 1`.
  - `skillMult` (`DaggerfallSkills.GetAdvancementMultiplier`): weapon skills and Hand-to-Hand 2, Archery 1, Critical
    Strike 8, Medical 12, languages 15, Running 50, Jumping 5, Dodging 4, most others 1 or 2. New skills (Melee and
    the gun skills) have no entry yet: they would return 0, which makes every point cost 1 use. **Any new skill must
    get a multiplier.** This proposal assumes 2, like the weapon skills.
  - `classMult` read from `ARENA2/CLASS*.CFG` (offset 54, 16.16 fixed point): Barbarian 0.64, Warrior 0.75,
    Ranger/Knight 0.79, Archer 0.83, Monk 0.87, Spellsword/Rogue/Assassin 0.91, Battlemage/Bard/Thief 0.96, most
    others 1.00, Mage/Sorcerer 1.04. Custom classes: 0.3 to 3.0 (`CreateCharCustomClass.cs:498`).
  - `level` is the character level, so every level makes every point about 4% dearer.
- A use is tallied **per hit** with the weapon (`WeaponManager.cs:427-435`: only when the swing hit an enemy). So the
  owner's anchor "a fight with 6 to 7 hits" = 6.5 weapon-skill uses (plus 6.5 Critical Strike, which is out).
- `PlayerEntity.RaiseSkills` (`PlayerEntity.cs:1360`) checks at most every 360 classic minutes = **6 game hours**, and
  raises each skill by at most 1 per check; uses reset to 0 on a raise (the excess is lost). Uses are scaled by the
  Reflexes setting (12.5% per step). A skill stops at 95 once any primary skill is at 100.
- Level (`FormulaHelper.CalculatePlayerLevel`): `floor((sum - startSum + 28) / 15)`, where sum = primary + major
  skills (minus the lowest major) + the highest minor (`SetCurrentLevelUpSkillSum`). **15 counted skill points = one
  level.** Skills outside the class's primary/major/minor lists never count.
- Guild training adds 10-20 x skillMult uses for gold and 3 hours (`DaggerfallGuildServiceTraining.cs:120`).

## 3. Proposal: Skill XP

### Formula

**Skill XP = HP of damage the player removes from hostile monsters. 1 HP = 1 Skill XP.**

- Damage counts only up to the monster's spawn health over its whole life (no overkill, no healing farm): a BFG ball
  on a 20 HP zombie gives 20.
- The player's own damage only (hitscan, projectiles, splash, the player's own summons if any). Infighting, crushers
  and other monsters give nothing.
- Resurrected and summoned monsters (Arch-vile raises, Pain Elemental lost souls, CH healers' raises) give nothing.
  Otherwise they are an endless XP farm. Kills only count once per monster (the mook ID already gives that).
- Townspeople and guards: open question (see questions). Lean: no XP, so murder is not a training method.

**Purchase price of the next point = `CalculateSkillUsesForAdvancement(skill, 2, classMult, level)` x 250 XP.**

250 is the anchor: 30 average level-1 monsters (55 to 72 HP measured) are 1,650 to 2,160 HP, and one Daggerfall
fight is 6.5 uses, so one use is about 250 HP of damage. Using Daggerfall's own formula keeps the rule in Daggerfall
(class difficulty, character level, skill level), and it escalates with the skill as asked.

Why damage, not kills: kills reward chip damage and finishing blows equally, and a Cyberdemon would equal a zombie.
Damage is what the owner suggested, needs no per-monster table, and only uses facts about the monster, so it fits
"the world does not scale to the player".

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1,000 | 4,000 | 8,000 | 11,750 | 15,000 | 15,500 |

class 237,000 / 568,000 / 1,172,000.

### How the anchor holds up (the main finding)

- **Per monster, at level 1: holds.** 30 monsters = about 6.5 to 8.6 uses' worth.
- **Per monster, higher up: mostly more than 30:1.** Worth of 30 average monsters in Daggerfall fights =
  30 x mean HP / 250 / 6.5 (mean HP from the section 2 table):

  | Area level | Stock | CH+DRLA |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | 1.3 (72 HP) | 1.0 (55 HP) |
  | 10 | 3.7 (199 HP) | 10.9 (588 HP) |
  | 20 | 3.8 (205 HP) | 8.0 (435 HP) |
  | 27 | 9.0 (485 HP) | 20.0 (1,083 HP) |
  | 30 | 2.3 (126 HP) | 4.0 (219 HP) |

  So 30 average L10 to L20 monsters are worth 3.7 to 10.9 fights. Prices also rise 1.04^level with character level;
  if the character's level equals the area level, divide by 1.48 at L10 and 2.19 at L20, giving 1.7 to 7.4 fights.
  Mean HP grows faster than 1.04^level at L10, L20 and L27, but not at L30 for stock (1.75x against 3.1x). Bigger
  monsters are worth more by design of a damage formula.
- **Per dungeon: very uneven, because pack size grows with area level.** Doom XP divided by Daggerfall's uses for
  the same dungeon (placements x 6.5): about 0.04 to 0.10 at level 1, 0.6 to 0.8 at level 10, 1.2 to 1.3 at level 20,
  1.3 to 4.8 at level 30.
- Concretely, a Warrior's balanced 25-50 (178,500 XP) is 58 to 142 level-1 crypts (3,090 stock, 1,260
  CH+DRLA), or 3.3 to 5.9 level-10 dungeons (the four level-10 plans: stock Mine 54,770 and Prison 30,440 HP, CH+DRLA
  Mine 47,790 and Prison 36,230 HP). 50-75 (427,000 XP) is 3.3 to 3.7 level-20 Covens (131,000 CH+DRLA, 116,090
  stock). 75-100 (880,000 XP) is about 1.4 level-30 Dragon's Dens (613,565 and 631,050). In Daggerfall, 25-50 is about 5
  level-1 crypts.
- **Early game risk:** at level 1 one point of a 25 skill costs 4,000 XP, and a whole level-1 crypt gives 1,260 to
  3,090. A new character gains less than one point per starting dungeon. This follows from the owner's 30x rule and
  the small level-1 packs, not from the formula's shape.

Options if the owner wants it flatter (not recommended without a decision):
- A. As proposed: 1 XP per HP everywhere. Simplest; high areas pay much more, low areas very little.
- B. Area-level rate: XP per HP = 55 / (mean monster HP at the area level), so 30 average monsters of any area level
  equal one fight. Needs a small table per area level; still uses only world facts.
- C. A small starting pool or a cheaper first band (for example skills below 30 cost half) to soften the early game.

## 4. Use-based gain beside purchases

Daggerfall's own path stays: `TallySkill`, `RaiseSkills`, the 6-hour gate, the 95 cap, the class and level factors.
Only the tallies are scaled, with a fractional accumulator per skill (Daggerfall's `skillUses` are whole numbers),
saved with Daggerfall's save.

The owner's wording supports two readings of "1/30 for skills whose use involves killing monsters":

- **Reading (a), 1/30 absolute:** each Doom kill feeds the weapon category's skill 1/30 of a Daggerfall fight. That is
  exactly the Skill XP rate (1 use per 250 HP of damage with that category), so the weapon skill gains passively as
  fast as the pool, and a one-gun player effectively gets double into that skill. Buying would not be the main source
  for weapon skills.
- **Reading (b), 1/100, then 1/30 again for Doom's monster counts (lean):** use-based gain is 1/100 everywhere, and
  for kill skills the 30x monster count is divided out too, so 30 Doom kills give 1/100 of a Daggerfall fight's uses
  (1 use per 25,000 HP with that category). Buying stays the main source. Example: Warrior 25-50 by passive use alone
  needs 714 uses = 17.9 million HP of damage. It is a trickle, about 1% of the pool's rate.

## 5. Level-ups

**Purchases should feed level-ups through Daggerfall's normal rule.** A bought point is a permanent skill point:
`SetPermanentSkillValue`, `SetSkillRecentlyIncreased`, `SetCurrentLevelUpSkillSum`, then `CheckForLevelUp`, exactly
as `RaiseSkills` does.

What character level still does (`PlayerEntity.Level` readers, checked in code; `WorldLevel.cs:1-6` says character
rules keep reading it while world scaling uses area level):

It is Daggerfall UI, and the attributes reach Doom through the sheet (`DoomerfallSheet`, live stats), so they change Endurance-based max HP and every Doom effect that reads stats. Probably the largest thing a level gives |

Judgement: **still feed level-ups.** Every effect above is Daggerfall's normal character-level rule, and all of them
already follow use-based raises, so a bought point should count the same; otherwise level, training prices, loans,
lockpicking and quest offers would drift away from the character's real skill. Nothing here scales the world. The
real consequence is pace: level now follows how fast Skill XP is earned. If Skill XP is fast (area level 30
dungeons, section 3), the character levels fast too. Each level gives 4 to 6 attribute points (and through Endurance, more
max HP), and once casting is built it strengthens the player's spells. It also raises guild training prices, unlocks
harder quest offers and higher guild quest ranks, raises the loan cap and improves interior lockpicking sooner. If
Skill XP is slow (area level 1), all of these come late, attribute growth and spell power included. The owner should know this before choosing the Skill XP rate.

Two catches:
- Only primary, major and the highest minor skill count. **The new gun skills are in no preset class's lists**, so
  buying them would never level a preset character. The classes need the new skills in their lists, or a rule. This
  is an owner question (classes and class maker are design).
- Purchases ignore the 6-hour gate (it is a use-based pacing rule). They keep the 95 cap once a primary is at 100.

Rough level pace: 15 counted points per level, early points about 4,000 to 5,000 XP each, so about 70,000 XP per
level at the start and about 300,000 to 450,000 per level near skill 75 at level 20.

## 6. Ownership

- Daggerfall owns the pool, prices, purchases, skills and level (permanent state, rules).
- Doom reports damage dealt per weapon category, after the cap rules above (live combat state). It never decides XP
  or prices. Without Daggerfall, Doom sends nothing and plays as before.
- The pool goes into Daggerfall's save like other player state.
