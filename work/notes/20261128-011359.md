# Exterior collision 02: one metric on the emitted map (2026-11-28 overnight, partial)

Stopped early: usage limit reached. The metric work is done and committed. The set baselines and the error reduction
are not done yet.

## Done

- **(a) Cache key follows the code.** `FidCodeHash.cs` walks the IL of `LoadOrBuildDfSide` and every method of this assembly
  it reaches (calls, lambdas, local functions). It hashes each method body with member references written as names
  (compiler ordinals removed), plus the values of static fields and primitive readonly arrays those methods read. Changing
  the legal-position rule, the body test, the flood, a tier constant, or exterior mode gives a new key. An unrelated
  converter change keeps the old one. `FidVersion` is now 3.
- **(b) Reach filter.** `FidLegal` (Fidelity.cs) finds legal positions with LegalPositions' rule, rows in parallel. It then
  floods from the entrance in 8-unit moves: up at most FloodDf's rise, down any drop, onto the highest floor within that
  rise, only where the body fits. Contact, Climb, the air-flood seeds and the worst-place viewpoints now use only these
  reachable positions. Check against Parity's validated FloodDf on Privateer's Hold: every FloodDf 16-unit column is
  reached, plus 369 more (3.7%, from the finer pitch). 79,197 of 112,181 legal positions are reachable. Privateer's Hold
  goes from **1.408 to 1.634**: the easy matching surfaces in unreachable pockets no longer count at full weight.
  The column scan now keeps going past the 4096-unit cast limit (tall maps).
- **One metric for exteriors.** `City.ExteriorMetric` (CityFidelity.cs) runs Fidelity.cs on the emitted exterior
  TEXTMAP. It is hooked by one line in CityExport.cs under `--fidelity`, with optional `--fidelity-cache`. Daggerfall's
  faces are the model colliders plus the terrain on walkable tiles. Exterior rules:
  - Sky ceilings are not surfaces.
  - One-sided lines are the map's outer edge, so they are skipped.
  - Levitation tier: a face whose open side has no face above it and ground below it (open sky). The room flood is not
    run outdoors.
  - The reach flood also seeds from the ground at the walkable area's outer edge, because Daggerfall's world continues
    past it. Walled yards stay unreached unless a path leads in.
  - Sampling uses a 4-unit grid and keeps 10% of every tier, hashed and unbiased. Face-grid cells are 16 units, which
    only affects speed.
  - The old raster metric (CityFidelity `Measure`, which read the converter's arrays and reused StackTop) is removed.
    `exterior_fidelity.py` now reports the new headline and the failure cases.
- **Failure cases are measured** in every report (dungeons too), by tier: *walkable top missing* (a visible floor with
  collision more than a step below it, e.g. a roofless wall walk you would fall through), *phantom wall* (collision wall
  more than 4 units in front of every visible face), and *invisible ledge*.
- The face grid now solves each column's layer range instead of testing every layer. It picks the same cells: Privateer's
  Hold dungeon headline is identical before and after (1.6338).

## First exterior result (Privateer's Hold + Gothway Garden, group 7154)

Headline 0.340. Reachable legal positions: 8.75M of 9.51M. Walkable tops missing: 4.06M of 37.7M units² in the
levitation tier (11%), and 3,572 units² in contact. Phantom walls: 8.1M of 17.0M units² in contact (48%) and 9.6M of
21.1M in climb. Axis walls have collision up to 8 units in front of the visible wall (mean 4.4, p95 8.0). This is the
8-unit raster making a whole cell solid when any face touches it. Farmstead (group 7) headline 0.0127. Metric time:
22 s for group 7154 and about 16 s for the farmstead.
