# Exterior collision 02: one metric, measured on the emitted map (2026-11-28 overnight)

## Result

- One metric (`Fidelity.cs`, from `collisionmetric` 01) now scores dungeons and exteriors from the **emitted TEXTMAP**
  against Daggerfall's faces, with signed distances both ways, weighted by reachability. Card 01's raster heightfield
  measure (CityFidelity `Measure`, which read the converter's arrays and reused StackTop) is removed.
- Every report lists three failure cases by tier, with their largest places: **walkable top missing**, **phantom
  wall** and **invisible ledge**.
- **Dungeon baseline re-recorded** after the reach filter: set **1.2453** (was 1.2117 before the filter). This changes
  the measurement, not collision.
- **Exterior baseline** on card 01's nine groups: set **0.2484**. Card 01's `--legacy-void` maps score **0.9473**.
- **No collision change in this card.** Both remaining error sources need a change in how town collision is
  represented, with a runtime cost (see "Reduction" below). That is an owner call, so it is raised as a question.

## Orchestrator's pre-steps

- **(a) Cache key follows the code.** `FidCodeHash.cs` walks the IL of `LoadOrBuildDfSide` and every method of this
  assembly it reaches (calls, lambdas, local functions). It hashes each method body with member references written as
  names (compiler ordinals removed), plus the values of the static fields and primitive readonly arrays those methods
  read. Changing the legal-position rule, the body test, the flood, a tier constant, or exterior mode gives a new key.
  An unrelated converter change keeps the old one. `FidVersion` is now 3.
- **(b) Reach filter.** `FidLegal` finds legal positions with LegalPositions' rule (rows in parallel), then floods from
  the entrance:
  - 8-unit moves onto the highest floor in the next column within FloodDf's rise; drops of any height are allowed.
  - Only where the body fits. Doors are open.

  Contact, Climb, the air-flood seeds and the worst-place viewpoints use only these reachable positions. Check on
  Privateer's Hold against Parity's validated FloodDf: every FloodDf 16-unit column is reached, plus 369 more (3.7%,
  from the finer pitch). 79,197 of 112,181 legal positions are reachable. The column scan also now continues past the
  4096-unit cast limit on tall maps.

## Exterior rules (CityFidelity.cs `ExteriorMetric`, hooked by one line in CityExport.cs under `--fidelity`)

- Daggerfall's faces are the model colliders (what DFU collides with and draws) plus the terrain on walkable tiles.
- Sky ceilings are the map's height limit, not surfaces.
- One-sided lines are skipped only where Daggerfall has no face beyond them (the true outer edge). The first version
  skipped every one-sided line. That hid card 01's sky-high walls, and the legacy maps scored *better* (0.2358 vs
  0.2484). After the fix, legacy scores 0.9473. Fixed maps are unchanged.
- Levitation tier outdoors: a face whose open side has no face above it and ground below it (open sky). The room
  flood is not run outdoors.
- Reach: from the start, and also from the ground at the walkable area's outer edge, because Daggerfall's world
  continues past it (this also covers multi-site groups). Walled yards stay unreached unless a path leads in.
- Sampling uses a 4-unit grid and keeps 10% of every tier, hashed by lattice index or position, so the score stays
  unbiased and repeatable. Face-grid cells are 16 units outdoors, which only affects speed. The face grid now solves
  each column's layer range (same cells; the Privateer's Hold dungeon headline is identical, 1.6338).

## Where the remaining error is (probed with `--probe`)

- **Walkable tops missing** (24% of levitation-tier walkable tops in the Daggerfall group, 11% at Privateer's Hold):
  horizontal surfaces 100-330 units above an open street cell. Examples: Daggerfall 17888,864 (street floor 100,
  surfaces at 295 / 374-388 / 427) and Gothway Garden -3744,-12512 (floor 9964, roof 10063-10191). These are arches,
  bridges, jutting upper floors and roof eaves. A heightfield cell holds one floor, so it keeps the passage underneath.
  Only a levitating or climbing player meets this, and they fall through.
- **Phantom walls** (about 47% of reached wall area stands more than 4 units in front of any visible face; axis walls
  mean 4.4, p95 8.0): the 8-unit raster makes a cell solid when any face touches it. A thin wall always fills a whole
  cell, so one side is at least about 4 units off.

## Verifier round 2 (same night)

- The cache key now holds the entrance, and the face-grid constructor is a second code-hash root. Proof on Privateer's
  Hold: editing the constructor (`+ 1e-6` to `+ 2e-6`) changed the code hash from 86:F577211C3542962A82664677 to
  86:BA830DDAB232212D2BAD5387 and forced a rebuild instead of a cache load. The edit was reverted.
- The reach flood now uses FloodDf's knee-height line test and its ceiling-limited lift for every move, precomputed in
  parallel. With all of FloodDf's per-move tests applied, the extra cells cannot come from walking through geometry.
  The likely cause is the finer pitch, not checked cell by cell. Saved evidence: `<slug>.floodcheck.txt`, written
  when a dungeon's Daggerfall side is built. Privateer's Hold: FloodDf 19,765 cells, this flood 21,727, FloodDf cells
  not reached 0, cells reached only here 1,246 (the first 200 are listed in the file). The first version had no
  wall test, and the notes above wrongly said it matched FloodDf.
- The dungeon baseline was re-recorded with this flood: set **1.2454** (`fidelity_baseline.csv`).
- `exterior_fidelity.py` reads the reachable count from the report, so cached runs show it too.

## Reduction (item 3): measured, not adopted

- Missing tops: put the overhang top back into the heightfield. That blocks the passage below, a walking and door
  regression the owner's rule forbids. The real fix is a solid 3D floor per overhang region in the runtime town map:
  control sectors, slopes for sloped eaves, and the door and walk checks rerun.
- Phantom walls: insetting the cell test makes thin walls vanish at cell edges (a gap). The real fix is sub-cell
  edges (as `dungeonedges` 01 did for dungeons) or a 4-unit raster (card 01: about 2x conversion time, +35% lines;
  this metric would now judge it on the emitted map, not its own grid).

Both change how town collision is represented, with a cost at runtime conversion, so I asked the owner instead of
choosing.

## Other changes

- `--probe` no longer crashes on points in another site's raster (one-line guard).
- `exterior_fidelity.py` reports the new headline, the cases, reachable legal positions, and the set headline. It takes
  `--cache` and `--converter`.

## Known gaps

- Exterior terrain on the Daggerfall side is the converter's tile terrain (the same mesh it draws), not DFU's smoothed
  heightmap.
- The walk flood does not follow teleports, lifts, jumps, climbing, or swimming-only routes. Surfaces only those reach
  fall to the levitation or unreached tiers.
- Faces under building footprints (hidden terrain) score as unreached at weight 0.05, a constant offset in exterior
  headlines.
- The dungeon baseline was recorded with the build before the per-case place lists. Headlines are unaffected.

## How to rerun

```
python tools/dungeon_converter/fidelity.py --out <dir> --jobs 4 --bin <own bin> --cache <own cache> --baseline tools/dungeon_converter/fidelity_baseline.csv
python tools/dungeon_converter/exterior_fidelity.py --out <dir> --converter <bin>\DungeonConverter.exe --cache <dir> [--legacy-void]
DungeonConverter.exe --group 7154 --ddt <dir>\g.ddt --out <dir> --fidelity [--fidelity-cache <dir>]   # report: <dir>\<slug>.fidelity.txt
```

Use your own `--bin` and `--cache`: fidelity.py's defaults are shared by every lane.
