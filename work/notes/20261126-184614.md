### Engineering and judgment calls

- **Castles count as holding armies.** Entering a city's dungeon (the palace) plans an army like any dungeon
  (`DoomerfallMonsters.BuildPlacements` runs for every dungeon), so the table's "only if they hold armies" applies, and
  they got 3/14/14 and Humanoid. **Side effect:** a city's area level is its dungeon level when it has one
  (`Level.Area`, an existing rule), so the cities of Daggerfall (7 to 3), Wayrest (16 to 14) and Sentinel (25 to 14) also
  change level for drops, ammo and the travel hint. This is a question for the owner below.
- **What "plain" means in DRLA.** DRLA has no class named like the stock chaingunner; its standard chaingunner is
  `RLFormerCommandoChaingun` (and `RLFormerCommandoBattleRifle`), Standard group, tier 1, 70 health, the same as stock.
  Leaving out every "Commando" would leave Privateer's Hold with no chaingunner at all, so I read the owner's "commando"
  as DRLA's Elite Commando (`RLEliteCommandoMinigun`, the minigun one the earlier run showed). Admitted (DRLA):
  RLFormerHumanPistol, RLFormerHumanBattleRifle 1-3, RLFormerSergeantShotgun, RLFormerSergeantCombatShotgun,
  RLFormerCommandoChaingun, RLFormerCommandoBattleRifle. Left out: all RLElite*, RLStealth*, RLFormerCyborg*,
  RLFormerCaptainPlasmaRifle (a Captain, 100 health and a plasma rifle), and the Armageddon troopers. Stock: Zombieman,
  ShotgunGuy, ChaingunGuy. Colourful Hell recolours are left out too (not plain).
- **More bodies.** Plain Former Humans are cheaper, so the same level 1 budget (pool 14700) buys more: 118 mooks instead
  of 73. The budget rule itself is unchanged. Worth a play test.
- One table in C# rather than a data file: the levels must also match `area_level.py`, and the runtime dump check below
  catches any drift between the two.
