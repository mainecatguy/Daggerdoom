## What was built

- **Bridge:** sheet format 7, `DFB_SHEET_INTS` 162, new `DFB_SHEET_AUTO_*` (155-161): damage bp; burst bp, shots and
  reset ms ("+x% on the first n shots", as the card asks); shots per ammo; sunder bp per hit and cap. Generated
  bindings refreshed (`bridge/generate_bindings.py`).
- **Daggerfall:** `DoomerfallAutomaticsRules.cs` holds the owner's numbers and fills the sheet (`DoomerfallSheet.Fill`,
  one line). Perk texts for 25/50/75/100 are marked built; 75 got its text.
- **Engine (UZDoom):** one native, `Actor.DD_SheetValue(index)`, next to `DD_SheetSkill`. Returns -1 with no bridge
  character, so stock Doom and Doom-only games see nothing.
- **Doom mod:** `mods/doomerfall/src/zscript/DoomerfallAutomatics.zs`. A holder item on the player (only while a
  Daggerfall character is loaded) counts shots, refunds saved ammo and adds the damage bonuses; `DoomerfallSunder` on
  an enemy adds the +1%-per-hit damage taken. `dd_perklog` (logging only) lifts the 200-line log cap for tests.

## Engineering calls (within the owner's rules)

- **Live skill** decides, as the gun's skill choice already does, so a drained or fortified skill counts.
- **Damage scaling is linear**: +0.6% per skill point, +60% at 100 and above.
- **A shot** is a tic in which the gun spent ammo with the trigger down (or hit something with it down). Bullets in one
  tic are one shot. The burst restarts when the gap since the last shot is over 0.3 s (10 tics).
- **Burst and skill bonus multiply** with each other and with Daggerfall's material bonus (gamedesign/damage.md: bonuses
  multiply).
- **Ammo saving** refunds to the ammo the gun fires from (AmmoType1): the stock reserve (then reported to Daggerfall as
  usual, Doom owns live ammo) or DRLA's magazine. The first shot of each group pays. Drops with the trigger up (a
  change-ammo unload) are ignored, so an unload never duplicates rounds.
- **Sunder** counts every bullet that hits (per hit), applies after the hit (a hit does not boost itself), stays on the
  enemy for good and is kept by Doom saves. A later lower cap never removes stacks.
- **Rounding**: fractional damage rounds up with the chance of the fraction, so +1% on a 5-point bullet is worth +1% on
  average instead of nothing.
