## Stacking (orchestrator, 2026-11-27)

The Strength and Agility gun factors are built **multiplying** (e.g. shotgun at Strength 100 and Agility 100:
1.2 x 1.1 = 1.32, not 1.30; Beretta, pistol + automatic, 1.2 x 1.2 = 1.44). The two Agility parts for rifles and
pistols already add (one line, `AgilityGunAt`, 0.8 to 1.2), as the card asks. The owner said to leave it as built; `damagestacking` 01 reviews it.
Not changed. The skill perk items (Energy etc.) act after the attribute item in the inventory chain and also multiply;
left as they are.

## What changed

`tools/attributes/attributes_paired.zs` only. The previous run's version destroyed `DoomerfallAttributeEffects` and
gave it back before each weapon (to get the order raw recorder, effects item, mid recorder). Now the effects item is
left alone: the raw recorder goes to the head of the chain as in the original harness, and the mid recorder is spliced
in directly behind the effects item (`Inv` links). The harness checks the order (raw before effects, mid right after)
before firing and otherwise waits, so a wrong order shows up as a stuck weapon, not a false pass.

Why re-giving the item made the railgun miss: not pinned down. Re-giving was the only difference between the passing
baseline and the 0-hit runs, and not re-giving fixes it. The item's own state (`meleeTic` history, the Luck blast
roll) is reset by a re-give, but none of it should stop a rail from hitting; I did not spend budget digging further.
