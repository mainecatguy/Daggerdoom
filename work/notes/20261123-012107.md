# Building and dungeon entry and exit, Doom side

## Result

The player can press Use on a building door in a generated town, arrive at Daggerfall's landing spot inside the real interior, and press Use on the inner door to return to the same outside door. Privateer's Hold works the same way from its entrance in the Gothway Garden location group.

Demo: `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\review\building-entry\play.bat`.

All runs were scripted and minimized. The test placed the player at each door and pressed Use through a test hook. **The real Use key, real walking up to a door, and game feel have not been tested.**

## How it works

- **Door table.** Each converted town now holds a `DDDOORS` list of its building and dungeon doors, using Daggerfall Unity's door texture rules.
- **Interiors on demand.** An interior is one more file in the existing size-capped town cache. Its id encodes the location group and building, and its map name is `DDS` plus five base-36 digits. The two nearest interiors convert in the background.
- **Placement.** The landing follows `PlayerEnterExit.TransitionInterior`: closest enter marker to the outside door, closest inner door to that marker, then a step along its normal. Leaving returns to the door the player used, three player radii out, facing away from the building.
- **World state.** Each interior or dungeon gets a private area of the world table, outside the real world's coordinates. The existing park and restore code then keeps monsters, corpses, and items.
- **Dungeon.** Privateer's Hold is a prebuilt package loaded beside the runtime package, because its door models must exist at startup.
- **Daggerfall seam.** `NotifyTransition` in `dd_treadmill.cpp` is the one place to tell Daggerfall about a transition. It only logs today.

### Load times

| Transition | Time |
|---|---|
| Into a building, already converted | 44 to 84 ms |
| Back out to the City of Daggerfall group | 1.8 to 2.5 s |
| Back out to the Gothway Garden group | 0.66 to 1.4 s |
| Into Privateer's Hold | 0.32 to 0.58 s |
| First conversion of an interior | 4 to 8 s, in the background |

Leaving into the big city group is the slowest step and is longer than the 1.0 s recorded for walking into it in the earlier stitch work. I did not find out why.

## Failed approaches and bugs found

- `sscanf("map %s")` also matches the `mapid` line, so the dungeon was registered under a wrong map name. Fixed.
- The dungeon first placed the player on the lowest floor at the start position. It needs `UsePlayerStartZ` in its MAPINFO.
- Block names do not identify buildings. My first "tavern" and "temple" tests were ordinary houses inside those blocks, so I reran using each record's real building type.
- Dungeon entrances are often block-level models, not part of a building record, so they were missing from the first door table.

## Not supported yet

- Only Privateer's Hold can be entered as a dungeon. Others say they are not available yet.
- Doors between rooms inside buildings are not modeled, so those doorways are open.
- Interior NPCs are omitted, as planned. Locks, shop hours, and quests are out of scope.
- Town gates still reset to closed on each return.
- Town cache files made before this change have no door table and must be regenerated.
- Upper floors and full interior reachability were not measured. The test only walks a short way from the landing.

## Play-test list

1. Walk up to several doors and press Use. Is the reach and aiming comfortable?
2. Does each arrival face the right way and feel like Daggerfall's placement?
3. Is the 2 s return to the City of Daggerfall acceptable?
4. Climb stairs in a tavern, a guild hall, and a large custom building. Look for stuck spots, floating furniture, and billboards at the wrong height.
5. Are open inner doorways acceptable for now?
6. Enter Privateer's Hold, fight, leave, and return. Is everything where you left it?
7. Save inside a building and inside the dungeon, then reload.
