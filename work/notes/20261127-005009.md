## (2) Tilted doors

`tools/runtime_dungeons/tilted_doors.py` (new) wraps `--list-tilted-doors`: 2,475 door objects in RDB blocks, 8 with
X/Z rotation, in 5 blocks, used by 4 dungeons (3 HumanStronghold: Daggerfall, Woodborne Hall, Wayrest; and Mantellan
Crux). Each of the 8 has an X or Z rotation of 180 or 360 degrees, which keeps the door upright or flips it
upside down. MeshExport's check (`|up.Y| < 0.999`) accepts these. I converted all 4 with
`runtime_dungeon_check.py --only ...` and none was refused. Three pass with 6 or 7 exits. Mantellan Crux fails only for
having no exit, as expected. **No dungeon is affected; nothing to handle.**
