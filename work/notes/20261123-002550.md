# Town building colliders

Demo: `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\review\town-colliders\play.bat`. Use the `fly` console cheat to reach roofs, because climbing rules are out of scope.

## Approach

- Each RMB subrecord is one building. City wall blocks, gates, and props keep their old full-height collision.
- A building's **sides** are its near-vertical faces that come down to its ground. Door-sized gaps are closed, then the inside is flood-filled from outside.
- Roof faces outside the sides are ignored, so overhangs do not block the wall below.
- Solid cells inside the sides become sectors at the highest roof surface, using the real plane for sloped roofs.
- Cells the street flood already reached stay open, so streets, doorways, and arches keep their ground floor.
- No physics or rendering change. The only engine edit is the cache tag, `DDT1` to `DDT2`, in `dd_towncache.cpp`. It only runs for generated towns.

Code: `tools/dungeon_converter/CityRoofs.cs`, with hooks in `CityExport.cs`. Check: `tools/dungeon_converter/roof_check.py`.

- The one Daggerfall outlier is 96.8 units off. Its sample belongs to a lower building where two buildings overlap, and the taller roof won that cell.
- Failed walks were not classified one by one. Likely causes are chimneys, height changes, and paths leaving a concave building.
- The ruin's models are not in building subrecords, so it keeps its old collision.

## Simplified colliders and known gaps

- **Wall cells that miss the roof** take the neighboring roof height. Beyond three cells they use the building's top as a flat height (549 cells in Daggerfall, 4,881 of 1,076,107 in Sentinel).
- **Arches and passages through a building** keep their ground floor, so the roof above them has a hole. One floor per spot is a limit of this raster.
- **City walls and gates are still full height.**
- **Sloped edges follow the 8-unit grid**, so ridges and diagonal walls are small stair-steps.
- **Roofs over 45 degrees cannot be run up** under Doom's slope rule, and the player slides on them. In the sampled runs, 1 of 12 such roofs gave 16 or more units of climb. The true pitch was kept, as the design asks. See the question below.

## Regression checks

- **Streets and gate frames:** all four City of Daggerfall routes completed, over overlapping segments. For the stretch through the gates, the walker turns off gate leaf collision, because routes run along the line where an open leaf rests. Gate Use was only seen swinging a leaf open.
- **Old cache:** a Warborne file tagged `DDT1` logged `unreadable, rebuilding` and was replaced with a `DDT2` file.
- **Runtime:** warping from the overworld into Warborne converted and loaded it.
- **Stock:** `DOOM.WAD` E1M1 reached the level on the new engine, both alone and with the DRLA set. The DRLA log contains script warnings. They were not compared with a baseline engine.
- **Dungeon:** dungeon conversion code is unchanged. An existing converted dungeon with its mesh and doors loaded on the new engine (26,041 triangles, no fatal error). It was not walked.

## Failed approaches

- Pressing Use from the walker stalled gates, because a leaf cannot swing through a player pressed against it.
- Steering the walker around open leaves stalled too. Turning off leaf collision for that walk was simpler.

4. Walk through narrow alleys, doorways, and arches, and open each gate.
