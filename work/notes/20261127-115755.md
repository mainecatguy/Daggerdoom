How each perk works (details in the file's header):
- **Fire-rate multipliers (25 spray x2.5->x1.5, 75 Deadeye x1.5->x3, multiplied).** A pistol's fire cycle is the sum
  of tics along its Fire state chain until it is back in Ready. DRLA's conditional jumps are 0-tic frames whose
  fall-through is the plain shot, so this is the unmodified gun's time between shots. Measured: stock Pistol 19, DRLA
  Pistol 19, Handcannon 19, Marksman Pistol 16 tics, each matching the harness's trigger-to-ready time exactly.
  Linear between the fast and slow cycles. **Daggerfall sends the fast and slow cycles as 0 (open), which turns both
  multipliers off**, so in game they do nothing yet. Damage only: the test checks the shot cycle is unchanged.
- **Reload 20/40/60%.** The Shotguns frame-cut logic, on DRLA pistols' Reload frames (reload key, empty-magazine pull,
  DRLA autoreload after the last round). The stock Pistol has no reload, so nothing changes for it (my call; the
  Shotguns code treated a stock shotgun's pump as its reload, but a pistol's recovery frames are its fire rate, and the
  card says the gun does not fire faster).
- **Bonus pool (50).** 100, refilling linearly from empty in 500 ms, added after the multipliers to the next pistol
  hit, which empties it. A miss spends nothing (my reading of "shots spend it"). A Marksman Pistol (16-tic cycle) gets
  91 on back-to-back shots; the 19-tic pistols get the full 100 every shot.
- **Carry-through (50).** On a killing pistol hit, the leftover (`-health` after the hit, read in `WorldThingDamaged`
  before the engine's `Die`) goes along the shot's line from the corpse to the first live shootable thing a bullet
  would hit (LineTracer: walls block, blocking two-sided lines block); repeats while each next victim dies.
- **Shrapnel (100).** Every pistol hit and every carry-through hit makes 3 fragments starting at the enemy's far side:
  straight on and +-15 degrees (a V), 768 units long, each `round(33% x hit)` (carry hits: of the carried damage). A
  fragment skips the enemy it split from, makes no fragments or carry-through, and turns to the nearest live monster
  within 5 degrees (across the floor) of its line that it can reach, if nearer than what the straight line hits.
  Visual: 3 additive sparks fly along each fragment's line; a `BulletPuff` where a line ends on a wall.

Engineering calls to review:
- **Carry-through and fragments fly level** (pitch 0) from the hit's height. With the shot's own pitch (autoaim aims
  down at a near enemy) the first test runs showed fragments diving into the floor 100 units behind the enemy.
- **They are dealt with `DMG_NO_ENHANCE` and no inflictor.** They are slices of a hit that already got every boost
  (Daggerfall's, items', powerups'), so boosting again would count them twice. The next victim's own damage factors and
  armor still apply. This only touches damage this feature creates; no existing damage changes.
- Dealt one tic after the hit (queued, then all chains in one `WorldTick`); the queue is not saved (a save in that one
  tic drops it).
- Spread 15 degrees and range 768 are Doom constants (asked below).

- The fire cycle is the unmodified gun's: DRLA weapon mods (agility, firestorm) that change the firing frames are not
  seen.

- The reload code is a copy of Shotguns' (about 50 lines); a shared helper would be a small refactor of Shotguns.

  metric, no interpolation, no multiplier in Doom; Daggerfall sends nothing for them. Sheet slots 173-178 stay in the
