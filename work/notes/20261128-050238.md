# Verify: narrowed monsters keep full-size hitboxes (run 20261128-050238)

## Verdict

- **Hitboxes: fixed.** Narrowed Zombieman, Imp and Demon (stock and DRLA versions) keep their stock radius and take
  bullets, melee and projectiles at that full width, in the open and while squeezed in a doorway.
- **Smaller radius only while squeezing: confirmed.**
- **Open doorways: Zombieman and Imp pass 45 of 46. A Demon passes 0 of 46.** This one part of the "done when" is not
  met. It is not new: the 2026-11-24 runs recorded Demons staying behind, and the owner accepted it ("Bigger monsters
  that still fit no open doorway are accepted", `work/notes/20261124-212938.md`). No game code was changed.

## The commit that made it so

- `741765b1` (2026-11-24, monsterhitbox): radius and hit tests go back to stock; only movement narrows.
- `6104a41c` (2026-11-24, monstersqueeze): monsters move at stock width and use the narrow width only when stuck in a
  gap in the level or at a door leaf. This is the current rule. `0a7eb06d` followed with the placer part and notes.

Code: `P_TryMove` (`UZDoom/src/playsim/p_map.cpp`) tries the stock move first and retries narrow only if the blocker is
not another actor (or is a door leaf) and `DD_InGap` (`UZDoom/src/dd_monsters.cpp`) says it is a gap.
`AActor::radius` is never changed; the narrow width (`SqueezeRadius`) applies only to the actor in `DD_SqueezeActor`
during that retry. Hit tests read `radius`.

-19 shot hits in the new script, which first finds an open spot. I left the old harness unchanged. It could be made to
pick an open spot if the owner wants it to stay in use.
