# Daggerfall action system

## Result

Action records now run in UZDoom, and the player can leave Scourg Barrow's entrance crypt. The only way out is one of two coffins: use the lid, jump the rim, and drop into the level below.

**The demo is not at the requested path.** This session could not write outside the worktree, so `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\review\action-system\play.bat` does not exist. To create it, run:

```powershell
python tools/dungeon_converter/make_action_demo.py
```

The builder was run against a git-ignored folder, and the in-game check passed on its output. `play.bat` itself was not launched, because it opens a normal window.

## Plan and design

- Doom does the physical part: triggers, chains, moving, rotating, carrying, and teleporting.
- Daggerfall's rules go through one seam, `DFActionRules` in `tools/dungeon_converter/action_objects.zs`. It logs the request and decides nothing. The bridge replaces its body.
- A riddle (type 12) holds its chain until the bridge calls `DFActionRules.AnswerAccepted`.
- Trigger, chain, reverse, and duration rules follow `DaggerfallAction.cs` and `RDBLayout.cs`, including their per-model special cases.
- Moving models leave the static mesh and map collision. Each becomes a drawn model plus a set of solid 16-unit cubes that can be stood on.
- A body in the path stalls a move until it is clear, like the doors. Bodies on top ride along.
- No engine change. Everything is in the generated `_doors.pk3` and is active only on a converted map with a `DFACTIONS` lump.
- No rooms are culled.

## Scourg Barrow traversal

The dungeon has 50 action records. Of these, 14 models move with full collision, 7 tilted levers work as triggers, and 2 wall switches are drawn as sprites.

Estimate from the walk graph (`action_reach.py`), with doors usable and moving objects out of the way:

| Added ability | Reachable positions |
|---|---|
| Walking | 434 of 56,918 (0.8%) |
| Falling | 434 (0.8%) |
| The two coffin shafts | 10,845 (19.1%) |
| The three lifts | 10,845 (19.1%) |
| Jumping up ledges of at most 56 units | 44,713 (78.6%) |

This is an estimate, not a play-through. The jump stage is generous, because it assumes every downhill link can be climbed.

The remaining 21.4% is 12,205 positions in 75 areas. **I did not establish what gates them.** Seven of the eight largest areas are 658 or more units from any action record, so action objects are unlikely to be their gate. Converter connectivity is the likely cause, but that is unproven. The area near (-2508, -4548) is 16 units from a record and may be action-gated.

No route was walked from the entrance to the dungeon's end, and quest gating was not assessed.

## Verification

- `action_check.py`: 38 of 38 in-game checks passed on Scourg Barrow with the 21-wide Daggerfall body. They cover real aim, both coffin exits with real jump physics, every lever and switch, door chains, a walk-on plate, reversal, stalling, and riding a lift both ways.
- To fit the time limit, that check runs objects other than records 1 and 5 at six times speed.
- `action_regress.py`: DOOM.WAD E1M1 loaded stock, with the DRLA set, and beside the action package. The action system stayed inactive.
- Privateer's Hold: 46 two-sided doors pass the door check. The 12 known dead-end doors are excluded.
- Scourg Barrow save and load keeps moved objects, collision, and links, and an object still reverses after loading.
- The DRLA set prints a translation error and DoomRPG class warnings on its own. These launches load none of our files.

## Known gaps

- The 7 tilted levers work but do not visibly swing.
- Moving objects do not block sight, so monsters can see through a closed portcullis or moving wall.
- Sounds are stock Doom placeholders and were not heard (`-nosound`).
- Locks are not enforced, as before. Doors that Daggerfall locks, including two opened by wall switches here, open on use until the bridge exists.
- Door text does not delay the first opening as Daggerfall does.
- A coffin lid waits if the player stands in its swing. It resumes when they step away.
- Flat switch size ignores Daggerfall's per-texture scale.

## Failed approaches and corrections

- Removing the lids alone did not open the crypt. The converter had also lost the air above each coffin's narrow rim, so `OpenLidRims` restores it inside lid footprints.
- An early "drop into the coffin" pass was false, because the test placed the player inside solid geometry. The real jump test replaced it as the proof.
- A Scourg save seemed to hang on load. That was my test: ordinary event handlers do not receive `WorldLoaded` from a save. There is no engine or map bug.
- Generated files are in `tools/__pycache__/action-system`, because git ignores that name and the session could not write elsewhere. It can be deleted.
