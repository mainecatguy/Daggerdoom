# Main quest trapdoors (20261123-170731)

## Result

All 7 named trapdoors now trigger, move, and collide through their real action chains: 5 in Wayrest and 2 in Lysandus' Tomb. **A player who falls through any of them cannot walk back out until climbing exists.** No temporary exit was added, as the owner decided.

Nobody has played this by hand. The checks below drive the real engine and real player body with scripted movement.

## What changed

- `ActionExport.cs`: enables the 4 action 18 tiles. Tiles in the two dungeons get one layer of collision at real thickness.
- `action_objects.zs`: these tiles drop away without carrying or waiting for a body, use the Daggerfall walk-on rule, and keep their collision in place when resting.
- New checks: `trapdoor_regress.py`, `trapdoor_check/`, `trapdoor_save_check/`.
- `--list-door-actions` now also counts hinged trapdoors.

## Bugs found and fixed

1. The old 16-unit collision put the 3 action 8 tiles' walking surface 16 units above the visible floor, so their walk-on trigger could never fire.
2. Riding a hinged tile could stall it or leave the player perched on the hanging edge.
3. A hanging tile could re-trigger from a body beside it.
4. Doom clamped resting collision cells to the floor of the sector below them, which moved an open tile's collision away from its model. The save check exposed this.

## Not enabled

The census found hinged trapdoor records in 1,261 dungeons (2,582 records). Only Wayrest's 3 are enabled here. The other 2,579 keep their old behavior, including the walking surface bug where they use the same flat model.

I tried enabling the other 6 main quest dungeons (19 tiles). Most worked, but Mantellan Crux record 46 did not fire from its chain root, and several Orsinium and Direnni Tower tiles have no level floor beside them. That needs its own task, so I restricted the change to the assigned dungeons.

## Failed approaches

- Reusing the thin panel collision: it assumes an upright panel.
- Testing walk-on with `TryMove` and `SetZ(floorz)`: `floorz` ignores the bridge actors, so the test put the body in the pit. The check now uses real velocity.
- Letting hinged tiles use the ordinary mover path: it carried the player and could stall.

## Known gaps

- Monsters on or near trapdoors were not tested. All runs used `-nomonsters`.
- Saving while a tile is mid-swing was not tested.
- Visual alignment of the moving models needs a human eye.
