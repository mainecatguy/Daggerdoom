# Flipped door models (20261123-124622)

## Result
Upside-down doors now draw, swing, and collide correctly in UZDoom. All 6 flipped doors in the game data were tested in real converted maps.

## Reproduced
- The old Castle Daggerfall package draws door 55004 with `m55004.obj` instead of `m55004f.obj`. `door_pose_check.py` measures it 67.5 units from its collision.
- In a UZDoom screenshot the leaf floats above an empty half of the doorway.

## Root cause and fixes
- `DoorExport.cs` wrote MODELDEF with `m<id>.obj`, but flipped doors are saved as `m<id>f.obj`. It now uses the saved name. This is the fix from `f261a74`.
- `door_check` could not test flipped doors. Their pivot is at the top of the doorway, so the test found no standing room and skipped them. It now stands on the door's floor.
- `door_check` also used the wrong swing side for flipped doors, and for flipped panels that extend to -X from the hinge (55014). `f261a74` handled only the first case. Unflipped doors keep the original probe.
- `--list-tilted-doors` now names the dungeons using each block. `--door-dump` prints rotations with four decimals, because one decimal caused false 0.03 errors.
- New: `tools/dungeon_converter/door_pose_check.py`, which compares each door's closed and open bounds with Daggerfall Unity's transform.

## Evidence
- Pose check: 877 doors in 6 dungeons, 0 failures, worst error 0.000, closed and open.
- `door_check` in UZDoom: all 6 flipped doors pass use, closed collision, walking through, traces, projectiles, swing, stall, and sight, on every side with standing room. No script errors on any map.
- Compared with the door check at HEAD, rows are identical on Privateer's Hold (58), Scourg Barrow (212), Mantellan Crux (43), and Woodborne Hall (167). On Daggerfall (173) only flipped door 14 differs, going from skipped to passing. Wayrest was not compared.
- Screenshots of doors 14, 1, 17, and 25 were reviewed. All 28 are in `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\lane-dungeon\flipped-maps\shots`.
- Stock startup with DOOM2.WAD exits 1337. No engine source changed.

## Failed or wrong turns
- Mirroring the probe for every door with a negative panel changed 6 unflipped Scourg Barrow rows, because some panels do not reach the probe point. I limited it to flipped doors.

## Known and unrelated
