# Shop levels 3: town classes and the town exterior level (run 20261127-110527)

Goal: outdoors in a town, the area level is the town's level, the higher of the map pixel's terrain level and the
town's class level (village 5, hamlet 10, city 15, capital 20). Design: `work/notes/20261124-124736-shop-levels.md`
section 4. Buildings, dungeons and the wilderness are unchanged.

## Plan

1. Town class and town level per classic location in `DoomerfallAreaLevels` (built with the rest of the table on
   its worker), mirrored in `tools/area_level/area_level.py`; `compare_runtime.py` checks the town level too. Done.
2. `ForCurrentPlace`: outdoors on a town's Doom map, return the town level. Done (boundary below).
3. Travel hint shows the town level for towns (`Level.Place`). Done.
4. Tests: town lines in `-dfarealeveldump` (new file `DoomerfallTownLevelsTest.cs`); `-dftownlevellog`
   (new file `DoomerfallTownLevelWatch.cs`) logs the level whenever it changes; `-dfworldleveltest` in the City of
   Daggerfall for guard levels; `tools/town_levels/town_run.py` + `gate_walk.zs` walk out through a city gate.
5. Demo `review/town-levels/play.bat`, notes.

## Decisions (engineering calls)

- **Class rule ("type" scheme, owner 2026-11-26):** TownVillage small 5, TownHamlet medium 10, TownCity large 15, a
  TownCity whose name equals its region's name (aliases from `tools/shop_levels/proposals.py`, case-insensitive) a
  capital 20. Town level = max(terrain level, class level). Other location types (temples, taverns, homes, dungeons)
  get town level 0 and are unchanged. Privateer's Hold is a dungeon, unaffected (still level 1).
- **`Level.Area` is unchanged** (dungeon level, else terrain level), because its callers are dungeon paths
  (`DoomerfallMonsters.BuildPlacements`, `WorldLevel.ForDungeon`). A new `Level.town` field and `Level.Place`
  (town level for a town, else `Area`) carry the town level. The travel hint uses `Place`. So the City of Daggerfall,
  which also has a dungeon (level 7), shows level 20 ("Very hard") on the travel map; inside its dungeon the dungeon
  level still applies.
- **Boundary: the same rectangle UZDoom's town map uses, extended by 20 tiles (5,120 Daggerfall units), Doom's exit
  line.** How it was chosen:
  - UZDoom's town map (`dd_treadmill.cpp`, `Town`) is the union of the group's location rectangles plus
    `margin` = 24 tiles. The player enters it 12 tiles out and hands off to the overworld 20 tiles out
    (`EntryMargin`/`ExitMargin`, hysteresis). So while the player is on the town map they are always within 20 tiles
    of the rectangle, and 20 tiles is where the town map ends for them.
  - The rectangle is not the block rectangle. The converter (`WorldTerrain.cs`) ports Daggerfall Unity's
    `TerrainHelper.SetLocationTiles`: the ground tiles of the blocks (texture record < 56) plus a clearance of 3 tiles
    for cities, 2 otherwise. `DoomerfallAreaLevels.TownRectangle` computes the same thing from Daggerfall's own block
    data. It matches the converter's DDTOWN rects tile for tile (City of Daggerfall 26507..26612 x 36619..36724,
    Gothway Garden 13982..14049 x 43558..43609). The block rectangle was my first try and is wrong for 8 x 8 cities,
    whose outer blocks are blend space: Daggerfall's block rectangle fills the whole pixel, so the level stayed 20
    four thousand units past the point where Doom left the town map (failed approach below).
  - Daggerfall's own `IsPlayerInLocationRect` (night spawns) is the block rectangle + 16 tiles: a different shape,
    not used for the level.
  - Daggerfall does not know which Doom map the player is on (the bridge reports "exterior" and a position), so this
    is a position rule. The only mismatch is on the way in: between 20 and 12 tiles out the player is still on the
    overworld map but already gets the town level. On the way out the level drops at the exact handoff point (paired
    run below).
  - The town's own map pixel and its eight neighbours are checked, because a town map near a pixel edge reaches
    into the next pixel. Where two towns overlap, the higher level wins. Rectangles are cached per pixel (main
    thread only, a few dictionary lookups per call).
  - A town group can hold other locations. Group 7154 holds Gothway Garden and Privateer's Hold; group 9183 (the
    City of Daggerfall's map) also holds the village Ripwych Commons. Each location's rectangle carries its own level:
    around Ripwych Commons the level is 5, inside Daggerfall's 20, and around a dungeon entrance or temple it stays
    the terrain level. Question for the owner below.
- **Inside buildings** `ForCurrentPlace` still returns the map pixel's terrain level (card 04 changes that). This
  includes the moment a building is laid out. `PlayerEnterExit.TransitionInterior` sets `interior` and runs
  `DoLayout`, which rolls the treasure piles of taverns and the Thieves Guild and Dark Brotherhood
  (`DaggerfallInterior` -> `LootTables.GenerateLoot` -> `WorldLevel.Current`). It sets `IsPlayerInside` only
  afterwards. So `ForCurrentPlace` treats a live, active `PlayerEnterExit.Interior` as indoors as well. (Verifier
  finding on the first version: those piles rolled at the town level, for example 20 instead of 2 in the City of
  Daggerfall. Fixed.) After a cleanup exit the interior is destroyed. After an exit without cleanup it sits under the
  inactive interior parent. Either way it no longer counts.
- Tests attach from existing test files (`DoomerfallAreaLevelsTest.Run`, `DoomerfallWorldLevelTest.Attach`); no game
  class gained test code.

## Known gaps

- **Doom's ammo drop tiers on town maps** still use the terrain level. Doom asks Daggerfall for the level of a map
  pixel (`dd_items.cpp` `PublishAreaLevel`) and caches the answer per pixel forever, so the same pixel key serves
  both the overworld and the town map. Fix (not done; needs a small UZDoom change): key the request on "town map"
  (for example add 3,000,000 when the current map is a generated town map) and answer such keys with
  `DoomerfallAreaLevels.TownAt` / the town level. The design note lists this as a consumer of the town level; the
  goal card does not ask for it explicitly.

- **Grouped town maps are not all at one level (deviation from "the whole town Doom map").** UZDoom's town map
  covers every rectangle in its group (`Town::Near`), including non-town ones. The town level covers only the town's
  own rectangle plus 20 tiles. So on Gothway Garden's map, the area around Privateer's Hold's entrance more than
  20 tiles from Gothway Garden's rectangle stays at the terrain level (1). On the City of Daggerfall's map, the area
  around the village Ripwych Commons is 5, not 20. Any temple or dungeon rectangle in a group falls back to the
  terrain level the same way. This is in the owner question below.
- **Building layout test is a recreation, not a real door.** `-dftownlevellog` recreates the layout state: it sets
  `PlayerEnterExit.interior` to a new root `DaggerfallInterior` by reflection, with `IsPlayerInside` false, and reads
  `WorldLevel.Current`. That is the exact state during `DoLayout`. A paired run that walks through a real tavern
  door and logs the pile's roll was not done (budget).

## Failed approaches

- **Block rectangle + 24 tiles** (Daggerfall's `LocationRect`, padded to Doom's full town map extent). The dump checks
  passed, but in the paired run the level stayed 20 at world X 6,821,020 while Doom had left the City of Daggerfall's
  map at 6,817,814. An 8 x 8 city's block rectangle covers the whole map pixel; the converter's rectangle is the
  ground tiles only. Replaced by `TownRectangle` (above).
- **First gate walk** picked the gate nearest the map start, which is outside the city near Ripwych Commons, and so
  walked into the city. The harness now takes the city centre from the cache's DDTOWN lump, uses the gate farthest
  from the start, and teleports inside the walls first. It was also a per-map `EventHandler` and lost its state at the
  handoff; it is a `StaticEventHandler` now. Past the gate the walk hits scenery, so it turns on no-clip only when it
  is stuck (the gate itself is walked normally).

## Play-test list

- New character, first night in Gothway Garden (town level 10, was 1): night spawns are picked at level 10 and the
  town guards are 13-16. Is that survivable for a fresh character with starting gear? Walk out of town and check
  that the countryside feels like level 1 again.
- City of Daggerfall with a new character: guards are 23-26 and night spawns level 20. Commit a crime in daylight
  and see whether the guards are a fair fight; stay out after dark and see what spawns.
- Travel map: Gothway Garden shows "Weak", the City of Daggerfall "Very hard", high mountain villages
  "Legendary". Does a capital reading "Very hard" put a new player off visiting it?
- Walk out of a city gate and onto the road: the step from 20 to the terrain level happens where the town map hands
  off. Walking in, the level rises about 8 tiles before the town map loads (out of sight, but spawns there would
  use the town level).

## Questions for the owner

- The goal asks for the town level over the whole town Doom map, but a Doom town map holds every location in its
  group, and today each keeps its own level. The City of Daggerfall's map also holds the village Ripwych Commons: 20
  around the city, 5 around the village. Non-town locations on a town map (Privateer's Hold's entrance on Gothway
  Garden's map, a temple or dungeon rectangle) fall back to the terrain level once you are more than 20 tiles from the
  town. Options: (a) keep each location's own level (today); (b) every rectangle on a town's map, non-town ones
  included, takes the highest town level in the group; (c) towns share the group's highest level, and non-town
  rectangles keep the terrain level.
