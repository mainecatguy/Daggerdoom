# Energy Weapons and Melee skills (placeholders) — 2026-11-26 (run 161434)

Goal: add Energy Weapons and Melee the way cards 01 and 03 (and 04, Rifles) added the gun skills. Skill only, no
perks. Energy Weapons for cell-fed and energy guns (stock Plasma Rifle and BFG, DRLA); Melee for melee weapons with
the fist excluded (Hand-to-Hand governs it). Works without DRLA. Do not change class lists.

## Plan
1. Energy Weapons takes slot 9 (classic Daedric), Melee slot 10 (classic Spriggan), by the same steps as Pistols (7)
   and Rifles (8). Slots 11-12 (Centaurian, Impish) stay removed.
2. Class lists unchanged: data written before this card still reads slots 9-10 as Daedric and Spriggan and gets card
   01's filler (`RemapRemovedSkills` with the right `firstLanguageSlot`).
3. Bridge: `DFB_SKILL_ENERGY 9`, `DFB_SKILL_MELEE 10`, sheet format 5 → 6, log lines carry energy and melee.
4. Doom: `SkillSlot(Energy)` = 9, `SkillSlot(Melee)` = 10. The fist must not report Melee.
5. Build UZDoom, bridge, launcher, Daggerfall Unity; run the weapon category test, the paired sheet test (old saves,
   raise, save/load, new character), stock E1M1 with and without DRLA.
6. Demo (`review\energymelee`), manual, notes.

  aliases like the other old language names. `IsRemovedSkill` is now slots 11-12.

  older save) starts them at Daggerfall's untrained default (random 3-6), the mechanism cards 01, 03 and 04 used; an
  old Daedric/Spriggan value is not carried over. `GunSkillsFormat` now written as 4 (`HasEnergyAndMeleeSlots`).
  Classic import resets slots 4-10 to untrained.
- Governing stats (placeholders, like the other gun skills): Energy Weapons **Intelligence** (as Explosives), Melee
  **Strength** (as Daggerfall's Axe and Blunt Weapon). Advancement multiplier **2** for both, like the gun skills.

  list gets both.

  instead of the obsolete enum name. Behaviour unchanged: slots 4-12 are still legacy language slots for Enhances

  cells (DRLA has no energy token: `ArsenalTokens.txt` lists rapid-fire, pistol, shotgun, efficient-ammo, melee,
