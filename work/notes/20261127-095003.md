# Value and weight for every supported mod's weapons (run 20261127-095003)

## Result

- **Support file:** `daggerfall-unity-master/Assets/Resources/Doomerfall/WeaponValues.txt`, 327 weapons: DRLA 222
  (its six tiers plus the basic/advanced/master assemblies; DRLAX adds no weapon pickup), stock Doom 9, Heretic 8,
  Hexen 21 (including the 9 fourth-weapon pieces), Schism 67 (base classes, monster shields and fists left out). Generated by `tools/weapon_values/build_weapon_values.py` from a scan of the real mod and IWAD data
  (`tools/weapon_values/scan_weapons.py`); hand-editable.
- **Why Daggerfall's side and not the Doom PK3:** value is economy and weight is inventory, both Daggerfall-owned
  (AGENTS.md). Daggerfall also needs the weight at take time, before Doom sends anything else about the item. The file
  is a Unity `Resources` text asset, so it ships inside the Daggerfall build.
- **Code:** `DoomerfallWeaponValues.cs` reads the file. `DoomerfallDoomItem.Create` and the after-load pass apply weight
  and value (old saves get them on load); the take check uses the weapon's weight. Daggerfall's trade window has one
  added condition: a Doom weapon is listed for sale wherever the shop buys `Weapons` (weaponsmith, armorer, pawn shop,
  general store — the same shops that buy Daggerfall weapons), except the gun Doom is holding (Daggerfall never offers
  an equipped weapon either; selling it would leave Doom holding a gun Daggerfall no longer has).

## Rules used

**Value** = base price of the Daggerfall weapon closest in role × Daggerfall's own material rule × tier multiplier.
The material part is computed in game by `ItemBuilder.CreateWeapon(template, material).value`, so it is exactly
Daggerfall's rule (base × 3 × 1, 2, 4 … 512 for Iron … Daedric). Tier: common ×1, uncommon ×2, rare ×3, very rare ×6,
legendary ×10 (owner).

Role → Daggerfall weapon (base price): unarmed/pistol/thrown → Dagger (1); melee → Tanto (3); smg → Shortsword (5);
staff → Staff (5); shotgun → Broadsword (10); mace → Mace (10); bow → Short Bow (10); rifle → Longsword (15);
heavy (chaingun class) → Warhammer (20); chainsaw/axe → Battle Axe (20); hammer → Warhammer (20); energy (plasma
class) → Katana (25); launcher / greatsword → Claymore (30); superweapon (BFG class) → Dai-katana (50).
Heretic and Hexen weapons take the role of the Doom slot they fill (Gold Wand = pistol, Ethereal Crossbow = shotgun,
Dragon Claw = chaingun role, Hellstaff = plasma role, Phoenix Rod = launcher, Firemace = BFG slot; Hexen slot 2 =
shotgun role, slot 3 = launcher role, slot 4 = superweapon), so a Phoenix Rod costs like a launcher but weighs like a
staff.

**Tiers.** DRLA: Standard common, Exotic uncommon, Superior rare, Unique very rare, Demonic and Legendary legendary
(the mapping in backlog `drla-rarity-tiers`). DRLA's assemblies are not rarity tiers; I used Basic uncommon,
Advanced rare, Master very rare (constrained call — they are built from mod packs, so a sale price above the parts
seemed right; easy to change in the script's `DRLA_TIERS`). Note DRLA puts its BFG 9000 in Standard, so the DRLA BFG
is "common" by DRLA's own tier (Iron 150 gold), while stock Doom's BFG, inferred from role and power, is very rare.
Stock / Heretic / Hexen / Schism tiers are inferred from role and power (table below); the fourth Hexen weapons are
very rare, legendary is kept for DRLA's legendaries and the few Schism jackpots (BFG10k, Unmaker, Nuke Launcher).

**Weight** = the kind's base weight × (pickup sprite's non-black area ÷ the kind's median area), clamped to 0.6–1.6×,
rounded to 0.25 kg. "Non-black" = opaque pixels whose brightest channel is above 24/255; area is multiplied by the
actor's `Scale`. Kind bases (kg, on Daggerfall's scale where a dagger is 0.5 and a claymore 7.5): fist/knife/wand/spell
0.5, grenade 1, pistol 1.25, gauntlets 1.5, staff 2, smg 3, crossbow 3, shotgun 3.5, rifle/sword/mace 4.5, energy 5,
chainsaw/axe 6, hammer 7, greatsword 7.5, launcher 8, flamethrower 9, heavy 10, superweapon 15. Weapons with no
pickup sprite (starting weapons, Schism dual-wield variants) and the two Schism weapons whose "pickup" frame is a
full-screen HUD frame (Deagle 160×120, Stormcast 320×200) get the kind's base weight. Weight does not change with
material (the owner's rule names only kind and size). Hexen's three pieces of a fourth weapon split its weight by
sprite area and each carries a third of its value.

**Top end.** The largest value is a Daedric legendary superweapon: 50 × 3 × 512 × 10 = **768,000** gold before shop
pricing (a Daedric very rare one is 460,800; Daedric DRLA legendary energy gun 384,000). For comparison Daggerfall's
dearest weapon, a Daedric Dai-katana, is 76,800. Daggerfall shops have no gold limit (they pay in letters of credit),
so the "beyond any shop's gold" condition never triggers and **no cap is set**. The file has a `cap` line (0 = off)
if the owner wants one; see Questions.

**Which shops.** Weaponsmiths and pawn shops (owner), and also armorers and general stores, because they buy
Daggerfall weapons too and the owner said "the way Daggerfall weapons sell".

## Failed approaches and findings

- Stock Doom's pistol has no pickup sprite in DOOM2.WAD or UZDoom's resources, so it gets the pistol base weight.
- Schism's floor pickups are CustomInventory "givers" (`Schism_ShotgunGiver`), not Weapons. The scan maps each giver
  to the weapon it gives and the file lists the givers as pickup classes. **But the bridge only treats `Weapon` and
  DRLA pickup descendants as guns (`IsGun` in `UZDoom/src/dd_items.cpp`), so a Schism giver taken into Daggerfall is
  a plain Doom item with no material.** It still gets its weight and an Iron-material value from the file and sells.
  Making Schism givers real guns is outside this goal.
- Some Schism pickups reuse stock sprites (BFG giver shows BFUGA0); the scan falls back to DOOM2.WAD sprites.

  - Town 7154 has no weaponsmith or pawn shop (converter building list: Bank, GeneralStore, GuildHall, Houses,
    Tavern, Temple), so those were checked against the trade window's own buy lists:
    `WeaponSmith buys ...: True`, `PawnShop buys ...: True`, `Alchemist buys ...: False` for all three guns.
  - **Known gap:** in this sync-test game every shop price is 2 and the sale paid 0 gold, for Daggerfall items too
    (Sandals, value 1, also cost 2). `ApplyRegionalPriceAdjustment` sets every price to 1, which suggests the region's
    `PriceAdjustment` is 0 in a `-dfsynctest` start. That is not from this change (reported as a suspected
    oversight). Real shop prices of Doom guns therefore still need a look in a normal game (play-test list).
  - The one exception in Player.log is a startup `DaggerfallUI.SetupSingleton` NullReferenceException, before any
    of this code runs.
- `sale_test.py check`: E1M1 of DOOM.WAD with no bridge: stock exit 0, no script errors; with DRLA exit 0, 3 script
  errors, all from DoomRL_Monsters' own `translation`/`menudef` lumps; no fatal. UZDoom and the Doom PK3s are unchanged.

## Play-test list

- In a normal game (not the sync test), the weaponsmith's Sell window shows Doom guns at prices that feel right next to
  Daggerfall weapons, and selling one pays gold. The demo town has only a general store: fast travel to a city.
- Info on a Doom gun shows a believable weight (a BFG heavy, a pistol light); carrying several big guns slows you.
- The gun in your hands is not in the Sell list; switch to another and it appears.
- A pawn shop and a general store also buy Doom guns; an alchemist does not.
