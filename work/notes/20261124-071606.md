# Doom health pickups ignore Daggerfall's max HP (2026-11-24)

Branch `agent/maxhp-20261124-071606`.

## Cause

Daggerfall already published max HP every frame (`DoomerfallHp.cs` `Fill`, `state.hpMax`), and Doom's
`P_GiveBody` hook already capped heals at it. Three things still let pickups be used up for nothing:

1. **DRLA's stimpack and medikit never ask Doom's max health.** `RLStimpack` / `RLMedikit` (DRLA
   `actors/misc/HealthItems.txt`), DRLA's special-start health items, and the medical-armor repair item check
   `A_JumpIfHealthLower (100, 1)`, a fixed 100, and fail only when it does not jump. With Daggerfall at 30/30 the
   jump passes (30 < 100).
2. **`HealThing` always reports success.** After the jump they call `HealThing(10, 0)`. In `LS_HealThing`
   (`p_lnspec.cpp`) that calls `P_GiveBody` and then returns true whatever happened. So the CustomInventory chain
   succeeds and the item is used up, even though the bridge hook healed nothing. (`HealThing(x, 1)` or a cap
   above 1 also bypassed the hook, because it set health directly.)
3. **Pickups in the same tic all compared against the confirmed HP.** Doom shows Daggerfall's confirmed HP, which
   only changes once Daggerfall applies the heal a few tics later. Three stock stimpacks walked over at 25/30 all
   looked useful: three heals of 5 were sent and two of them were wasted. This also hits stock Doom items.

Also, Doom's own idea of max health (`Player.MaxHealth`, `P_GetRealMaxHealth`, ACS `APROP_SpawnHealth`) stayed
at 100 (DRLA's enter script `RLInit` even sets it to 100).

## Fix (all in UZDoom, marked `[Doomerfall]`)

- `dd_bridge.cpp`
  - `HpExpected()`: Daggerfall's HP once it applies every HP event Doom has sent that it has not confirmed yet,
    clamped to 0..max as Daggerfall's `SetHealth` does. The heal hook now uses this, not the shown health.
  - `HpFollowMax()` / `HpReleaseMax()`: while Daggerfall owns HP, the pawn's `MaxHealth` is set to Daggerfall's
    max HP every tic, so a change such as a level up follows on the next tic. When Daggerfall stops owning HP,
    the pawn gets back the value it had. Changes are logged (`hp: Doom max health 30 -> 50 from Daggerfall`).
  - `DD_BridgeHpOwns()`, and `DD_BridgeHpCheckLimit()` for the jump check below.
- `checks.zs` `A_JumpIfHealthLower` + native `Actor.DD_HealthCheckLimit` (`actor.zs`, `vmthunks_actors.cpp`): on
  the bridged player, a limit of exactly 100 (Doom's stock full health, what DRLA writes) means Daggerfall's max
  HP, after heals and hits that are still in flight. Every other limit, and every other actor, is unchanged. Without
  a bridge session it returns the limit unchanged.
- `p_lnspec.cpp` `LS_HealThing`: while Daggerfall owns the player's HP, heal through `P_GiveBody`, which is capped
  at Daggerfall's max, and return whether it healed. A pickup that only heals then stays on the floor. Unchanged
  without a session.

Engineering call: treating the literal 100 as "full" is a heuristic. It is right for DRLA (every DRLA use of 100
in `A_JumpIfHealthLower` is a "below full health" check on the player), and it means a Daggerfall character with
more than 100 max HP can still use DRLA medkits between 100 and max. Other limits such as `(1, ...)` or `(2, ...)`
are untouched.

The checks cover: at full HP (30/30) both stimpacks stay; Doom's max health is 30; three stimpacks in one tic at

the heals 5, 10, 10, 10. Without a bridge, Doom's own rules are unchanged (max 100, full HP leaves both, clusters
take one each). The DRLA run's bridge log shows DRLA's `RLInit` resetting max health to 100 on map start and the
bridge putting back 30 on the next tic.

## Not done / limits

- Not run in a real paired session with Daggerfall Unity. The fake peer fills the same `hp_current`, `hp_max` and
  `hp_applied_event` fields that `DoomerfallHp.Fill` publishes every frame.
- Mods that compare health against numbers other than 100, or that read `health` in ACS and assign it directly,
  still use their own numbers. Direct assignment is still caught by the existing "direct assignment" path and
  clamped by Daggerfall, but such an item may still be used up. None of DRLA's health items do this.
- A Doom save made in a session stores the pawn's MaxHealth as Daggerfall's value. After loading it, the value
  restored when the session ends is that number rather than Doom's default. This only matters if Doom keeps running
  without Daggerfall.
- Health bonuses and soulspheres still stop at Daggerfall's max (the existing cap in the hook). Doom normally lets
  them go above max. That is unchanged by this task.

## Play-test list

- With a low-level character (max HP well under 100), walk over a DRLA stimpack and medikit at full HP: they should
  stay, with no pickup sound or message.
- Take some damage and walk over a pile of stimpacks: only as many as are needed should be taken.
- Level up (or otherwise raise max HP) and check that the Doom HUD and pickups follow the new max.
- With a character above 100 max HP, at e.g. 120/150, a DRLA medikit should be taken and heal.
