## Design and why

- **Reload and switch speed.** Each tic, while the weapon's current state is a reload or switch state, the item owes
  `0.4 x Agility/100` extra weapon tics; whenever a whole tic is owed it runs one extra, exactly as Doom's own
  `PowerDoubleFiringSpeed` does in `PSprite.Tick`: a state with tics left loses one, a state on its last tic moves on
  now and runs its action. The animation therefore runs at x(1 + 0.4 x Agility/100), x1.4 at 100. Actions run as
  normal, so `A_Raise` and `A_Lower` (switches) and DRLA's ammo transfer states (reloads) are just called sooner. The
  fraction carries over, so short switches average to the full bonus.
- **Which states.** DRLA reloads start with 0-tic jump chains that hop between labels (`Reload`,
  `ReloadStartAmmoCheck`, `Reload1`..`4`, `ReloadWorking`, `ReloadFinish2`, `FancyReload`, `GrenadeReload`, ...), so
  "the state sequence starting at Reload" (InStateSequence) misses most of the reload. A new read-only native
  `Actor.DD_StateLabel(state)` returns the label a state is declared under (nearest label at or before it among its
  class's own states). Reload = label contains "reload", but not "noreload" and not starting with "ready" (DRLA's
  `ReadyReload`, `ReadyNoReload`, `NoReloadLoop` are ready loops). Switch = label starts with "select" or "deselect"
  (stock `Select`/`Deselect`; DRLA `SelectLoop`, `DeselectLoop`, `SelectSetWeapon`). Survey: all labels containing
  "load" across DRLA and DRLAX weapons.
- **Evasion.** The item's defensive `ModifyDamage` sets the damage to 0 with chance `0.15 x Agility/100`
  (`Random[DoomerfallEvade]`, a playsim RNG). P_DamageMobj returns at once on 0 (p_interaction.cpp, "reduced to 0"),
  before pain, push, armor and `DD_BridgeHpHit`, so Daggerfall's HP is untouched (shown on the real player below).
  Scope follows the owner's words, "a chance to evade damage": any damage, including self-damage and damage with no
  attacker (floors, crushers, falls). Only Doom's unstoppable damage is left alone: telefrags (a teleport into an
  occupied spot must still clear it) and `DMG_FORCED`. A first version also excluded self-damage and attacker-less
  damage; the verifier rejected that as an unapproved narrowing of the rule, so it is removed and the scope is a
  question for the owner (below).
- **Ownership.** Daggerfall owns the sheet and sends it; Doom applies it per hit and per tic (AGENTS.md pillar). Nothing
  new crosses the bridge. Without a Daggerfall character the item is never given: stock Doom and plain WADs unchanged.

## Failed approaches and gotchas

- First paired run: only one measurement (Agility 50: switch 24, reload 32 tics, 157 of 2000 evaded = 7.85%, expected
  7.5%). Setting Agility to **0** made Daggerfall kill the character at once (`hp: publishing 0/100 ... dead True`
  right after `agility test: agility 0`); Daggerfall treats an attribute at 0 as fatal. The test now uses 1, 100, 50,
  10 and scales the Agility-1 baseline back to 0. Card 01's test and demo also set Strength and Speed to 0.
- `InStateSequence(FindState("Reload"))` only covers the states up to the first `Goto`; DRLA reloads hop labels
  through 0-tic jumps, so it would miss most reloads. Hence `DD_StateLabel`.

## Known gaps and judgment calls

- Reload and switch states are found by label name. A weapon whose reload lives under a label without "reload" in its
  name (for example a shell-by-shell loop called `LoadShell`) would not be sped up. None found in DRLA/DRLAX by the
  label survey; the measured weapon is the DRLA pistol.
- The speed-up applies to the weapon layer (`PSP_WEAPON`) only; overlay layers a weapon might animate separately keep
  normal speed.
- Evasion is silent: no message or sound. See questions.
- Manual: only the Attributes heading, the intro and the Agility line (card 01 writes the other lines). The 100 cap is
  stated on the Agility line only, because Endurance goes past 100 (Health section).

## Questions for the owner

- Which damage can Agility evade? It is built as you wrote it: any damage, including your own explosions, harmful
  floors, crushers and falls. Only telefrags and Doom's forced damage are never evaded. Should it be limited to
  attacks by enemies? And should a dodge show a cue (a "Dodged" popup or a sound)? Today it is silent.
