# Daggerfall climbing rules for Doom (research only)

No game, bridge, or converter code was changed. Throwaway probes lived in the session scratchpad.

**Evidence labels:** **[code]** read in source. **[measured]** run in real UZDoom. **[assumed]** not verified.

## Summary

- Daggerfall Unity has **two climbing modes**. Classic is the default (`defaults.ini.txt:187`, `AdvancedClimbing=False`). Advanced adds climbing down, strafing, wall jumps, and rappelling. Which one Doom copies is the owner's call.
- Climbing is a **hold forward into a wall for about 0.8 s** action, followed by a skill roll about every 0.86 s. A failed roll means slipping, not instantly letting go.
- **Daggerfall's walkable limit is 70 degrees, and Doom's is 45.** On true slopes Doom is *stricter* than Daggerfall. Setting the Doom player's `MaxSlopeSteepness` to `cos(70)` moved the limit to between 69 and 71 degrees in a real run.
- The "Doom walks up very steep things" problem comes from **step height**, not slope. Doom steps 24 units and Daggerfall steps 0.5 m (15 units at our scale). A 24-unit staircase of any depth was walkable in Doom, including one averaging 71.6 degrees.
- **The player cannot use weapons while climbing** in Daggerfall (`WeaponManager.cs:236`). In a slaughter-pack game this is a major design question.

## 1. Rule table

Times use Unity's 0.02 s physics tick. A timer unit is `0.0549254 s`. All paths are under `daggerfall-unity-master/Assets/Scripts/Game/`.

### Skill roll [code]

`chance = (int)(Lerp(base, 100, skill/100) + Lerp(0, 10, luck/100))`, success if `Random(0..99) < chance` (`Formulas/FormulaHelper.cs:293`, `Utility/Dice100.cs:20`).

`skill` is live Climbing, +30 if Khajiit, then doubled by the Climbing spell effect, then clamped to 5..95.

| Check | Base | Skill 5 | Skill 25 | Skill 50 | Skill 75 | Skill 95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start from the ground | 70 | 76% | 82% | 90% | 97% | 100% |
| Grab wall while airborne | 40 | 48% | 60% | 75% | 90% | 100% |
| Continue climbing | 50 | 57% | 67% | 80% | 92% | 100% |
| Regain hold while slipping | 20 | 29% | 45% | 65% | 85% | 100% |

The percentages assume Luck 50, which adds 5.

### Advanced mode only [code]

- Forward climbs, back descends, and strafing moves along the wall, including around corners (`ClimbingMotor.cs:677-753`).
- Jump or crouch leaves the wall as a wall jump (`:328,407`).
- Letting go of forward does **not** drop the player. Holding still also prevents slipping (`:450-454`).
- Once nothing is in front of the player near the top, later rolls pass automatically (`:686-687`).
- **Rappel:** walk backward off a ledge with a drop of at least 2.9 m. The player swoops 1.6 m down over 0.7 s and attaches to the wall (`Player/RappelMotor.cs:74-78,103-115,259-273`). Each swoop tick adds a Climbing use, about 35 per rappel (`:192`). Attachment still uses the airborne roll, with immediate retries. Saving is blocked during a rappel (`:66`).

### Surfaces [code]

- **Anything with a collider can be climbed**, with no material or surface list. The only exclusions are **enemies** and **action objects that are currently moving** (`ClimbingMotor.cs:546-565`). Stationary action objects are climbable.
- **Ceilings cannot be climbed.** `HangingMotor` exists, but its calls are commented out (`PlayerMotor.cs:315`, `ClimbingMotor.cs:423-427`).

**Doom measurements [measured]** (real UZDoom, scripted running player, stock settings):

| Surface | Result |
|---|---|
| Ramps 30, 40, 44 degrees | Walked up, no idle slide |
| Ramps 46, 50, 60, 70 degrees | Blocked, and the player slides to the bottom when idle |
| Stairs 24 high, 32 / 16 / 8 deep (the last averages 71.6 degrees) | All walked, up to 576 units high |
| Stairs 25 high | Blocked |

With `MaxSlopeSteepness 0.34202` (`cos 70`): ramps at 46, 60, 65, and 69 degrees were walked with no idle slide. Ramps at 71, 75, and 80 degrees were blocked.

**Generated maps [code].** Dungeon floors use real planes only up to about 41 degrees (`AngledCollision.cs:24`, normal 0.75). Steeper faces stay as steps on the converter grid so routes remain walkable. Overworld terrain uses real planes, and an earlier run measured a steepest cell of 51.8 degrees with 419 cells over 45 (`work/notes/terrain-treadmill-results.md:18`). **Those cells are walkable in Daggerfall but blocked in Doom today.**

## 2. What Doom must know from Daggerfall

**Recommendation: publish the four finished percentages, not raw skill.** Daggerfall computes them with `FormulaHelper.CalculateClimbingChance`, so race, spells, vampirism, lycanthropy, luck, and formula mods all stay correct, and Doom needs no copy of the formula.

Daggerfall to Doom (`dfb_df_state` has 208 reserved bytes):

| Value | Why |
|---|---|
| `climb_chance_start`, `_grasp`, `_continue`, `_regain` | The four rolls |
| Walk, run, and crouch speed (m/s) | Climb speed, including Daggerfall's speed modifiers |
| `enhanced_climbing` flag | Doubles climb speed |
| Levitating and on-foot flags | Conditions that stop or prevent a climb |
| Paralyzed flag | Needed for a decision only. `ClimbingMotor` has no paralysis check, so Daggerfall appears to let a paralyzed player keep climbing **[code, not run]** |
| `advanced_climbing` | Only if the owner wants the setting honored |

Doom to Daggerfall (`dfb_uz_state` has 176 reserved bytes):

| Value | Why |
|---|---|
| `is_climbing`, `is_slipping` | Daggerfall must charge climbing fatigue. Its own hidden motor will never be climbing |
| Cumulative Climbing-use counter | `TallySkill`, which survives lost or repeated events |
| Fall distance on landing | Daggerfall applies fall damage through the existing HP event path |

## 3. Proposed Doom implementation

A ZScript climb state on the player, inside the Doomerfall mod. No engine change appears necessary.

1. **Detect contact.** Each tic while pushing forward, check `MovementBlockingLine`, `BlockingFloor`, and `Blocking3DFloor`, and use `LineTrace` at waist height for the wall normal. All exist in `actor.zs:302-306,917`.
2. **Reject** monsters (`HitActor`) and moving surfaces (`Sector.PlaneMoving`, `mapdata.zs:523`). Converter doors and action objects are actors (`DFDoor`, `DFAction`), so these need an explicit exclusion.
3. **Run the Section 1 state machine** at 35 Hz using time-equivalent tic counts: start 28, airborne 11, continue 30, regain 11. Exact tick rounding differs slightly from Unity.
4. **Move.** Disable gravity while climbing, raise Z by `speed / 3 × 30 / 35` units per tic, and keep pushing toward the wall with `TryMove`. At walk speed with Speed 50, this is about 1.27 units per tic. One meter is 30 Doom units (40 Daggerfall units × 0.75).
5. **Top out.** When the wall ends, push forward onto the ledge.
6. **Slope limit.** Set the player's `MaxSlopeSteepness` to `cos(70)`. This also removes Doom's idle sliding below 70 degrees, matching Daggerfall.

**Detecting steep steps on generated maps.** A stepped face looks like ordinary small ledges to Doom. Steps at or below the step height never block the player, so a climb would never start there. The converter knows the true face angle, so it should export a marker on those boundaries, such as a UDMF user field. Whether those steps should then block walking depends on the step-height question below.

### Main risks

- **Lowering step height can break routes.** Dungeon connectivity was tuned around 24 units, and stepped slopes rely on it. Any change must rerun the route checks on every converted dungeon.
- **Raising the slope limit changes movement.** An earlier run saw up to 77 units per tic on slopes. Steeper walkable ground may amplify this.
- **Thin geometry.** Doom has no real wall thickness or capsule, so corners, overhangs, and eaves will need special handling. The planned roof colliders add sloped tops that a climb must transition onto.
- **Treadmill swaps.** Overworld window shifts during a climb must preserve the climb state.
- **Save and load.** Doom must restore or cancel the climb when a save loads. Daggerfall's own climb save data applies only to advanced mode.
- **Exhaustion can kill.** Daggerfall's rule becomes much more dangerous in a game where enemies are almost always near.

## Unverified

- **Unity's real slope behavior was not measured.** The throwaway Unity probe stayed at "Rebuilding Library" until its 240 s and 200 s limits on two attempts. A third attempt with a 560 s limit had produced no result when this note was written. The 70-degree limit is the prefab value, and its meaning relies on Unity's documented `CharacterController` behavior. In particular, whether a 70+ degree slope reports side contact and can start a climb is **assumed**.
- No climbing was run in Daggerfall Unity itself. Every Daggerfall rule above comes from reading source.
- Whether exhaustion also ends an active climb is not established.
- The owner's actual `AdvancedClimbing` setting was not checked. Only the shipped default was.
- The Doom probe used simple test ramps and stairs, not converted Daggerfall maps.
