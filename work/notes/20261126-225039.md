# Smooth dungeon collision: diagonals and ramps (run 20261126-225039)

Goal: in Privateer's Hold, angled halls get straight diagonal collision edges and sloped floors become ramps; sample
dungeons still load and stay navigable.

Demo: `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\review\dungeonedges\play.bat` (new map), `play_before.bat` (old map). Top-down views,
old left and new right, in its `screenshots` folder.

## Findings

- **Ramps.** Real slope planes were written only up to 41 degrees. Privateer's Hold's ramps are mostly exactly 45
  degrees (normal z 0.7071, 1,595 floor spans). Doom's walking limit (`STEEPSLOPE` = 46342/65536 = 0.70712) is 0.00002
  short of that, so they became grid steps. Steeper faces: 686 spans near 60 degrees, 398 near 69.
- **Jagged diagonals.** Only 46 staircase corners were left inside boundary chains. The sawtooth came from about 1,500
  one- and two-cell "sliver" sectors along diagonal walls, wall feet, and sloped ceilings. One side of a sliver snaps to
  the real edge and the other cannot.

## What changed (converter only, no engine change; dungeons only)

`tools/dungeon_converter/AngledCollision.cs`, small hooks in `Program.cs`. The new passes run only in the dungeon
pipeline (`smoothDungeon`). Building interiors (`SiteExport.cs`) and `--plain-angled` take the old path, which I
checked is byte-identical to the original converter's Privateer's Hold WAD (`cmp`).

- **45-degree ramps become slopes.** Faces from 45.6 degrees (normal 0.70) up get a plane, written no steeper than 44.3
  degrees (0.715) so Doom's stock walking rule accepts it. Each face turns about its own middle; its ends sit within
  about 2% of the rise (about 4 units on a 384-unit ramp). Movement physics are unchanged.
- **Diagonal slivers merge into a neighbour** (`AbsorbSlivers`). Conditions: at most 2 cells; a non-axis real edge
  crosses it at the heights that differ; floors within 4 units (so stairs keep their treads); ceilings within 24; door
  and water spans match. A sliver gap may close only if no crouching body (28) fits in it. Crawl spaces always stay.
  262 slivers merge.
- **Smoothed staircase fallback.** A boundary vertex that loses its real-edge fit goes first to the staircase midline,
  ((previous + 2 x corner + next) / 4, steps of at most 2 cells), and only then to the grid corner.
- **Diagnostics.** `--debug-chains x0,y0,x1,y1`, `--plain-angled`, more stats lines,
  `tools/dungeon_converter/sector_view.py` (top-down collision picture). `parity.py` and `reach_all.py` take
  `--converter-arg`.

## Failed approaches

- The staircase fallback alone did not change the picture.
- A 24-unit floor tolerance merged stair treads. It is now 4.
- Closing gaps under standing height cut crouch routes (above). Only gaps under crouch height close now.

## Known gaps

- **No paired (Daggerfall + Doom) walk-through yet.** The run budget ran out before building the play data
  (`scripts/play/install.py`) and driving a paired game through the launcher. The evidence is the two-way parity flood
  and the engine walker, not a walking player. This is the next step.
- Ramps steeper than 45 degrees stay stepped (about 1,100 spans). A real plane there would make the player slide, so
  it needs a movement rule decision (see the question).
- With crawl spaces kept, the wall-foot pockets of the beveled diagonal corridors still leave some sawtooth (1,148
  small sectors remain).
- Interiors keep the old path until the owner approves the new passes for them.
