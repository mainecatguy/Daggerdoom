# Doom weapons have a Daggerfall material (run 20261124-190702)

Goal card: a Doom weapon item in Daggerfall's inventory carries a real Daggerfall material, rolled with the approved
odds at the area level, saved with the item, shown in its name and info. Prices are not part of this card.

## Plan

1. Find every place Daggerfall creates a Doom weapon item. Done (grep for `DoomerfallDoomItem(`):
   - `DoomerfallDoomItems.OnTake`: the player takes a Doom gun from the floor with Use.
   - `DoomerfallHotkeys.Adopt`: Doom's own new game guns become Daggerfall items (new game start, both the kept and the
     stashed ones).
   - `DoomerfallHotkeys.BeginPlainStart`: a plain Daggerfall save with no Doom gun gets the starter pistol.
   - The rest are test files (`DoomerfallChangeAmmoTest`, `DoomerfallEquipAmmoTest`), left as they are.
2. Store the material where Daggerfall stores a weapon's: `nativeMaterialValue`. Daggerfall's own rules read it there
   (`GetWeaponMaterialModifier`, used by the damage formula and the to-hit bonus), and `ItemData_v1` already saves it.
   No new save field, no bridge change.
3. One factory, `DoomerfallDoomItem.Create(class, flags)`, used by all three places: builds the item and, for a gun,
   rolls `DoomerfallDrops.RollMaterial(WorldLevel.Current)` (the approved odds; `WorldLevel.Current` is the area level
   in a bridge session).
4. Name and info: `ItemName` is "<Material> <Doom name>" for guns ("Ebony Plasma Rifle"). Doom items now implement
   `ICustomItemInfo`; a gun's info shows its name, "Material: X" and weight; other Doom items keep Daggerfall's misc text
   (1003), as before.
5. Test: `DoomerfallMaterialOddsTest` (run by `-dfworldleveltest`) gains a "doomguns" source: 8,000 guns per area level
   (1, 10, 20, 30) through the same factory, and a save round trip through Daggerfall's own item serializer.
   `tools/world_level/world_level.py odds` checks the doomguns shares on their own against its independent copy of the
   approved table (|z| < 4 per tier) and that the round trip passes.
6. Real save and load: `tools/doomitems/doomitems.py take` / `load` (now `--builds items`) takes a real RLPistolPickup in
   the paired game, autosaves, loads in fresh games; the Daggerfall log lists each Doom item's material and name after
   the load.

## Decisions and why

- Starter and adopted guns roll too, because the card says every place that creates a Doom weapon item rolls. Daggerfall's
  own starting gear uses fixed materials by class (`ItemHelper.AssignStartingGear`, mostly Iron). Asked the owner.
- The roll at take time is not seeded. Doom does not send a stable id for the floor item, so a reload before picking a
  gun up can change its material. Seeding it needs a bridge field (the item's world id), which changes the bridge layout
  hash and breaks every other lane's test folders; not worth it for this card.
- Guns saved before this change have `nativeMaterialValue` 0 and read as Iron. Exploratory saves only; no migration.
- Weight and value are untouched (prices wait on the owner; weights are a separate backlog item), so
  `ItemBuilder.SetItemPropertiesByMaterial` is deliberately not called.
- The material has no effect in Doom yet: damage is the separate *Weapon material damage bonus* item.
