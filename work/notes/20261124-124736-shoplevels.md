## Findings summary

- 14,790 shops, all in towns (plus 22 alchemists in DungeonKeep locations). Quality follows town size (Spearman 0.30)
  and nothing about the building. Names are drawn from a fixed seed, independent of quality.
- Quality barely affects classic shelf stock (only quality 1-2 skips body armor). The shelf count multiplies stock,
  because every shelf rolls its own.
- With the approved odds, a whole weapon smith shows a Daedric item on 36% of visits at level 15, 67% at 20 and 90%
  at 30. Shelves restock daily.
- Recommended shop formula: C, band-locked rank. Daggerfall's quality text fixes the level range, and quality, town
  class and a seed order shops inside it. It holds "a better described shop is never worse" and makes the top shops
  the rarest. Proposal B (quality plus town size) breaks that rule for 4,004 shops.
- Town classes from the location type are clean (villages 2-4 blocks, hamlets 4-12, cities 30-64). Capitals are the
  39 cities named after their region. Risks: Gothway Garden (New Game arrival, hamlet) jumps from 1 to 10 (or 5), the
  City of Daggerfall from 2 to 20 with level 23-26 guards, and 1,308 villages in high regions drop by up to 25.

## Failed approaches and dead ends

- Palace buildings as the capital signal: 322 palaces in about 300 of the 410 cities, so they are useless.
- Footprint and shelf count as "size": they do not track quality, and weapon smiths are the only partial exception.
