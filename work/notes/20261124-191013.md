# Daggerfall's hidden 3D world: measure it, then neutralize it (hurdle 4)

Branch `agent/nodraw-20261124-191013`. Goal card: measure what Daggerfall's hidden world costs and list every way it
can act on the game, then turn off or lobotomize what is safe to, keeping the same world objects in memory as Doom
and keeping drawing switchable by `--nodraw`.

## Plan (as revised)

1. Read what is already off. `DoomerfallPositionSync` already: camera culling mask 0 under `-dfnodraw`; hidden
   player's controller, motor, mouse look, activation, weapons and footsteps off; `DisableAI` on; `PreventEnemySpawns`
   on; Daggerfall's swimming water guard. Dungeon entries already skip Daggerfall's own enemies (`importEnemies`
   false in `DoomerfallTransition` and New Game).
2. Audit Daggerfall Unity's code for every way the hidden world can still act on the game (below).
3. Add a measurement component (`DoomerfallWorldCost.cs`, `-dfworldcost` or `DOOMERFALL_WORLDCOST=1`) and a route
   runner (`tools/hidden_world/routes.py`, `analyze.py`) that reuses the existing harnesses unchanged.
4. Turn off what is safe and measurable, A/B it on the routes, and show results do not change.

## Decision: lobotomize, do not tear down

Turning the world off wholesale is the wrong route here, for reasons found in the code:

- Quests, NPCs, containers, doors, building Info, townspeople and city lights all look up live Daggerfall objects
  (`StaticNPC`, `StaticBuilding`, `DaggerfallLoot`, `StaticDoor` owners, `MobilePersonNPC`, the location's lamps).
- Quest ambushes (`FoeSpawner.PlaceFoeFreely`) place foes with `Physics.Raycast` against the hidden world's
  colliders, so physics and colliders must stay. The hidden player's own `Apply` reads `Terrain.SampleHeight`.
- Steady-state cost is already tiny: the position-sync run measured a 0.24 ms median Daggerfall frame with
  `--nodraw` (`work/notes/20261122-235129.md`).

So the world stays built and simulated but cannot act: enemies are frozen by `DisableAI` (it gates every
`EnemyMotor`, `EnemySenses` and `EnemyAttack` fixed update, including gravity, fall damage, door opening and spell
casting), and the player cannot collide, fall, trigger or swim. What this task changes is the size of the world
and one timing gap (below).

## What changed

- **Streaming radius 1 in sessions** (`DoomerfallHiddenWorld.cs`). Doom's exterior window is 128 x 128 tiles of 256
  Daggerfall units: exactly one map pixel wide around the player, so it touches at most 2 x 2 map pixels. A Doom
  town map is one location, which fits in one map pixel. Daggerfall's `TerrainDistance` 1 keeps the player's pixel
  and its 8 neighbours (9 terrains with their locations), a superset of what Doom has loaded. Daggerfall Unity's
  setting here is 3 (49 terrains). Applied to the streaming world directly, never to the settings file, and only in
  a bridge session. `-dfworldradius N` / `DOOMERFALL_WORLDRADIUS=N` overrides it for comparisons (0 = the setting).
- **Random encounter gap closed.** `PreventEnemySpawns` was set only on the paths of `PositionSync.Tick` that reach
  the world, and `PlayerEntity` clears it after each update. On frames where Tick returned early (door or load in
  progress, transition not yet engaged) a game minute could pass with spawns allowed, and Daggerfall would roll a
  random encounter into its hidden world. It is now set every frame while the hidden player is engaged.
- `DoomerfallPositionSync.Engaged` exposes the existing engaged flag.

frames (above) but cannot rule it out, because a city build still reaches 0.7 to 1.4 s (reported as an oversight).

### The city return freeze

Daggerfall builds the City of Daggerfall in 0.7 to 1.4 s at radius 1 (1.0 to 2.1 s at radius 3), in its own
process. Doom does not wait for Daggerfall at doors, so this is not Doom's 1.2 s freeze
(`work/backlog/city-return-freeze.md`), which is Doom's own level load. It does matter in two ways: Doom refuses
Daggerfall menus and saves until Daggerfall answers again, and a frame of 1 s or more marks Daggerfall stale.

## Failed approaches and corrections

- `Process.WorkingSet64` reads 0 in the Unity player; memory now comes from `K32GetProcessMemoryInfo`.
- The first logger skipped the frame on which the place changed, which is the biggest transition frame. Fixed and
  everything re-run (`worldcost2`).
- The route drivers find Doom's window with `EnumWindows`, which cannot see quiet_run's private desktop, so New Game
  stalled at the race screen. `tools/hidden_world/quiet_user.py` adds `--user-desktop` for these drivers only; UZDoom
  still starts minimized with `-noactivate`, and the launcher's `--minimized` keeps Daggerfall on its own private desktop.
- `run_travel.py`'s 32 s limit is too short for two trips here; the route passes 70.

## What stays on, and why

- **The world itself** (terrain inside the radius, towns, interiors, dungeons, colliders, physics): quests, NPCs,
  containers, doors, building Info, townspeople and lamps refer to these objects, and quest ambush placement raycasts
  against the colliders. Owner decision: keep the same world objects as Doom. Radius 1 keeps all of them.
- **Frozen enemies stay in memory** (quest foes, guards): quest resources point at them. `DisableAI` already makes them
  inert; disabling their components as well would add nothing measurable (0 such enemies on these routes).
- **Physics simulation**: nothing moves under it (no controller, no AI), and turning `Physics.autoSimulation` off risks
  raycast queries against stale transforms, for no measurable gain.
- **Drawing**: unchanged and still switched only by `--nodraw`.
