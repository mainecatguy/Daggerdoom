# Stand-in for an unaffordable regional favourite (2026-11-27)

Goal: dungeons in one region cell share a favourite kind, guaranteed about 10% of the army (`work/notes/20261124-100533.md`).
A low-level dungeon often cannot afford it. Owner (2026-11-26): pick an affordable stand-in that fits the theme and region,
preferring the favourite's own colour or DRLA family, then the nearest kind on the kind ring. Same seeds, same stand-in.

## Design as built (round 3)

- **When the favourite counts as purchasable.** This is a chosen rule, not a limit of the budget, and it needs the
  owner's preference (see the questions). The favourite kind counts as purchasable when one of its usable, uncapped
  classes costs at most `region_afford` × the area level's average points per encounter. `region_afford` is 1, so an
  ordinary pack can buy it. The value is `plan.regionAfford`.
  - The budget itself can buy dearer monsters: giant rooms (5 to 10 times the split weight, with no cap), sleeping
    hordes, the lone brute, boss leaders (up to 3/4 of a boss budget, or all of it in the fallback), and carried
    points. So "not purchasable" here means "too dear for ordinary packs". It does not mean the budget can never buy
    one.
  - Results with this rule: in stock, a Spider Mastermind favourite (46 points) gets a stand-in up to level 21 and
    appears itself from level 22. A Cyberdemon (43) appears itself from level 21. Logged values: `regionafford` is
    4200 at level 20, 4533 at level 21 and 4892 at level 22 (`ENCBUD region` lines). The other stock region kinds can
    be bought earlier: most from level 0, the dearest of them, Arachnotron (10.6), from level 7 (computed from the
    budget table: level 6 affords about 10.56).
- **A purchasable kind is planned exactly as before.** There is no cost filter on the region pick and no cap on DRLA
  spread variants. A diff against the original planner, ignoring the new `regionref` field, found 0 changed armies
  apart from those that gained a stand-in: stock 434 identical with 60 stand-ins, drla 441 with 53, chdrla 434 with 60.
- **A kind that is not purchasable always gets a stand-in.** This holds even when another role (core, support,
  wildcard or theme) already holds a dearer class of the kind. That class then serves as the reference for the colour
  or family match, and its entry stays in the army (see the questions).
- **Stand-in ranking** (`RegionStandIn`, fixed, no random draw). Eligible: another kind, usable, uncapped, not in the
  army, within the colour lock and family rules, and costing at most `regionAfford`. Ranked by:
  1. Theme fit (`theme.Matches`) whenever any eligible monster fits. The owner said the stand-in must fit the theme.
  2. The favourite's colour or DRLA family (owner's first preference).
  3. Nearest kind on the wrapping kind ring (`RingDistance`, owner's second preference).
  4. Shared look traits.
  5. Dearest.
  The stand-in's own DRLA family variants also stay within the bound.

## Verification

- `tools/encounter_planner/run_test.py` (planner, 5 runs): **62/62 passed**. It checks every plan: a stand-in appears
  if and only if no class of the favourite kind is within the bound. The stand-in is of another kind and within the
  bound. Its theme fit is recomputed from the data file and asserted whenever a fitting eligible candidate existed.
  There are no regional warnings. The level ladder sits in the cell with the dearest favourite (Spider Mastermind in
  every setup): stand-ins at levels 0 to 20, the favourite at level 30, and no warnings. Runs at skill 1 and skill 3
  printed identical ENCPLAN lines (5,112), and so did the runs with different `+rngseed` and config.
  - Stock ladder: DoomImp, Zombieman, DoomImp, Spectre, HellKnight, Zombieman, Arachnotron, Zombieman, LostSoul,
    ShotgunGuy, then SpiderMastermind. The types are Orc Stronghold, Prison, Mine, and so on, so the theme-fitting
    picks vary.
  - chdrla ladder: cyanZombie2, GrayZombie2, RLCyberneticImp, BlueImp, CommonHK, CommonCGuy, RLArachnotron,
    BlueFatso, FireBluRevenant2, PurpleZombie, then RLNightmareSpiderMastermind.
- `tools/encounter_planner/run_budget_test.py`: **120/120 passed**. That is 117 existing checks plus 1 new check per
  setup. New "region runs": 93 dungeons with real placements (10, 20 or 40) at every level from 0 to 30 in the
  dearest-favourite cell, run through the whole budget. Each prints what was actually bought.
  - **Stand-ins are actually bought** in 59 of 66 stand-in dungeons in stock (89%) and 55 of 66 in chdrla (83%). The
    favourite itself, under the existing guarantee, is bought in 25 of 27 (93%) and 19 of 27 (70%) of the dungeons
    where it is purchasable. The regional guarantee is a 10% share of the army that squads draw from. It is not a
    promise to spawn, for the favourite as well as the stand-in. So the check asserts that stand-ins are bought in at
    least 75% of dungeons and at least about as often as the favourite (within 10 points). Making the regional entry
    always spawn would change the squad and budget rules (see the questions).
  - Median stand-in share of points spent: 69‰ in stock, 64‰ in chdrla. The range runs from 0‰ to about 800‰. The
    high end is a cheap stand-in that a swarm room fills.
  - **An unpurchasable favourite held by another role still spawns sometimes:** in 7 of 66 stock stand-in dungeons
    and 3 of 66 chdrla ones, through giant rooms or bosses. Example: stock idx 2044 at level 14 has 11 Spider
    Masterminds next to its ShotgunGuy stand-in. See the questions.

Planner warnings: the region rule and the ladder raise none. The remaining warnings existed before this work: Coven
has no Civil War safe monster, and stock has no Laboratory monsters.

## History and failed approaches

- **Round 1:** "affordable" = 1 average, but the favourite was cost-filtered at every level, the ring did not wrap,
  theme was the 3rd tie-breaker, and the favourite held by another role blocked the stand-in. Rejected.
- **Round 2:** fixed theme, wrap, and role blocking. It defined "cannot be purchased" as above 3/4 × `boss_share_max`
  × average, claimed as "the dearest monster any budget rule can buy". Rejected: that claim was false (see the list
  of budget rules above). The rejected notes also gave a wrong figure, level 4 affording 37.8. The log said 42.64.
- **Round 3** (this one): the threshold is stated honestly as a chosen rule. One ordinary encounter was chosen
  because it makes the stand-in something ordinary packs can buy. That is checked in the budget output, not only
  against the planner's own number.
- Family rule blocking the reference pick (idx 997, drla): fixed by using the army's own class of the kind as the
  reference.
