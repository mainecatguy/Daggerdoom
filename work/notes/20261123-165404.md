# Player position sync at 10 Hz, including indoors

Branch `agent/sync-20261123-165404`. Demo: `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\review\position-sync-10hz\play.bat`. It starts in Gothway Garden, which has houses and the Privateer's Hold entrance.

## Result

Daggerfall's hidden player now follows the Doom player at **10 Hz** in the wilderness, towns, building interiors, and dungeons. The cost did not show up in frame time, so no place needed a lower rate.

## Plan as carried out

1. Publish Doom's position inside a space over the bridge.
2. Apply it in Daggerfall through that space's transform, only once both games are in the same space.
3. Keep the hidden player from drowning, stepping, or triggering anything.
4. Measure 1 Hz against 10 Hz in each kind of place, then rerun the door, save, load, travel, and stock checks.

## How indoor positions map

Doom's interior and dungeon maps are Daggerfall's own local layout at 75%, with Y and Z swapped. No per-building table is needed.

| Value | Formula |
|---|---|
| Published space position | `doom / 0.75`, in Daggerfall units |
| Daggerfall local position | `(spaceX, spaceZ, spaceY) / 40` |
| Scene position | `interior or dungeon transform.TransformPoint(local)` |
| Player center height | feet + controller height / 2 + 0.05 |
| Yaw | `space rotation * Euler(0, 90 - doomYaw, 0)` |

Evidence for the mapping:

- A building landing computed by Daggerfall matched Doom's to **0.0 units**.
- In Privateer's Hold, Doom's start is **1.0 unit** from Daggerfall's Start marker.
- Daggerfall's own landing in that dungeon is 1,332 units away. This is expected: the existing transition uses the Enter marker, which the converter notes is a sealed room there, and Doom uses the Start marker. The first 10 Hz update moves Daggerfall to Doom's place.

The bridge carries `space_x/y/z`, `space_yaw`, `space_target`, and `space_valid` in previously reserved state bytes. The protocol stays at 4 and the layout hash changed, so a stale DLL is refused at attach.

## Measurements

Three runs per cell on the final build, through the real launcher. **Both games were minimized, 3D drawing was off, and vsync was off, so frame times are relative and not real play performance.** Error is how far Daggerfall trails Doom on each frame, in Daggerfall units, over the whole run. Outdoor speed was 20 Doom units per tic. Indoor speed was 8.

| Place | Mean error, 1 Hz | Mean error, 10 Hz | Mean frame, 1 Hz | Mean frame, 10 Hz | Apply share of frame time, 10 Hz |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Wilderness | 343.6 | 35.1 | 0.394 ms | 0.394 ms | 0.016 to 0.022% |
| Town | 351.6 | 35.9 | 0.401 ms | 0.413 ms | 0.024 to 0.039% |
| Building | 81.1 | 9.7 | 0.405 ms | 0.403 ms | 0.006 to 0.008% |
| Dungeon | 116.6 | 12.8 | 0.483 ms | 0.472 ms | 0.006 to 0.008% |

- The frame-time differences are within run-to-run noise, which was about 0.05 ms.
- The 95th percentile error at 10 Hz was 84 units outdoors and 32 indoors.
- The slowest single update at 10 Hz was 5.69 ms, in a town. Outdoor streaming hitches of about 50 to 110 ms appear at both rates.
- Indoor worst errors of about 500 units at 10 Hz are the test route teleporting the player to the exit, not drift.

The hidden player's controller, motor, activation, and weapons stay off. Daggerfall's scripts have no `OnTriggerEnter` handlers, and the two collision handlers need the disabled controller, so moving the transform cannot fire doors, traps, or action objects. This rests on reading that code. No test walked through a real trap. Footsteps are now also off while synced.

## Known gaps

- Privateer's Hold has no real flooded block, so the water guard was tested by flooding one in memory.
- Height follows Doom's feet indoors, except where the water guard lifts it. Height accuracy was not measured separately.
- Whether a save restores the exact indoor spot was not checked. Doom's next update corrects Daggerfall's position after a load.
- Not run: death indoors, loading a save made before this change, and real focused performance.
- Only Privateer's Hold can be entered as a dungeon.

## Failed approaches and corrections

- The first indoor numbers showed 0.0 error because the test handler reset at the map change and the player never walked. It is now a static handler, and those numbers were discarded.
- The first water test proved nothing because Privateer's Hold has no flooded block. The test now floods the player's block in memory, with a guard-off control.

## Housekeeping

Unity builds rewrite the Addressables asset files, including `addressables_content_state.bin`. They are deliberately left out of the commit and still show as modified.

Tools are in `tools/position_sync_10hz`: `make.py`, `measure.py`, `run_doors_suite.py`, `run_travel.py`, `check_stock.py`, and `check_demo.py`.

## Play-test list

1. Run `play.bat`. Walk around town with Daggerfall's window visible and confirm it follows smoothly at about 10 updates per second.
2. Enter several buildings, walk around, and compare your room position in both windows.
3. Enter Privateer's Hold and do the same. Daggerfall should jump from its sealed-room landing to your spot right away.
4. Walk up and down stairs indoors and check that Daggerfall's height looks right.
5. Save indoors, walk elsewhere, load, and confirm both games return to the saved spot.
6. Die indoors and use Load Last Save.
7. With both windows focused, watch for hitches while crossing map cells.
