## Which guns are pistols (checked in GameMods/ExtractedData, DRLA 1.1.5)
- 62 DRLA weapon files give `RLPistolTypeWeapon`. **None of them also gives `RLSingleShotRifleTypeWeapon`**, and none of
  the rifles in the rifles research (`work/notes/20261126-003658.md`, every DRLA rifle; DRLAX adds none) gives the pistol
  token. So "pistol token or stock Pistol" puts no rifle in Pistols.
- Pistol-token guns that land elsewhere because the existing precedence (Melee > Explosive > Energy > Shotgun >
  Precision > Automatic > Pistol, card 01's lean) puts another sign first:
  - Uzis (Uzi, High Power, Storm, Burst Cannon, Demolition) also give the rapid-fire token → **Automatics** (the
    Plasma Uzi uses cells → Energy).
    The Assault Uzi and Nanomachic Uzi give only the pistol token → **Pistols**.
  - Storm pistols, Marathon Shotguns, Mysterious Magnum, Grammaton Cleric Beretta also give the shotgun token → **Shotguns**.
  - Cell-fed pistols (plasma pistols, blasters, Baron Blaster, Nuclear Plasma Revolver ...) → **Energy** (no skill yet).
  - Mini-Missile Pistol: Explosive if its ammo is rocket ammo (not checked in game).
  This is a judgment call inside the owner's words ("DRLA's pistol-type guns") and is asked below.
