# 20261127-134921: Burn fire look pick + rendered frame times (goal card 07, continued)

Continues `work/notes/20261127-132535.md`, which built and tested the burn damage. This run picked the fire look from
three candidates using pictures, and measured rendered frame times. Burn pain is left as built (owner question pending).

## Method
- Own UZDoom build from this worktree: `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-build\uzdoom-burnlook` (the shared one is stale).
- `tools/burn/look_shots.py` builds a test-only candidate pack (`tools/burn/candidates/`, never shipped) and runs
  every case through `quiet_run.py` on DOOM2.WAD, the game's IWAD; the Arch-vile flame sprites are Doom II only.
  Styles: 5 engine particles (the previous look, copied unchanged into the pack), 1 Arch-vile flame, 2 Schism lite
  (candidate), 0 the shipped `DoomerfallBurnLook`, 3 burning with the look off, 4 not burning.
- **Staging (verifier round 1 fix).** Each scene searches the map on a 96-unit grid for the spot whose +-40 degree
  forward cone is clear the furthest (36 line traces per spot), moves the player there, and looks slightly down. On
  MAP15 the cone is clear for the full 1200 units it checks. Every monster is then checked as inside the level, on
  the player's floor, and in line of sight. The log reports `placed=N of N`, and all scenes had every monster placed:
  5/5, 3/3, 60/60, 400/400. The first attempt (MAP01, longest single trace) only looked through a narrow window: a wall
  hid half the view, and the large and frame scenes were not what the notes claimed.
- Scenes: small (ZombieMan, Imp, ShotgunGuy, Demon, Lost Soul at 170 units), large (Baron, Cyberdemon, Mancubus at
  330 units), pack (60 imps), frames (400 ZombieMen, 20 x 20 at 24 units, 300-756 units ahead, all inside the field
  of view). Monsters are spawned stock even with DRLA loaded, because DRLA's replacements are self-removing spawners.
- Frame times come from RenderOverlay timestamps with vsync off and no frame cap, 3 s to 13 s after spawn. The frame
  scene takes its screenshot after that window, so saving it is not measured. Every launch keeps its own
  `log-<time>.txt` and appends one line to `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-build\burnlook2\results.txt` (copied to the demo
  folder).

## Frame times, 400 ZombieMen burning, all on screen (MAP15)
ms per rendered frame. Private desktop, 1280x720. Other lanes were running Unity, Daggerfall Unity, and UZDoom on the
machine at the same time. All lines are in `results.txt`.

| case | runs | p50 | p99 | worst | runs with frames > 33 ms (count) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| not burning | 1 | 4.8 | 7.5 | 10.7 | 0 |
| burning, look off | 7 | 4.5-7.0 | 7.4-55.2 | 8.7-419.3 | 4 of 7 (11, 25, 4, 2) |
| particles | 1 | 7.1 | 10.7 | 14.1 | 0 |
| Arch-vile | 1 | 7.0 | 13.9 | 16.6 | 0 |
| Schism lite (candidate) | 3 | 7.8-11.4 | 14.6-63.5 | 23.8-346.0 | 2 of 3 (2, 20) |
| **shipped** | 8 | 7.4-12.0 | 10.9-51.7 | 13.3-249.9 | 5 of 8 (15, 3, 6, 1, 1) |
| shipped, DRLA loaded | 1 | 10.0 | 16.1 | 22.7 | 0 (look off with DRLA: p50 6.8, worst 15.5) |

- **Cost:** with 400 monsters burning and every flame on screen, the shipped look adds about 3-5 ms to the median
  frame (8.7-12.0 against 4.5-7.0 with the look off).
- **Hitches:** long frames happened as often and as long with the look off (worst 419 ms, no flames at all) as with it.
  This machine was shared with other lanes, so these numbers cannot pin a hitch on the look. In the four interleaved
  look-off/shipped pairs, look-off peaked at 419.3, 51.4, 11.7, and 16.6 ms, and shipped at 34.8, 36.6, 24.5, and
  25.2 ms (1 frame over 33 ms in each of the first two). The quietest shipped runs peaked at 13.3, 22.7 (DRLA), 24.5, and 25.2 ms with no frame over
  33 ms. That is a strong hint but not proof: "no visible hitch" stays on the play-test list.
- If it needs to be cheaper: about 6-7 flames are alive per ZombieMan, so about 2,600 for 400. Spawning every 6 tics
  instead of 4 cuts that by a third. It is a one-line edit in `Draw`.

## Burn test with the final look (`quiet_run.py --timeout 300 -- python tools/burn/run_test.py --exe ...uzdoom-burnlook...`)
All three setups (look on, look off, DRLA set) pass every check on DOOM.WAD E1M1: pack 400/400 lose exactly 600,
take 150/150, player untouched, fractions and Take(1.0), pain 400/400. The burn handler's tick, flame spawning
included, peaks at 0.81 ms (look), 0.24 ms (look off), and 2.05 ms (DRLA). E1M1 loads with no doomerfall script
errors stock and with DRLA. Nothing outside tests calls `Apply` yet, so normal play is unchanged.
