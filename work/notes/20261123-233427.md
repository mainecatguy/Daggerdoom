# Guards become hostile Doom bots (20261123-233427)

Goal: when Daggerfall spawns city guards in a bridge session, Doom spawns matching hostile bots, and Daggerfall's own
copies stay inert.

## Plan

1. Bridge: `DFB_KIND_GUARD_SPAWN` (160, DF to UZ, one event per guard with id, context, target, Doom position, mobile
   type, index and count) and `DFB_KIND_GUARD_DEATH` (161, UZ to DF). Regenerated bindings.
2. Daggerfall: one line in `PlayerEntity.SpawnCityGuard` calls `DoomerfallGuards.Spawned` (null outside a session).
   `DoomerfallGuards.cs` disables each copy's `EnemyMotor`, `EnemySenses`, and `EnemyAttack`, batches the frame's
   guards into one group, and sends them for interiors. A Doom death removes the copy.
3. Doom: `dd_guards.cpp` puts each guard into a free player slot as a real UZDoom bot (`DBot`), spawned at the
   guard's position, with one random vanilla weapon (Pistol, Shotgun, Super Shotgun, Chaingun, Rocket Launcher,
   Plasma Rifle, Chainsaw; never the BFG; fist and starting weapons removed) and plenty of ammo. Drawn with
   Daggerfall archive 399 (the guard's texture) through the townspeople sprite code: records 0-4 move, 5-9 attack,
   10-14 hurt, 15 idle.
4. Small engine hooks, all keyed on the guard tag in `ddRecordId` (`dd_guards.h`), so no stock game sees them:
   - `b_func.cpp` `Find_enemy`: a guard bot hunts players as in deathmatch, all around it.
   - `b_think.cpp`: a guard bot picks no mate and never presses Use to respawn.
   - `b_game.cpp` `FCajunMaster::End`: guard bots are removed before a level change.
   - No damage hook: `AActor::IsTeammate` already treats two players as teammates only while both are
     `MF_FRIENDLY`, so the guard pawn just clears `MF_FRIENDLY`.
5. Test hook `-dfguardtest` (`DoomerfallGuardsTest.cs`): once both games are inside an open shop, calls
   `SpawnCityGuards(true)` (the same call a detected theft makes) and logs Daggerfall HP and guard copies.
   `tools/containers/containers.py` got `--df-arg`, `--until`, `--show`, `--bridge-dll`, `--launcher`.

## Findings

- Daggerfall's hidden guards could not hurt the hidden player before this change either, as long as the bridge has
  engaged: `DoomerfallPositionSync.Tick` sets `GameManager.DisableAI` every frame, and `EnemyMotor`, `EnemySenses`,
  and `EnemyAttack` all return early on it. The guard surrender prompt (`EnemyAttack` line ~272) only appears after a
  guard's hit lands, so it cannot appear either. Disabling the three components on each guard copy makes this hold
  even if DisableAI is off for a frame (for example while Doom reports no Daggerfall place).
- `AreEnemiesNearby` (rest, travel, save checks) needs `EnemySenses` to see the player or `WouldBeSpawnedInClassic`;
  with senses off neither is set, so the inert copies do not block resting. Doom's hostile bots do not block resting
  either, since Daggerfall does not know about them. Noted for the owner.
- Real UZDoom bots normally need `multiplayer = true` (set by `DoAddBot`). That would turn a Daggerfall death into
  a multiplayer respawn. `dd_guards.cpp` repeats `DoAddBot` without that line and spawns with `SpawnPlayer` at the
  guard's position instead of `DoReborn` at a player start (which could telefrag the player).
- A dead guard bot gives its slot back at once; its body stays as a normal player corpse (`player = nullptr`, as
  `DoReborn` does), drawn with Daggerfall's guard corpse `dfmesh/a380r01.png` when that texture exists.

## Not done / out of scope

- Exterior spawns (guards from townspeople, `CreateFoeSpawner`): Daggerfall keeps them inert and logs them; Doom does
  not get them yet. Exterior Doom actors go through the treadmill registry, which needs its own design.
- Other spawn paths that reach Daggerfall enemies (quest `create foe`, random encounters, `SpawnCityGuards` from a
  countdown, which creates a `FoeSpawner`) are not relayed. `PreventEnemySpawns` already blocks random spawns.
- Killing a guard has no Daggerfall consequence yet (crime and reputation are the crime-flow task).
- Save and load: Doom saves its bots; Daggerfall saves its inert copies in the interior. After a load, a later Doom
  guard death has no matching copy and is only logged.
