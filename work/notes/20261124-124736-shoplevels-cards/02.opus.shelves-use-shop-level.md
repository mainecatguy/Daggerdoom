# Shop levels 2: shop shelves roll at the shop's level

Read D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall\AGENTS.md first. The design is `work/notes/20261124-124736-shop-levels.md`.

**Depends on card 01.**

## Goal
Everything a shop shelf rolls (weapon and armor material, magic items, modded weapons and armor) uses the shop's
level instead of the map pixel's terrain level.

## What to build
- Wrap the shelf restock (`PlayerActivate.ActivateLootContainer`'s `StockShopShelf` call and any Doomerfall path that
  restocks shelves) in a `WorldLevel.Scope` with the shop's level, through a provider set by the bridge, as
  `WorldLevel.DungeonProvider` is set. Keep the vendored change to a line or two.
- Outside a bridge session nothing changes.
- Do not change shop ammo odds (`DoomerfallAmmoTiers.StockShop`). They wait on the open owner question about tiered
  ammo in shops. Leave a one-line pointer saying the shop level is available there.

## What must stay true
- Approved material odds unchanged. Only the level fed to them changes.
- Shop quality text on entry unchanged.

## How to prove it
- Extend `DoomerfallShopStockTest` (or a new test file) to stock a weapon smith and an armorer shelf 1,000 times at
  three shop levels (for example 4, 18 and 30) and log the Daedric rate. It should agree with the notes' per-shelf table
  (12.8% at level 15, 29.1% at 20, 50.0% at 30 for a weapon smith shelf) within sampling error.
- A paired run: enter a real shop of known level and log the level used for its restock.
