## The curve (my call within the owner's rule)

- Jump height = apex of a ground jump by Doom's own physics (move by vz, then lose gravity each tic:
  `P_ZMovement` / `FallAndSink`): with v = JumpZ, n = floor(v/g), h = (n+1)v - g n(n+1)/2. JumpZ 8 -> 36 units.
  Measured in game: the probe's first rise equals it exactly at every level (36.0, 53.0, 61.4, 69.8, 78.0).
- Safe fall = 1.5 x that; past it, 5 HP per metre (Daggerfall's rate, linear, "ramps up as today").
- It follows the player's *current* JumpZ and gravity, so it also follows anything else changing jump height.
- Numbers (default JumpZ 8, 30 units/m): safe fall 1.8 m at Jumping 0, 2.6 m at 25, 3.1 m at 50, 3.5 m at 75,
  3.9 m at 100. A 9 m drop: 36 HP at 0, 25 HP at 100 (was 20 HP below 75 and 0 from 75).
- **Consequence the owner should see:** every character now takes more falling damage than Daggerfall's old 5 m free
  fall, and Jumping 75+ is no longer immune. That is what "1.5x jump height" gives with Doom's real jump height
  (about 1.2 m). Raised as a question. The multiplier is one constant (`SafeFallJumps`).
- The Jumping 75 perk ("Falling no longer hurts you.") is removed from `DoomerfallPerks.cs`, so 75 has no perk now
  (a missing entry shows nothing). Raised as a question.

## Jump height on load (Q&A item 71)

`ScaleJumpZ` on first activation after any world load now takes the base from the pawn's *default* JumpZ and scales it
by the current Jumping skill. Only when JumpZ equals the value this session itself last wrote (the pawn carried to a
new map, or a save of this session at the same Jumping value) does it keep its remembered base, so a mod's own JumpZ
change made during play survives map changes. Known gap: a mod's runtime JumpZ change does not survive loading a save
from another session (it falls back to the default). DRLA only sets JumpZ as a class default (Scout.txt), which
`default.JumpZ` covers.

## Training (Q&A item 7)

1 use per jump = Daggerfall's rate (`PlayerEntity.FixedUpdate`, `TallySkill(Jumping, 1)` once per jump). Ground jumps
count too: the game-designer triage `decisions/20261127-triage-q09-jumping-uses.md` records that as following clearly,
and the manual said Doom jumps did not train yet. Daggerfall's jump fatigue cost (`JumpingFatigueLoss`) is still not
applied for Doom jumps (out of scope; noted).
