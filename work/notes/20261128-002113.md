# Collision fidelity metric (2026-11-28 overnight)

Goal: a metric that gates converter collision changes. It compares the emitted map's collision with the visible
Daggerfall faces, weighted by how reachable each surface is.

## Result

- `tools/dungeon_converter/Fidelity.cs` adds the metric to the converter. It runs only with `--fidelity <dir>`
  (and `--fidelity-cache <dir>`), after the WAD is written. It is offline, opens no window, and is never used at the
  dungeon door. The hook in `Program.cs` is two argument lines plus one call.
- `tools/dungeon_converter/fidelity.py` is the battery. It converts the fixed set in parallel, writes `summary.csv`,
  `set.csv`, and per-map reports and worst views, and with `--baseline` prints each map's change and a set verdict.
- `tools/dungeon_converter/fidelity_set.txt` is the fixed starting set: 14 main quest dungeons plus one per dungeon
  type (19 types), 33 maps in all.
- `tools/dungeon_converter/fidelity_baseline.csv` is the baseline on current integration (after `dungeonedges`), before
  any other collision change. **Set headline 1.2117** (mean of the 33 map headlines).

Usage:

    python tools/dungeon_converter/fidelity.py --out D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-build\fidelity\runs\<name> --jobs 4 ^
        --baseline tools/dungeon_converter/fidelity_baseline.csv [-- <converter args for the variant>]
    # one map: DungeonConverter.exe "#187853213" --out <dir> --fidelity <dir> --fidelity-cache <dir>

## How it measures

- **Doom side, from the emitted TEXTMAP** (`FidMap`). Parses vertices, lines, sidedefs, and sectors (heights and
  `floorplane_*`/`ceilingplane_*`) and `Sector_Set3DFloor` (160) control lines. The collision solid at (x, y, z) is:
  outside every sector, or below the floor, above the ceiling, or inside a solid 3D floor (type & 3 == 1). Point in
  sector uses the nearest line crossing to the +x side, indexed by 8-unit row bands. Control sectors are excluded.
- **Collision samples.** Floors, ceilings, and 3D floor faces come from 2-unit scan rows. They are the bounds of each
  column's open intervals, with area divided by |nz| on slopes. Walls are sampled every 2 units along each line and
  every 2 units in height, wherever one side's column is open and the other's is solid. That covers height bands,
  one-sided lines, and 3D floor sides.
- **Daggerfall side.** The static model faces (`tris`) on a barycentric lattice of about 2x2 units, each sample
  carrying its share of area.
- **Doors** are runtime objects on both sides. The leaves are not in `tris`, and the door sight blockers are
  non-solid 3D floors (type 19). Doorways are measured open, the same as Parity.cs's open mode without the leaves.
  Door collision itself stays with the existing door checks.
- **Mesh to collision.** Signed distance along the face normal. It marches from the face point (0.5-unit steps to
  8, then 1-unit steps) and bisects 5 times. + means collision in front of the face (invisible wall, snag, shots
  hitting air). - means collision behind it (walking into visible walls). Capped at 32.
- **Collision to mesh.** Distance to the nearest visible face (3D grid, ring search), signed by the face's side.
  Capped at 32.
- **Tiers** come from `LegalPositions()` (Parity.cs, doors open), indexed by 16-unit columns:
  - Contact: floor samples (nz >= cos 70) within the body radius of a legal position and within a step of its height.
    Wall samples within radius + `Touch` (12, DoomerfallClimb.zs) of a legal position on their open side, from foot
    to head height.
  - Climb: the same walls above head height, up to the Daggerfall ceiling over that position. **Climb band height:**
    the climbing system has no height limit (holding still never slips, owner decision 2026-11-26), so the band runs
    to the ceiling. `Touch` and the 70-degree limit are its reach constants.
  - Levitation: the sample's open side (4 units out along the normal) is in an air voxel connected to legal positions.
    This is a 16-unit voxel flood from legal positions, stepping between voxel centers that no face separates (two
    sided), and only into voxels whose nearest face above faces down and nearest face below faces up.
  - Unreached: everything else.
- **Headline:** sum of weight x area x |error| over sum of weight x area, both directions together. Lower is better.
  The set headline is the mean of map headlines, so each map counts the same.
- **Output per map:** for each tier and direction, area, weighted mean, p95, share within 1/2/4, and share in front
  and behind by more than 1/2/4. Sub-rows by orientation (axis, 45-degree, and other walls; flat floors, slopes,
  steep floors; flat and sloped ceilings), tier-weighted. Worst places use 64-unit cells ranked by weighted error area
  beyond 1 unit, 16 non-adjacent cells. `<slug>.worst_views.txt` is in `compare.py` format (Daggerfall units, first view
  the entrance, camera at the nearest legal position 48-384 units away with a clear line, positive pitch down).

## Speed

- **Daggerfall side cached** per dungeon (`--fidelity-cache`, default `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-build\fidelity\cache`).
  The cache holds legal positions, the air bit set, and each lattice sample's tier. The key covers scale, body, face
  count, and a face checksum, so a change to geometry loading rebuilds it. The lattice itself is rebuilt from the
  faces, which is cheap.
- **Samples are kept in proportion to weight** (contact 1, climb 0.3, levitation 0.1, unreached 0.1). Each kept
  sample's area is divided by its keep rate, so the score stays unbiased. Selection hashes the lattice index (faces)
  or the position (collision), so two variants keep the same samples where geometry is unchanged.
- Dense arrays for the legal index and the air bit set. Parallel lattice, sampling, and error passes. Parallel maps in
  the battery.
- **Deterministic:** repeated runs gave identical headlines on all 33 maps (cold vs warm, and before vs after the speed
  work).

Timings on this machine (8 cores / 16 threads, other overnight lanes probably running, so wall times are noisy):

| Run | Wall |
|---|---|
| Privateer's Hold alone, warm | 9.7 s process (2.0 s metric, the rest is the converter) |
| Scourg Barrow (22 blocks) alone, warm | 28.9 s process (7.5 s metric) |
| Whole set, 4 jobs, warm (before the dense-array work) | 381 s |
| Whole set, 6 jobs, cold cache | 314 s |
| Whole set, 6 jobs, warm, longest first | 445 s (contention: the six biggest maps ran together) |

The converter itself is now most of the cost: about 70% of each map's process time. The metric for the whole set is
about 1.5-2 minutes of CPU. Mantellan Crux is the slowest map: 168 s cold (7M air voxels), about 75 s warm under load.

## Baseline findings (current integration)

- Set headline **1.2117**. Best map Coven 0.55, worst Mantellan Crux 5.35, next HarpyNest 1.97 and DragonsDen 1.82.
- Sanity check on Privateer's Hold: `--grid-collision` (old axis boundaries) scores **2.03**, worse as expected.
  **`--plain-angled` scores 1.24, better than the current pipeline's 1.41.** The difference is almost all in sloped
  ceilings (mesh to collision: mean 4.44 now vs 1.40 plain; 58% of the area has collision more than 1 unit behind the
  visible ceiling). It matches the current "leaning-wall ceilings made flat at their top" pass, which raises the
  collision ceiling above the visible sloped ceiling. Climb-tier walls also improve under plain (3.09 to 2.14). This
  is a design trade-off for the owner. I changed nothing.
- Mantellan Crux: 94M units² of collision wall in the climb tier, half of it more than 4 units in front of any visible
  face (capped at 32). These are tall phantom walls in open air, the kind of thing the collision-to-mesh direction is
  meant to catch. That one map adds about 0.13 to the set mean.
- In general, flat floors match well: 97% within 1 unit on Privateer's Hold. Flat ceilings are within 2 units 98% of
  the time, and the 1-2 unit error is ZQuant rounding. Axis walls in reach are the largest contact error (14% of the
  area has collision behind by more than 1).

## Failed approaches

- The first air flood stepped only between voxel centers that no face separated. It leaked through openings with no
  model (the exit door has none) and filled the whole bounding box: 8.3M voxels on Privateer's Hold, nearly everything
  levitation. Adding the "inside a room" test (nearest face above points down, nearest below points up) brought it to
  325k.
- The first cache stored all samples: 280 MB for Privateer's Hold, slower than rebuilding. It now stores tiers only.

## Not done / known gaps

- Interiors (`SiteExport.cs`) are not in the set. They are candidates for a larger set once the owner picks a size.
- Tapestries and the exit door have no Daggerfall collider, so they are not in the face list. They are visible but
  not measured.
- Worst views were rendered on the Doom side only (`compare.py --skip-df` on Privateer's Hold: 15 of 17 views captured
  before my 90 s timeout). The Daggerfall capture build was not run. A full side-by-side needs `DFCapture.exe`.
- Timings are noisy under a shared machine. The owner should judge set size from the per-map numbers.
