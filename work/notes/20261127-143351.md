# Shops 7: lots that tell a story, and rare exceptions (run 20261127-143351)

## Plan (as carried out)
1. Data-driven lot templates in `Resources/Doomerfall/ShopLots.txt`, rolled with Daggerfall's item and material code
   (`DoomerfallShopLots.cs`).
2. Hook them into the Shops 5 ledger (`DoomerfallShopLedger.Deliver`). The classic delivery is still rolled, so Doom
   ammo and the horse and cart still go on the first shelf as before. Its other goods become the "regular supply" lot.
3. Doom guns for sale when guns are enabled, rarer by tier, valued by the weaponvalues 01 rule.
4. Rare exceptions (part D), seeded per building and window, never tied to the player.
5. A survey test (`-dfshoplotstest`, `DoomerfallShopLotsTest.cs`), the existing ledger tests, an in-game route, and a demo.

## What was built
- **Lot templates** (weights per shop type in the data file):
  - **regular**: the shop's classic Daggerfall delivery, plus Doom guns.
  - **estate**: one dead adventurer's kit. Seven archetypes: warrior, knight, mercenary, archer, thief, spellsword
    and mage. One material roll covers the kit's weapon(s), and plate armor takes the same material, so the kit
    matches itself. A kit can also hold potions, a book, a ring or amulet, magic items or clothes.
  - **salvage**: mixed weapons and armor, each piece rolled on its own. It does not yet lean to the nearest dungeon;
    that is backlog `shop-salvage-lots`.
  - **caravan**: another province's goods by item kind (Hammerfell blades, Breton arms, Nord axes, Bosmer bows,
    Summerset gems, Black Marsh herbs and others). A caravan is never one from the shop's own province (region →
    province table in the data).
  - **bulk**: many identical items, one template and one material roll: a crate of the same weapon, 30-50 arrows,
    helms and shields for a garrison, or general store supplies.
  - **townsfolk**: one odd item.
  - **Pawn shops take only estate, salvage and townsfolk lots.**
- **Worn goods**: estate weapons and armor arrive at 55-90% condition, salvage at 35-80%. Value falls by the same
  percentage. Daggerfall's classic `CalculateCost` ignores condition, so the price cut is made on the item's value.
  That cut stays after a repair (see questions). The price formula is unchanged.
- **Fitting rule**: an ordinary lot arrives only when the shelf has room for its core (the items that make it read
  as itself, for example an estate's weapon and two armor pieces). Items past the room are dropped. Stocked goods
  never go over the Shops 5 share. A first attempt let lots arrive whole and go over the share. The StockSize test then
  failed on the mean (19.9 against a cap of 18 at level 1), so I dropped that attempt and kept the check unchanged.
  Only an exception shipment (a rare break) may go over the share, by at most its core.
- **Doom guns** (guns enabled; `DoomerfallShopLots.GunsEnabled`, always true today, as the owner says guns are
  always on for now):
  - Each regular-supply lot at a shop that stocks weapons (weapon smith, armorer, general store) makes 3 rolls. Each
    roll picks a gun evenly from the loaded set's guns in `WeaponValues.txt` and keeps it with Daggerfall's own stock
    rule. A tier gives a Daggerfall rarity (common 4, uncommon 8, rare 12, very rare 16, legendary 20). The gun must
    have rarity ≤ the shop's quality, and it stocks with chance = the shop's weapon chance × 5 × (21 − rarity) / 100.
  - Tiers are DRLA's for the DRLA set. For other sets, `WeaponValues.txt` already infers a tier from role and power,
    and that serves as the DPS tier (see questions).
  - Pawn shops get a Doom gun only from the estate exception.
  - Value: the role's Daggerfall weapon at Iron × the tier multiplier × share, the weaponvalues 01 rule.
- **Exceptions**: each building's days are cut into 7-day windows. A window is an exception window when a hash of
  (location, building, window) falls under 4%. It depends on nothing else, so it is the same in every game and never
  tied to the player.
  - Weapon smith: one shipment of a full plate suit, 4-6 pieces of one material.
  - Armorer: one archery shipment, 3-5 bows and arrows.
  - Pawn shop: one estate lot with a Doom gun.
  - General store: siege. On the first day the supplies are bought out (everything but weapons, armor, ammo and the
    horse and cart), then the whole window delivers only weapons.
  - Village or hamlet weapon smith: every delivery in the window is one weapon kind.
  - Other shop types have no exception yet.
  - The proposal's "archery outfitter" needs Shops 6's specialty identity, which is not built. These are the
    building-type versions.
- `WeaponValues.txt` (+ metas) is copied byte for byte from `integration`, because this branch is 719 commits behind
  integration and lacks weaponvalues 01. It is the same blob, so a merge has nothing to conflict on. After the merge,
  `DoomerfallShopLots.LoadGuns` should use `DoomerfallWeaponValues` (and the guns' materials) rather than its own
  small parser.
- Log lines (bridge log only): each in-game restock line now ends with `today's lots: ...`.
- `tools/shop_levels/shelf_run.py --lots` runs the survey.

## Known gaps
- Shop identity (Shops 6: specialty leans, suppliers, rhythm) is not built. Templates and exceptions key on building
  type only.
- Lot labels and visible pattern (part E) are not built. The story is in the goods, and the log names each lot.
- Which Doom set is loaded is not known on Daggerfall's side on this branch: `GunSet = "DRLA"`. Integration's
  starter-pistol message could set it after the merge.
- Doom guns on shelves have no material on this branch (Iron value). Integration gives guns materials.
- In the test (no Doom art loaded) gun names show the pickup class. In play, Doom's tag names them.
- The exception share (4% of windows), the template weights and the core sizes are first guesses in the data file.
