# Privateer's Hold: the first enemy room is easy (20261126-192455)

Goal: the first enemy room (just outside the cave a new character wakes in) holds Former Humans who drop battle rifles,
and no chaingunners. Fixed per seed. The rest of Privateer's Hold plans normally. Small targeted rule, not a planner
change for every dungeon.

## Plan (as revised)

1. Find which placements are "the room outside the cave". Done, with a detour (below).
2. A story-theme start room rule in the planner: replace the monsters at those placements with battle rifle Former
   Humans, keeping their number. Done.
3. Check the drops really are battle rifles. Done (UZDoom test under `tools/`).
4. Planner test, budget test (I touched `EncounterBudget.zs`), a real paired New Game, E1M1 regression. Done.

## What was built

- `doomerfall_encounters.txt`, the PlainHumanoid row (Privateer's Hold only) gets three new keys:
  `start=RLFormerHumanBattleRifle,RLFormerHumanBattleRifle2,RLFormerHumanBattleRifle3,Zombieman startat=510,1672,960 startradius=400`.
- `EncounterBudget.zs` `StartRoom()`, run after `Fill()`: each placement whose origin is within `startradius` (straight
  line) of `startat` keeps its number of monsters, but each one becomes a start class the pool has. The pick uses a new
  RNG purpose (`P_START`), so it is fixed per seed. The role becomes `start`. Two placer hooks keep the room easy:
  `Promote` there buys only start classes, and `Move` never sends points into a `start` placement. Rows without the
  keys are unchanged, so every other dungeon plans exactly as before. No new log or console output.
- Tests: `EncounterBudgetTest.zs` (test hook file) plans Privateer's Hold at 13 seed offsets, with one placement in the
  room outside the cave, one at the real position of the floor below, and one just outside the radius. It also runs a
  Move where only the start room is free. `run_budget_test.py` checks both. New under `tools/first_room/`: a drop test,
  and a walk flood from the cave (`run_cave_walk.py`, `cave_walk.zs`).

### Which room is "the room just outside the cave" (evidence)

- The placer measures walking distance from the map's player start. In Privateer's Hold that is the exit-door marker
  (852,372: the converter picks Daggerfall's record 10 start marker, `tools/dungeon_converter/Program.cs:313`), not the
  record 8 enter marker in the cave where New Game puts you (510,1672, floor 960, measured in game). So I could not use
  the placer's walk.
- `tools/first_room/run_cave_walk.py` loads the converted map alone (no Daggerfall) and floods a walk grid from the cave
  with the placer's own parameters: an 8-unit grid, a radius-5, 32-high body, up at most 24, down any height, doors
  open. It reaches 88,922 grid nodes (the placer's flood from the exit door: 88,471) and **the exit door marker at walk
  2264**, so it connects the cave to the whole walkable dungeon. It reaches 29 of the 42 placements, the same count as
  the placer's `walk-known 29`.
- From the cave on foot: **p0 at walk 328** (258 units in a straight line, same floor 960), then p37 at 984, p15 at
  1504, p2 at 1800 and p10 at 1816. p25 (542 units in a straight line, on the floor below) is 10th, at walk 2552, past
  the exit door.
- So the room the cave opens into is p0. `startradius=400` takes exactly p0. The next placement in a straight line is
  p25, at 542 units.
- **Failed first try:** my first flood used a 16-unit grid and a radius-16 body. It missed doorways (8,395 nodes, only
  14 placements) and wrongly called p25 unreachable. A verifier caught it. The order it gave for p0 and p37 happened
  to be right, but its numbers were not evidence. The numbers above are from the corrected flood.
- My first version also used a radius of 640, which rewrote p25 as well and removed its boss. It is reverted: p25 is
  near the cave in a straight line but far on foot.

### Engineering and judgment calls

- **Which classes.** DRLA's three battle rifle Former Humans (`RLFormerHumanBattleRifle`, `2` and `3`) have the same 20
  health and the same attacks. They differ only in sprite, and all drop `RLFormerHumanBattleRifleDrop`, which is
  `RLBattleRiflePickup` when the Arsenal is loaded. Each monster picks one of the three at random (seeded). Without
  DRLA, the stock zombieman stands in.
- **Same number of monsters, not the same points.** The room keeps its count, because the owner asked for an easy room.
  In tonight's plan, p0's 2 pistol zombiemen cost the same as 2 battle rifle zombiemen, so the totals do not change.
- **The owner's "3 chaingunners" do not match.** In tonight's plan, p0 held 2 pistol zombiemen. I also replayed card 01's
  earlier Humanoid-theme plan (a throwaway pk3 with the PlainHumanoid row set to Humanoid's classes; `seedas=Humanoid`
  keeps the same seeds; it reproduced card 01's 73 escorts). There, p0 held 2 elite battle rifle humans
  (RLEliteHumanBattleRifle), p37 held 3 elite battle rifle humans, and p25 held a battle rifle cyborg. No chaingunner
  appears in the first rooms of either plan. The chaingun commando packs in tonight's plan are at p21, p31, p38 and p9,
  4,472 or more on foot from the cave, or unreachable. I cannot tell which room the owner meant, so it is a question
  below. p0 is changed because the walk evidence makes it the room right outside the cave.

## Failed approaches and findings

- Walking distance from the placer as "nearest the start" picked rooms about 1,300 units from the cave, because the
  walk starts at the exit door.
- A radius of 640 also caught p25, on the floor below. It is not reachable from the cave on foot, so it was reverted.
- A `DDENC startroom` summary line printed in normal launches. The verifier flagged it, and it is removed.
- At placement time `players[0].mo` stands at 382.5,1254,0, not at the cave, so the planner cannot read the start from
  the player.
- Harness issues: UZDoom writes `+logfile x.log` as `log-x-log.txt`, and DRLA's `Midas_Statue_Fatality` matched a
  plain "Fatal" stop check. One New Game run timed out in the menus at 50 s and passed with `--set TIMEOUT=80`.
