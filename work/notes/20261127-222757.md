## How DRLA's Scout decides fast and slow (reverse-engineered)
- The Scout's class text (`DoomRL_Arsenal_1.1.5/language.classes`): "Pistol weapons do between 8x to 3x damage, based on
  their firerate, faster weapons dealing less."
- There is no formula. Each DRLA pistol has the number written above its Fire state (`// 8x damage with Scout, half
  spread`) and implements it as a second `A_FireBullets` taken with `RLSharpshooterPerk` (the Scout's perk) with a fixed
  damage of that multiple (DRLA Pistol: normal 1d3 x 6 -> Scout a fixed 48 = 8 x 6). So the definition is a hand-made
  per-gun list. `tools/pistols/scout_scale.py <DRLA folder>` extracts it (61 guns).
- The list, by Scout multiplier (3 = fastest, 8 = slowest):
  - **8 (slowest):** Pistol, Handcannon, Blaster, Nuclear Plasma Pistol, Hunting Revolver, PP7, Silenced Pistol,
    Speedloader Pistol, Nuclear Plasma Revolver, Plasma Pistol, Plasma Handcannon, Overcharged Blaster, Supercharged
    Nuclear Plasma Pistol, Demolition/Nanomanufacture Pistol and Handcannon and Blaster, Heavy Pulse Blaster, Death's
    Gaze, Baron Blaster, Longhorn, Anti-Freak Jackal, Grammaton Cleric Beretta (single mode), Hellsing ARMS Casull,
    Mysterious Magnum, Assault Rifle Uzi.
  - **7:** Combat Pistol (and its Plasma, Demolition, Nanomanufacture versions), Storm Pistol, Storm Handcannon, Storm
    Blaster, Storm Nuclear Plasma Pistol, Burst Cannon Uzi, Laser Shotgun.
  - **6:** Storm Combat Pistol, High Power Uzi. **5:** Shredder Pistol. **4:** Uzi, Plasma Uzi, Demolition/Nanomanufacture
    Uzi, Unknown Herald. **3 (fastest):** Storm Uzi, Marathon Shotguns.
  - **Outside the scale:** High Power versions 9-10 (a power mod adds to the number; counted as 8, the slow end); the
    Marksman Pistol family is "2x max damage" (its Scout shot is a fixed 48 against a normal 22-24, about x2), below the
    fast end, so it counts as the fastest. Asked below.
  - (The Uzi family, except the Plasma Uzi, is Automatics in Doomerfall, so its numbers are never used.)
- Stock Doom's Pistol is not in DRLA; it counts as DRLA's Pistol (8), which replaces it with the same shot and fire
  rate. A pistol-skill gun from another mod that is not listed sits in the middle (5.5). A subclass uses its parent's entry.
- **Mapping:** slow share t = (Scout - 3) / 5, clamped to 0..1. Skill 25: x(2.5 - 1.0 t). Deadeye: x(1.5 + 1.5 t).
  Together: Marksman (t 0) x3.75, Shredder (t 0.4) x2.1 x 2.1 = x4.41, Combat Pistol (t 0.8) x1.7 x 2.7 = x4.59,
  stock Pistol / Pistol / Handcannon (t 1) x1.5 x 3 = x4.5.
