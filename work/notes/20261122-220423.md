# Parity test for swinging doors (20261122-220423)

## Problem

`tools/dungeon_compare/parity.py` launched UZDoom with only `<slug>.wad`. The door slabs and door line specials were gone from the converter, so on the Doom side:

- no door existed in the "closed" stages, so they measured an open map;
- `level.ExecuteSpecial(254, ...)` for the "open" stages did nothing;
- the probe had `+THRUACTORS` and used `PCM_NOACTORS`, so door collision actors would have been ignored anyway.

Meanwhile the Daggerfall side modeled closed doors as bounding boxes. The closed stages compared a boxed map to an open one.

## Change

- `parity.py` now runs the converter with `--export-mesh` at the same `--world-scale` into `<out>/doors`, and loads `<slug>_doors.pk3` after the map wad.
- `parity_doom/zscript.zs`:
  - the probe is solid, without `THRUACTORS`, and moves with `CheckMove` without `PCM_NOACTORS`, so the door collision actors block it;
  - the player start is made non-solid so it does not block the probe;
  - the open stages call `Used()` on every `DFDoorBlocker` link, the real use path, with the user at the link height. This swings each door through `DFDoor.TryUse`;
  - shots no longer use `TRF_THRUACTORS`, so closed doors stop them;
  - the "does a body fit here" queries still ignore actors, as before.
- Guards: the run exits with an error unless the log shows door links loaded, none moved before use, and links moved after use. The log prints `DFDOORSTATE closed|opened links N moved M`.
- The Daggerfall side is unchanged: closed = door bounding box, open = no door.

## Results (world scale 0.75, 25 seeds, `parity.py` default)

Door check: Privateer's Hold 58 doors, 467 collision links, 0 moved closed, 467 moved after use. Scourg Barrow 212 doors, 1644 links, 0 then 1644.
Output: `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\review\door-parity\{ph,sb}` (not committed).

## What this proves

- The closed stages now run against the real door objects. Doors are loaded and solid, and the probe is blocked by them. Scourg and Privateer's Hold doorway false walls/openings are small (4 to 19 of thousands), so door positions agree about as well as the rest of the map.
- The open stages open every door through the real use path, and all links moved.
- The test would fail loudly if the door package were missing or doors did not respond.

## What it does not prove

- The closed Daggerfall side is still a bounding box of the door model, not the model. A door box is a decent stand-in but not Daggerfall Unity's actual collider. It was not checked against Unity.
- The open stages are not apples to apples by design: Daggerfall Unity makes an open door non-solid (modeled as no door), while this build keeps the open door solid, swung 90 degrees (owner's brief). Some open-stage mismatches near doorways are that intended difference, and the test does not separate them. A fair open comparison would need the Daggerfall side to model the swung door box.
- Doors are never tested closed-to-open-to-closed or while blocked; that is `door_check`'s job, which already passes.
- The comparison does not show that the pre-change closed numbers (older notes) were "wrong" by any amount, since the older runs have no recorded scale and are not directly comparable.
- Most remaining false walls and openings are "narrow gap" cases that are not door related (Scourg `w01`, `w02` etc. are in the old known list).
- 25 seeds, one fixed random seed, 16-unit grid. Scourg has one seed (index 23) Doom cannot stand on in every mode.
