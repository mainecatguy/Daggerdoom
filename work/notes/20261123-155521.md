# Door and door-action route bugs (20261123-155521)

## Result

- **Privateer's Hold door 15 was a false alarm.** It fails only at Doom's default radius of 16. The real game loads `overworld.pk3`, which sets radius 10.5, and door 15 passes with it.
- **Non-door models with door actions were a real bug.** There are 7 objects in 3 of the 4,232 dungeons, all main quest dungeons. Three are secret door panels, and they now work. Four are trapdoor tiles, and I left them static on purpose.

## Lead 1: door 15

Same map and door check, three player setups:

| Setup | Radius | Height | Door 15 |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Default conversion alone (the lead) | 16 | 56 | **Blocked** |
| Default conversion with the real `overworld.pk3` | 10.5 | 56 | Passes |
| `--daggerfall-player` | 10.5 | 54 | Passes |

- Root cause: the test setup, not the door. The real launch scripts I checked (`tools/combined_bridge`, `tools/fast_travel_arrival`) load `overworld.pk3`, and runtime dungeons also get 10.5 from `ddruntime.pk3`.
- Fix: `door_check` now applies the game's 10.5 itself, and `action_regress.py` tests a default conversion without `_player.pk3`. That is the real game's body (56 tall, step 24), which the old regression did not test.

## Lead 2: door-action models

`tools/dungeon_converter` now has `--list-door-actions`, which scans every dungeon.

| Dungeon | Object | Trigger | What it does in Daggerfall |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orsinium | 2 secret panels (m55032) | Walk-on record 61, chain | Swings open a 28-node pocket each |
| Lysandus' Tomb | 1 secret panel (m55017) | Lever record 9 | Swings open a 20-node pocket |
| Lysandus' Tomb | 2 trapdoors (m54001) | Walk-on | Drops into a sealed pit |
| Wayrest | 2 trapdoors (m54000, m54001) | Walk-on | Drops into a sealed pit |

Daggerfall facts (`RDBLayout.cs:914`, `DaggerfallActionDoorSpecial.cs`, `GameObjectHelper.cs:198`): the model swings 90 degrees about its own Y in 1.5 s. Its added box collider becomes a trigger, but its mesh collider stays solid and turns with it. So an open panel is still solid at its new angle. The player cannot open these directly.

**Do they gate progress?** The panels gate small dead-end pockets, not a main route. In the engine walker, each Orsinium panel adds 24 reachable positions. The Lysandus panel is in the part of that dungeon the walker does not reach from the entrance, so only the converter graph measures it (a 20-node pocket). I did not check what is inside the pockets.

### The fix (panels only)

- Panels leave the static mesh and become moving action objects (`ActionExport.cs`, `action_objects.zs`).
- They swing as Daggerfall's do, through their real trigger chain. A body in the swing is not carried. A cell that overlaps a body stays non-solid until the body leaves, because Doom has no controller push.
- Their collision is one row at real thickness. The normal 16-unit grid made the open panel so thick that a 21-wide player could not pass.

### Trapdoors stay static

All 4 tiles open onto a pit of 16 walk positions, 96 or 192 units deep, with no walkable exit in the converter's graph. Daggerfall's way out is climbing, which is out of scope. Enabling them now would add a soft lock, so they behave exactly as before. This is the question for the owner below.

## Verification

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| `special_door_regress.py`: all 3 panels, real player | Block closed, pass open from both sides, close again, stay solid, no carry, real chain fires |
| `action_regress.py`: stock E1M1, E1M1 with DRLA, E1M1 with the door package | Pass |
| Same: all 58 Privateer's Hold doors, real player and Imp | Pass, 0 failed rows |
| Same: Scourg Barrow save and load | Pass |
| Privateer's Hold and Scourg Barrow `.wad`, before vs after | Byte-identical |

No engine files changed, so stock WAD behavior cannot differ through this work.

## Failed approaches

- The first version moved all 7 objects. It worked mechanically, but the pit check showed the trapdoors strand the player.
- The 16-unit collision grid blocked open panels. Replaced with a thin row.
- The reach walker cannot stand on moving cells, so its numbers for closed trapdoors were not meaningful. I used a real-physics player for those.
- Trapdoor model m54001 is also an ordinary action 8 mover in Wayrest (3 records), so per-model cell edits leaked between records. Special doors now keep their own cells.

## Known gaps

- Nobody has played this by hand. The demo was launched and its start position checked, but its keyboard input was not exercised.
- Saving and loading while a panel is open or swinging was not tested.
- The 3 action 8 trapdoor records in Wayrest already move today. I did not test whether they can strand the player the same way.
- Doors and panels were only regression-tested in the dungeons named above, not all 4,232.
- Monsters do not react to panels beyond ordinary collision.

## Play-test list

Demo: `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\review\door-routes\play.bat`. You start facing a closed Orsinium panel.

1. Walk into the panel. It should stop you.
2. Press Use. In the demo this fires the panel's real trigger chain.
3. Watch the swing, then walk through into the pocket. Does the swing look right against its frame?
4. Stand in the swing path and fire it. You should not be carried or stuck.
5. Is anything in the pocket that matters?
