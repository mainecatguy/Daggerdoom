# Travel from the character sheet or logbook goes through normal fast travel (2026-11-27 21:23)

Goal: the journal's "find this place" (logbook > quest > Yes) moved Daggerfall without Doom when the journal was
reached from the level-up character sheet (Doom menu 7, `DFB_UI_DAGGERFALL`). Owner (Q&A item 53): "when they click
it, close the menu and then open fast travel."

## Plan (as done)

1. Find where the journal starts travel: `DaggerfallQuestJournalWindow.FindPlace_OnButtonClick` closes the journal,
   calls `DfTravelMapWindow.GotoPlace(place)` and posts the travel map. Inside a Doom menu that travel map lived in
   whatever menu was open. Doom followed it only for menus 9 (journal) and 12 (sheet) from outdoors, and never for
   menu 7 (`showingTravelMap` false, `RefuseJournalTravel` did not cover it). That was the desync.
2. Replace it, in a bridge session, with: close the whole menu, then ask Doom for its normal fast travel, opened on the
   quest's place.
3. Test it in paired runs through Doom's window as a player would.

## What changed

- `DaggerfallQuestJournalWindow` (2-line hook): in a bridge session with a Doom menu open, `DoomerfallUi.TravelThroughDoom`
  handles the Yes instead.
- `DoomerfallUi.TravelThroughDoom`: closes the journal; if the window under it is the character sheet, closes it the
  way its Exit button does (`DaggerfallCharacterSheetWindow.DoomerfallTryClose`, a 1-line public wrapper of its private
  `CheckIfDoneLeveling`). With level-up points unspent, that shows Daggerfall's own "distribute your points" message and
  the menu stays open, with no travel (the player spends the points and clicks again). Otherwise it remembers the place,
  marks the close as a choice (`UI_REASON_RESULT`), and pops to the HUD (the same pop Doom's close key uses).
- When the menu has closed (`Finish`), Daggerfall sends `KIND_UI_ASK` once with `ui_id = DFB_UI_TRAVEL_MAP`.
- Doom (`dd_bridge.cpp`, `HandleUiAsk`): that ask runs the same code as the fast travel key (`OpenFastTravel`, split out
  of `DD_BridgeNetEvent` unchanged): refused indoors with "You cannot travel from indoors.", otherwise `OpenUi(DFB_UI_TRAVEL_MAP)`.
  Only reachable with a bridge session; DOOM.WAD runs are unaffected.
- `DoomerfallUi.OnOpen(DFB_UI_TRAVEL_MAP)`: if a journal place is waiting (3 s, real time), `GotoPlace` it, so the map opens
  on that place and Daggerfall asks "travel there?" as stock. The place is forgotten on any open, or after 3 s, so a
  refused ask (indoors, mid-door) does not leak into the next K press.
- Removed: `RefuseJournalTravel` and its hook in `DaggerfallTravelMapWindow` (the journal never opens the travel map inside
  a Doom menu now), and the journal/sheet `showingTravelMap` context rule.
- `bridge/doomerfall_bridge.h`: comment on `DFB_KIND_UI_ASK` (no layout change); bindings regenerated.
- `manual.md`: one entry under the character sheet.

Applies to every route to the journal inside a Doom menu: J (menu 9), P > Logbook (menu 12), level-up sheet > Logbook (menu 7).

## Findings / failed approaches

- First two harness runs failed only on the harness side: on the private desktop Doom had a 640x400 client area but a
  320x200 screen, and it reads posted mouse positions as screen pixels. The harness now posts in the size of the latest
  Daggerfall surface (`ui: surface WxH for a` in df.log).
- The P > Logbook route (menu 12) goes through the same code as menus 7 and 9 but has no automated run of its own.

## Known gaps

- With level-up points unspent, the travel does not wait and run later. The player has to click again after spending
  them. That is Daggerfall's Exit rule, and it seemed safer than closing the sheet and losing the bonus points.
- Pre-existing, not changed: Doom's own close key (and Escape all the way out) on the level-up sheet pops to the HUD with
  `PopToHUD`, which skips `CheckIfDoneLeveling`, so the unspent bonus points look lost. Not checked in a run.
