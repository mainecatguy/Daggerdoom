## Findings: three separate faults

1. **Mod bug (real, not just the harness): the projectile roll never saw the projectile.** The engine sends a thing's
   spawn event (`WorldThingSpawned`) from `PostBeginPlay`, which it defers to the thing's first think
   (`OF_JustSpawned`, in the thinker loop). The missile is linked after the player, so its spawn event, which tags the
   shot and sets `shotMissile`, comes *after* the player's `DoEffect`, where `ExtraBullet` rolled. Debug log, every tic:
   `TMPDBG tic 37 last 36 ... missile 36` (DoEffect saw the previous tic's shot), then the `projectile BandTestBall`
   line. So in play a projectile Automatics gun would never get its extra.
   **Fix:** a projectile shot now rolls in `WorldThingSpawned`, right after the shot's first projectile is tagged. The
   projectile has not moved yet then. `DoEffect` rolls only for a hitscan shot (`bulletTic == now`). `rolledTic` still
   gives one roll per tic, so a shot that fires a bullet and a projectile in the same tic gets one extra (the bullet's).
2. **Mod bug: `out Vector2` is not supported by the VM.** The first time `MirrorMissile` ran, the game stopped with
   `REGT_ADDROF not implemented for vectors` (map time froze at the first shot). The aim now goes back through a field
   (`firedAim`). The field could not be called `missileAim`, because ZScript names ignore case and `MissileAim` is a
   method. `bands_test.py` now treats `not implemented for` as a fatal error.
3. **Harness: the target was out of sight.** A at 300 units and 9 degrees left was in a wall (fits 0, sight 0). At 260
   straight east it fit but was still out of sight. The harness now *searches* for a spot (`PlaceAside`): A 260 (else
   200) away, and the player turned 12 degrees right of it, where `AimLineAttack` from the view misses A and from
   view +5.625 degrees hits it. That is exactly the case where the engine's autoaim has to turn the shot. It found
   A at 30 degrees, player angle 18, autoaim aim 23.625 / pitch 2.749.

## The no-autoaim case

`BandTestNoAimUzi` (FPF_NOAUTOAIM) is shown by its aim: every extra mirrors about the view (18.000, -5.000), and
MirrorMissile reports "no autoaim". My first version also checked that its shots miss A, but that is wrong for this
geometry: A's square box and the ball's radius reach about 13 degrees from A's centre at 260, so shots 12 degrees off
still graze it (73 hits). That check now only asks that the gun fired. The aim check is the real test.
