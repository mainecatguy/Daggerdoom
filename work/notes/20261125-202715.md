# Fixed drops: summoned monsters drop nothing; arrival from a town

Branch `agent/fixeddrops-20261125-202715`, continuing `work/notes/20261125-082544.md` (Known gaps).

## Plan

1. Owner rule (2026-11-25): monsters that enter play without a Daggerfall id drop nothing, on Daggerfall maps in a
   bridge session only, whoever kills them. One general rule, not one per class.
2. Extend the paired test: real summon actions next to the Privateer's Hold start, kills by the player and by a placed
   monster, and any drop from a summoned death fails.
3. Show placed monsters' drops are unchanged against the last passing series, and stock E1M1 unchanged.
4. A second arrival route: a character in a town reaches Privateer's Hold overland and walks in through its door.

## Design (engine, all `[Doomerfall]`)

The rule is decided at death, so it does not matter how the monster entered play:

- `DD_LootDied` (`dd_lootrng.cpp`, already called at the start of `AActor::Die`): a monster (`ISMONSTER`, not a
  player) that dies **without a Daggerfall id**, while a bridge session is up and a generated Daggerfall map is running
  (`DDTreadmill_OnDaggerfallMap()`, new one-liner: `DDTreadmill_Active || townActive`), is marked `ddNoDrops` and its
  death runs under a loot state (from its live state if a placed monster's live state made it, else from map time and
  position). Its `ddLootId` stays 0, so `DD_LootSeed` still reads 0 for it.
- `DD_LootInherit` (already called for every spawn during a loot scope): a non-monster, non-missile child copies the
  running owner's `ddNoDrops`. So everything its death spawns carries the mark: `DropItem` drops, DRLA's `RL...Drop`
  helpers, random spawners, and in turn what those spawn in their own actions and `PostBeginPlay`.
- `DD_LootVanish`, one line at the top of `AActor::CallPostBeginPlay`: a marked **loose** `Inventory` (no owner) is
  destroyed before its first `PostBeginPlay`. Fresh thinkers get `PostBeginPlay` in the same tic they are spawned,
  after every regular thinker, so no one can see or touch it, and Doomerfall's ammo tiering (`WorldThingSpawned`) and the
  floor item table never see it. An item given straight into an inventory (`A_GiveInventory` tokens) has an owner and is
  left alone. Corpses, gibs, effects and death-spawned monsters (a Pain Elemental's death souls) are not items and stay.
- Field `AActor::ddNoDrops` (saved as `ddnodrops`, only when nonzero), cleared by `Revive`.
- Outside a bridge session, or on stock maps (no treadmill, no town), `DD_LootDied` returns before anything changes, as
  before. `DD_LootVanish` returns false for every actor without the mark, which no stock game can set.

Routes this covers (all are "a monster dies without a Daggerfall id"): `A_PainAttack`/`A_PainDie` Lost Souls,
`A_SpawnItemEx` minions (DRLA Overwatch drones, Armageddon Baron skulls, Agony Elemental spawners), DRLA "reborn" forms,
boss brain cubes, ACS and ZScript `Spawn`, and anything else. Daggerfall-placed monsters (encounter mooks and leaders,
claimed spawner results, Civil War armies, quest foes, guards, townsfolk) carry ids and keep their seeded drops.

### Cases where summoned and placed cannot be told apart (reported, not changed)

1. **DRLA monster-flagged spawners.** On DRLA's Adaptive skill (and some Armageddon spawners such as
   `RLArmageddonArchvileSpawner`), the replacement is a `Monster`-flagged spawner (`RLAdaptiveSpawner`: `Monster`,
   unshootable, health 0x7FFFFFFF) that spawns the real monster 5 tics later by ACS. `SpawnMook` (`dd_monsters.cpp`)
   gives the Daggerfall id to the first `ISMONSTER` actor it gets, the spawner, so the real monster has no id. Before,
   its drops were random; now it drops nothing. Every launcher and test uses `-skill 3`, where DRLA uses non-monster
   random spawners that the claim path handles (all 112 paired victims and 231 Civil War monsters had ids). Not fixed:
   it is in encounter placement, outside this goal. See questions/oversights.
2. **Claims can take a summoned monster.** `RunClaims` gives a pending claim's id to the nearest id-less living
   monster within range for a few tics after placement. A summoned monster near such a spot in that window could be
   taken as placed. Narrow (first seconds of a map), pre-existing.
3. **Reborn forms** (a Nightmare/Cybernetic Arch-vile's resurrection replaces the corpse with a new `...Reborn` actor)
   have no id, so they count as summoned and drop nothing. Their first life already dropped its seeded loot.
4. A stock Arch-vile resurrection (`Revive`) keeps the id, so the monster drops its seeded loot again on its second
   death (same items again). Unchanged by this work; stock Doom also drops twice.

## Test

`tools/fixed_drops/paired.zs` / `paired.py` (test only):
- New phase 6, after the gun take: at the New Game start, spawn by script an `RLPainElemental`, an `RLFormerOverwatch`
  and a `ZombieMan` (DRLA's replacement); the Pain Elemental runs its own `A_PainAttack("RLLostSoulPE")` three times and
  the Overwatch its own `A_SpawnItemEx("RLUACDefenceDrone", ...)` twice. Each kind of minion is killed alternately by the
  player and by a placed (Daggerfall id) monster from the dormant rest of the dungeon; then the Pain Elemental (by the
  player, so `A_PainDie` sends out its souls) and the Overwatch (by the placed monster); then the death souls. Every
  loose item that appears is printed as `PDSUMMONDROP`.
- `compare` fails a run on any `PDSUMMONDROP` or `PDSUMMONALIVE`, or if no Lost Soul or no drone died by the player and
  by a monster. `PDNOID` (a no-id death) still fails before phase 6.
- New mode `travel`: Daggerfall's test start (`-dfsynctest`, a character in Daggerfall city, map pixel 208,213, who has
  never been to Privateer's Hold), Doom on the overworld. The harness warps 100 tiles south of Privateer's Hold
  (`dd_tm_warp`, the overworld handoff fast travel uses), walks north into the location (DDT07154 loads as it would on
  foot), stands at the dungeon door (from the town cache) and presses Use (`dd_tm_use`). The player arrives at the
  dungeon **entrance** (852,372), not New Game's start marker (383,1254). Then the same kill, drops and take as fresh.
- `--out <folder>` names the results folder.

## Failed approaches

- Fast travel through Daggerfall's own travel map (driven by window messages: I'm At, Enter, F, type the name, pick,
  Yes, Begin) works, but Find Location lists only discovered places, and the test character has not discovered
  Privateer's Hold, so it travelled to the nearest-named other place (pixel 155,183). Making the place discovered would
  need a Daggerfall test hook and a Unity build, so the overland route above was used instead.
- First overland try warped onto the door's tile: a location is entered only by walking in from outside it
  (`holdEntry`), so the player stayed on the overworld. Landing 100 tiles away and walking in works.
