## Verdict

- **+100% is too much.** At skill 100 the Plasma Rifle and Laser Rifle match the Plasma Uzi (1.0-1.1 s against
  0.9 s on a Baron). The largest buff that keeps them below mid guns is **+25% at 75 and +50% at 100**. Only the
  Blaster stays weak even at +100%.
- With +25% / +50%, the list kills trash well at any skill and stays clearly behind mid guns on Barons and bosses, so
  it works as a safety net.
- **Another reading:** "another 50% and then 100%" more likely means **more cell savings** (75% fewer cells at 75, free
  at 100). That fits "safety net / trash killer" better, since it gives no extra power, and no damage cap is needed.
