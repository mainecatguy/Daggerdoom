# Show Daggerfall items in Doom (20261123-134538)

## Result

UZDoom shows all 311 real Daggerfall items as `DaggerfallItem` actors: 288 item templates and 23 artifacts.

- 283 of 311 items use their original sprite.
- 28 of 311 have no usable sprite and show a magenta placeholder. They still exist as `DaggerfallItem` actors with their real IDs.
- The gallery holds 460 entries, because each distinct sprite of a template gets its own entry. 432 use a real sprite (395 unique images) and 28 are placeholders.
- The engine reported `DFITEMS: shown=460 total=460 broken=0`.

## Plan and design

- No engine change. `DaggerfallItem` is ZScript in `mods/daggerfall_items/`, so a game that does not load the package is untouched.
- `tools/item_gallery/ItemGallery.cs` compiles Daggerfall Unity's own `TextureFile` and `ItemEnums.cs`, and reads `ItemTemplates.txt`.
- It writes the package, with sprites, table, and gallery map, outside the repo. Nothing generated is committed.
- `tools/item_gallery/check.py` runs the gallery and the E1M1 regressions.

## How IDs and sprites map

Each actor stores template index, variant, artifact index, item group, `TEXTURE` archive, record, and name.

The sprite follows `DaggerfallUnityItem` and `ItemHelper.GetItemImage`:

1. Useless items, ingredients, arrows, religious items, and misc items use the world texture.
2. Other items use the player texture, plus the variant. Cloaks add 1 to skip their interior image.
3. Katanas add 1 to the record.
4. If there is no player archive, the world texture is used.
5. Artifacts use `TEXTURE.432` (male) with Daggerfall Unity's artifact record table.
6. Palette index 0 and mask index 255 are transparent.

The texture name is `DI` + 3-digit archive + 3-digit record, such as `DI234012`.

## Items with no usable sprite (28)

- 27 furniture and decoration templates (217-234 and 237-245: beds, tables, chairs, curtains, rugs, tapestries, skins) have archive 0 and record 0 for both world and player textures.
- Flowers (template 251) points at `TEXTURE.213` record 18, but that file has only 18 records (0-17).

## Known limits

- Dye and material colors are not applied. Sprites show the undyed art, which is why clothing is blue. Daggerfall Unity recolors these at runtime.
- Female artifact art (`TEXTURE.433`) and gender or race armor archives are not shown. Templates show their default archive.
- Potions show the template sprite, because the recipe-specific record needs a recipe.
- Only frame 0 of each record is used.
- Flowers, Large Statue, Font, and Candle list extra variants, but Daggerfall Unity has no image for them. They show the template's world sprite once.
- Group labels: six decoration templates are in no group enum and report group -1. Some templates are in two enums, and the first is used except for gold and deeds. No sprite depends on this.
- Save and load in the gallery was not tested.

## Failed approaches

- `+logfile` with an absolute Windows path wrote nothing. UZDoom writes `log-<name>-log.txt` in the working directory.
- A play-scope `IsGallery()` could not be called from `RenderOverlay`. It is now `clearscope`.
- Treating only archive 0 and record 0 as "no inventory image" produced bogus archive 0 entries for decoration variants.

## Play-test list

- Walk the rows. Do the sprites look right, with clean edges and no stray colored pixels?
- Stand near items and check that the gold label names the item you see.
