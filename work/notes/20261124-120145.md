## What was built

- `EncounterLeaders.zs` `Faction(plan, index)`: -1 for a united dungeon (or a Civil War plan that found no side B),
  else the placement's army | stance << 4. Stances come from the planner's `civilwar_stances`: hunt 3, untilshot 1,
  ignore 5 (`DoomerfallCivilWar.StanceOf`).
- `dd_encounter_placer.{h,cpp}`: each `DDEncMookOut` carries `side` (-1 united) and `stance`.
- `dd_monsters.cpp`: `SpawnMook` calls `RigFaction` for a Civil War mook. A spawner's claim carries the faction
  and is rigged when claimed. United mooks never reach the ZScript call, so united dungeons are unchanged.
  It logs `DDWAR civil war: army A n mooks stance s, army B ...`.
- `CivilWar.zs`, `DoomerfallCivilWar.Rig(actor, side, stance)`:
  - `ChangeTid` (A 31741, B 31742; the converter writes no thing TIDs);
  - `A_SetSpecies('DoomerfallArmyA'/'B')`;
  - `Level.ExecuteSpecial(177, a, null, false, 0, enemyTid, stance)`.
  - It refuses to make an army hate its own TID.
  - It logs `DDWAR unsafe <class>` if a pool class that is not Civil War safe is ever rigged.
- `DoomerfallCivilWar` thinker (STAT_USER, one per level, saved with it): it counts both armies every 35 tics
  (first count 70 tics after the last Rig, so claims land first). When one army is empty, each survivor gets
  `Thing_Hate(0, 0, 3)` (hate TID cleared, a normal monster that sees and hunts the player). A mook rigged after
  the war ended (a quest foe coming back) gets the same turned state.
- Test hooks, kept out of the main classes:
  - `CivilWarTest.zs` (`+set dd_civilwar_test AB`);
  - `-ddtestsave <tic> <name>` and `-ddtestquit <tic>` in `dd_encounter_test.cpp`.
- Drivers: `tools/encounter_placer/civilwar_test.py` (generates an open 4096² test map in the review folder), and
  `placer_test.py --dungeon INDEX TYPE`.

The local war surprise room (side 2, united dungeons only) is left unrigged, as before. Question to the owner.

### 100 against 100 (`civilwar_test.py war AB NAME`, logs in `review\civilwar`)

100 Imps (A) against 100 Shotgun Guys (B), player in god mode in plain sight. Timeline:
- the player shoots one monster per army every 5 tics from tic 350 to 420;
- the rest of army B is killed at tic 560;
- the summary prints at tic 980.

| Run | A stance | B stance | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| w31 | hunt | untilshot | PASS: B never targeted the player before being shot, answered the shot; 95 survivors turned |
| w53 | ignore | hunt | PASS: A never targeted the player, even when shot, until the war ended |
| w15 | untilshot | ignore | PASS: A answered the shot; B ignored it |
| s31 | hunt | untilshot | Saved at tic 245 (mid-war), loaded: all 133 living rigged monsters kept TID, species, hate TID and stance flags, and the watcher kept its state. The war went on after the load, and the turn happened (92 survivors). PASS |

In every state check, bad species, bad hate TID and bad stance flags were 0. The `unsafe` count was 0.

Findings:
- **Hunt rarely picks the player during a war.** With enemies in sight, `P_LookForTID` finds them first, and players
  are looked for only when no hated actor is visible. So "hunt everyone" mostly shows when enemies are out of sight.
  This is engine behaviour.
- **Untilshot answers only about half of the hits** while fighting a visible enemy (`OkayToSwitchTarget`, "don't be
  too quick to give up things we hate", 50%). One single shot failed the first version of the test, so the test now
  shoots repeatedly.
- **Hitscan armies hurt their own side.** The Shotgun Guys did 2,500-2,800 damage to their own army per run, many of
  their deaths. They never retaliated against a teammate (same-army targets 0, as designed). Species protects only
  against projectiles and splash. Imps did 0 same-army damage.
- **Frame time.** Tic-to-tic time averaged 28.2-29.2 ms, which is the 35 Hz tic rate, so the game never fell behind
  with 200 fighting monsters. Single spikes of 165-1,082 ms happened in a minimized background window and are not
  real performance (AGENTS.md). Real frame time with a visible window goes on the play-test list. ZScript's
  `MSTimeF` between `WorldTick`s cannot isolate think time.
- First test bug: an "ignore" Imp answered at tic 571, after the war ended and it had turned on purpose. The
  retaliation window now ends at the wipe.

## Play-test list

1. Enter a Civil War dungeon (Privateer's Hold is one) and watch the two armies fight. Does it read as a war?
2. In an "ignore" or "untilshot" dungeon, walk among them, then shoot one. Does the reaction feel right?
3. Wipe out one army and check the survivors come for you.
4. Save mid-war, reload, and check the war carries on.
5. Frame rate with a visible window during a big war (level 30 RuinedCastle, index 1028).
6. Hitscan armies shooting their own side: acceptable, or a problem?
