### Known gaps

- Monster positions inside a placement come from the C++ placer after planning. The hall check counts what stands in
  the hall at spawn for seed offset 0; another seed offset could put a mook of a neighbouring placement (for example
  p26, under the landing) into the hall. Awake monsters elsewhere can also walk in.
- The points are tied to Privateer's Hold's converted map coordinates; a converter change that moves the map would
  need them redone (the tools in `tools/first_room/` do that).

## Failed approaches and findings

- The demo engine in `review\firstroom` and `DoomDaggerfall-build\uzdoom` are older than this branch's mod
  (`removeskill`, `DD_SheetSkill` missing), so the budget test failed to load the pk3 there. I built UZDoom from this
  branch into `DoomDaggerfall-build\uzdoom-stairsroom`.
- The `review\ammo-pile-demo` play folder's Daggerfall Unity predates card 01 (theme `Humanoid`, `markers 0`, stock
  monsters), and pairing card 02's older Daggerfall Unity with the new engine failed to attach to the bridge (error -5).
  So the New Game needed a Daggerfall Unity build and `install.py` from this branch.
- ZScript is case-insensitive: an array named `S` clashed with a loop variable `s`. And a `Map` declared in a loop body
  kept its contents between iterations; it needs `Clear()`.
- A door-blocking flood (actors solid) stopped at its first node everywhere, so doors could not separate rooms. The
  ceiling height did instead.
