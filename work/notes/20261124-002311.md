# Health and cheap ammo pickups (20261124-002311)

## Design
- Converter (`tools/dungeon_converter/PickupExport.cs`, called from `WriteMap`) places pickups in dungeons and building interiors.
- What counts as a room: a connected patch of walkable nodes at least N graph steps (24 Doom units each) from any wall, ledge, or door. In dungeons N is 3, and patches smaller than 6 nodes are skipped. Interiors use N = 1, with a minimum of 8 nodes. Each room gets 1 or 2 pickups, decided by the seed. Areas larger than one typical room (90 core nodes in dungeons, 60 in interiors) count as several rooms, so large caverns get more pickups at the same density.
- Items: stimpack 40%, clip 30%, shells 30%. This mix is my call.
- Fixed seed: a hash of a pickup salt, the kind of place, the map id or space id, and the room's first node. A converter run twice gives identical things in every dungeon surveyed.
- Floor counts if it connects to the entrance or to any walkable part of at least 300 nodes. The walk graph does not follow lifts, so walking strictly from the entrance left some dungeons almost empty.
- Spots under water or on doors are refused.
- Things are editor numbers 31640 to 31642, which become Doomerfall marker actors (`mods/doomerfall/src/zscript/DoomerfallPickups.zs`). In BeginPlay each marker spawns RLStimpack, RLClip, or RLShell when DRLA is loaded, and Stimpack, Clip, or Shell otherwise. It spawns the concrete class directly, because DRLA's Clip spawner rolls pistols 8 times in 26.
- **No engine change.** Taken pickups stay taken through the existing treadmill world table: items are parked on exit, a revisit drops the map's default items, the table is restored, and the table is saved in Doom saves. Pickups pick up by touch under the existing rules. Daggerfall does not track these pickups.

## Counts per room (converter survey, `python tools/pickups/pickups.py survey ...`)
Format is "pickups in a room : number of rooms". Rooms with 0 are flooded.
- Privateer's Hold: 58 pickups in 27 rooms. 1:10 2:13 3:2 4:1 12:1
- Direnni Tower: 117 in 56. 0:2 1:29 2:23 3:1 39:1 (one huge open area)
- Ruins of The Kington Farmstead: 64 in 47. 0:2 1:26 2:19
- The Cavern of Morkitherulla: 85 in 43. 0:1 1:18 2:17 3:4 4:2 13:1
- Homunorgonivah Minster: 87 in 40. 0:2 1:17 2:17 4:1 5:1 7:1 20:1
- Earlier strict-reach numbers for Scourg Barrow, Shedungent, and others are in the run log. They now get more pickups.
- Town 7154, a sample of 15 buildings: 0 to 6 pickups per building, mostly 1 or 2 per room. One house got none.

## In-game test (`pickups.py first`, both real games, DRLA)
- Privateer's Hold: 58 pickups, all DRLA classes, no markers left, no pistols or spawners.
- 13 take, leave, and re-enter cycles: each re-entry count equals the count after the take (58 to 57, 57 restored, and so on).
- The first touch failed on the first tic inside. Every later touch took the item and gave +10 health or ammo.
- The harness loops because a plain EventHandler resets on each map change. It should be a StaticEventHandler. Because of that loop, it never reached the autosave, so **save and load are untested** and `pickups.py load` has not run.
- `pickups.py check` (E1M1 stock and DRLA) has not run.
