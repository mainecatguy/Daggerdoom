## 3. Why the owner may see "no sign" even on a current build

These are not bugs. They come from the owner's own numbers (`gamedesign/decisions/20261126-skill-xp-rate-owner.md`,
`XpPerUse = 7.5`, buy multiplier 2):

- Earning is slow at the start. The starting RLPistol does about 10 HP a hit, so a Demon (150 HP) is 1.5 Skill XP and
  a zombie (20 HP) is 0.2. One point of a skill at 3 costs 23 XP (about 15 Demons). A skill at 30 costs about 188 XP
  (18,800 HP of damage), which is roughly 125 Demons.
- Nothing in Doom tells the player that Skill XP went up. The pool shows only in the Skills menu and on the sheet. This
  is asked as a question below, not built: it is a UI design choice.
