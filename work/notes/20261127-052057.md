# Visual parity testing: Doomerfall against Daggerfall Unity (20261127-052057)

Branch `agent/visualparity-20261127-052057`. Tool: `tools/visual_parity/` (README there). Demo:
`D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\review\visualparity\play.bat` opens both review pages. Screenshots stay outside the repo in

## Result

- **Pass 1 (scenes): works.** `scenes.py` puts both games at 12 seeded random spots in Privateer's Hold with no
  monsters or NPCs and writes 12 matched pairs (`s1_00` to `s1_11`). Geometry lines up almost exactly in all 12, so the
  camera transform, eye height and field of view match.
- **Pass 2 (menus): half works.** `menus.py` opens the same 5 windows (inventory, travel map, map, quest journal,
  rest) with the same character and items in both. All 5 open, are screenshotted and close in Doomerfall, and the
  Daggerfall Unity side saves its 5. But **Doom's screenshot is pure black while a Daggerfall window is open**, also
  with an unminimized window, so there are no usable Doomerfall menu pictures yet. See "Pass 2 gap".

## Issue log (for triage)

Paths are under `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\review\visualparity\`. "Pair" = `<id>.side.png` (Daggerfall Unity left).

### A. Lighting (all 12 scenes)

1. **Doomerfall is about twice as bright.** Mean brightness Daggerfall Unity 19-33 vs Doomerfall 39-50 in 11 of 12
   spots (`scenes\scenes.csv`). How bad: high, visible everywhere. Example pairs: `scenes\s1_01.side.png`,
   `s1_06.side.png`, `s1_10.side.png`.
2. **Less fall-off into the distance in Doomerfall.** Far walls stay lit; Daggerfall Unity fades them to dark
   (`s1_00`, `s1_11` corridors). How bad: medium. Probably the same cause as 1.
3. **Doors read differently.** Doors are dark slabs in Daggerfall Unity and show their planks clearly in Doomerfall
   (`s1_06`, `s1_08`, `s1_09`). How bad: low; likely lighting, not texture.

### B. Sprites and objects

4. **Candle and skull-candle sprites only in Doomerfall.** Eight skull candles on the floor (`s1_01`), two candles on
   a table (`s1_09`), a wall torch flame (`s1_00`, `s1_04`). Daggerfall Unity shows the light glow but no sprite at
   these spots. How bad: medium. Needs a check whether Daggerfall Unity really draws no flat there (the capture camera
   renders the campfire flat in `s1_07`, so flats in general do show).
5. **Doom pickups lie on dungeon floors in Doomerfall only.** 22 DRLA stimpacks and 38 Doomerfall ammo piles remained
   after monsters were removed (`scenes\doomerfall_session.json`); they show as small dark boxes with red marks
   (`s1_06`, `s1_07`, `s1_08`, `s1_10`). Allowed by the contract (Doom may place its own items), so likely expected;
   listed so the owner can decide.
6. **A small dark square sprite where Daggerfall Unity has a chair** (`s1_01`, room centre). How bad: low; unconfirmed
   what it is.

### C. Camera defaults (the as-played view)

7. **Doomerfall's own eye height and FOV differ from Daggerfall Unity's.** Eye 48 vs 51.3 Doom units (1.60 m vs
   1.71 m) and FOV 90 vs 80.7 horizontal. Compare `scenes\s1_00.native.side.png` with `s1_00.side.png`. How bad: low to
   medium; a design choice for the owner (Doom feel vs Daggerfall framing).

### D. Menus (from code and the Daggerfall Unity side; Doomerfall pictures missing)

8. **Doomerfall draws every Daggerfall window over opaque black** (`dd_bridge.cpp:886`, `Dim(..., 1.f, ...)`), while
   Daggerfall Unity shows the 3D world behind translucent or partial windows (rest window, quest journal, see
   `menus\m1_2_rest.df.png`, `m1_1_journal.df.png`). How bad: medium for the rest window; none for the full-screen
   inventory and maps.
9. **No Doomerfall menu screenshots** (gap above). Not a game bug as far as known; a test gap.

### E. Matches (no issue)

Room shapes, wall and floor textures, ceiling beams, statues, coffins, tables, the campfire and banners line up at all
12 spots, including the stacked levels (floors 0 to 1088).
