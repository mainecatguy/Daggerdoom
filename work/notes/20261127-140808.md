# Skill purchase menu and Skill XP (2026-11-27, run 140808)

Goal: the player earns Skill XP by damaging enemies in Doom and spends it in a new Skills menu; weapon skills also gain
uses from damage. Rules: `work/backlog/skills-in-doom.md`, research `work/notes/20261126-010200.md`.

## Plan (as run)

1. Doom counts the player's damage per hit, capped at the health the hit took, in the mod; reports whole amounts once
   a second through a new bridge kind. Done.
2. Daggerfall keeps the pool in the player's save data, adds XP, tallies weapon uses with its own `TallySkill`. Done.
3. Skills menu (Daggerfall window): list, price, Buy, perks at 25/50/75/100; opened by a SKILLS button on the
   character sheet and by a Doom key (`netevent df_skills`, new `DFB_UI_SKILLS`). Done.
4. Bought points: permanent point + Daggerfall's level-up sum + `CheckForLevelUp`; the sheet runs the level-up. Done.
5. Paired test (`tools/skillxp/skillxp.py`), E1M1 checks, demo, manual. See results below.

## Engineering calls (within the owner's design)

- Bought points keep the skill's use tally (Daggerfall only zeroes it when uses raise the point). The tally then
  counts toward the next, dearer point.
- Bought points skip the 6-hour check (it paces use-based gains); the 95/100 caps stay.
- A level-up from buying: closing the menu opens the character sheet (as `RaiseSkills` posts it); opened from the sheet,
  the sheet runs it on return.
- Price ignores the Reflexes setting (the owner's formula does not mention it; Reflexes scales uses, not purchases).
- Each enemy counts at most its max (spawn) health over its whole existence, across heals and resurrections
  (owner's rule; a `DoomerfallXpTally` item on the enemy keeps the total). This caps XP and weapon uses alike.
  Friendly monsters (allies) give nothing; guards and townspeople do.
- A bought point that takes a primary skill to 100 shows Daggerfall's "master" message and fanfare, as RaiseSkills does.
- Perks with no text in the menu read "Not available yet." (Energy Weapons is designed but its text is not added yet).
- Buyable includes Rifles and Dodging (both playable skills); Dodging only lacks use-based gain (backlog).
- Damage to the bridged player's own body never counts; guard bots count (owner: guards count).

### Second verifier finding: SKILLS during a level-up (fixed)

**The problem.** The sheet's SKILLS button could open the menu while the sheet was leveling, with the stat rollout
showing. A purchase that made another level ready would then, on return, re-roll the rollout from the uncommitted
stats. The attribute points already assigned for the first level would be lost. This path is new with the button,
because use-based gains cannot happen while the sheet is open.

**The fix.**
- The sheet's one-line hook now passes its `leveling` flag: `AddSheetButton(this, NativePanel, () => leveling)`.
- While the sheet is leveling, the button shows the message box "Assign your attribute points and close the sheet
  before buying skills." and does not open the menu.
- `manual.md` is reworded: a bought level-up comes when the menu closes, and the button is refused while points are
  being assigned.
- (A note here first said "the Doom key opens the menu only from the HUD, so it cannot reach this state". That was
  wrong; see the third finding.)

### Third verifier finding: Doom's close key leaves the points pending (fixed)

**The problem.** Doom's close key (P on the sheet, or P inside the menu after a level was bought from the sheet) goes
through `DoomerfallUi.OnClose`, which calls `PopToHUD`. That skips the sheet's own close, so the sheet stays in its
leveling state, with the points only in its rollout. The Skills key then opened the menu from the HUD, and a purchase
through another threshold would re-roll the points.

**The fix.**
- `DoomerfallSkillXp.Buy` now refuses while `AttributePointsPending()`, however the menu was opened.
  `AttributePointsPending` is the sheet's `leveling` flag. The sheet's existing one-line hook hands it over, and
  `AddSheetButton` stores it, because the sheet is a single persistent window.
- The menu greys BUY POINT, shows "Level-up first" next to it, and puts "Assign your new level's attribute points on
  the character sheet." in gold on the bottom line.
- Nothing is lost: the sheet keeps its rollout and shows it the next time it opens, as Daggerfall does.
