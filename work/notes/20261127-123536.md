# Shops 5: shelves are a ledger, seeded restocks (run 20261127-123536)

## Plan
1. Fix first: shelf weapon/armor material uses the owner's approved odds (`DoomerfallDrops.RollMaterial`) at the shop's
   level, not `FormulaHelper.RandomMaterial`'s classic curve.
2. Ledger: restock never clears; items sell off per day with a value-based chance; player-sold goods go on the shelves;
   stock size from the shop level shared by the building's shelves; every roll seeded by (location, building, delivery
   number = day, shelf).
3. Test over a simulated clock (`-dfshopledgertest`), paired run through `tools/shop_levels/shelf_run.py run NAME --ledger`.

## What was built
- `Doomerfall/DoomerfallShopLedger.cs` (new). Active only in a bridge session (`WorldLevel.ShopProvider != null`);
  outside one, Daggerfall's classic restock runs unchanged.
  - Each day since the shelf was last stocked is one period (max 120 simulated; a longer absence equals 120 days).
    Per period: Unity's generator is seeded by `Seed(location MapID, buildingKey, day, shelf index)`; every item rolls
    "sold to other customers"; then if the shelf is below its share of the building's stock target, one classic delivery
    is rolled into a scratch collection, shuffled (seeded), and items are added until the share is full. Doom ammo from
    the delivery is always added (it restocks every day and sells fast). At most one horse and one cart.
  - Material: during a ledger restock `FormulaHelper.DoomerfallMaterialRoll = DoomerfallDrops.RollMaterial`, so
    weapons and armor (classic and modded) roll the approved odds at the shop's level (`WorldLevel.Current` inside
    `WorldLevel.ForShop`). Armor maps through `RandomArmorMaterial`'s own weapon-to-armor mapping.
  - `TakeSold`: hook in the trade window's sell confirmation. Sold items go to the building's emptiest shelf instead of
    vanishing (shelves not yet stocked today are restocked first, so the sold goods are saved; Daggerfall skips saving
    shelves with stockedDate 0). Quest items are skipped. Outside a shop (no shelves in the interior) the classic clear runs.
- `Internal/DaggerfallLoot.cs`: `StockShopShelf` body split into `AddShopDelivery(building, into)` (rolls one classic
  delivery into a collection) plus a one-line ledger hook. Classic path is the same code as before.
- `Game/UserInterfaceWindows/DaggerfallTradeWindow.cs`: one-line hook before `remoteItems.Clear()`.
- Persistence: shelf items and stockedDate are already saved by `SerializableLootContainer`; no new save data.

## Engineering calls (constrained, within the approved proposal) - owner may want to tune
- Sell-off chance per day = clamp(0.5 * sqrt(25 / unit value), 1%, 50%). 25 gold or less: 50%/day (gone in ~2 days);
  400 gold: 12.5%/day; 2,500 gold: 5%/day (~20 days); 10,000+ gold: 1-2.5%/day (one to three months). Ammo: 50%/day.
- Stock target per building = 15 + 2 x shop level (level 30: 75 items), split evenly over the building's shelves.
  Deliveries stop adding (except ammo) once a shelf holds its share.
- One period = one day (the existing stockedDate granularity).

## Known gaps
- "All shelves share one stock" is implemented as one stock target split over the shelves; each shelf still shows its
  own items in the trade window (Daggerfall's trade UI is per shelf). Merging shelves into one trade list would change
  Daggerfall's container/trade UI and was left out.
- Stock target and sell-off curve are first guesses, not tuned by play.

## Results
Build: `CliBuild result: Succeeded`. Run: `python tools/shop_levels/shelf_run.py assemble` then
`python tools/shop_levels/shelf_run.py run ledger --ledger` (DRLA, private desktop; output in
`review/shop-shelf-levels/run/ledger/df.log`). The test uses a simulated clock: 180 days at a level-30, quality-20
weapon smith with 3 shelves.

| Price band | sold off | mean days on shelf | still there at day 180 |
|---|---|---|---|
| under 100 gold | 290 | 2.7 | 2 |
| 100-999 gold | 227 | 6.6 | 12 |
| 1000+ gold (not Daedric) | 135 | 24.4 | 20 |
| Daedric | 13 | 36.5 | 8 (mean age 22.8) |

- PASS: expensive items stay on the shelf longer than cheap ones. Items leave only by sell-off: the code has no other
  removal path, and nothing is cleared.
- PASS: a player-sold steel longsword (90 gold, 26%/day) was on a shelf the day it was sold (day 30) and sold off on day 34.
- PASS: two runs give identical stock (a per-day signature of every item: group, template, material, value).
- **Daedric on a top weapon smith's shelves** (60 shops per level, 3 shelves each, approved odds at the shop level):
  - Level 30: a fresh shop has a Daedric item on its first stocking in 73.3% of shops (card 02 measured 50.0% for one
    shelf; this building has 3). After 60 days of the ledger, **100% of shop-days have at least one Daedric item**.
  - Level 20: 43.3% on first stocking, 91.9% of shop-days after 60 days.
  - So finding one is more than "rather feasible" at a top smith: Daedric items accumulate, because they linger about
    37 days while deliveries keep coming. If that is too generous, raise the Daedric sell-off chance or lower the stock
    target. That is an owner tuning call, so I asked.
- Material odds (the fix-first item): the Daedric share of weapons and armor on first stocking was 3.98% at level 30
  (84 of 2110). The approved weapon odds give 5.00%. The gap comes from armor: Daggerfall's `RandomArmorMaterial` rolls
  leather 70% and chain 20%, and only the 10% plate roll uses the approved material odds. Enemy drops go through the same
  path. Level 20: 2.25% (approved 2.50%).
- Item count: 116 items on the 3 shelves at day 180 against a target of 75. The extra items are Doom ammo stacks, which
  are always delivered and sell off at 50% a day. Ammo now joins matching stacks. The first run, without stacking, had 141.
- Player.log has one NullReferenceException, from `DaggerfallUI.SetupSingleton` at startup. It is not from this work and
  appears before any shop code runs.
- No UZDoom changes, so the E1M1 check does not apply.
