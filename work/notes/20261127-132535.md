# 20261127-132535: Burn damage system (goal card 07)

## Plan
1. A Doom-side burn pool per monster: unlimited stacking, each application spread over 3 s, plus a Pool/Take API for card 08.
2. A fire look kept in its own file and switched by a cvar, so pulling it leaves the damage intact.
3. A test that burns 400 monsters, checks exact damage with the look on and off and with DRLA, checks that the player is immune, and times the system.

## What was built
- `mods/doomerfall/src/zscript/DoomerfallBurn.zs`: an `EventHandler` (per level, not saved: the owner said performance matters more than persistence).
  - `DoomerfallBurn.Apply(victim, damage, source)`, `Pool(victim)`, `Take(victim, share)` (returns the amount removed for the caller to deal; card 08), `Burning(victim)`.
  - Each burning monster gets one record holding a ring of 15 slots (3 s in 0.2 s steps). Apply adds damage/15 to every slot, so any number of stacks costs 15 additions and fixed memory. One slot is paid out every 7 tics with `DamageMobj(null, source, n, 'Fire', DMG_THRUSTLESS)`. The fractional remainder carries over to the next payout.
  - Each record pays on its own phase (0..6), so a big pack's `DamageMobj` calls spread over all 7 tics instead of landing in one.
  - Players are refused (`victim.player`). The fire never damages the player.
  - Pain: each payout goes through the monster's normal pain chance, so burning monsters flinch. A burning monster also turns on its `source` (normal Doom pain retargeting), so kills and aggro go to whoever set the fire.
- `DoomerfallBurnLook.zs`: the look. Fire-coloured fullbright particles rise off the body, one every 3 tics per monster, staggered per monster. It is called from one line in the burn handler, only while the `dd_burn_look` cvar (user, default on) is on.
- `DoomerfallBurnTest.zs` + `tools/burn/run_test.py`: test, behind `+set dd_burn_test N`.

## Fire effect choice
- **Schism `BurnParticlesNoDamage`**: each spawn creates 3 actors (RedFlare plus PlasmaSmoke and FlameTrails missiles). With hundreds of burning monsters that is thousands of actors a second, a real hitch risk, so I rejected it for a slaughter-sized pack.
- **Arch-vile fire**: one following actor per monster. Workable, but it is one big flame sprite sized for a player and looks odd on small or large monsters.
- **DRLA flamethrower**: fails "must work without DRLA".
- **Chosen: engine particles (`A_SpawnParticle`)**. They create no actors, scale to any monster's radius and height, need no mod sprites, and are capped by `r_maxparticles`. The choice was made on cost and reuse. **I did not do a side-by-side visual comparison** (budget), so how it looks goes on the play-test list. Swapping the look means editing only `DoomerfallBurnLook.Draw`.
