## What was built
- `DoomerfallShopAmmo.cs`: which shops sell ammo and the seeded amounts.
  - Shop types: general store 1x, weapon smith 1x (and it still rolls the higher tiers as before), armorer 0.5x (my
    call: "appropriate sounding", it outfits fighters). Pawn shops: none, pending the open question.
  - Genome per shop: size (12% near empty 0.35x, 73% 0.75-1.25x, 15% depot 2-3x) and lean (40% balanced, 45% leaning
    hard toward one type 2.5x vs 0.7x, 15% specialist 4x vs 0.25x). Never zero (at least one Doom pickup of each type).
    Normalised by sampling so each type averages exactly the target. Quality 1-20 scales stock 0.7x-1.3x (average 1x).
  - Each restock varies by ±15% around the shop's fixed amounts (UnityEngine.Random, like Daggerfall's own restock).
  - Stock rounds to Doom's small pickups (10 bullets, 4 shells, 1 rocket, 20 cells).
- `DoomerfallAmmoTiers.StockShop` now takes the `DiscoveredBuilding` (one-line hook change in `DaggerfallLoot.cs`).
  General stores now always list all four types (they had a 60% chance per type before). Regular in weapon smiths comes
  from the seeded stock, not the old 2-4 pack rule.
- Prices: Regular `priceMul` changed from 3 to 1, and cell `basePrice` from 5 to 10 (owner, 2026-11-26). Higher-tier
  bullet, shell and rocket prices are unchanged in gold. Higher-tier cells cost twice what they did.
- Tests: `DoomerfallShopAmmoTest.cs` logs statistics over 2,000 seeded stores, stocks a real general store shelf,
  buys every ammo stack through Daggerfall's trade window handlers and Yes, and logs what the inventory gained. The harness
  `SHOPRUN` waits for Doom's Clip and Cell counts to rise, then holds fire with a stock Chaingun and a PlasmaRifle and
  checks that the counts drop. `ammo.py shop` runs it and `shop_checks` grades it. `pack_checks` was updated for the
  new Regular rule.
