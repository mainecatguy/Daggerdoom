# Shops sell ammo, early (run 20261127-005134)

## Plan
1. Seeded Regular ammo stock per shop (`DoomerfallShopAmmo.cs`): a general store averages 300 bullets, 120 shells,
   30 rockets and 300 cells, varied by a fixed seed per building (mapID + buildingKey).
2. Prices: Regular = base prices (bullets 1, shells 3, rockets 10, cells 10), times Daggerfall's normal shop pricing.
3. Test: `-dfshopammotest` (`DoomerfallShopAmmoTest.cs`), plus a `shop` run in `tools/ammo/ammo.py` and its harness.
4. Paired run, the E1M1 checks, and a review demo.

## What was built
- `DoomerfallShopAmmo.cs` decides which shops sell ammo and the seeded amounts.
  - Shop types: general store 1x and weapon smith 1x (smiths still roll the higher tiers as before), armorer 0.5x. The
    armorer is my call: the owner said "maybe others that are appropriate sounding", and an armorer outfits fighters.
    Pawn shops carry none, pending the open question about them.
  - Genome per shop: size (12% near empty 0.35x, 73% 0.75-1.25x, 15% depot 2-3x) and lean (40% balanced, 45% leaning
    hard toward one type 2.5x vs 0.7x, 15% specialist 4x vs 0.25x). No type is ever zero: there is always at least one
    Doom pickup of each. Normalised by sampling, so each type averages its target. Quality 1-20 scales stock 0.7x-1.3x
    (average 1x). It reads only fixed facts about the place, so nothing adapts to the player.
  - Each restock varies ±15% around the shop's fixed amounts (UnityEngine.Random, like Daggerfall's own restock).
    Restock timing is Daggerfall's normal shop restock (`StockShopShelf` when the shelf's stocked date is old).
  - Stock is rounded to Doom's small pickups (10 bullets, 4 shells, 1 rocket, 20 cells).
- `DoomerfallAmmoTiers.StockShop` now takes the `DiscoveredBuilding` (one-line hook change in `DaggerfallLoot.cs`).
  General stores always list all four types (before, each type had a 60% chance).
- Prices: Regular `priceMul` 3 -> 1, and cell `basePrice` 5 -> 10 (owner, 2026-11-26). Higher-tier bullets, shells and
  rockets cost the same gold as before. Higher-tier cells cost twice what they did (question raised).
- **Whole-stack pricing for Doom ammo** (`DoomerfallShopAmmo.StackCost`, a one-line hook at each of the two price
  lines in `DaggerfallTradeWindow.cs`). Found in the paired run: Daggerfall prices one unit, applies the regional price
  adjustment with integer division, then multiplies by the stack. With 1-gold bullets in any region priced under 100%,
  bullets were free (the first run paid 925 gold for about 5,000 gold of base value, and 0 for the 140 bullets). Ammo
  stacks are now priced as a whole stack's value, which is the same formula without the per-round rounding. Every
  other item keeps Daggerfall's formula. Buying only: selling ammo keeps Daggerfall's per-round formula (the verifier
  rejected an unrequested sell-side change). Whether selling should also price whole stacks is a question for the owner.
  Side effects to report: whole-stack buy pricing also shifts higher-tier prices slightly (no per-round rounding), and
  cells at 10 gold double every higher-tier cell price through the tier multipliers.
- `manual.md`: a "Shops are where you buy ammo" paragraph.
- Tests: `DoomerfallShopAmmoTest.cs` (`-dfshopammotest`):
  - logs statistics over 2,000 seeded general stores;
  - stocks a real general store shelf through `StockShopShelf`;
  - opens Daggerfall's trade window in Buy mode and buys every ammo stack through the window's own click handler,
    DoModeAction, and Yes. The test money is a letter of credit, because 50,000 gold coins weigh 125 kg. The window's
    building is set to the test store, since the test is not inside a shop.
  - logs the gains, the gold paid, the base value and the region price.

  The harness `SHOPRUN` waits for the bought ammo in Doom's counters, then fires a stock Chaingun and a PlasmaRifle
  through `PlayerPawn.FireWeapon` and checks that the counts fall. `ammo.py shop` runs it and `shop_checks` grades it.
- `tools/ammo/ammo.py` now uses current builds (`D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-build\shopammo`, built from this worktree),
  data from `review\runtime-dungeons`, the town cache with the test door from `review\ammosizefix`, and
  `doomerfall-drla.pk3`.

## Results so far (paired, minimized, private desktop)
- Seeded stats over 2,000 stores: bullets mean 300.7 (min 10, max 3000), shells 120.8 (4 to 1896), rockets 29.4
  (1 to 367), cells 299.0 (20 to 3580). About 39% of stores hold over twice the average of some type. The same store
  gives equal fixed amounts twice.
- A general store shelf listed `[Regular bullets x140] [Regular shotgun shells x56] [Regular rockets x17]
  [Regular energy cells x460]`. Buying through the trade window added +140, +56, +17 and +460 to Daggerfall's
  inventory, and Doom's counters followed (Clip 60 -> 200, Cell 0 -> 460). Final counts MATCH in both games.
- Failed attempts, kept for the record:
  - Test gold as coin made the character over-encumbered, so nothing transferred.
  - Outside a shop, the window priced at quality 0 and showed "old save, no trade".
  - Setting `player.cmd.buttons` from WorldTick did not fire the gun.

## Final verification (all on the shopammo builds from this worktree)
- `ammo.py shop`: **shop: PASS, MATCH**.
  - The shelf held 110 bullets, 56 shells, 17 rockets and 400 cells. Buying them through the trade window cost 5,472
    gold for 4,448 gold of base value, in a region priced at 90%. Daggerfall's 2x buy price is 8,006 before mercantile
    and personality, which bring it down.
  - Doom fired them: the Chaingun went Clip 190 -> 172 and the PlasmaRifle went Cell 400 -> 396.
  - The test character's regions never had Daggerfall's price roll (PriceAdjustment 0; a real game rolls 750-1250 in
    `FormulaHelper.cs:2174`). The test sets 900 when it finds 0.
- `ammo.py tiers`: **packs: PASS** (18 stacks, 10 of higher tiers). Regular stock comes in whole Doom pickups and
  higher tiers in 1-2 packs. MATCH.
- `ammo.py check`: E1M1 stock exit 0, 0 script errors; DRLA exit 0, 3 script errors (DRLA's own translation and
  menudef lumps, the same as in every paired run); `-norun` exit 1337. No engine change in this work.
- Demo: `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\review\shop-ammo\play.bat`, with README.txt. A smoke launch through quiet_run
  started both games with no exceptions in either log. I could not stop that smoke launch's two processes (taskkill was
  denied): UZDoom pid 51836 and Daggerfall Unity pid 39924, on a private desktop.
