## DRLA corpses vanishing (owner bug)

Cause: DRLA Monsters' deaths never rest on a `-1` frame (`MonsterBase.txt`). `Death` goes through `DeathMain` and
`DeathEnd` to `DRLADeathEndLoop`, a 1-tic `CanRaise` frame that loops forever. `XDeath` ends the same way in
`DRLAXDeathEndLoop`. `FinalFrame` therefore found no resting frame, and `MakeRecord` marked every corpse gone. No DRLA
death spawns a separate corpse actor (checked with grep). `DestroyedEnd` shows TNT1 for monsters that leave no body.

Fix (`dd_treadmill.cpp`, `Settle`):

- A death now settles on a `-1` frame or on the loop a corpse ticks in.
- The walk follows DRLA's by-name jumps (`A_JumpIf(1, "DeathMain")`) into the dying class's own states. For the parser,
  the base class's `Goto DeathMain` points at its own state, so the class's frames would otherwise be skipped.
- The look is the last sprite and frame the walk sets, because the loop's frames are `"####" "#"`.
- A dead monster is always stored as a corpse. On restore, its look comes from its class and death label.
- `XDeath` labels resolve now. The engine stores XDeath as `Death.Extreme`, so before this fix gibbed corpses came back
  as ordinary deaths, in vanilla too.

## Other fixes found on the way

- **Loads failed with "Daggerfall stopped responding"** (p1load, p4load). This happened in town too, not only in
  dungeons. The bridge broke a load as soon as Daggerfall's heartbeat was 1 s stale, but Daggerfall's load blocks for
  2-3 s. A load now fails only after 10 s of silence (`dd_bridge.cpp`, `LoadStallNs`), close to the new-character
  path's 30 s.
- **Restored loose items had health 0.** The codec now sets 1000.
- **The town pile was dropped before position sync engaged,** at a bogus position. Piles and the test now wait for
  `DoomerfallPositionSync.Engaged`.

## Failed approaches

- **Loop detection alone (p2first).** Corpses came back, but 51 of 53 showed their spawn frame. The `####` frames had no
  sprite to take until the walk followed DRLA's by-name jumps.
- **Looking up `FindState(NAME_XDeath)`.** It always returned null, because the engine names that state
  `Death.Extreme`. I found this with a temporary state-name dump, which has since been removed.
- **Blaming the dungeon build for the load stall.** I first thought only saves made in a dungeon failed. The town save
  failed the same way.
- **Harness state in an inventory item.** DRLA swaps the player's class ("Player swapped to Portia", then "Terri" after
  a Phase Device), which loses the item or hands the new pawn a fresh one. The harness now keeps a copy in its
  EventHandler.

## Not done, untested

- **Card 5, doors and actions.** `door_objects.zs` (in ddruntime.pk3) and `action_objects.zs` (in each dungeon's doors
  pk3) are generated by `tools/dungeon_converter`, so the change needs a converter rebuild and a re-convert. Doors keep
  only what Doom's snapshot holds of the current map.
- **The snapshot-stands rule** in `DDTreadmill_ApplyPlace` refills the cache from the record when the loaded snapshot
  already holds the place. It has never fired. After loading a save made inside the dungeon (c4inload), Doom did not
  ask for the current place at all: the snapshot stood, and the place was distilled on leaving as usual.
- **After loading a save made inside the dungeon, 19 alerted monsters were stored as unchanged on leaving** (63 monsters
  became 44). On return they came back asleep on their spawn spots. The alive count still matched (95). The likely
  cause is that alertness (targets) does not survive the save, but I have not checked it.
- **FireMelt's stretched scale is not kept.** A burned DRLA corpse comes back at normal scale.
- **Leave and return with the DRLA set used an area-level-1 army** (about 50 monsters). The big-army runs were vanilla.
  The code paths are the same, but a level-30 army with DRLA was not run.
- **`exterior.py` and `piles.py` save only outside a dungeon,** and c4inload loaded the inside save. A pile inside a
  dungeon whose save was made inside it was not tested after the load fix.

## Suspected oversights

- **Exterior piles may be invisible in Doom.** The item marker space (`ItemSpace`) covers indoor spaces only, so a pile
  dropped in town is kept and restored in Daggerfall but may not be drawn or usable in Doom. Confidence: medium, from
  reading the code, not from a run.
- **Retaking a dropped gun may grant its ammo again.** `GiveAmmoOnly` uses class defaults, so dropping and retaking a
  gun can be an ammo source. Confidence: medium.
- **The exterior chunk formula is not checked against Doom's.** Daggerfall's piles use chunk = floor(world / 8192), and
  this has not been cross-checked against Doom's `ChunkKey`. Piles came back in every run. Confidence: low that it is
  wrong.
