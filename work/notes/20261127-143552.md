## Verifier round 2: killing hits convert and burst too
- Bug: `DoomerfallEnergyBurn.Hit` only converted while `victim.health > 0`. On a lethal hit, `WorldThingDamaged` runs
  after health has dropped and before `Die`, so a burning enemy finished off by a direct hit spread nothing, even at
  100. The design has no such exception.
- Fix: the conversion no longer checks health. The burn record still exists at that moment, so the hit takes the convert
  share of the dead enemy's pool. Nothing is dealt to the corpse, but at 100 the converted amount still bursts.
- New test, `Lethal` in `tools/energy/energy_test.zs` (burn100 rules): a burning enemy (pool 100) with two neighbours
  is set to health -10, the state the engine gives `Hit` on a lethal hit, and is then hit. Result in stock and DRLA:
  "neighbours burning 2 of 2 (pools 16.00 16.00), victim pool 60.00", which is 40 converted and 40% of it to each.
  - Limitation: the test sets health directly rather than firing a lethal shot.
- Fake peer, full run: 582/583. The one failure was a DRLA stress volley of **24.1 ms**: the last of six, while the
  other five took 2.8-4.2 ms. A DRLA-only rerun passed 372/372 (volleys 3.5-4.4 ms, max tic gap 33.1 ms). I cannot pin
