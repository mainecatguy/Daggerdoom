## Engineering calls (owner may overrule)
- Only **direct** hits (no `DMG_EXPLOSION`, not the perk splash, not the burst) cause or convert burn. I read
  "Splash neither causes nor converts burn" as covering a gun's own blast damage too.
- A hit converts the burn that was there **before** it, then adds its own burn (a hit never converts itself).
- Converted damage uses damage type Fire, inflictor `DoomerfallEnergyBurst` (no damage bonus, no splash, no recursion).
- At 100 the hit enemy still takes the converted damage; the burst only sets *other* monsters within 128 units that the
  hit enemy can see burning for 40% of it (no direct damage to them). 128 comes from the approved Revision 2 numbers.
- Burst look: 8 of the card-07 flame actors thrown out to the radius; off with `dd_burn_look 0`.
