# Attributes in Doom 03: Willpower (run 20261127-004045)

Goal: Willpower is a final modifier to all damage the player takes: x1.2 at 0, x1 at 50, x0.8 at 100, linear, live
value, above 100 counts as 100. Applied as the very last step, after everything else. Manual line, mentioning that
Willpower also affects spellcasting.

## Plan

1. Find the true last step of player damage in UZDoom. Done: `P_DamageMobj` (p_interaction.cpp), after the player
   armor block and the invulnerability check, right before `DD_BridgeHpHit` reports the hit to Daggerfall (or Doom
   lowers health without a bridge). Everything else (DamageMultiply, Daggerfall's damage bonus, PowerDamage /
   PowerProtection and other ModifyDamage items such as Agility's evasion, damage factors, TakeSpecialDamage,
   team damage, armor) has already run there.
2. A small engine hook there (C++), not ZScript: a ZScript defensive `ModifyDamage` runs before damage factors and
   armor, so it would not be last.
3. Paired test with hitscan, projectile, melee, environment and a "layered" source that proves it runs after armor.
4. Manual line.

## Design and why

- `DD_BridgeWillpowerDamage(player, damage, telefrag)` in `UZDoom/src/dd_bridge.cpp`, called from the two places
  where damage to the bridged player is reported to Daggerfall:
  - `P_DamageMobj` (p_interaction.cpp, one line marked `[Doomerfall]`), after armor:
    `if (damage > 0 && (damage = DD_BridgeWillpowerDamage(...)) <= 0) return 0;`. The guard keeps stock behavior for
    hits that were already 0. A hit Willpower takes to 0 counts as no hit, the same way Doom treats armor absorbing
    everything.
  - `P_PoisonDamage` (Hexen-style poison ticks, which never go through `P_DamageMobj`), after its damage factors.
    It subtracts the (now scaled) amount from health itself; in a session `DD_BridgeHpThink`'s direct-assignment path
    then reports that drop to Daggerfall unchanged, so poison is scaled exactly once.
  - Health that scripts set directly is **not** scaled: `DD_BridgeHpThink` sends the raw change, as before this card.
    Round 1 of this card scaled every downward assignment; the verifier rejected it because those are health costs
    and set-to-N effects, not damage: DRLA's `DRLA_SoulShatterSplit` (halves health), DRLAX's Cursed Dagger (sets
    health to 10; scaled at low Willpower it would kill), DRLAX's Phase Sisters class swap (exchanges stored health;
    scaled, swapping would gain or lose HP), and scripts that set health to 0 to kill. It is now reverted, and whether
    such costs should count is a question for the owner.
- Factor `1.2 - 0.4 x min(Willpower, 100) / 100`, computed exactly in integers as `(300 - Willpower) / 250`.
  Daggerfall's live Willpower via the existing sheet (`DD_BridgeStatLive(2)`, the same data `Actor.DD_SheetStat`
  reads). Nothing new crosses the bridge.
- **Rounding:** the remainder (in 1/250 points) carries to the next hit, so small hits still scale by the exact factor
  on average: at Willpower 100, four of five 1-point hits land. With plain rounding, 1-point hits (common with DRLA
  shrapnel and sector damage) would never change. The carry is not saved (Doom's live state; under one point). The
  first version used doubles; the verifier found `1.2 - 0.4 x 100/100` is 0.7999999999999999, so a 5-point hit at
  Willpower 100 gave 3 instead of 4. Integer math fixes it, and the paired test now replays every logged hit exactly.
- **Inactive outside a bridge session:** returns the damage unchanged without a session, without a valid character
  (`-1`), for any player but the bridged console player, and for telefrags (they must stay at or above
  `TELEFRAG_DAMAGE` for Doom's later checks; they kill either way). `DMG_FORCED` damage is scaled: the owner said
  all damage.
- **Ownership:** Daggerfall owns the sheet and HP; Doom applies the rule per hit and reports the final number to
  Daggerfall as before (AGENTS.md: Daggerfall passes rules to Doom for fast calculation).
- Diagnostics: one `willpower: tic N player takes A -> B (willpower W)` line per hit in the bridge log, capped at 1000,
  through the bridge's `Log`, which reaches the console only with `dd_debugmessages`.

## Known gaps and judgment calls

- Damage Daggerfall itself deals the player is **not** scaled: its own spell effects and its poison and disease ticks,
  which go through `DaggerfallEntity.DecreaseHealth` (for example `PoisonEffect.cs` and `DiseaseEffect.cs`). This
  card's rule is applied in Doom. Covering them is a question for the owner (below), so the manual line only claims
  combat and environment damage.
- Health that Doom scripts set directly (costs and set-to-N effects such as DRLA's Soul Shatter, DRLAX's Cursed
  Dagger and Phase Sisters swap) is not scaled. They are not damage taken, so they wait on the owner (question below).
- A Doom poison tick still lowers Doom's own health (by the scaled amount) and runs its own death check before
  Daggerfall hears of it a tic later. That poison bypasses the bridge is older than this card (reported as an
  oversight).
- Willpower 0 cannot be reached in a live game: Daggerfall kills any character whose live attribute is 0
  (`EntityEffectManager.cs` around line 1751, "Kill host if any stat is reduced to 0"). The test uses 1 (x1.192);
  the formula at 0 gives x1.2.
- Doom's end-of-level sector hack (`SECF_ENDLEVEL`, keeps the player at 1 health) runs before the Willpower step, so
  at low Willpower such a sector could still kill. Stock maps only (E1M8 style); generated Daggerfall maps do not use
  it.
- The step is after armor but before Doom's auto-use of inventory health on a lethal hit, which judges the hit by the
  damage before Willpower. In a bridge session Daggerfall owns HP and death, so this does not matter there.

## Questions for the owner

- Should Willpower also scale damage that Daggerfall itself deals you (its own spell effects, poison and disease
  ticks on the Daggerfall side)? Today it scales the damage Doom deals you: monsters, projectiles, melee, your own
  explosions, harmful floors, crushers, falls and Doom poison. Health a Doom script sets directly (DRLA's Soul Shatter
  halving your health, DRLAX's Cursed Dagger setting it to 10, the Phase Sisters swap) is left unscaled, because it
  is a cost or a set value rather than damage. Should any of those be scaled too?
