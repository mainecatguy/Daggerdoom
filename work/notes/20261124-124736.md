# Records 6: lights in dungeons, interiors and towns

Branch `agent/lights-20261124-124736`. Survey: `work/notes/20261124-104520-record-types.md` sections 1.1 and 1.2.

## Result

Converted places are now lit where Daggerfall Unity (DFU) lights them, with GZDoom dynamic lights. Dynamic lights cost
nothing measurable in the worst dungeon, so baked sector light was not needed and the "ask before baked light"
question does not arise.

| Place | Source of lights | Doom side |
|---|---|---|
| Dungeons | RDB light records (`RDBLayout.AddLight`): 71 in Privateer's Hold, 319 in Wayrest castle | Converter writes `DFDLIGHTS` (x y z DFU range) into `<slug>_doors.pk3`; `action_objects.zs` spawns one random-flicker attenuated white light per record, intensity 0.8, flickering by up to 1 m like `DaggerfallLight`; dungeon sectors go to `dd_dungeon_light` (112) |
| Interiors | Every archive 210 flat (`DaggerfallInterior.AddLight`), with DFU's per-record range, intensity, colour and height table | `UZDoom/src/playsim/dd_lights.cpp` attaches a user light to each lamp billboard; interior sectors go to `dd_interior_light` (48) |
| Towns | Archive 210 flats, City prefab (18 m, white, flicker) | Same file; on only while Daggerfall's `WorldTime.Now.IsCityLightsOn`. Daggerfall sends bridge text `citylights 0/1` (`DoomerfallCityLights.cs`, on change and every 5 s). `dd_citylights -1/0/1` overrides for tests |

The essential finding: **the visible walls of converted maps are the `DFMesh` layer, which was drawn full-bright**
(`hw_dfmesh.cpp` set light level 255 and no light index). Sector light and dynamic lights never touched it. The mesh
now (a) takes its brightness from the viewer's sector light (176 and above = full, as before; lower dims it) and
(b) is split at load into 1024-unit cells, each drawn with its own dynamic light list (lights whose sphere touches the
cell's box). `dfmesh_lights 0` restores the old full-bright mesh.

Also fixed on the way, because interior lamps could not exist otherwise: `SiteExport.cs` drew only interior flats with
`FactionID == 0` and centred them on their position. DFU (`DaggerfallInterior.AddFlats`) draws every interior flat,
standing on its position. The tavern tested went from 11 billboards (people only) to 31, including 20 lamps.

## Frame times

Uncapped, through `quiet_run` (window minimized on a private desktop). "Scaled" renders the scene at 960x720 with
`vid_scalemode 5` even while minimized, which is the closest available stand-in for a normal window; "minimized" is
the minimized window's own size. A true normal-window measurement needs the owner's desktop (play-test list).

| Place, 6 views (ms/frame range) | Lights off | Lights on |
|---|---|---|
| Privateer's Hold, scaled | 2.33-2.76 | 2.34-2.87 |
| Wayrest castle (319 lights), scaled | 3.44-5.05 | 3.32-3.68 (final defaults) |
| Wayrest castle, minimized | 3.75-4.96 | 3.99-4.51 |
| Orsinium castle (302 lights), scaled | 2.75-4.43 | 3.07-6.22 |
| Tavern (20 lamps), scaled | not measured | 3.67-5.76 |
| Gothway Garden town, 3 views, scaled, lamps forced on | 9.30, 14.80, 14.86 | 13.85, 16.44, 17.51 |
| same, lamps on, `gl_light_sprites 0` | | 8.73, 15.03, 15.20 |

Dungeons: no consistent cost; run-to-run noise is large (other lanes share the machine). Orsinium's worst view went
from 2.8 to 6.2 ms while two others got faster, so treat up to about 3 ms as possible; the worst frame was still
161 fps. Towns: lamps cost 1.5-4.5 ms because GZDoom lights every one of the town's 3,585
billboards (per-sprite light lookup); the cost disappears with `gl_light_sprites 0`. Lamps are only on at night.
Wayrest has the most light records of any dungeon (319).

## Findings and failed approaches

- First attempt changed only sector light and spawned lights: no visible change, because of the full-bright DFMesh.
- First interior attempt found no lamps: the engine's texture names did not match my pattern. Lamp textures are now
  registered from the DFFLATS names when the treadmill resolves them (`DD_LightsTextureName`).
- Then no lamps at all in interiors: SiteExport's FactionID filter (above).
- `+logfile` prefixes `log-` to the name given; the harness reads stdout.
- Lights do not cast shadows (no shadow maps on the mesh), so a light can bleed through a thin wall into a
  neighbouring room within its reach. DFU's dungeon lights cast soft shadows.
- Not done: torch burning sounds (`RDBLayout.IsTorchFlat` records 0, 1, 6, 16-20). Doom would need Daggerfall's
  Burning sound exported by the converter; small follow-up.
- Not done: an end-to-end paired run showing the `citylights` text arriving from Daggerfall. The DF side compiled in
  the capture build; the Doom side was tested with `dd_citylights`.
- Existing town caches (no converter version check) keep interiors without scenery flats or lamps until reconverted.
