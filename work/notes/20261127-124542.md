- `mods/doomerfall/src/zscript/DoomerfallEnergy.zs` (new, registered in MAPINFO/ZSCRIPT):
  - damage: a perk item scales every hit whose inflictor carries the shot tag of an Energy Weapons gun (or a puff
    while one is held), direct or explosion.
  - splash: on `WorldThingDamaged`, a hit from such a gun without `DMG_EXPLOSION` deals 20% of the damage it did to
    every other live, unfriendly monster within 64 units (edge distance) that the hit enemy can see. Inflictor is a
    short-lived invisible `DoomerfallEnergySplash` with no target, so it is untagged: no damage bonus, no new splash,
    no other gun perk. Never the player, never the hit enemy. `DMG_THRUSTLESS` (no shove from a splash).
  - speed: on `WorldThingSpawned`, a generation-0 tagged missile of an Energy Weapons gun gets `vel` and `Speed` x
    (1 + bp). Children (fragments a ball spawns) keep their own speed. Hitscan guns are unaffected.

## Engineering calls (owner may overrule)
- **"Non-exploding" is per hit, not per gun.** A hit flagged as explosion damage (`A_Explode`/`RadiusAttack`) never
  splashes; any other hit does. So the stock BFG9000's ball impact and each BFG ray splash (the stock BFG has no
  `A_Explode`), and a Tristar ball's direct impact splashes while its blast does not. The 2026-11-26 damage note read
  "non-exploding" as leaving out exploding guns entirely (every BFG). Asked in the run report.
- Splash amount is 20% of the damage the hit actually did (after the damage bonus and the enemy's own damage factor).
- Splash needs sight from the hit enemy (so it does not pass through walls).
