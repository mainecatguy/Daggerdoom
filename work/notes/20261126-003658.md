Goal: list every rifle, show which would be unsafe under the Rifles and Pistols perks
(`work/backlog/skills-in-doom.md`), and ask the owner where rifles go. Research only, no game code. This note does not
decide the skill split.

Brief: `work/backlog/rifles-research.md`. That file is in the main checkout (`D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall\work\backlog\`) but
not on this branch. I read it there.

## Plan (as run)

1. Find DRLAX's weapons in every format (DECORATE, ZScript, ACS).
2. List DRLA rifle candidates: everything named rifle, everything carrying `RLSingleShotRifleTypeWeapon`, and other
   long guns that work like rifles.
3. Trace each gun's default fire path (no mods, no class perk) for damage, cycle, magazine and tokens. I used a small
   tracer script for this and read the complex guns by hand, because DRLA's state jumps fool a simple tracer. The
   first pass had wrong cycles for 7 single-shot rifles (see "Correction" below). All rows were then re-read from the
   DECORATE with line numbers, and the table and DPS were recomputed.
4. Model the perks and compute DPS next to the DRLA Pistol, Handcannon and Minigun.

## Finding 1: DRLAX adds no rifles (no new hand weapons at all)

- DRLAX's `DECORATE.txt` includes only class files, `actors/DRLAX_Misc.txt` and a replacement `RLFist`
  (`actors/weapons/standard/Fist.txt`). `ZSCRIPT.txt` includes classes, familiars, gizmos, items, unlocks and menus.
  No file defines an `RLWeapon` or `Weapon` subclass other than `RLFist`. So the earlier token searches were right:
  DRLAX has no weapons of its own.
- What DRLAX does add for rifles is new ways to get DRLA's rifles:
  - **Magnetic Coil Kit** (`zscript/DRLAX/Items.zscript:2037`): combines a Hyperblaster and a Laser Shotgun into DRLA's
    **Railgun**.
  - **Blueprint EX** (`zscript/DRLAX/BlueprintEX.zscript`, `menudef.txt:173-208`): unlock entries for DRLA assemblies,
    including Sniper Rifle, Assault Rifle, Gauss Rifle and Anti-Materiel Rifle.
  - Gizmos (`zscript/DRLAX/Gizmos.zscript`) such as the Weapon Recycler and Tome of Sins name DRLA rifles in their
    loot lists. For example, the Tome of Sins can give the MA-75B (Marathon) and Chameleon Rifle uniques.
  - The DRLAX Sarge class has a shoulder-mounted MA-75B (`RLShoulderArsenal_MarathonAssaultRifle`,
    `zscript/DRLAX/classes/SargeWeapons.zscript:945`). It is a class ability, not a hand weapon, and Doomerfall has no
    Doom classes.
- So "pistol balance tuned for DRLAX" means tuned for DRLA's pistols as DRLAX plays them. Every rifle below is a DRLA
  1.1.5 weapon.

## Finding 2: in Doom terms, every DRLA gun fires automatically while the trigger is held

A Doom weapon that returns to `Ready` (or calls `A_ReFire`) fires again while fire is held, unless it has
`+WEAPON.NOAUTOFIRE`. Only three DRLA weapons set that flag (Duke 2 rifle, Neural Stunner, Nanomachic Armament
Generator). So the engine flag cannot define "semi-automatic". The fire modes below follow each gun's design: DRLA's
own description and how long one trigger cycle takes. "Semi-auto" means one bullet per cycle, at roughly 7 to 20 tics,
with a magazine. "Single-shot" means a bolt action, a one-round chamber that reloads after every shot, or a charge shot.

The `SingleShotRifle` token is not a clean rifle list either. It sits on the semi-auto Marksman Rifle and on the
Assault Chaingun, Assault Minigun and Scoped Rifle. It is missing from the bolt-action Sniper Rifle. Steel Beast
carries it beside the Shotgun token.

## Method and assumptions

- Damage is the average per trigger pull against one target. A Doom `A_FireBullets` without `FBF_NORANDOM` multiplies
  damage by 1d3 (average x2). `random(a,b)` counts at its middle. Explosions are added at full value for a direct hit.
  The Autocannon puff's `A_Explode(128,128)` adds 128.
- Cycle is the tics between trigger pulls with fire held: up to `A_ReFire`, or to the end of the fire state when it
  goes straight to `Ready`. For guns with a one-round chamber, it includes the automatic reload after each shot
  (`DRLA_AutoReload`). For magazine guns, it leaves out magazine reloads, so DPS is "within a magazine". 35 tics = 1 s.
- Magazine is the base size from each gun's Reload check. A Bulk mod raises it.
- Default path only: no modpacks, no DRLA class perks, no set bonuses, unscoped unless noted.
- Paths are relative to `GameMods/ExtractedData/DoomRL_Arsenal_1.1.5/actors/weapons/`.

## DPS under the Rifles and Pistols perks

The perk text gives only end points, so I made a model for it:

- "Fast" means a 4-tic cycle (8.75 shots/s). "Slow" means a 35-tic cycle (1 shot/s). Between these, the multipliers
  scale in a straight line by cycle, and they stay at the end values outside it. These are my anchors, not the
  owner's. At 75 and up, the combined multiplier only ranges from 3.75 to about 4.6. (2.5 - t) x (1.5 + 1.5t) peaks at about 4.59 at t = 0.75,
  a cycle of about 27 tics, and is 4.5 at 35 tics or more. So the anchors barely change the result.
- **25:** reversed Deadeye, x2.5 (fast) to x1.5 (slow).
- **50:** the 100-point bonus pool refills in 0.5 s. I assumed one shot can spend the whole pool and that the bonus is
  not multiplied by Deadeye. That adds min(100 per shot, 200 per second) of DPS. Overkill carry-through is not in the
  numbers. It only matters when shots kill.
- **75:** Deadeye, x1.5 (fast) to x3 (slow), multiplied with the 25 perk.
- The perks' faster reloads (20%, 40% and 60% at 25, 50 and 75) are not in the numbers. They would raise the
  single-shot rifles most, because reload is most of their cycle. For example, the Anti-Materiel Rifle's cycle would
  fall from 58 to about 36 tics at 75 (22 fire + 36 x 0.4 reload), which is about 1.6x more DPS.

How to read it:

- With the perks, pistols reach about 330-410 DPS. That is a little above an unperked Minigun. The 50 pool provides
  most of it (+200 DPS on a gun that does 30).
- The **semi-auto rifles** reach 455-694 DPS, 1.1x to 1.7x the perked Handcannon (1.4x to 2.1x the perked Pistol).
  Their base DPS (58-117) is already 1.2x to 2.4x the Handcannon's 48, or 1.9x to 3.9x the Pistol's 30. They are
  stronger, but in the same range.
- The **single-shot rifles** get about the largest multiplier: x4.5 at a cycle of 35 tics or more, and the Railgun
  (23 tics) about x4.57. The Railgun, Anti-Materiel Rifle, Autocannon and full-charge Gauss reach
  about 1280-1690 DPS, or 3.1x to 4.1x the perked Handcannon, before the faster reloads. Each shot is also 200-1350
  damage. With the 50 perk's unlimited overkill carry, one Anti-Materiel shot (600 x 4.5 = 2700) would pass through a
  line of monsters. **These are the unsafe ones.** The Sniper Rifle, Steel Beast and tapped Gauss (about 780-880, or
  1.9x to 2.1x the Handcannon) are borderline. Their per-shot damage (160-350, x4.5) and the overkill carry make them
  riskier than the DPS alone suggests. The Elephant Gun (about 480) is in line with the semi-auto rifles.

## Recommendation (for the owner to decide)

1. The owner's concern holds for the **single-shot heavy rifles**: Anti-Materiel Rifle, Gauss Rifle, Autocannon,
   Railgun, Sniper Rifle and Steel Beast. The pistol perks would roughly quadruple guns that are already the strongest
   single hits in DRLA, and the overkill carry would turn them into line-clearers. Keep them out of Rifles and Pistols.
   They match the backlog Marksmanship skill. The Railgun could instead go to Energy Weapons, because it uses cells.
   The Steel Beast could go to Shotguns, because it carries the Shotgun token and fires pellets.
2. The **semi-auto ballistic rifles** are a short list: Marksman, HP Marksman, Scoped Rifle, Assault Chaingun, Assault
   Minigun and NAG M14 mode. Under the pistol perks they land 10-70% above the perked Handcannon, which looks
   manageable.
   They could stay in Rifles and Pistols as the skill's name says, perhaps with a smaller Deadeye cap for rifles. They
   could also join Marksmanship if the owner prefers "rifles separate, period".
3. The automatic and burst rifles belong in Automatics under any split.
4. Whatever the split, Doomerfall needs its own weapon list, because DRLA's `SingleShotRifle` token does not match fire
   mode. It tags the Assault Chaingun and Minigun but not the Sniper Rifle.

## Failed approaches and limits

- **Correction after verifier review.** My first hand reads used a display filter that hid every line containing
  `A_GunFlash`, `A_WeaponReady` or `A_PlaySound`, even when that frame had tics. That dropped real frames: the first
  fire frame of the Autocannon, Anti-Materiel Rifle, Gauss and Steel Beast, a reload frame of the Elephant Gun, the
  last bolt frame of the Sniper Rifle, and the Railgun's 3 no-fire `A_WeaponReady(62)` tics. The corrected cycles are:
  Sniper 33 -> 37, Elephant Gun 39 -> 40, Autocannon 35 -> 37, Gauss tap 66 -> 68 (full charge 165 -> 167), Railgun
  20 -> 23, Steel Beast 36 -> 40. Anti-Materiel stays 58, but its split is 22 + 36, not 20 + 38. I fixed the filter to
  keep every frame with tics, then re-read every row: the semi-auto table, the automatic table (all 4 tics, MA-75B 2,
  Burst 22 per burst) and the Pistol and Handcannon references (14 and 16 to `A_ReFire`). Those were all unchanged.
  The Assault Minigun's ~15 holds: after each shot it resets its spin counter to 1 and spends 13 no-fire tics in
  `ReadySpin` (lines 55-67, 181-185) before `ReadySpinActive` lets it fire. The conclusions did not change. The unsafe
  group is now 1280-1690 DPS instead of 1300-1750, and the borderline group is 780-880 instead of 900-970.
- **Second review.** I had written the semi-auto ratio as 1.1x-2.1x the Handcannon, but 2.1x was against the Pistol.
  Against the Handcannon it is 1.1x-1.7x (10-70% above), fixed in both places. The Railgun magazine gives 9 shots
  per 40-cell load, not 10. The energy rifles, which were listed by name only, now have full rows.
- **Third review.** The NAG M14 magazine is a fixed 20, not "varies". The NAG "other modes" row was wrong: the
  damage ranges from 12 to 24, and each mode has a fixed magazine. It is now one row per mode, with the SMG and
  novelty modes marked and the Dog Gun reported separately as unmeasured. The perk-model peak is about x4.59 (at a
  cycle of about 27 tics), not x4.5.

- A tracer that follows only the default jump path cannot follow DRLA guns that toggle inventory flags each shot
  (Minigun, burst and spin-up guns) or that branch on charge (Gauss). I read those by hand. Cycles marked "~" come from
  that reading and may be off by 1-2 tics.
- I did not apply monster damage factors. DRLA marks many rifle puffs "Piercing", and some DRLA monsters resist or take
  extra from that type.
- I did not include modpacks (Power, Firestorm, Sniper, Bulk), which raise damage and fire rate a lot. They are DRLA
  systems, and Doomerfall's handling of them is open.
