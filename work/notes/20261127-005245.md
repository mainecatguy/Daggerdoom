## The fix
`DoomerfallAutomatics.FromGun` is now the one check behind the damage bonus, the burst bonus and Sunder:
- **Bullets:** the damage comes with its puff as the inflictor (UZDoom always passes a puff for hitscan, a temporary one
  if needed). A bullet lands in the tic it is fired, so if an Automatics gun is held then, that gun fired it.
- **Projectiles:** tagged in `WorldThingSpawned` when the player fires one next to themselves (within their radius +
  64) with an Automatics gun held and the trigger down. The tag keeps the shot's burst state and the gun, so the hit and
  its splash count when it lands, even after a weapon switch.
- Everything else gets nothing: rockets, grenades, barrels, projectiles fired before the trigger was pulled, and
  player-owned projectiles spawned out in the world (fragments).
- Hit log lines now end with `by <inflictor>`, and each tagged projectile logs `projectile X from Y`.

## Known gaps
- A projectile that explodes in its own spawn check (fired point blank into a wall or enemy) hits before
  `WorldThingSpawned` runs (UZDoom runs PostBeginPlay on the projectile's first tick), so it gets no bonus.
- Tags are not saved, so a projectile in flight at a save loses its bonus.
- A hitscan attack fired from something other than the gun, such as an inventory item that fires bullets, would count
  while an Automatics gun is held. I found no such item in stock Doom or DRLA's automatic guns.
- The paired run checks the perks with real Daggerfall but not the source rule. The source rule is Doom-side only and
  is covered by the fake-peer test.
- No stock or DRLA Automatics gun fires projectiles that I found (DRLA's Chaingun, Battle Rifle and Minigun use
  `A_FireBullets`), so the projectile path only matters for other mods' guns.
