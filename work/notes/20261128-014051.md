# Health pickups lean per dungeon too (run 20261128-014051)

Goal: owner Q&A items 34/35 (2026-11-27): health leans per dungeon; keep hard leans about 1 in 5.

## What changed
- `tools/dungeon_converter/PickupExport.cs`: health is now one of the lean kinds (base odds 30 of 98; powerups keep a
  flat 2%). A health lean comes from its own roll of the same fixed loot seed (`LootSeed.DoomPickupLean`, map id).
  Otherwise the ammo kind comes from the same roll as before, so every dungeon that still leans to ammo keeps its kind
  and strength. The strength curve is unchanged (`0.8 * v^3`), so the hard-lean rate is unchanged. Each kind becomes the
  lean kind at its base odds, so the expected mix is exactly the base mix. Nothing reads the player. Only the converter
  changed: no engine or game code.
- `--pickup-lean-survey` now prints health's spread, strong leans by kind, and health in the average.
- `manual.md`: one phrase about health leaning.

## Results (`python tools/pickups/mix_survey.py lean`, all 4232 dungeons)
- Average: health 30.15%, clips 29.94%, shells 24.94%, rockets 9.97%, cells 3.01%, powerups 2.00%.
- Health share per dungeon: min 6.0%, p10 15.2%, median 29.5%, p90 46.7%, p99 78.3%, max 84.1%.
  458 dungeons are over 45% health and 725 are under 20%.
- Strength over 0.4: 857 of 4232 (20%, the same as before). By kind: health 280, clips 257, shells 209, rockets 86,
  cells 25.
- Lean kinds: health 1264, clips 1299, shells 1088, rockets 447, cells 134.

## Real conversions (`python tools/pickups/mix_survey.py convert 40`)
- 40 dungeons, 2736 pickups: clips 29.3%, shells 29.9%, health 27.9%, rockets 7.8%, cells 3.3%, powerups 1.8%
  (sampling noise around the base; 2735 pickups before, so placement is unchanged). A second conversion was identical
  in 3 of 3.
- Health-leaning examples: #114772378 health 75.0% (lean 0.53), #142709492 52.7% (0.37), #239543676 46.5% (0.22).
