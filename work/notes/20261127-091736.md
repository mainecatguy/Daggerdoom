# Doom pickups: dungeons only, new mix, uneven per map, powerups (run 20261127-091736)

Goal: `work/goal.md` (owner, 2026-11-26). Earlier pickup work: `work/notes/20261124-002311-pickups.md`.

## Plan
1. Converter: no pickups in any building interior (`SiteExport.cs`), dungeons only.
2. New mix with a fixed per-dungeon lean (`PickupExport.cs`).
3. New marker actors for rockets, cells and six powerups (`DoomerfallPickups.zs`, `MAPINFO.txt`).
4. Survey many dungeons, check every building of a town, marker spawn test, paired runs for house, shop, dungeon and
   taken pickups, DOOM.WAD E1M1 stock and DRLA. Demo folder.

## What was built
- **Interiors:** `SiteExport.cs` no longer passes nodes to `AppendPickups`; it logs `pickups: none indoors (<type>)`.
  The house-only rule `PickupsInBuilding` and the interior room sizes are gone.
- **Mix:** health 30% and powerups 2% in every dungeon. The other 68% is ammo: base clips 30, shells 25, rockets 10,
  cells 3 (percent of all pickups), so the overall base is exactly the owner's mix.
- **Lean (my engineering call within the owner's "use our loot system to make this unpredictable or uneven"):** each
  dungeon draws one lean kind among the four ammo kinds with the base odds, and a lean strength
  `s = 0.8 * v^3` (v fixed in [0, 1)). Its ammo mix is `(1 - s) * base + s * (all of the lean kind)`. Because the lean
  kind is drawn with the base odds, the expected mix over many dungeons is exactly the base mix. The seed is
  Daggerfall's loot seed (`LootSeed.Of`, copied into the converter as `LootSeedOf`) with a new kind
  `LootSeed.DoomPickupLean = 8` and the dungeon's map id, so it is the same place identity Daggerfall's loot uses, the
  same in every game, and never reads the player. Positions and room seeds are unchanged from before.
- **Why health does not lean:** the owner wants health restricted ("restrict them a little more than we do now but not
  massively"). A health-leaning dungeon would reach ~84% health. So health is fixed at 30% (down from 40%). Asked below.
- **Powerups:** Berserk 25, Partial Invisibility 25, Light Amplification Visor 25, Soulsphere 15, Megasphere 5,
  Invulnerability 5 (shares of the 2%). Markers spawn the concrete DRLA class (`RLBerserk`, `RLBlurSphere`,
  `RLInfrared`, `RLSoulsphere`, `RLMegasphere`, `RLInvulnerabilitySphere`) or the stock class without DRLA. DRLA's own
  replacements are spot spawners that also roll a supply crate, so they are skipped (as for clips).
- Editor numbers 31643-31650 (rockets, cells, then the powerups), only in the Doomerfall mod's MAPINFO and only placed
  by the converter. **No engine change.** Rockets and cells go through the existing ammo tier swap
  (`DoomerfallAmmo.zs` already handles RocketAmmo and Cell).
- Converter option `--pickup-lean-survey` prints every dungeon's lean and the average.

## Results
Tools: `tools/pickups/mix_survey.py` (lean, convert, markers), `tools/housepickups/housepickups.py` (survey, assemble,
run, wad), `tools/taken_pickups/run_taken.py --root review\dungeonpickups`. Output outside the repo in
`D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-build\pickupmix`.

- **Lean over all 4232 dungeons** (`mix_survey.py lean`): average clips 29.85%, shells 24.97%, rockets 10.03%,
  cells 3.14%, health 30.00%, powerups 2.00%. Lean kinds: clips 1827, shells 1560, rockets 633, cells 212. 857
  dungeons (20%) lean with strength over 0.4; 131 are rocket-heavy and 47 cell-heavy at that strength.
  Spread of each kind's share (min / median / p90 / p99 / max) before health was fixed: rockets 2 / 9.3 / 10 / 60 / 80.
- **40 real conversions** (`mix_survey.py convert 40`, a fixed spread through the map table): 2735 pickups. Overall
  clips 29.7%, shells 27.8%, health 29.5%, rockets 8.2%, cells 3.0%, powerups 1.8%. Individual dungeons lean, for
  example #524697359 shells 55.7%, #29406720 cells 22.1%, #88534608 clips 54.2% and no shells. Powerups: berserk 15,
  invisibility 10, visor 14, soulsphere 8, megasphere 1, invulnerability 2 (50 total, matches 25/25/25/15/5/5 within
  noise). Second conversion identical 3/3.
- **Rocket-leaning dungeons:** Yagertor's Nest (#254227629) 53 rockets of 103 pickups; The Krorchem Graveyard 6 of 10.
- **Privateer's Hold:** 58 pickups (same spots as before): health 18, clips 17, shells 16, rockets 2, cells 2,
  berserk 1, megasphere 2. Lean clips 0.08 (barely any).
- **Markers** (`mix_survey.py markers`, DOOM.WAD E1M1): all 11 become the right item, stock classes without DRLA and
  `RL*` classes with DRLA; 0 markers left; no unknown classes.
- **E1M1** (`housepickups.py wad`): stock exit 0, E1M1 entered, 0 unknown things, 0 script errors, 0 fatal. DRLA the
  same except DRLA's own 3 known script errors (translation, Monsters menudefs).
- **Paired runs** (both games, minimized, DRLA, fresh interior cache so buildings convert on demand with this
  converter): general store DDS4D0L4 0 pickups (converted on demand, `T8325752.ddt`), house DDS4D0JK 0, Privateer's
  Hold 77 loose pickups — all pass. The treadmill logged 58 map items at map start (exactly the converter's 58); the
  other 19 appear after map start from other systems (not investigated, not from these markers).
- **Taken stays taken** (`run_taken.py all`): the take harness now also takes a powerup when the map has one. Took
  RLStimpack, RLBerserk and a tiered rocket (`DFAmmo_Rocket_0`). take 5/5, loadin 5/5, loadout 2/2 pass: gone after
  leave and return, after loading the inside save, and after loading the town save in a new launch (rebuilt from
  Daggerfall's record).
- **Town survey** (`housepickups.py survey`, every building of 7154): at the time of writing, 155 buildings converted
  and every one logged `pickups: none indoors`, with 0 placing any. Some House5 records log "no usable interior" (no
  map at all). The survey was still running when this was committed.
