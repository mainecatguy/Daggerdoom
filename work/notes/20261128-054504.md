# Townspeople who walk and use buildings: architecture proposal (run 20261128-054504)

Research only; no game code changed and nothing launched (the review builds date from 2026-11-24).

## 0. Walking already exists

The persistent townspeople (2026-11-23) have daily schedules in `Townsfolk.cs`. Each step is *inside* (hidden),
*outside* (stand, or pace up to 6 cells per axis and back), or *walking* (A* on Daggerfall's city nav grid between buildings).
Daggerfall sends the nearest 128 visible people every 0.5 s as position + velocity; `dd_townsfolk.cpp` moves them
with `SetOrigin`. The 2026-11-23 run saw walkers move. Possible reasons for "they don't move" (none measured today):
35% of outside steps stand still, and every pacing step pauses 20-120 game s (2-10 real s) at each end
(`Townsfolk.cs:413`); walkers are slow, about 20-40 Doom units/real s against a player's 300+; most people are home
at night; or a regression. **Speeds, derived from the code, not measured** (1 DF unit/game s = 9 Doom units/real s
at the 12x clock): base speed is 5 x 0.8-1.2 per person. A walk lasts `distance*1.35/speed + 72 game s`
(`:331`, `:349`) and moves at path length / duration (`:395-399`, `:444-448`). With a path 1.2x the straight line, a
10-cell trip runs about 28 Doom units/s and a 5-cell trip about 22; long trips approach about 40. Pacing is
`speed*0.7` (`:413`), about 25-38 Doom units/s; guards are a further 0.85x. Short walks are much slower than the base
speed, which also makes walking hard to notice. Night exceptions: a third of guards work 12 h night shifts outdoors; NightOwls wake around
15:00 for 10-13 h (about 37.5% of their stops at a tavern, 35-80% of each stay outside); Pacers are up 17-21 h, 95%
outside.

**People pop or jump with no door, five ways.** (1) Walks end at a personal anchor up to ±3 cells from the site
cell, which is itself the walkable cell nearest the building centre (`Anchor`, `Townsfolk.cs:480-496`). (2) Stays are
cut into 0.4-1.4 h chunks, each randomly inside or outside (`BuildPlan`, `:338-343`); an outside chunk followed by an
inside one vanishes wherever pacing took them. (3) **Street teleport:** every new outside chunk restarts pacing at the
anchor (`Pose`, `:402-416`, u = 0), and every walk starts at the anchor (`:395`), so after an outside chunk the person
jumps back to it. `Wander` picks up to 6 cells per axis (`:502-504`), so up to about 8.5 cells (roughly 400+ Doom
units) in a straight line, beyond Doom's 128-unit smoothing (`dd_townsfolk.cpp:170-174`).
(4) **Wake and sleep:** at home, a day whose first or last chunk is outside has no walk step (one is added only when
the place changes), so the person appears or vanishes at the pacing spot. (5) **No path:** a walk whose path search
fails (start or goal not walkable, unreachable, or the 60,000-cell limit; `:548-583`, cached as null at `:542`) gets
an invisible pose (`:395-398`), so the person is hidden for the whole walk. How often this happens is unmeasured.
Also missing: inside, only residents at home are shown; walkers ignore Doom geometry and actors; nobody reacts.

## 1. What Daggerfall Unity has, and who reads positions

Vanilla `PopulationManager` keeps up to 96 anonymous day-only mobiles near the player, random-walking on
`CityNavigation` and forgotten when recycled. Doomerfall's roster replaces it in bridged games: up to 1200 people per
town, positions a pure function of (seed, time); only deaths and corpses are saved. Position readers: quests none
(`Game/Questing` never uses `MobilePersonNPC` or `PopulationManager`; quest people are `StaticNPC`s). Talk
(`DoomerfallTalk`) uses id and distance. Crime: vanilla promotes nearby guard mobiles; Doom uses `dd_guards.cpp` and
card `outdoorguards`. **Find NPC is built** (`DoomerfallFindNpc.cs:521-549`, `dd_findnpc.cpp`): it calls `FindStep`
and `Pose`, and for someone inside reports the building `Sites[step.a].key`. Any schedule change must keep it right.

## 2. Who owns what

- **Daggerfall:** roster, schedules, homes, the living and the dead, and where each person should be now.
- **Doom:** the body in the live area: motion, collision, sprites, damage, combat AI once hostile.
- **Handoff (recommended).** Daggerfall sends *legs* (id, door-to-door path, start/end game time) when something
  changes; Doom walks them with `P_TryMove` and snaps a body more than ~2 s behind schedule while out of view. Today's
  0.5 s list stays as the fallback. Doom reports deaths (exists) and *detached* (turned hostile or fled); Daggerfall
  stops listing a detached person for the visit.
- **Saves today:** the ledger only. After a load, `DD_TownsfolkResync` (`dd_townsfolk.cpp:459-470`) clears the rows
  and the tick destroys every saved townsperson; the next list respawns them on schedule. Walkers can keep that.
  Detached people must be a separate Doom class the resync leaves alone, and Doom re-sends their ids after a load.

## 3. Walking in Doom

- **Paths:** waypoints on Daggerfall's nav grid (48 Doom units per cell), which matches the converted town; UZDoom
  needs no pathfinding. A blocked walker waits, side-steps, then snaps out of view.
- **Doors:** `DoomerfallFindNpc.BuildingDoor` (`:244-290`) already gets a building's door from map data alone, since
  Daggerfall's town scene is inert under Doom. It takes the first Building door and falls back to the building origin
  when the model has none. Move it into a shared helper. Door-less buildings keep today's site cell. Multi-door
  buildings: use the door nearest the site cell (one door per building; more doors is later work). Interiors are
  separate maps: going in is "reach the door, disappear"; coming out is "appear there, walk off".
- **Inside:** list everyone whose step is inside this building (workers, customers, tavern visitors), with arrivals
  and departures at the entry marker. Indoor moves between the markers the at-home feature uses.
- **Crowds:** Doom cost is estimated at one move and floor check per walker per tic for at most 128 rows. That is an
  estimate, not a measurement, but small next to a slaughter pack. Daggerfall city's roster (1200 people, 647
  places) builds in 13-20 ms (`D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\play\run\df.log:139676`: 18 ms). **Unmeasured:** the
  first-poll A\* cost (the 2026-11-23 note guesses a one-time stall of tens to a few hundred ms; searches up to
  60,000 cells on a 512x512 grid), the half-second `Collect` pass over up to 1200 people
  (`DoomerfallTownspeople.cs:374-398`), and Doom's cost for today's walkers and for proposed `P_TryMove` walkers.
- **Guards and hostile townspeople** keep walking until `outdoorguards` or `npc-self-defense` bots take over.

## 4. Options, risks, first step

- **A. Puppet (today) + doors + inside lists.** Small, C# only. Walks through Doom props; no reactions.
- **B. Leg handoff (recommended).** A plus a bridge leg event (version bump) and about 200 lines in
  `dd_townsfolk.cpp`. Risk: drift from the schedule, so it needs the snap rule.
- **C. Doom owns schedules.** Breaks "Daggerfall owns permanent state". Rejected.

Risks: a big-town stall (above); door-less or multi-door buildings; doors blocked by converter objects; menus that
run the clock must re-send legs; Find NPC must keep reading Daggerfall's pose.

**First card, "Townspeople use real doors"** (`Townsfolk.cs`, a door helper shared with `DoomerfallFindNpc.cs`, and
the logging in `DoomerfallTownspeopleTest.cs`):
1. On a fresh build, measure in Gothway at 12:00 the share of listed people moving and their 10 s displacement. If
   walking regressed, stop and report it as its own card.
2. Site = building door (pushed ~1 cell out along its normal, snapped walkable), with the fallbacks above.
3. Walks between buildings start and end at the door; the anchor stays only as the standing spot.
4. Every outside chunk ends with a walk from the pacing spot back to the anchor, whatever follows (inside, outside,
   or a walk). Leaving the anchor for the building (going inside) or for another building (a walk) adds anchor to
   door. Arriving at the door (coming out, or a walk that ends before an outside chunk) adds door to anchor. Waking
   at home starts with a door walk, and going to sleep ends with one.
   A walk with no path: retry between the old site cells (today's ends); if that also fails, keep today's hidden walk
   and log it (`town: no path`, with person and both buildings) so the check counts it.
5. Check at 8:00, 12:00 and 18:00 with `-dftownreport`: at every **schedule** boundary (chunk, walk, wake, sleep), a
   visible person moves less than one cell between polls, and every hide or show is within one cell of the
   building's door point. Entering or leaving the list through range or the 128 cap is not counted. Find NPC still
   points at the right person or building.
   No-path walks are reported as a count per town and must be zero in Gothway, or each one explained.
6. Log on arriving in Daggerfall city: the first-poll time (path searches) and the steady `Collect` time per poll.
