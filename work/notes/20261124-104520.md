## Findings worth reading first
- **Bug:** 70 passive fixed-enemy markers (castle guards, `Action == 99`) spawn hostile Doom monsters, and the
  converter also exports each marker as a flag 99 action record (`ActionExport.cs:402-410`). Card 1.
- **Bug:** the single fixed marker with mobile type 99 (Wayrest) spawns a monster that DFU skips. Card 1.
- Eleven action types (spell, text, riddle, lock, unlock, hurt, poison, drain, dialogue, quest global, door text)
  only print `DFRULE` (`action_objects.zs:10-15`). Traps are in hundreds of dungeons. Cards 2 and 3.
- 19 treasure markers carry action chains; their blocks are in 867 dungeons. Doom's Use opens loot only. Card 4.
- 25,922 interior door records are never converted (`SiteExport.cs` has no `BlockDoorRecords`). Card 5.
- 4,268 dungeon light records are never read; sectors use a flat light level. Card 6.
- 432 dungeon doors carry a starting lock; nothing enforces it. Already on the backlog, which waits on the owner.
