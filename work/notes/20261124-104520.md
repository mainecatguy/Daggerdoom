## Findings worth a quick look
- **Stale popup closer.** `DoomerfallMonsters.ClosePopups` closes any Daggerfall message box for 4 seconds after a
  quest foe dies. It predates Doom showing Daggerfall's own windows, so it now hides kill messages and could close a
  prompt. Card 01 removes it.
- **Quest foes only in dungeons.** The pilot quest named in the earlier survey, `M0C00Y11`, puts its scorpion in a
  town house, where Doom shows no quest foes today. Card 02.
- **Most quest people are not layout NPCs.** Daggerfall places a quest's people at markers in their home building when
  the player walks in. 139 quests have such people. Doom shows none of them. Card 03.
- **Quest foes in Doom are random stock monsters**, picked from the id, not the Daggerfall foe type.
