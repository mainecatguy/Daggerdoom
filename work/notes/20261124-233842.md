# Ammo pickups and drops use Doom's amounts (run 20261124-233842)

## Source for Doom's amounts

doomwiki.org refused automated fetches (HTTP 403), so the amounts come from id Software's released Doom source,
`linuxdoom-1.10/p_inter.c` (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/id-Software/DOOM/master/linuxdoom-1.10/p_inter.c):

- `int clipammo[NUMAMMO] = {10, 4, 20, 1};` (clip, shell, cell, missile): one "clip load".
- `P_TouchSpecialThing`: SPR_CLIP gives 1 clip load (or 0 = half a clip load `if (special->flags & MF_DROPPED)`),
  SPR_AMMO 5 clip loads, SPR_SHEL 1, SPR_SBOX 5, SPR_ROCK 1, SPR_BROK 5, SPR_CELL 1, SPR_CELP 5.
- `P_GiveAmmo`: `if (num) num *= clipammo[ammo]; else num = clipammo[ammo]/2;` then doubled on sk_baby and sk_nightmare.

| Pickup | Small | Large | Dropped by a monster |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bullets | clip 10 | box 50 | 5 (vanilla: only the clip is ever dropped) |
| Shells | 4 | box 20 | 2 |
| Rockets | 1 | box 5 | 1 |
| Cells | 20 | pack 100 | 10 |

Vanilla Doom only halves the dropped clip, because no vanilla monster drops other ammo. DRLA monsters can drop
other ammo, so I used UZDoom's rule for every dropped pickup: half, at least one (`Ammo.ModifyDropAmount`,
`UZDoom/wadsrc/static/zscript/actors/inventory/ammo.zs:191-226`). It gives 5 for the dropped clip, matching vanilla.
The skill doubling (ITYTD, Nightmare) is not applied: tiered pickups go to Daggerfall's inventory, not through Doom's
ammo pickup code. Previously they did not double either.

## What changed

- `mods/doomerfall/src/zscript/DoomerfallAmmo.zs`
  - `DoomerfallAmmoPickup.Rounds(type, tier, box, dropped)` is now the single source of pickup amounts: Doom's small
    or large amount, halved when dropped, times the tier's share of Regular. Daggerfall gets the count with `dfa_take`
    and never computes a pickup amount itself.
  - Conversion keeps the size of the pickup it replaces: `PlainKind` tells a box from a small pickup (ClipBox inherits
    Clip, so box names are checked first), and `WasDropped` tells a monster drop from a placed pickup. `SpawnTier` sets
    the amount on the new pickup. The treadmill already stores and restores each item's Amount, so the size survives
    leaving and coming back, and saves.
  - Before, every pickup became the same size, whatever it was: 80 Regular bullets, 20 shells, 8 rockets, 100 cells.
- `daggerfall-unity-master/Assets/Scripts/Doomerfall/DoomerfallAmmoTiers.cs`: `BaseStack` renamed `ShopPackBase`,
  with a comment. It now sets only shop pack sizes. I left shop quantities alone because they are shop stock and
  prices, not pickups (see questions).

### Telling a monster drop from a placed pickup

DROPPED cannot tell: every Inventory starts DROPPED, and pickups spawned by the converter's markers keep it. Doom's
drop code marks a drop TOSSED and throws it. With DRLA, the dropped Clip is replaced by `RLClipSpawner`, a
RandomSpawner, which passes neither flag to the RLClip it spawns. It does copy the spawner's toss velocity and its spawn
flags, which are empty for anything the map did not place. So `WasDropped` = TOSSED, or (no spawn flags and moving at
spawn). Known gap: with the compatibility option "no toss drops" a DRLA drop is not moving and would count as placed
(full size). A script-spawned pickup at rest counts as placed.

### Tier amounts (proposed rounding, awaiting the owner)

Tier share = the owner's stack multiplier / Regular's 2.0: Steel 3/4, Silver 1/2, Moonstone 3/8, Dwemer 1/2,
Adamantium 3/8, Orichalcum, Mithril and Ebony 1/4, Daedric 1/8. Rounded half away from zero, at least 1 (my proposal):

| | Reg | Steel | Silver | Moon | Dwemer | Adam | Orich/Mith/Ebony | Daedric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clip 10 | 10 | 8 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 1 |
| Dropped clip 5 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| Box of bullets 50 | 50 | 38 | 25 | 19 | 25 | 19 | 13 | 6 |
| Shells 4 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| Box of shells 20 | 20 | 15 | 10 | 8 | 10 | 8 | 5 | 3 |
| Rocket 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Box of rockets 5 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| Cell 20 | 20 | 15 | 10 | 8 | 10 | 8 | 5 | 3 |
| Cell pack 100 | 100 | 75 | 50 | 38 | 50 | 38 | 25 | 13 |

The minimum of 1 means a single rocket never shrinks, so rare tiers are not rarer per rocket than Regular. That is the
question for the owner.

### Failed approaches

- First version: `WasDropped` used `bDROPPED`. Every pickup came out half size. Every Inventory starts DROPPED
  (`inventory.zs:197`). Only real map things clear it, and the converter's pickups come from markers, so they keep it.
  Switched to `bTOSSED`, which only Doom's drop code sets (`inventory_util.zs:681`), plus the RandomSpawner test.
- The harness at first killed the Zombieman it spawned, but with DRLA that object is a replacement spawner that has
  already removed itself. It now kills the monsters found at the spawn spots.
