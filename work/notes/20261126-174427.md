# 2026-11-26 17:44: Weapons that belong to two skills

Goal: a weapon may have up to two weapon categories where it truly fits two, and anything that reads a weapon's
skill uses the higher of the two (owner, `manual.md` "Weapons that belong to two skills").

## Plan, as it went

1. Find what reads a weapon's skill today. Result: nothing in combat does yet (`CHANGELOG.md`: "Nothing in combat
   reads the category yet"). The only readers are `DoomerfallWeaponCategory.SkillSlot(Of(w))` in the category test.
   ZScript had no way to read Daggerfall's skill values; only C++ (`dd_climb.cpp`) read the sheet.
2. Settle which pairs count. The owner's detail decision (`gamedesign/decisions/20261126-two-skill-weapons-owner.md`,
   present in the main checkout; the index line is in `gamedesign/decisions.md`) says: "a Shotgun or Pistol weapon and
   an Energy Weapon". So the only pairs are **Pistol + Energy** and **Shotgun + Energy**. Other token overlaps
   (Storm pistols' shotgun token, the Autoshotgun's rapid-fire token, Uzis' rapid-fire token, missile pistols'
   rockets) stay one category, unchanged.
3. Engine: one read-only native, `Actor.DD_SheetSkill(slot)` (live skill from the character sheet, -1 without a
   valid Daggerfall character). Files: `UZDoom/wadsrc/static/zscript/actors/actor.zs` (1 line),
   `UZDoom/src/scripting/vmthunks_actors.cpp` (thunk), `UZDoom/src/dd_bridge.cpp` (`DD_BridgeSkillLive`). It only
   reads; without a bridge session it returns -1, so stock Doom is unaffected.
4. Mod (`mods/doomerfall/src/zscript/DoomerfallWeaponCategory.zs`):
   - `Both(w)` returns the first and second categories. `Of(w)` returns the first, exactly as before.
   - `SkillSlotOf(w)`: the skill slot a weapon uses. With two categories, whichever live skill is higher; on a tie,
     or with no Daggerfall (Doom only), the first category. Anything that later reads a weapon's skill (perks, damage,
     Skill XP from weapon use) should call this.
   - Energy sign gained the self-charging energy pistols (they were deliberately left out before, when energy pistols
     were Pistols only).
5. Tests: the category test now prints the second category and the skill used; a new paired test checks the higher
   skill wins with Daggerfall running.

## Weapons with two categories

Both lists come from DRLA's type tokens and ammo in `GameMods/ExtractedData` (DRLAX adds no pistol- or shotgun-token
guns). Without DRLA, stock Doom has no gun that fits two, but a mod's cell-fed subclass of Pistol, Shotgun or
SuperShotgun gets both, since the stock signs are class and ammo based.

**Pistol, then Energy Weapons** (pistol token, and Cell ammo or on the recharging energy list):
- Cell-fed: Plasma Pistol, Plasma Combat Pistol, Plasma Handcannon, Plasma Marksman Pistol, Plasma Uzi, Baron Blaster,
  Neural Stunner.
- Self-charging: Blaster, High Power Blaster, Overcharged Blaster, Storm Blaster, Nano-Manufacture Blaster, Heavy
  Pulse Blaster, Nuclear Plasma Pistol, High Power Nuclear Plasma Pistol, Storm Nuclear Plasma Pistol, Supercharged
  Nuclear Plasma Pistol, Nuclear Plasma Revolver.

**Energy Weapons, then Shotgun** (Cell ammo or recharging list, and the shotgun token):
- Laser Shotgun, Plasma Shotgun, Plasma Shotgun Mk II, Heavy Plasma Shotgun, Plasmatic Shrapnel Plasma Shotgun,
  Hell's Reign, Nuclear Plasma Shotgun, Nano-Shrapnel Plasma Shotgun.

Not paired, on purpose: Storm Pistol / Storm Combat / Marksman / Handcannon (pistol + shotgun token: Pistol only),
Grammaton Cleric Beretta, Mysterious Magnum, Marathon Shotguns (pistol tokens: Pistol only), Autoshotgun and Auto
Double Shotgun (Shotgun only), Mini-Missile Pistol and Unknown Herald (rockets: Pistol only), MIRV Launcher and
Shrapnel Cannon (rockets: Explosive only), Steel Beast (shotgun + rifle token: Shotgun only), Energysaw (Melee only).

## Failed approaches and gotchas

- ZScript names are case-insensitive: locals named `energy` and `shotgun` hid the enum values `Energy` and `Shotgun`,
  so every gun read as category 1 (melee). Renamed to `isEnergy` / `isShotgun` with a comment.
- `+logfile` with an absolute path writes nothing; it takes a plain name and writes `log-<name>.txt` in UZDoom's
  working folder (as `tools/fixed_drops/paired.py` already does).
- The harness pk3 needs a `version` line, or it cannot derive from StaticEventHandler.
- DRLA prints its own non-fatal script errors (menudef, translation), so the paired test's error check is scoped to
  our pk3s, like the category test's.

## Known gaps

- DRLA gives its type tokens only while the gun is held, so a DRLA energy pistol that is not the held gun reads as
  Energy only (no pistol token) until it is selected. Same limitation as before for every DRLA category.
- The paired test proves both outcomes for the Shotgun + Energy pair; the Pistol + Energy pair only showed the first
  skill winning, because the test character's Pistols was always higher. It runs through the same `SkillSlotOf`.
- Tie rule (first category wins) is my engineering call. The skill value is the same either way; it only matters once
  perks or other per-skill effects read the slot.
