## Lightweaver

- **Ammo:** none. It recharges itself.
- **Primary fire:** one instant beam, exactly 500 plasma damage, one target (no piercing). Then a recharge of about
  1.7 s, so about one shot every 2 s (roughly 250 damage per second).
- **Alt fire:** short wind-up, then a white beam for exactly 2000 damage, one target. Its recharge is about 5 s and
  blocks both fire modes. It needs a full charge; with none, the alt button only charges the gun.
- **Special:** the view darkens while it recharges. The recharge starts when you press fire or reload (holding fire
  works), and switching guns restarts it.
- **Mods:** Sniper turns the alt fire into a slow, accelerating projectile (3000 damage). Firestorm throws off
  damaging sparks during recharge. Nano gives cells after each recharge (20, or 80 after an alt shot).
- **Skill:** Energy Weapons. A self-charging energy gun, as you define it. It is not a pistol or shotgun.

## Nano-shrapnel Plasma Shotgun (DRLA name: "Nano Plasma Shotgun")

- **Ammo:** none at all. DRLA's description says a generator replenishes cells, but the code uses no ammo.
- **Fire:** 7 plasma balls per shot in a spread, 3 to 15 damage each (average 63 per shot). Holding fire repeats about
  every 0.57 s, about 110 damage per second if every ball hits. No alt fire, no reload.
- **Mods:** Power raises each ball to 4 to 20. Bulk adds a ball. Agility and Sniper tighten the spread. Firestorm fires
  faster.
- **Class:** the Renegade gets extra balls (11 instead of 7). DRLA marks it as a shotgun.
- **Skill:** Energy Weapons + Shotguns, using the higher. This is your two-skill rule, and the game already does it.

## Duke 2 Rifle (DRLA name: "Rigelatin Assault Weapon")

A legendary gun based on Duke Nukem II.

- **Ammo:** none for the normal shot. The three special modes use their own temporary charges, not Doom ammo.
- **Normal:** one shot per click (no hold-to-fire), a travelling bolt for 20 damage. Fast clicking gives about one shot
  per 4 tics (up to about 175 damage per second).
- **Special modes:**
  - Laser: 20 per hit, passes through enemies and can hit each one several times.
  - Rocket: 160 on the target hit. The explosion is only visual (no splash).
  - Flamethrower: a fast 40-damage burst that pushes you backwards.
- **Getting modes:** Bulk, Power and Technical mod packs give limited charges (32 laser, 32 rocket, 64 flame). Sniper,
  Firestorm and Nano mods unlock laser, rocket or flame permanently, chosen with alt fire. An Agility mod pack gives
  hold-to-fire for 60 s. Pickups in DRLA's Duke 2 special start do the same.
- **Damage type:** its own ("RigelBlast"), in every mode.
- **Skill:** Energy Weapons. It uses no ammo and DRLA describes it as an energy weapon. Rifles is already ruled out
  (q05), and its rocket mode has no explosion, so Explosives does not fit.

## Mother-in-Law

A master-assembly rocket launcher.

- **Ammo:** none. It makes its own rocket.
- **Fire:** one rocket, then a cooldown of about 14 s (500 tics, started by the shot). You can switch guns meanwhile.
  No alt fire.
- **Damage:** fire type. 300 on a direct hit, plus a blast that **grows the longer the rocket flies** (maximum blast
  damage / radius in map units, falling off with distance):

  | Flight time | Blast damage | Radius |
  |---|---|---|
  | Point blank | about 1,050 | about 390 |
  | 1 second | about 1,700 | about 650 |
  | 3 seconds | about 3,100 | about 1,170 |

- **Special:** the blast can hurt you, and firing kicks you backwards (grappling boots prevent this on the ground).
- **Skill:** Explosives. It is plainly a rocket launcher. It does recharge itself, but I read "self-charging" in your
  energy rule as meaning energy guns, and this fires an exploding rocket.

## Shredder Pistol: Pistols and Shotguns

**Both class abilities affect it, so by your rule it is both.**

How it works: one shell loads a 10-round clip, and one pull fires all of it, one pellet every 2 tics (about 0.6 s), then
a reload of about 1 s. Each pellet does 6, 12 or 18 (average 12). Mods can raise damage, tighten spread, enlarge the
clip (13 or 16), speed up fire, or regenerate the clip.

| DRLA ability | Applies? | Evidence in `actors/weapons/basic/misc/ShredderPistol.txt` |
|---|---|---|
| Scout pistol multiplier (Sharpshooter) | **Yes** | Every fire state checks `RLSharpshooterPerk`: each pellet does a fixed 30 instead of 6 to 18 (2.5 times the average; DRLA's comment says "5x", counting from 6), and spread is halved except where already zero. |
| Renegade free shell shot (Shellshock) | **Yes** | At the end of the clip it checks `RLRenegadeExtraShot` (earned by picking up shells) and refills 10 rounds free for a second burst. |
| Renegade +50% pellets | No | No Renegade fire states. Shotguns have separate ones (the stock Shotgun goes from 7 pellets to 11). |
| Renegade instant weapon switch | Not counted | Present, but every gun checked here has it, so it says nothing about the type. |

The free shot is a shotgun ability: `language.classes` says it is "a free shot for any shotgun". 32 weapon files check
it: this one, the Elephant Gun (shell-fed, but marked by DRLA as a single-shot rifle) and 30 with DRLA's shotgun
marker. DRLA's own type marker on the Shredder Pistol is pistol only, so its in-game perk list would call it a pistol; your rule looks at the abilities, which say both.
