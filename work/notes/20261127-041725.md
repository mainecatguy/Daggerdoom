## The new melee rule (engine facts it rests on)

- Doom flags every `P_LineAttack` hit and every `P_RailAttack` hit `DMG_INFLICTOR_IS_PUFF` (p_map.cpp 4944, 5571),
  with a puff spawned that tic as the inflictor (a rail's plain-Spawned puff or `P_SpawnPuff`'s). Fists, saws and the
  Energysaw all land this way, while the attack runs, so the weapon held now is the attacker. That replaces the two
  special cases (ISPUFF puff, and the `DMG_RAILGUN` rule from the end of the last run) with one engine marker.
- The offensive damage modifier runs only on the damage **source**'s inventory (p_interaction.cpp 1240). A hitscan
  fired by a projectile or a spawned actor has that actor as its source, so the player's item never sees it. The one
  exception is `A_Explode` with nails from a missile (`LAF_TARGETISSOURCE`, attacks.zs 615): the source is the player,
  but the puff's `DamageSource` (p_mobj.cpp 7025, readable in ZScript) is the missile. The rule then judges the hit by
  that missile (fired while melee was held or not), instead of the weapon held now. This settles last run's open
  question ("a hitscan puff from a gun projectile's death while fists are held would count as melee"): it cannot.
- A puff's own effect on its first tic (an exploding melee puff) still counts while melee is held, as before, but
  only a puff of the owner's own hitscan (DamageSource is the owner or unset).
- Projectiles and their splash: unchanged (the tic it was fired decides).
- Not melee, unchanged: no inflictor, the player as inflictor (BFG tracers, an `A_Explode` in a weapon state), barrels,
  spawned explosions such as DRLA's Torgue boots kick explosion (a boots set bonus, not the punch).

## Test changes (`tools/attributes/`)

- The paired run loads `doomerfall-drla.pk3` (DoomerfallPlayer instead of a random DRLA class), as the real game and
  `tools/agility` do.
- Real weapon test (`WeaponTest` in the harness): for RLFist, RLChainsaw, RLPiercingChainsaw, RLDoubleChainsaw,
  RLRipper, RLEnergysaw, and the controls RLPistol and RLRailgun: really switches to the weapon, gives ammo, puts a
  bleeding target (`AttrBleeder`, no NOBLOOD) in front and runs the weapon's own Fire state through
  `PlayerPawn.FireWeapon`, as the fire button does. An offensive recorder given after the effects item (so first in the
  inventory chain) sees each hit's damage before Strength; a passive recorder on the target sees it after. Every
  melee hit must equal `max(1, int(raw * (1 + Strength/100)))` with the live Strength, every gun hit must equal raw,
  and `MeleeAttack` must classify each hit that way.
- `-dfattrtest 10`: each value is held 10 s so the weapon test fits between walk measurements; quiet_run timeout 150 s.
