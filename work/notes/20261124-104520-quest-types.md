## 4. Moving NPCs (old backlog item)

**Finding: no quest or other Daggerfall script needs Doom to walk an NPC somewhere.** Daggerfall's quest system only moves people in jumps, and Doom can follow every jump with the presence lists it already uses. [code, data]

What I checked:
- **Quest actions.** Every action in `Questing/Actions/` was listed. None makes an NPC walk. NPC actions are `place npc`, `create npc`, `create npc at`, `hide npc`, `restore npc`, `destroy npc`, `mute npc`, `add/drop face`, and `when npc is available`. [code]
- **Escorts are not followers.** In Daggerfall an escorted or rescued person is hidden and shown only as a face on the HUD. For example, `A0C00Y16` runs `add _missingperson_ face` and then `hide npc _missingperson_`, and `C0B00Y03` does the same with `_cleric_`. At the end the face is dropped and the person is placed at a new site. 26 quests use faces this way. [data]
- **Hot placement.** A quest person can appear while the player is already in the building: `place npc` into the current site, or a home person placed on entry (`Person.cs:389-403`). That is a jump to a marker, not a walk.
- **Wandering townspeople.** Daggerfall's mobile NPCs wander at random and no quest refers to one. Doom's persistent townspeople already replace them.
- **Guards and quest foes** move by AI, which is Doom's job by the ownership table.
- **One possible exception, not checked in the data:** a quest item placed on a marker that belongs to a moving dungeon action object is parented to it so it moves with it (`GameObjectHelper.cs:1144-1148`, "mobile quest objects parented to action marker"). That is an item riding a platform, not an NPC. If any classic quest places an item on such a marker, Doom's quest item (card 05) must ride the same mover. Card 05 includes a check for it.

**What Daggerfall must tell Doom:** for each quest person, in each space Doom has loaded: who is present (id, Daggerfall flat for the sprite, marker position, facing), and every change to that list as it happens: placed, hidden, restored, destroyed, or moved to another site. Doom keeps the actor and does nothing else on its own. This is card 03's list. Faces need no actor. Daggerfall draws them on its own HUD, which the player does not see in Doom, so card 06 shows them in Doom.

## 5. Questions for the owner

These are in this run's `questions` field too.
1. **Escorts.** Daggerfall hides a rescued person and shows only a face while they "follow". Card 06 keeps that. Should rescued people instead follow the player as bodies in Doom (they could then be shot by packs and die)?
2. **Quest foes outside dungeons, and ambushes.** "Quest targets in mook packs" leaves timed ambushes and anything beyond the leader rule open. For 38 quests that put a foe in a town building (often a small house) and 100 that use `create foe` (near the player anywhere, including inside houses and on the road), what should spawn? Interim proposed in cards 02 and 07: exactly Daggerfall's count of single Doom monsters, each one the counted leader, with no escort. Is that acceptable for now, and should house foes and ambushes get packs later?

## 6. Method

- Scratch classifier (not committed, in the session scratchpad): reads each enabled quest's QBN section and matches each action line against simple patterns per action, and each `Place` line to dungeon or building. A quest's main type comes from its placed kill targets, placed items, placed people with faces, and so on, in the priority order of the table above. Ambiguous `permanent` places count as buildings, which affects 4 main quests.
- The counts differ a little from the earlier survey (`work/notes/20261123-154554.md`), which counted the 187 classic quests with each action's own regex. For example it found 77 `place npc` quests, and this finds 71 across all 226. I did not reconcile the two. The grouping does not depend on exact counts.
- Support status comes from reading the Doomerfall source listed above on the `integration` base of this branch (commit 3805d1e).
