# Rest says "enemies nearby" after the nearby Doom monsters are dead (2026-11-28)

Goal: rest is refused only while living hostile Doom monsters or hostile guard bots are near, measured in Doom, with
Daggerfall's distance and Daggerfall's rule for which monsters count. Same after a save and load.

## What Daggerfall counted (findings)

A rest from Doom goes `netevent df_rest` -> `RestState()` in `UZDoom/src/dd_bridge.cpp` (Doom counts its monsters and
sends `DFB_REST_MONSTERS_NEAR`) -> Daggerfall's own `GameManager.AreEnemiesNearby(resting: true)`. Three faults:

1. **Doom's 12 m was 30 m.** `RestState` used `12 * 75` = 900 Doom units, from a wrong "75 units per metre" comment.
   Doom maps are Daggerfall's layout at 75 %, and Daggerfall has 40 units per metre, so 1 m = 30 Doom units
   (`DoomerfallPositionSync.cs` header, `DoomPerDaggerfall = 0.75`, work/notes/20261126-200628.md). With slaughter-size
   packs, killing the monsters next to you still left monsters 12-30 m away (often behind walls) counting as "near".
   This is the most likely cause of the owner's report.
2. **Daggerfall's hidden world still voted.** When Doom said "none near", `AreEnemiesNearby` went on to check
   Daggerfall's hidden enemy entities and its foe spawners (within 1024 classic units). Those are Daggerfall's
   placements of the monsters Doom actually runs; a Doom kill updates the death ledger and quest foe
   (`DoomerfallMonsters.OnDeath`) but never removes the hidden entity or spawner. Hidden enemies are normally held
   by `DisableAI` (their senses never update, so they usually do not count), but any that ran senses for a frame before
   `DisableAI` (a load or transition) freeze with "would be spawned"/"target in sight" set, and quest foe spawners
   always count. Either way they are not live state; Doom owns the live area.
3. **Guard bots never counted.** Guard bots (`dd_guards.cpp`) are hostile player pawns (bots), not `MF3_ISMONSTER`,
   so `RestState` skipped them. Their Daggerfall copies are made inert (senses off), so Daggerfall did not count them
   either. The owner rule says hostile guard bots block rest.

## Changes

- `UZDoom/src/dd_bridge.cpp` `RestState`: 12 m = 360 Doom units; living guard bots count as hostile, with "sees the
  player" meaning the bot's enemy is the player and it has sight (a monster's `target` for monsters). Runs only inside a
  bridge session (the rest net event), so stock Doom and DRLA are untouched.
- `GameManager.AreEnemiesNearby` (one-line hook): for a rest asked for from Doom, Doom's answer is the whole answer
  (`DoomerfallRest.DoomMonstersNear`). Rest outside a bridge session is unchanged. Fast travel's "enemies nearby"
  check (not resting) is unchanged.

Rule kept (owner, 2026-11-27): Daggerfall's resting rule applied to Doom's living monsters: a monster that targets the
player and can see them counts at any distance (Daggerfall's `enemyCanSeePlayer`); any other living hostile monster
counts within 12 m, by distance only (no sight check), awake or asleep. Daggerfall's extra "would be spawned in
classic" condition for the unaware ones is a spawn-distance test (9.6-25.6 m by type) that is at least 12 m for almost
every type, so it is not modelled separately.

## Test

`tools/rest_enemies/rest_enemies.py` (paired run on quiet_run's private desktop, harness in `harness.zs`):
- `run fresh`: town at noon with DRLA; ZombieMan at 160 u and one at 600 u (20 m): refused. Near one killed: rest window
  opens (the far one would have counted under the old 900 u). Tavern with `-dfguardtest` and `-dfresttestroom`: refused
  next to a living guard bot; guards killed: rest window opens. Autosave.
- `run load`: loads that autosave: rest opens; ZombieMan at 120 u: refused; killed: opens.
- `check`: DOOM.WAD E1M1 stock and DRLA, `-norun`.

## Play-test list

- In a dungeon, kill a pack around you and press the rest key: rest should open unless a living monster is within
  about 12 m (roughly 4-5 Doom player widths... about 360 units) or one that has noticed you can see you.
- Steal in a shop to call the guards; rest is refused while a guard is alive nearby, allowed once they are dead.
- After a save and load in a cleared dungeon area, rest still opens.

## Known gaps

- The save/load case in the test is in the tavern (guards dead, then a summoned monster), not in a cleared dungeon.
  Dungeon monsters restored dead from the ledger are absent from Doom's level, so they cannot count; not run here.
- Fast travel's "enemies nearby" (`AreEnemiesNearby()` without resting) still reads Daggerfall's hidden entities; out of
  this goal.
