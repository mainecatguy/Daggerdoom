# Encounters 2: the planner's genome and army (2026-11-24)

Card 2 of the encounter queue. Design: `work/notes/20261123-205402-fractal-encounter-variety.md`, sections 4, 5, 9 and appendix A. Builds on card 1 (`work/notes/20261124-094754.md`).

## Plan (as carried out)

1. Add a private random source: an integer hash of the place's facts, a purpose and an index.
2. Compute the genome from `dungeonIndex` with appendix A's polynomial.
3. Turn raw gene settings into meanings, with the dungeon type's leaning (4.1) and a regional favourite kind.
4. Build the army: anchor, core, support, wildcard, the colour lock, the DRLA family spread, caps, and Civil War with a second army.
5. Add a test flag that plans a fixed list of fake area facts, plus a Python runner that checks the output in stock Doom, DRLA, and CH plus DRLA.

## What was built

- `mods/doomerfall/src/zscript/EncounterPlanner.zs`
  - `DoomerfallEncounterRequest` holds the area facts from section 9.2, minus placements and dead mooks, which later cards use.
  - `DoomerfallEncounterRng` is the private random source. It uses lowbias32 integer mixing with wraparound and logical shifts. Its key comes only from the place: `dungeonIndex`, `locationId`, `regionIndex` and `seedOffset`. Each decision has its own purpose stream (anchor, core, support, wildcard, spread, theme, region, Civil War, and side B variants). It never calls Doom's `random()`.
  - `DoomerfallEncounterPlanner.Plan(request)` returns a `DoomerfallEncounterPlan`: raw genes, a themed flag per gene, tier lean, size (in thousandths), placement, awareness, Civil War, region cell and favourite kind, army A, army B, and warnings. It spawns nothing and touches no map.
  - `DoomerfallEncounterPlanner.Genome(index, seedOffset, out genes)` is static. `ShuffledOrder` is appendix A's fixed shuffle, `(i*2897 + 1013) mod 4232`. 2897 is coprime with 4232, so it is a permutation.
- `mods/doomerfall/src/zscript/EncounterPlannerTest.zs` is the test hook: `+set dd_encounter_plantest 1`, declared in CVARINFO and not saved. It prints all 4,232 genomes and 352 fake plans as `ENCPLAN` lines.
- `tools/encounter_planner/run_test.py` builds the PK3 and runs 5 setups. Each run is minimized, uses a private `-config` and a 40 s limit, and stops at `ENCPLAN done` or on any doomerfall script or fatal error.
- `doomerfall_encounters.txt` has a new `list` rule and a planner section:
  - the kind ring
  - meanings for tier lean, size, placement and awareness
  - Civil War stances
  - the DRLA families
  - the titan groups
  - spread weights
  - `region_cell` and `guarantee_share`
- Pool fix in `EncounterPool.zs`: a stock class stays in the pool only when no other class of its kind is loaded (`DropShadowedStock`). Without this, DRLA at skill 1 kept `Zombieman`, `ShotgunGuy` and `ChaingunGuy`, because DRLA's baby skill names `RL*SpawnerEasy` classes that do not exist. Skill 1 and skill 3 then planned different armies. The new skill-comparison check found this.

### How each part works

- **Genes.** The order is Anchor, Coherence, Tier lean, Size, Factions, Placement, Awareness, Swing, Novelty, Exceptions. `seedOffset` is added to every gene mod 13, and 0 means fixed dungeons. Swing, Novelty and Exceptions are computed and printed but nothing uses them yet. They belong to the budget and squad cards.
- **Leanings (4.1).** For each theme and gene, a fixed shuffle of the 13 settings marks 9 as fitting (`theme_fitting_settings`). The shuffle is seeded by the theme name, so it does not depend on the dungeon.
  - A fitting setting takes the theme's meaning:
    - Anchor: a theme monster, with the preferred theme kind turning with the setting.
    - Tier lean: the group's tier, such as Technophobia 6.
    - Size: one of the three most extreme settings at the titan or swarm end.
    - Placement and awareness: the theme's word.
  - The other 4 settings keep the plain meaning.
  - When the anchor follows the theme, core and support picks also favour theme traits and classes.
  - **Guarantee:** if nothing in the army matches the theme by kind, class or trait, one theme monster is added with role `theme` and 10% of the points.
- **Region.** Dungeons in the same `region_cell` (48 map pixels) of `worldX` and `worldY` share a favourite kind. It is drawn from the cell coordinates alone, among the kinds that have a Civil War safe monster. It is guaranteed the same way, with role `region`. The rest of each army differs because the genes differ, by at least 8 genes among the 10 neighbours tested.
- **Army (5.2).**
  - **Anchor:** each gene setting covers a slice of the 17-kind ring, so every kind is reachable. If no monster of those kinds is loaded, the nearest kind on the ring is used. Each kind in the slice is equally likely. Within a kind, the class leans toward the tier lean, with weights 16, 8, 3 and 1 for a distance of 0, 1, 2 and 3 or more tiers.
  - **Core:** at coherence 0, none, which gives Oops All One. Otherwise 1 to 3 picks, weighted by resemblance to the anchor (same kind, shared traits, close tier) raised to a power of 4 when tight and 1 when loose.
  - **Support:** 1 to 3 picks, favouring traits the army lacks and kinds it does not have.
  - **Wildcard:** chance coherence/16, from anywhere, and not colour locked.
  - **Shares:** core 75%, support 20%, wildcard 5%, in thousandths. The anchor counts double in the core. Missing roles hand their share to the core.
  - **Caps:** Hell Mines never anchor or join the core, and are capped at `capped_share`.
  - **Tiers:** higher tiers are not rarer. The tier lean settings cover tiers 1 to 7 evenly.
- **Colour lock (5.4).** The first Colourful Hell colour of a kind locks that kind for the army, except for wildcards. Titan groups (`EX`, `BlackWhite`) are left to the titan rule and never picked for an army. DRLA families are not locked. Each kind with a DRLA entry rolls how many families it uses (1 to 4) from a U-shaped row chosen by coherence, then adds variants of the other families, which split the parent's share.
- **Civil War (5.5).** Factions settings 8 to 12 (5 of 13) are Civil War, each with a pair of stances for side A and side B. Side B is a second army with its own coherence, tier lean and anchor, drawn from the Civil War stream. Its anchor must differ by kind or by 2 or more tiers. Both armies use only `civilWarSafe` monsters.

## What felt off (for the owner)

- **The regional favourite can be any kind, including Cyberdemon or Spider Mastermind.** Every dungeon in that 48-pixel cell then carries it at 10% of the army's points, even at area level 0. The budget card will have to deal with monsters a level 0 dungeon cannot afford. This is a question below.
- **Guarantees soften Oops All One.** A coherence 0 army still gets the regional kind, and the theme monster if the anchor missed the theme, so it is 90% one class rather than 100%.
- **Theme by trait is loose.** Volcanic Caves (`traits=projectile`) and Harpy Nest (`traits=flies`) count almost any ranged or flying monster as on theme, so the guarantee rarely has to add anything for them. That is a data tweak if it matters.
- **Spread can make big armies.** With DRLA, one army reached 21 entries (several kinds × 4 families), and some variant shares drop to 25 thousandths. These are shares of the points, not counts, so the budget card decides whether those variants actually appear.
- **Stock Laboratory has no theme monster.** Its theme is only classes (`RLCybernetic*` and so on), so with stock Doom alone its theme is "unmet" and a warning is printed. That is expected.
- **DRLA bosses can anchor.** At tier lean 7, a tier 8 DRLA boss can be chosen, at weight 8. Their placeholder prices are an open question from card 1.

## Engineering calls made

- The titan groups (EX and all Black and White) are kept out of normal army picks and left to the titan rule (6.3). The design calls Black and White monsters titans. The non-EX ones are already an open pricing question.
- The anchor ring order, the resemblance weights, core and support counts by coherence, the wildcard chance, the spread rows, the region cell size and the guarantee share are engineering picks. They are all in the data file, apart from the resemblance weights and wildcard chance, which are in code.
- `worldX` and `worldY` are taken as world map pixels. The Daggerfall side must send them in those units.
- A theme that names no monsters never steers the anchor.

## Failed approaches

- The first compile failed for two reasons: `name` is a ZScript type keyword, and a field named `plan` clashed with the method `Plan`, because ZScript names ignore case. Both were renamed.
- The fake facts first reused one dungeon index for two plans, and the test caught it.
