# Rework Doom autopickup

Branch `agent/autopickup-20261123-212059`. Demo: `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\review\autopickup\play.bat`. It starts at the Privateer's Hold door with DRLA, DRLA Monsters, and DRLAX. Press Use to enter. Six DRLA items wait just inside: a stimpack, a box of bullets, and a box of shells to walk over, and a pistol, a vest, and a blur sphere to take with Use.

## Result

On a generated Daggerfall map in a bridge session, only health and ammo pick up by touch. Everything else waits until the player presses Use while looking at it. Use also takes Daggerfall loot piles (Grab, or Steal while crouching) and still opens doors. Outside that case nothing changes.

## Design

- **The rule is in the engine** (`UZDoom/src/dd_items.cpp`, called at the top of `P_TouchSpecialThing`). A ZScript rule could not work. DRLA weapons turn their own `SPECIAL` flag back on when they land, so clearing it does not hold. DRLA also gives health and ammo through `CustomInventory`, and uses `Ammo` classes as hidden tokens for armor, skulls, and phase devices, so a parent-class test misclassifies items.
- **Active only when** a bridge session exists and Doom is on a generated Daggerfall map, meaning the published world context is not `DFB_WORLD_NONE`. Stock UZDoom and regular WADs never reach it.
- **Touch classes** (each name covers its subclasses): `Health`, `HealthPickup`, `Ammo`, `Megasphere`, plus DRLA's `RLHealthBonus`, `RLStimpack`, `RLMedikit`, `RLAaaghSphere`, `RLMarathonStimpack`, `RLMarathonMedikit`, `RLDuke2AtomicHealth`, `RLClip`, `RLClipBox`, `RLShell`, `RLShellBox`, `RLRocketAmmo`, `RLRocketBox`, `RLCell`, `RLCellPack`. DRLA's `RLSoulsphere` and `RLMegasphere` derive from `Megasphere`, and its Marathon clips derive from `Clip`.
- **Use classes:** every other visible `Inventory` item, including weapons (DRLA weapon pickups too), armor, armor bonuses, keys, backpacks, powerups, berserk, and DRLA phase devices and modpacks.
- **Still touch:** invisible inventory helpers (sprite `TNT1` or `RF_INVISIBLE`), because nobody can aim at them, and non-inventory specials.
- **Use press** (`DD_UsePress`) is checked from both the town door code and `P_CheckUse`, cached per tic. It picks the best item within Daggerfall's 96-unit reach. Ranking is crosshair first (about 25 degrees), then an item on the floor ahead (Doom players rarely look down), then one underfoot. Line of sight is required.
  - A taken item spends the press, so a door behind it stays shut.
  - A refused item, such as a weapon already held or armor that is not better, stays and leaves the press for the door. A pile Daggerfall just refused (too heavy) does the same on the next press.
- **Interact** (`df_interact`, E in the existing launchers) now runs the same code without the door use. The ZScript `Interact` was removed.
- **Grab and Steal:** crouching sends `DFB_TAKE_STEAL` in `dfb_item_take.mode`, a former padding field, so no new message kind was needed. Daggerfall Unity's `ActivateLootContainer` treats Grab and Steal the same on treasure piles, so Daggerfall only logs the mode for now. Shop shelves and house containers, where Steal matters, are not in Doom yet.

## Failed approaches and test notes

- A real Use press could not be scripted. `+alias` with `+use` never reaches the command stream of a minimized, unfocused UZDoom. The harness saw no `BT_USE` at all. That door test was dropped, and the real-button door check is on the play-test list. The town door code changed only to skip a press an item already took.
- Harness pitfalls, all in the test and not the game:
  - A level event handler restarts on each map, so the harness has to notice it already starts inside.
  - Setting health to 50 every case drove Daggerfall's synchronized HP to death.
  - The Megasphere case leaves 200 armor, so Doom correctly refused GreenArmor afterwards. That refusal also showed the item staying on the floor as intended.
  - Under DRLA, stock `GreenArmor` spawned without replacement is refused by DRLA's armor system, so the DRLA run tests `RLGreenArmorPickup` instead.

## Known gaps

- DRLA's own extra pickup behavior is unchanged. For example, holding reload while picking up a weapon still converts it to ammo, but now only with Use.
- An item and a door in the same view: the item wins the first press.
- Use takes one item per press. Several items stacked in one spot need several presses.
- Doom items picked up with Use go to Doom's inventory as before. Converting them to Daggerfall items and giving them weight is out of scope.
- The touch list names DRLA classes. A new mod with its own CustomInventory health or ammo would need its names added. Its other items already default to Use.

## Play-test list

1. Run the demo. Walk over the stimpack and boxes, which should be taken. Walk over the pistol, vest, and sphere, which should stay. Look at each and press Use.
2. Press Use at the dungeon door and the exit with nothing in view. Both must work with the real key.
3. Stand near a door with an item in view. The first Use takes the item and the second opens the door. Does that feel right?
4. Look straight ahead at a weapon on the floor a step or two away and press Use. Does the reach (96 units) feel right?
5. Crouch and press Use on a loot pile. It should be taken, and `play\df.log` shows `(steal)`.
6. Kill DRLA monsters and use their dropped weapons and armor. Health and ammo they drop should still pick up by touch.
