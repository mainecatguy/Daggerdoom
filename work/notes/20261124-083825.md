# Daggerfall tells Doom the player's damage bonus (run 20261124-083825)

Branch: `agent/ammotiers-20261124-083825` (lane-items worktree, started from integration at 0699292).

## Plan

1. Add a field to Daggerfall's state half (`dfb_df_state`), which Daggerfall already publishes every frame next to
   max HP. A state field, not an event: the value is a level, not a change, and Doom always sees the latest one.
2. Daggerfall: `DoomerfallDamageBonus.cs` tabulates the bonus as a product of elements and fills the field each frame.
3. Doom: one hook in `P_DamageMobj`, next to the source's `DamageMultiply`, scales damage the bridged player deals.
4. Prove it with a fake-Daggerfall harness (like `maxhp_pickup_test.py`), on the generated IWAD and on DRLA E1M1.

## What was built

- `bridge/doomerfall_bridge.h`: `dfb_df_state.damage_bonus_bp` (int32) plus `pad0`; `reserved` shrinks by 8 bytes,
  so the state half stays 256 bytes. Units are 1/10000ths over base: 0 is none, 10000 is +100%, -5000 is -50%.
  0 is also what Doom reads before Daggerfall's first publish, so "no value yet" and "no bonus" are the same thing.
  Bindings regenerated with `python bridge/generate_bindings.py`. The layout hash changes, so old launchers, old
  `doomerfall_bridge.dll` copies and old UZDoom builds will not attach to new ones (expected for any header change).
- `DoomerfallDamageBonus.cs`: `Multiplier()` = product of (1 + element). The only element is
  `AmmoMaterialBonus()`, which returns 0 until tiered ammo exists (cards 04 and 05). No other elements were added.
  Outside a game (`sync.InGame` false) it publishes 0. It logs each change of the published value.
- `DoomerfallDamageBonusTest.cs` (test only): `-dfdamagetest <multiplier>` on Daggerfall forces the multiplier,
  for example `2` for +100%. The hook in `DoomerfallBridge.cs` is one line.
- `UZDoom/src/dd_bridge.cpp` `DD_BridgeDamageBonus`, called from `p_interaction.cpp` right after
  `damage = int(damage * source->DamageMultiply)`. It returns the damage unchanged unless all of these hold: a bridge
  session exists, the bonus is not 0, the source is the console player's own pawn (not predicting), and the target is
  not the player itself. Then damage = max(1, int(damage * (1 + bp / 10000))), truncated like `DamageMultiply`.
  It sits inside Doom's `!telefragDamage` and `!DMG_FORCED` block, so telefrags and forced damage are never scaled.
  Later modifiers (DRLA powerups via `GetModifiedDamage`, target protection, damage factors, DRLA resistances) still
  apply afterwards, so the bonus multiplies with them.

## Engineering calls (not design)

- Self-damage (the player's own rocket splash) is not scaled: the bonus is for damage the player deals.
- A penalty below -100% is clamped to -100%, and a hit that was at least 1 stays at least 1.
- Damage by the player's summons or friends is not the player's damage and is not scaled.

## Verification

Builds:
- UZDoom: `cmake -S UZDoom -B D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-build\uzdoom-damagebonus ...` then `--config Release`: built
  `Release\uzdoom.exe` with no errors.
- Bridge DLL: `cmake -S bridge -B D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-build\bridge-damagebonus`: built `doomerfall_bridge.dll`.
- Daggerfall Unity: CliBuild via `quiet_run.py --desktop`; `Logs/cli-build.log` says `CliBuild result: Succeeded`.

How a non-zero bonus was proved to change Doom's damage: `bridge/test/damage_bonus_test.py` acts as Daggerfall
on the real bridge DLL and publishes `damage_bonus_bp`. A ZScript handler in a generated PWAD spawns a stock
Cyberdemon in front of the player and hurts it for 10 through Doom's real code: `DamageMobj` with the player as
source (as a projectile or melee hit does), `LineAttack` from the player (a bullet, puff as inflictor), and
`DamageMobj` from another monster. Health lost:

| run | direct | hitscan | monster |
|---|---|---|---|
| +100% (10000 bp) | 20 | 20 | 10 |
| +50% (5000 bp) | 15 | 15 | 10 |
| 0 bp | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| no bridge session | 10 | 10 | 10 |

9 of 9 checks passed on the generated IWAD (`python bridge/test/damage_bonus_test.py <uzdoom.exe> <dll> <work>`)
and 9 of 9 on `DOOM.WAD` E1M1 with DRLA, DRLA Monsters and DRLAX (`... --drla <DOOM.WAD> <GameMods>`). Doom's
bridge log shows each scaled hit, for example `damage bonus: Cyberdemon hit by the player, 10 -> 20 (10000 bp)`.
With 0 bp and with no session the results are identical, and no `damage bonus:` line is logged.

Startup checks: `uzdoom.exe -iwad DOOM.WAD -norun -nosound -stdout +map E1M1` (private `-config`) exits 57, which
is 1337 truncated to a byte by the shell, for stock and for DRLA+Monsters+DRLAX. The DRLA harness runs above also
loaded and ran DRLA E1M1 in all four sessions.

Not done: a real paired Daggerfall + Doom session with `-dfdamagetest 2`. The Daggerfall side was built but not
launched against Doom; it needs a launcher rebuilt for the new header (the layout hash changed). The C# struct is
generated from the header and the attach check compares layout hashes, so a field mismatch would refuse to attach
rather than send a wrong value. This is on the play-test list.
