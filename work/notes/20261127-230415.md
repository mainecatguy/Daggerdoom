## Findings

- **Why no blood spawned before:** in the harness, gun C's bullet (the only hitscan) hit E1M1's wall 138 units ahead
  of the start, in front of the targets at 160 (checked with a `LineTrace`: hit type wall at x 1194.7). Projectiles
  and radius damage never spawn blood actors in UZDoom (only `P_TraceBleed` decals, or `P_BloodSplatter` with
  `+BLOODSPLATTER`), so no case could bleed.
- **Fix (test only):** in gun C's case the harness puts one more target 100 units ahead, so the bullet hits it.
  `P_LineAttack` then calls `P_SpawnBlood` *inside the player's fire action*. This is the case that matters: without
  the engine's `DDShotScope(nullptr)` in `P_SpawnBlood`, that blood would name the player as its parent and become a
  carrier of gun C. The test now requires blood in gun C's case (it can no longer pass with zero), and checks it is
  untagged and outside every gun's chain. The puff still spawns (PUFFONACTORS), so the fragment family is unchanged.
- Result: 1 `GunSrcBlood` per gun C case, untagged, `root=other` (stock and DRLA).
