## Finding: b2b was a harness bug, not game code

The harness turned the player 90 degrees away at step 23 and was meant to turn back after 15 tics. But the turn-back
line ran only when the step advanced. During the 70-tic wait `WorldTick` returns early, and `timer` had just been set to 0,
so its `timer > -45` test was meaningless. The player stayed turned away for both clicks, so neither hit. (The previous
run's magazine refill did nothing because ammo was never the problem.)

Fix (`tools/backstab/harness.zs`): while waiting in b2b, the harness notes the tic the first click's shot leaves the
weapon's ready state (`BSNOTE b2b first shot at <tic>`) and turns the player back 10 tics later. The runner's second
click comes about 30 tics after the first. No game source changed in this run.
