## Finding: b2b was a harness bug, not game code

The harness turned the player 90 degrees away at step 23 and was meant to turn back after 15 tics. But the turn-back
line ran only when the step advanced. During the 70-tic wait `WorldTick` returns early, and `timer` had just been set to 0,
so its `timer > -45` test was meaningless. The player stayed turned away for both clicks, so neither hit. (The previous
run's magazine refill did nothing because ammo was never the problem.)

Fix (`tools/backstab/harness.zs`): while waiting in b2b, the harness notes the tic the first click's shot leaves the
weapon's ready state (`BSNOTE b2b first shot at <tic>`) and turns the player back 10 tics later. The runner's second
click comes about 30 tics after the first. No game source changed in this run.

## Current "unaware" rule (final form; replaces 20261127-183500.md lines 26-50)

`DD_BackstabUnaware` (`UZDoom/src/dd_backstab.cpp`) counts a monster as unaware when all of these hold:

- it is a live, unfriendly monster with no live target;
- either it has heard nobody, or it heard the player and the noise was the opening noise of the attack now hitting it.
  Opening noise means the monster *first* heard the player at or after the attack's start, and *last* heard it no
  later than `start + OpeningTics` (35 tics, 1 s).

The attack's start is the last tic the weapon was ready at or before the hit's launch time (now for an instant hit,
the spawn time for a projectile). A shot fired after the weapon is ready again starts a new attack.

So the one second runs from the attack's start, and any spin-up is included in it. The DRLA Assault Minigun spins up
for about 20 tics, so only about the first 15 tics of firing are excused. A spin-up longer than 1 s leaves the
firing noise not excused at all. A reload is not a rule of its own. Noise after a reload inside one held attack counts
when it comes more than 1 s after the attack began, like any later noise in a long burst. If the weapon returned to
ready in between, a new attack starts and the earlier noise alerts the monster.

## Known gaps (current; replaces 20261127-183500.md lines 129-147)

- A monster that hears your gunshot and runs its next look before the bullet lands takes a target and is aware; with
  slow-firing guns that will happen sometimes.
- A wind-up longer than 1 s means the monster heard noise past the opening second, so the first bullet is not a sneak
  hit. No current DRLA weapon was checked beyond the Assault Minigun (about 20 tics, fine).
- Projectiles still flying after the attack ended belong to the attack they were fired in (by spawn time), but only
  the last 8 attacks are remembered.
- Hearing is recorded only while a bridge session runs; a monster that heard the player through another route that
  sets `LastHeard` directly (ACS, a few ZScript actors) counts as alerted. The detection bar is meant to refine this,
  in `DD_BackstabUnaware`.
- A sneak hit is counted where the bonus is applied, before the target's own damage modifiers, so a hit the target
  then shrugs off completely still tallies.
- Projectile pellets that land in different tics on the same enemy get the bonus only on the first; hitscan pellets
  and blasts land together.
- The queued weapon-switch case has no real-weapon test. It is covered by construction: the switch leaves the ready
  state through `DPSprite::SetState`.
- Use-based rates rework (`skillresearch` 02) not applied: Daggerfall's 1 use per hit is the "normal" base. No perks.

## Manual correction (verifier round)

`manual.md` now says the noise excused is what you make in the first second after you start the attack, spin-up
included. Later noise counts, even in the same burst. A new shot after the weapon is ready again is a new attack.
The old text read as "spin-up plus a second of firing" and treated a reload as a rule of its own.
