## Bug found and fixed: mooks moved into a later placement were never spawned

`dd_encounter_placer.cpp` reads every placement's mooks in `ChooseLeader` before it processes any placement. Suppose
a placement fills up, and the planner's `Move` sends its points to a placement that has not been processed yet.
`MovePoints` then returned with "placed when its turn comes". But `Process` used the mook list it had read earlier, so
the moved mooks stayed in the plan and never spawned.

- Stock, level 10: the plan had 406 mooks and 83,950 centipoints, but only 390 mooks (80,910) were handed out.
- Stock, level 30: 1,524 planned, 1,460 placed. 35,070 centipoints (about 9% of the pool) were silently lost.
- Card 5's "0 dropped" numbers were wrong in the same way. Its `dropped-points` counter only counted `Move` failures.
- **Fix:** `Process` re-reads the placement's mooks first (one line, `ReadMooks(p)`).
- **After the fix,** every run's contract balances. Stock level 30 now places 1,581 mooks.
- **Commit:** 8c34c1c.

- every placement has at least one mook and its member-0 leader;

**Wrapper fix.** In `encounter_facts.py` `patch_route`, the re-entry count is now compared with the living count when
the player leaves, not at the save. Civil War and infighting can kill a monster after the save. The route logs
`MDLATE n died after the save` when that happens. This does not weaken the check: deaths still have to persist, and
no monster may come back.

The Daggerfall `Player.log` has one `NullReferenceException` in `DaggerfallUI.SetupSingleton` at startup, before any
game code runs. It is not related to encounters and was not investigated.

## Play-test list (for the owner: feel)

1. **Pack sizes.** Enter Privateer's Hold at levels 1, 10 and 30. Do the packs around each Daggerfall enemy spot
   feel right?
   - Level 1 has about 2 monsters per spot.
   - Level 10 has about 9.
   - Level 30 has about 38 stock, 27 DRLA, or 15 CH+DRLA per spot.
   - At level 30, two thirds of stock mooks are crowded to touching distance.
2. **Bosses.**
   - Find the four boss placements (31, 21, 38 and 25 in Privateer's Hold standalone; 42, 28, 31 and 33 in the paired
     run).
   - Do the bosses read as bosses, and are their escorts reasonable? An earlier question covers the 240-escort
     Cacodemon case.
3. **Titans.**
   - In a level 25+ Dragon's Den with Colourful Hell, the Black Cacodemon EX should sit at the deepest boss. Is it a
     fitting climax?
   - At level 20+ in other dungeons, a Black Imp EX may appear at a random spot. Is it a surprise or an annoyance?
4. **Civil War.**
   - Privateer's Hold is a Civil War dungeon (two armies, hunt stance at level 1).
   - Does the war read clearly?
   - Does it make the dungeon easier, because the armies thin each other out before you arrive?
   - Hitscan armies hit their own side, as already asked.
5. **Is level 30 at 90 points too easy?** The pool at level 30 is 90 points per placement (378,000 centipoints over 42
   placements).
   - Stock Doom: 1,581 mostly cheap monsters (Lost Souls 667, Shotgun Guys 345, Spectres 271).
   - CH+DRLA: 641 stronger monsters.
   - Is that the slaughter-map pressure you want at the top of the game?
6. **Frame rate** with a visible window when a level 30 stock Privateer's Hold wakes up (about 1,500 monsters). Think
   time alone averaged 7 ms with a 31 ms worst tic.
7. **The load hitch** when monsters first appear at level 30: about 2.7 s stock, 0.6 s with DRLA or CH.
