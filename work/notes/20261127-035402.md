**Why the two dead stops happen** (evidence `evidence\moveprobe-new.txt`, `evidence\bevel151-new.txt`,
`evidence\meshfaces-bevel52.txt`; new tools `tools/dungeon_converter/move_probe.py` and `mesh_faces.py`):

- **The place.** The corridor north of 52,620 runs along a 45-degree bevel (x 144-162). Above the bevel, the visible
  wall leans inward at 76.8 degrees. The mesh's leaning face is at 1046 / 1024.8 / 1003.5 / 986.5 at x 146 / 151 / 156 /
  160 (`meshfaces-bevel52.txt`).
- **Ceiling: unchanged.** Both maps already give these sectors the leaning face as a sloped ceiling (old 1759 / new
  1566 have the identical `ceilingplane`). The pre-existing `CeilingNormal = 0.2` rule from e9974648 allows that.
  At (151,710) the same face is the underside of a 3D floor (`moveprobe-new.txt`, corner sectors with 3D floors;
  the engine's ceiling there is 1023.8).
- **Floor: what changed.** The old map's floor is flat 968. The new map's floor is the bevel plane (44.4 degrees,
  968 to 978 across the strip), matching the mesh to within 1 unit (`bevel151-new.txt`). This is the intended change.
- **Clearance at the wall.** The rising floor meets the falling ceiling. At the player box's wall-side corner (x 161.7)
  in sector 1566, floor 978.2 meets ceiling 979.3, about 1 unit of room (`moveprobe-new.txt`).
- **What the engine does.** A direct move along the wall works (`trymove 1` to 150.1,936.4). But Doom's slide picks the
  sector boundary at y 930 as the blocking line, and its opening at that corner is closed. So the player stops instead
  of sliding.
- **The old map was not better geometry.** With the flat floor the wall-side corner had about 11-20 units, also under
  the 56 height, but the slide picked the wall instead. In both maps the 56-tall body cannot stand within a few units of
  that leaning wall. The middle of the corridor is open in both: 57 units at x 151, 83 at x 146.

**Possible fixes. I did not do them; each changes how the place plays.**

- Flatten the bevel floor only under a leaning ceiling. That brings back the 8-unit steps the goal removed.
- Drop steep ceiling slopes so the head can enter the visible wall. That makes the walls see-through when hugged, and
  it changes the collision of 1,106 sloped-ceiling sectors, so it is a broader call.
- Make wall-side cells with under-crouch-height clearance solid. That conflicts with the "crawl spaces always stay"
  rule only where clearance is 28 or more, so it may be feasible, but it is new converter logic beyond this goal's
  remaining budget.

I left the map as it is: the dead stops cost two of 64 runs. They sit where the visible wall leans in, and a player
steering slightly away from the wall passes.
