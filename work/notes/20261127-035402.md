## What caused the dead stops (corrected)

The first version of these notes blamed the bevel floor meeting a low leaning ceiling. That was wrong in its detail: the
verifier pointed out that my own probe showed the blocking line at (151,922) is a one-sided wall, and that I had saved no
old-map evidence. I checked both maps with new tools:

- `tools/dungeon_converter/walk_replay.py`: Doom-only replay of harness runs, printing the blocking line each tic. It
  reproduces the paired walk's positions exactly.
- `tools/dungeon_converter/move_probe.py`: puts the player at a point and tries one move. Prints the opening, the sector
  under each box corner, and the blocking line.

Findings:

- **The 710 stop: a new straight edge, plus a leaning ceiling** (`evidence\replay-new.txt`, `replay-old.txt`,
  `moveprobe-old.txt`, `moveprobe-new-oldspots.txt`).
  - In the old map the player sliding north stays at x 149.5, held by the diagonal edge (156,684)-(162,710.9) of a
    low-ceiling pocket (sector 1283, ceiling 276).
  - The old map cannot hold the player at (151.04,710.32) at all: `fits 0`, the box overlaps that pocket.
  - The new converter fits that edge to the real wall at x 162. The converter's `--debug-chains` output shows chain
    1119|975 fitted to `S(162,576)-(162,768)`. The mesh shows the bevel floor visible at (160,700), so the new edge is
    more faithful.
  - The player now gets 1.5 units further east, to 151.04. There the next move north fails at line 712 with a ceiling
    1019.7 over a floor 967.8 (`moveprobe-new.txt`). That ceiling is a 76.8-degree leaning wall face, stored as a
    sloped collision ceiling (the underside of a 3D floor). It drops about 4.25 units per unit toward the wall, so a
    unit of position flips pass and block.
  - From x 149.51 the same north move passes in both maps (`moveprobe-old.txt`, `moveprobe-new-oldspots.txt`).
- **The 922 stop: same walls, the player got closer** (`replay-*.txt`, `moveprobe-old.txt`).
  - Wall 772, (156.8,997.5)-(162,930), is identical to the old map's line 830. It is one-sided and leans inward 4.4
    degrees.
  - A straight north move into it is blocked in both maps, from both maps' positions.
  - The old run reached the corner 0.89 units off the east wall and slid west along 772. The new run reached it 0.31
    units off, after climbing the sloped bevel, and the slide failed every tic.
  - The leaning-face ceilings are identical in both maps (old sector 1759 and new 1566 have the same `ceilingplane`).
    Only the floor changed: flat 968 in the old map, the 44.4-degree bevel plane in the new (`bevel151-new.txt`, mesh
    within 1 unit).
  - My earlier claims that the old map's wall-side clearance was 11-20 units and that its slide picked the wall instead
    were not measured. I withdraw them.

## The fix (`tools/dungeon_converter/AngledCollision.cs`, dungeon path only)

**The rule.** A ceiling face steeper than 60 degrees (`DungeonCeilingNormal = 0.5`) over a span whose floor is a
sloped plane is no longer a sloped collision ceiling. The span gets a flat ceiling at the face's highest point over its
cell, clamped under the next span up.

- **Why that height.** The ceiling never gives up headroom the visible face leaves. Where the face leans over the
  walkway, the collision ceiling is up to one cell's worth of slope above the visible face.
- **Interiors.** They take the old path (`smoothDungeon` false), so they are unchanged.

Variants I tried and rejected:

| Variant | What it did | Result |
|---|---|---|
| fix2 | Every steep ceiling flat at the span's own ceiling height | Lowered the ceiling below the face on the open side. Dead stops in the 52,620 sweep 24, but heading 45 stopped at 919 with 2 snags, and three ramp headings gained snags (`compare-fix2.txt`). |
| fix3 | Flat at the face's top, everywhere | Fixed 52,620, but the ramp descents over a steep stair at y -58.6 gained snags (3 to 6 at heading 22.5; `compare-fix3.txt`). |
| fix4 | Flat at the face's top, only over sloped floors | Kept. Ramp results identical to ab2ca91f. |
