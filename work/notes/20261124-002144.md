## Findings
- **Crash cause:** `DD_BridgeFrame` (d_main.cpp:1593) runs between tics, after `P_PredictClient` (d_net.cpp:2337). So UZDoom 5's
  rollback prediction is active even in single player. Anything spawned then is marked "spawned while predicting" and destroyed by
  `DisablePrediction`, and `P_UnPredictClient` restores players from the snapshot. Guard pawns spawned there broke player 0.
  Fix: `DD_GuardsFrame` calls `P_UnPredictClient()` before spawning or burying guards (the save code does the same).
  After the fix there was no abort in 9 of 9 paired runs (g5 to g14).
- **Possible latent bug elsewhere:** `DD_MonstersFrame` (quest foes) and `DD_TownsfolkFrame` also spawn actors from
  `DD_BridgeFrame`, so they may be destroyed the same way. Not investigated (outside this goal).
- Guards spawned on the player's landing were pushed past the door line. `Settle` now accepts only spots reachable from the door spot
  (no blocking or one-sided line, tall enough opening, within step height). If no spot qualifies, it uses the door spot itself.
- Guards now spawn as plain `DoomPlayer` pawns (they would otherwise take the human's DRLA class). This alone did not change hits.
- Attacks: with stock mods (g8), player HP fell from 100 to 40 within a second. With DRLA (g12, g14), plasma and bullet hits reached Daggerfall
  through the HP bridge (g14: the player died, Daggerfall reported the death, and Doom killed the pawn). Some runs (g7, g9, g13) got no hits in 8 s:
  rockets never connected, and the guard behind furniture shot into it. This looks like Cajun bot pathing plus mesh weapon collision
  (bots do not path around counters). Not a bridge bug, but a play-test item.
- Guard deaths: the player kills every bot, Doom sends `GUARD_DEATH`, Daggerfall logs `removed Daggerfall's copy`, and the slots are freed (g6, g7, g9, g12).
- Inert copies: Daggerfall HP stayed at 100 for about 16 s with live copies (g9, where Doom guards landed no hits). The old test reported
  "copies 0" because `FindObjectsOfType` skips inactive objects. It now uses `Resources.FindObjectsOfTypeAll` (built, not yet rerun).
