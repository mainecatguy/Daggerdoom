# skillperks 09: Automatics 25 free shot, 50 bands, 75 suppression, 100 bigger stack (run 20261127-231039)

Goal card: Automatics 25 every third shot free, 50 fire-rate band bonuses, 75 suppression slow, 100 damage-taken stack
+2% per hit to +30% (one ammo per 4 shots stays). Owner decisions: `gamedesign/decisions/20261127-automatics-tuning-owner.md`.

## What was built

**Daggerfall** (`DoomerfallAutomaticsRules.cs`) decides every number and sends it in the character sheet. Nine new
sheet values, 194-202 (`DFB_SHEET_AUTO_FREE_EVERY`, `_SLOW_DAMAGE`, `_SLOW_PEN`, `_MID_DAMAGE`, `_MID_PEN`,
`_FAST_EXTRA_BP`, `_SLOW_HIT_BP`, `_SLOW_MAX_BP`, `_SLOW_FADE_MS`), sheet format 13, 203 ints. By skill:
- 25-49: free every 3rd shot (sent as 0 from 50, so it never stacks with one-per-2/one-per-4).
- 50+: slow band +25 dmg / +1 pen, middle +7 / +1 pen, fast 1000 bp (10%) extra bullet.
- 75+: sunder 100 bp to 1500; suppression 500 bp per hit to 4000, fade 5000 ms.
- 100: sunder 200 bp per hit to 3000 (the final total, not added to 75's); one ammo per 4 shots (unchanged).

The perk popup text (`DoomerfallPerks.cs`) and `manual.md`'s Automatics section say the same.

**Doom** (`mods/doomerfall/src/zscript/DoomerfallAutomatics.zs`, new `DoomerfallAutomaticBands.zs`):
- *Bands* are part of the gun: `DoomerfallAutomaticBands.TicsOf(class)`. Stock Chaingun is 4 (CHGG A 4 / B 4). DRLA guns
  come from a generated table, `tools/automatics/derive_bands.py --write` (`--check` for staleness), which reuses
  `derive_rifles.trace_fire`: the Fire state walked with the trigger held, no mods, ammo loaded, until it loops; the
  closest two shots in that loop are the base (spun-up) rate. 58 guns listed (rapid-fire token, the owner's Automatics
  list, and the Grammaton Beretta). Examples: Battle Rifle 4, DRLA Chaingun 4, Uzi 3, RCP90 3, Thompson 2, Minigun 1.
- *Flat damage* (+25/+7) is added in the perk's ModifyDamage before its percentage factors. Because P_DamageMobj
  applies `DamageMultiply` and Daggerfall's damage bonus (material of gun and ammo) *before* ModifyDamage, the flat
  amount is multiplied by those two first, so it scales exactly like base damage. That needed one tiny read-only
  native, `Actor.DD_DamageBonusBp()` (dd_bridge.cpp + vmthunks + actor.zs, 0 with no session). Nothing changes on a
  stock map.
- *Penetration*: after a direct hit (not splash, `DMG_EXPLOSION`), the bullet goes on from the far side of the enemy
  along its (level) line and hits the next live shootable thing with the hit's damage before the first enemy's own
  modifiers. Same mechanism as Pistols' carry-through (`DMG_NO_ENHANCE`, no inflictor, next WorldTick; reuses
  `DoomerfallPistolTracer`). A penetration hit counts as the gun's hit for sunder and suppression. Works for projectile
  guns too (the continuation is a trace along the projectile's flight).
- *Extra bullet* (fast band): rolled once per shot in the player's DoEffect (after P_PlayerThink and WorldTick, so the
  shot, its puffs and projectiles are known). Hitscan: one more LineAttack with the gun's puff and the base damage
  (before every multiplier) the gun's last bullet hit had, so all bonuses apply to it like any bullet. Projectile: the
  same projectile class spawned again, tagged as the shot's (DoomerfallShots.Tag) with its burst state. Its spread is
  the first bullet's offset from the aim, mirrored about the aim: same distribution as the gun's own symmetric scatter.
  Costs no ammo (nothing is spent).
- *Every third shot free*: counted per shot (tic with an ammo drop, trigger down); refunds the whole shot's rounds on
  every 3rd; the count resets when the gun is (re)raised.
- *Suppression*: `DoomerfallSuppress` on the monster scales its `Speed` (what A_Chase steps by; attacks untouched).
  Current slow = slow-at-last-hit x (1 - elapsed/5 s); a hit sets slow = min(current + 5%, 40%). If the monster changes
  its own Speed meanwhile, that becomes the base. Removed (speed restored) when faded. Kept in Doom saves.
- *100 stack*: unchanged code; Daggerfall just sends 200/3000.

## Known gaps and judgment calls

- **The small explosion graphic on slow-band hits is not built.** The goal card says it is a separate later card
  (`automaticspop` 01, already in todo), though its "done when" line mentions it. Nothing else depends on it.
- *Alternate fire*: the table holds each gun's primary fire rate only. DRLA uses AltFire mostly for reloads/modes;
  hits are attributed to the gun, not the fire mode, so an alt-fire mode gets the primary's band.
- *No band* for RLStormUzi, RLSussGun, RLNuclearOnslaught: the DECORATE walk finds no shot with fire held (their fire
  is driven by ACS or custom functions). RLStormUzi is an owner-listed Automatic, so it gets no 50 band bonus.
- *Extra hitscan bullet damage* is the gun's last observed bullet base damage; before the gun's very first bullet hit
  in a session there is none, so that one extra bullet is skipped (logged). DRLA damage rolls vary per bullet; the
  extra copies the last one rather than rolling its own.
- *Extra bullet spread* mirrors the first bullet; with autoaim snapping pitch it would mirror around the player's
  pitch, not the autoaim pitch.
- *Penetration line* is level (as Pistols' carry-through), so a steep up/down shot carries on flat behind the enemy.
- *Ammo-check weakness in the test*: extra100 refills the magazine when low, so "extra bullets spend no ammo" is only
  shown by construction (the extra is a LineAttack/Spawn, which never spends ammo), not by the ammo count.
