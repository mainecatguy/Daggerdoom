# Daggerfall people and creatures die and leave corpses in Doom

Branch `agent/townscorpse-20261127-084126`.

## What Daggerfall Unity actually does (checked in the code)

- **Townspeople (mobile NPCs).** `WeaponManager.WeaponDamage`: a commoner hit by a weapon shows a blood splash
  (`EnemyBlood.ShowBloodSplash`: TEXTURE.380 record 0, one shot at 10 fps) and its GameObject is switched off. There is
  **no corpse** in Daggerfall Unity, and no death frames. A guard townsperson does not die at all: it turns into a live
  city watch knight on the spot (assault).
- **Enemies (guards and creatures).** `EnemyDeath.CompleteDeath`: the enemy vanishes and a lootable corpse billboard
  appears (`MobileEnemy.CorpseTexture`, or `FemaleCorpseTexture`), with the body-fall sound. Daggerfall's mobile
  archives have no death frames; "the death animation" is the blood splash and the corpse.
- **Every human class** (Mage ... Knight, and the city watch) has corpse TEXTURE.380 record 1 (`EnemyBasics`).
- **Static NPCs** (shopkeepers, quest givers, `DFNpc` in Doom) cannot be hurt in Daggerfall Unity and take no damage
  in Doom (`DFNpc`: "blocks nothing and takes no damage"). They have no death, so nothing to do.
- **Daggerfall creatures in Doom: none today.** Dungeon enemy placements become invisible encounter markers
  (`tools/dungeon_converter/EnemyRoster.cs`: "Doom's encounter planner decides the monsters at load, so the converter no
  longer picks any"); Doom monsters fill them. The only Daggerfall enemy drawn in Doom is the city guard (bot with
  archive 399). The new runtime reader (below) can draw any Daggerfall billboard archive, creatures included, and the
  corpse actor takes any archive and record, so showing a Daggerfall creature later needs no new art path.

## Plan (as built)

1. **Read Daggerfall's images at run time** (`UZDoom/src/playsim/dd_dftextures.cpp`). A port of `TextureFile.cs`
   (uncompressed, multi-frame and RLE records, ART_PAL.COL, index 0 transparent) reads TEXTURE.### from `-ddarena2`
   on first use. Before decoding, it looks for a Daggerfall Unity texture-pack replacement in `-ddtextures`, named as
   Daggerfall Unity names it (`TextureReplacement.GetName`: `380_1-0.png`), and keeps the original's world size
   (`SetDisplaySize`), as Daggerfall Unity does. Size in Doom is `MeshReader.GetBillboardMesh`'s rule, the same as the
   converter's `BillboardScale`. Nothing is extracted or written. The launcher now passes
   `-ddtextures <dfu>\<exe>_Data\StreamingAssets\Textures` (Daggerfall Unity's `TextureReplacement.TexturesPath`).
   Precedent: `dd_weather.cpp` already reads the sky from ARENA2 at run time.
2. **Townspeople and guards draw from it.** `dd_townsfolk.cpp`'s sprite choice now asks the runtime reader first and
   animates frames itself (walk 4 fps, idle 1 fps, as `MobilePersonBillboard`); the converter's `dfflat` copies are only
   a fallback when the game files cannot be read. So a texture pack that replaces a townsperson replaces them in Doom too.
3. **Death in Doom** (`DD_CorpseFell`, `dd_townsfolk.cpp`): Doom tells Daggerfall where the body fell
   (`DFB_KIND_CORPSE_PLACE`, before the death event), plays Daggerfall's blood splash (`DFBloodSplash`, 380/0 at
   10 fps), and lays an imageless `DFCorpse`. Guard bots do the same (`dd_guards.cpp`); the dead pawn is hidden instead
   of being retextured with the converter's `dfmesh/a380r01.png`.
4. **Daggerfall decides and keeps the corpse** (`DoomerfallTownspeople.OnCorpsePlace`, `DoomerfallCorpses.cs`): a
   townsperson gets the human corpse 380/1; a guard townsperson gets none (it becomes the city watch, as in Daggerfall
   Unity); a city guard gets its own Daggerfall copy's corpse texture, picked as `EnemyDeath` picks it. The corpse is
   stored in the townspeople ledger (`DoomerfallTownspeople.json`, new `corpses` list) with the town's map id, the
   building key (0 on the streets) and the position (world units outside, Doom units inside), so it survives leaving,
   returning, saving and loading. Crime and reputation are unchanged: the existing `OnDeath` still runs.
5. **Listing.** Each townsfolk list (streets within 150 m, or the building the player is in) now also carries the corpses
   in sight (`DFB_KIND_TOWNSFOLK_CORPSE`, same generation and coordinates as the people). Doom keeps one `DFCorpse` per
   listed corpse and shows it with Daggerfall's image; a body that fell this visit waits for Daggerfall's answer.

## Judgment calls (engineering, within the owner's design)

- Townspeople get the human corpse 380/1 although Daggerfall Unity gives them none, because the owner asked for a
  corpse and 380/1 is what Daggerfall gives every human class.
- A guard townsperson still leaves no corpse: Daggerfall turns it into a live city watch knight. Outdoors that knight is
  not relayed to Doom yet (guard bots are interior-only), so the guard just vanishes after the blood splash. See
  questions.
- Doom shows the blood splash for guards too (Daggerfall shows blood on every hit of an enemy); Doom's own death sound
  plays rather than Daggerfall's body-fall sound.
- `.dfmod` texture packs are Unity asset bundles; Doom cannot read them. Only loose-file packs work. See questions.
