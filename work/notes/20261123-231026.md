- The first `--back` run listed 0 people after the return. The stitch lands the player in the entry band outside the
  town's edge, more than 2,500 units outside Daggerfall's town rectangle, where Daggerfall deliberately lists nobody.
  This was not a bug. The harness now walks 200 more tics into town.
- Starting `play.bat` from Git Bash: `cmd /c` becomes `cmd C:\` (path rewriting), and a bare `play.bat` was not found
  under `--cwd`. `cmd.exe //c "<full path>\play.bat"` worked.
- Process listing (tasklist, Get-Process) needed approval I could not get, so the demo was stopped with taskkill on
  the PIDs from its own launcher log.
- The Unity build rewrites `Assets/AddressableAssetsData/Windows/addressables_content_state.bin`. It was left
  uncommitted as a build byproduct.
