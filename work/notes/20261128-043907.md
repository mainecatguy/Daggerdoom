# lockpickshops 01: where lockpicks come from (2026-11-28, run 043907)

Goal: lockpicks regularly at pawn shops and general stores, occasionally at other fitting traders, and sometimes in
Daggerfall-engine monster loot, all seeded (owner, 2026-11-27, `gamedesign/decisions/20261127-starter-materials-doors-lockpicks-owner.md`).

## What was already there

`DoomerfallLockpick.cs` (lock-rethink work) already had:
- `StockShop`, called from `DaggerfallLoot.AddShopDelivery`: general stores 70% and pawn shops 80% per delivery roll
  (quality scaled), a stack of 5-15.
- `AddLoot`: 8% for 1-3 picks in treasure piles and on **human class enemies only** ("Monsters never do").

Problems found against the goal and the "Adding things to random pools" principle:
1. **Crowding.** In a bridge session every shelf goes through `DoomerfallShopLedger`. Its `LeanPick` builds a pool from up
   to six `AddShopDelivery` rolls and picks goods up to the stock target by the shop's identity weights, so a lockpick
   stack took a stocked-goods slot (crowding out other goods) and, as a kind the shop does not favour, specialists'
   0.03 floor weight made picks rare there. Not "regularly".
2. No other traders.
3. No monsters (the owner now wants Daggerfall-engine monster drops).
4. `AddLoot` restored the random state after its roll, so the roll did not shift later rolls, but it **reused** the next
   values: a lockpick find (first value < 0.08) meant the next seeded roll also started with a low value (for treasure
   piles, the "randomly add map" chance). Seeded, but correlated.

## What I changed

- **Shops: a daily staple outside the stock target, like Doom ammo.** `DoomerfallShopLedger.DailyAmmo` (shelf 0, every
  day) now calls `DoomerfallLockpick.DailyStock`. It rolls on its own seed stream (`LootSeed.Of("LPCK", location,
  buildingKey, day)`) and restores Unity's generator, so the ledger's own rolls are unchanged. The day's stack tops up an
  existing stack (max, not add), as ammo does. Lockpicks no longer count in `Stocked` (stock target) or enter the
  `LeanPick` pool; `StockShop` (the classic delivery path) does nothing while the ledger is active.
  - Daily chance before the quality scale (0.55x at quality 1 to 1.5x at 20, cap 95%): general stores 70%, pawn shops
    80%, **weapon smiths and armorers 8%** (my pick for "occasionally other fitting traders": metalworkers who would
    make or carry small tools). Alchemists, booksellers, clothiers, jewelers never.
  - A stack sells off at 50%/day (`SellChance` for 25 gold), so presence is about 2p/(1+p): ~82% of mid-quality general
    stores, ~89% of pawn shops, ~15% of metalworkers. Stacks: 5-15 at general/pawn, 1-5 at metalworkers.
- **Daggerfall-engine enemy loot.** `EnemyEntity` now calls `AddEnemyLoot(entityType, LootTableKey, items)`. Picks are
  possible on class enemies (as before) and on **monsters whose Daggerfall loot table carries gold** (`MaxGold > 0`):
  imps, orcs, centaurs, nymphs, harpies, skeletons, giants, zombies, mummies, daedra, vampires, liches, dreugh, lamia.
  Animals (no loot key), spriggans (B) and ghosts/wraiths (I) never. Same 8% for 1-3 as before. In Doomerfall this is
  the pack leader's drop (`DoomerfallMonsters.Roll` -> `DoomerfallDrops.RollEnemy` -> `SetEnemyCareer`), seeded by the
  placement's loot seed. Followers never roll (existing rule), so this adds picks only where Daggerfall rolls loot.
- **Decorrelated loot roll.** `AddLoot` takes the next value the seeded generator would draw, hashes it into its own
  seed (`LootSeed.Of("LPCK", value)`), rolls there, and restores the state. Still fully determined by the place's
  seed; no longer correlated with the following roll.
- Manual: the lockpick sources line.
- Survey test `DoomerfallLockpickSurveyTest.cs` (`-dflockpicksurvey`, attached with the other shop tests), run with
  `tools/shop_levels/shelf_run.py run NAME --lockpicks`.

Rates of the shop roll per the "stated target" rule: lockpicks are a separate daily staple, never taking a slot, so
they do not crowd anything; the target is the presence share above.

## Play-test list

- Visit a few general stores and pawn shops on different days: lockpicks should be on the shelf most days, in stacks of
  5-15. Weapon smiths and armorers only now and then, 1-5.
- Kill orc / skeleton / zombie pack leaders in a dungeon: now and then a lockpick or few in the drop; never from rats,
  bats, spiders or ghosts.
- Buying out a shop's picks: they come back on a later day (the daily top-up), not on the same day.
