Only the player can hurt a townsperson in Daggerfall: crime handling runs only when the source is the player
(`HandleAttackFromSource`), and enemies never target civilians. Doom now refuses damage to townspeople from anyone else
(stray monster shots, guard bots, crushers).

## What was built

- **Doom** (`UZDoom/src/dd_townsfolk.cpp`): a townsperson actor is now shootable with 1 health, sized to its billboard
  (radius 30% of its width, 6-20; height 16-96), still non-solid, `NOTAUTOAIMED`, `NEVERTARGET`, `DONTTHRUST`.
  `DD_TownsfolkRefuseDamage` refuses any damage whose source is not a player. One-line hook in `P_DamageMobj`
  (`p_interaction.cpp`), only for actors with a `ddRecordId`. Death already reached Daggerfall
  (`DFB_KIND_TOWNSPERSON_DEATH`); the dead actor has no death state and is removed on the next tic, as Daggerfall
  switches the billboard off. Bullets show Doom's blood.
- **Daggerfall** (`DoomerfallTownspeople.OnDeath` / `ApplyCrime`): the same calls as `WeaponManager.WeaponDamage`.
  No new bridge kind; the header is unchanged, so old launchers and bridge DLLs still match.
- **Home occupants** (people at home inside their house, a Doomerfall addition) are the same roster people as on the
  street, so they follow the same rule. Indoors, `SpawnCityGuards` sends 2-5 guards in at the door, and those are
  relayed to Doom as guard bots.

### Guards spawned outdoors (out of scope; what happens)

Daggerfall spawns its city watch outdoors (`SpawnCityGuards`, or one knight for a hit guard), and `DoomerfallGuards`
logs `not relayed (only interiors are)`. Doom shows nothing, and Daggerfall's knight stays inert. So outdoors, a murder
today has its crime recorded but no guards to fight. In one night run with only a commoner killed, Daggerfall logged no
outdoor guard at all: `SpawnCityGuards(true)` outdoors takes guards from Daggerfall Unity's own nearby mobiles within
77.5 m (`PlayerEntity.cs:659-666`), and at night there may be none.

## Judgement calls (engineering, within the owner's rules)

- **HP.** Daggerfall's weapon rule ignores the civilian's HP: any hit kills. So Doom gives townspeople 1 health. All
  Doom damage counts as a weapon hit, because Daggerfall spells are not cast on NPCs through Doom yet. When they are,
  `DaggerfallEntityBehaviour.cs:207-235` (non-lethal spell = assault) will need a real HP from `CivilianEntity`.
- **No body.** Daggerfall switches a dead mobile off and leaves no corpse or loot (`WeaponManager.cs:509`), so the Doom
  actor is removed on the next tic. Bullets still show Doom's blood puff.
- **A hit guard is gone for good.** Daggerfall turns the mobile guard into a knight. Doomerfall's roster is persistent,
  so the guard person goes in the death ledger and is not listed again. The knight is Daggerfall's (relayed indoors only).
- **Home occupants** follow the street rule, because they are the same roster people.
- **Solid?** Townspeople stay non-solid to the player. Daggerfall's mobiles have colliders, but changing movement
  blocking was not part of this goal.
- **Racial feeding.** A vampire or lycanthrope gets the same `OnWeaponHitEntity` call, with a stand-in dead civilian
  entity because Doomerfall keeps no Daggerfall object for a townsperson. Not tested (no infected character in the tests).
