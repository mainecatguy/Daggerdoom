# Climbing in Doom (20261124-002144)

Goal: the Doom player climbs walls on Daggerfall maps with the character's real Climbing skill, following the
owner's design of 2026-11-23. Branch `agent/climb-20261124-002144`.

## What was built

| Piece | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Sheet values | `bridge/doomerfall_bridge.h`, `DoomerfallSheet.cs` | Sheet format 2 (155 ints). `DFB_SHEET_CLIMB_CHANCE` (150-153) holds Daggerfall's four finished chances from `FormulaHelper.CalculateClimbingChance` (bases 70 start, 40 grab while airborne, 50 continue, 20 regain), so race, the Climbing effect, luck, and formula mods stay Daggerfall's. `DFB_SHEET_CLIMB_ENHANCED` (154) is 1 while a Climbing effect doubles speed. |
| Skill uses | kind 170 `DFB_KIND_CLIMB_USES`, `DoomerfallClimb.cs` | Doom reports how many skill rolls it made. Daggerfall calls its own `TallySkill(Climbing, n)`, one use per roll as `ClimbingMotor` does. |
| Engine | `UZDoom/src/dd_climb.cpp` (+3 hook lines in `dd_bridge.cpp`, 1 in CMake) | Turns the sheet into a `dfi_climb:` network event for the mod when it changes, and the mod's `dfc_uses:` events into kind 170. Only runs in a bridge session. |
| Climb | `mods/doomerfall/src/zscript/DoomerfallClimb.zs` | The climb. It is active only after `dfi_rules:1` (generated Daggerfall map in a bridge session) and valid chances. |
| Test | `tools/climb/` | `run_test.py` runs a hand-made test map with a scripted player (no Daggerfall). `paired_probe.zs` is the real-session probe. |

### Climb rules as built

- **Start:** forward held into a touching surface that the view faces within 60 degrees, with under 2 units of sideways drift per tic. After 35 tics (owner: more than 1 s), the climb rolls Daggerfall's start chance each tic until it succeeds, as Daggerfall rerolls a failed ground start. Airborne starts use the grab chance, and a failure restarts the 1 s wait (Daggerfall's rule).
- **Surfaces:** walls and 3D-floor sides taller than Doom's step height (traced at 28 units above the feet), and floor planes of 70 degrees or steeper. Not actors (doors, monsters), not ceilings, and not walls of a moving sector (Daggerfall excludes moving objects).
- **Moving:** forward climbs, back climbs down, and strafe moves along the wall in the direction the view's strafe points. The player can turn freely. No input keeps the player still on the wall.
- **Speed:** Doom's top ground speed for the run state when the climb began, divided by 3, which is Daggerfall's climb-to-move ratio (`PlayerSpeedChanger.cs:427`). It is doubled by the Climbing effect and fixed for the whole climb, as in Daggerfall. Walking gives 2.778 units per tic (97 units/s, 3.2 m/s) and running gives 5.56. Daggerfall's absolute climb speed at Speed 50 is about 1.27 units per tic.
- **Rolls:** continue every 30 tics (0.86 s) while moving on the wall. A failure slips: gravity returns, and a regain roll comes every 11 tics (0.3 s). Regaining clears the fall. Reaching the ground ends the climb. A failed roll in water counts as success (Daggerfall). Each roll, pass or fail, is one skill use, sent in batches once a second.
- **Ending:** Jump lets go with a small push off the wall. When the wall ends within step height of the feet, the player is carried onto the ledge. Otherwise the player lets go and falls.
- **Message:** "Climbing" on Doom's message line when a climb starts, plus a gold "Climbing" or "Slipping" overlay above where "Sneaking" shows.
- **Weapons:** untouched, so they work while climbing.
- **Slope limit:** on Daggerfall maps the player's `MaxSlopeSteepness` is cos 70 (Daggerfall's 70-degree walkable limit). Doom's default comes back off those maps. Step height stays 24.
- **Saves:** the climb is not saved. A save made mid-climb loads with gravity back on and the player not climbing.

## Failed approaches and fixes on the way

- `Stop` is a ZScript keyword, so the method is named `EndClimb`.
- An `out Vector2` parameter compiled but hit "Unexpected JIT error: Unknown REGT value passed to EmitPARAM" at load. It is replaced by a member field.
- "Blocked" was first total movement under 2 units per tic. Doom bounces the player on a 75-degree slope (0.7-2.1 units per tic along the normal), which kept resetting the timer. It now measures only sideways drift along the surface, which is Daggerfall's criterion anyway.

## Judgment calls (report to owner)

- **Holding still does not roll and does not slip.** This is Daggerfall's advanced-climbing rule. The owner's "the player sticks to the surface" needs it, and it lets the player stop and shoot. Classic Daggerfall drops the player when forward is released, which the owner's design replaces.
- **Back climbs down** (advanced Daggerfall). The owner's design did not say.
- **Strafe and climb-down speed** equal the climb speed.
- **70-degree walkable limit on Daggerfall maps.** The owner's 70-degree climb threshold implies that 45-70 degree ground is walked, as in Daggerfall. Doom alone would block it, and it would not be climbable either.
- **Daggerfall rule vs owner design:** Daggerfall starts a ground climb after about 0.8 s. The owner's 1 s is used. Otherwise no conflict changed how climbing plays.

## Not done (out of scope or later)

- Climbing fatigue (Daggerfall charges 22 fatigue per game minute while climbing). Doom does not tell Daggerfall it is climbing.
- Fall damage after a slip. Doom does not do Daggerfall's fall damage at all today.
- Converter markers for stepped steep faces (`AngledCollision.cs` keeps faces over about 41 degrees as steps). A stepped face of 24-unit steps is walked, not climbed, as the owner's 24-unit rule says.
- Overhangs, eaves, and roofs get no special handling. Corners end the climb when the trace leaves the wall.
- The climb state across a treadmill window shift was not tested.
