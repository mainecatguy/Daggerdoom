## What was wrong (two separate causes)

1. **Ground texture.** The overworld grid painted every terrain triangle with one flat, `DFGROUND`, a summer grass tile
   baked into `overworld.pk3` by `gen_overworld.py --ground-from`. Town meshes (and the distant view of a town, the
   "LOD" mesh that covers a square of about 338 tiles around it) draw Daggerfall Unity's real tile map
   (`CityExport.AddTerrain`: dirt/grass/stone transitions, climate ground archive, +1 in winter). So in winter the
   mesh's square was snow and everything past its edge was green: the owner's hard straight seam.
2. **Light.** The mesh takes Daggerfall's daylight flat (`DD_WeatherMeshLight`, soft light 255). Doom's sectors take a
   light level (70..192 on a curve) and Doom's default software light mode darkens with distance, strongly at low
   levels. Snow lowers Daggerfall's daylight (`SnowSunlightScale` 0.45), so the light level dropped to ~126 and the
   green ground went near black a short way off. `before-out/winter-snow/high.png` shows it.

## Changes

- `dd_nature.cpp/.h`: the tile lookup now keeps Daggerfall Unity's rotate/flip bits (copied from `CityExport.BuildLookup`;
  `LayoutNature` already masked them off, so nature flats are unchanged). New `DDNature_Tiles` (the pixel's 128x128
  tile bytes) and `DDNature_GroundArchive` (`GetWorldClimateSettings` ground archive, +1 in winter outside desert base
  types, as `TerrainMaterialProvider.GetClimateInfo`).
- `dd_treadmill.cpp`: `RewriteGrid` gives each tile's two triangles `dfmesh/a<archive>r<record>.png` (already in
  `ddruntime.pk3`: all 56 records of 2/3/102/103/302/303/402/403) and a floor angle for the tile's rotation. The flat's
  192-unit repeat matches the tile grid and all four DFU transforms are quarter turns (0/270/180/90; derived from the
  texture matrix in `hw_flats.cpp` and checked visually: transitions join, `after-out/summer-noon/down.png`). Tiles are
  cached per map pixel (16 pixels). Heights come from a new `PixelHeights` shared with `NatureOf`. Without DDNATURE
  every pixel counts as woodland (as for nature flats). When Daggerfall's season changes on the overworld,
  `RefreshSeason` redraws the ground and respawns the nature flats in the window (logs `DDTM season ...`). After a
  save is resumed the ground is redrawn once (the save holds the old floors).
- `dd_weather.cpp`: in exteriors, every sector's floor gets absolute plane light 255 and a grey floor colour of
  Daggerfall's daylight (the mesh's value), so the ground no longer darkens with distance and matches the mesh's
  brightness at its edge. The sector light level (used by sprites, monsters, trees) keeps its old curve, so nothing
  but the floors looks different. Log line `DDWEATHER exterior light L ground G ...`.

Inactive elsewhere: the grid code runs only on DDOVER01 with the treadmill; the floor light only when Daggerfall's
weather line says exterior.

## Known gaps

- **Not compared against Daggerfall Unity's camera.** Ground brightness now equals the mesh's, which the day/night run
  matched to Daggerfall (ratios 0.91 to 1.12, `20261126-145333.md`), so it should be close, but no paired shot was taken.
- The snow sky line in the tool and demo is an estimate (ambient and sun scaled by 0.45/0.65 from Daggerfall's real
  winter noon line); a real snowy Daggerfall line was not captured.
- A town mesh (and the LOD view of a town) keeps the season it was loaded in (day/night gap). After a mid-game season
  change, or when the first season line arrives after the first place loaded, the overworld ground switches but the
  town's square does not until the next load: the seam can come back reversed for that visit.
- Overworld tile classes use DFU's exact beach jitter (`IndexedFloat`); the converter's town mesh uses a hash stand-in.
  They can differ on beach tiles only, so a coastline could show a small mismatch at a town square's edge.
- Monsters and nature flats still use Doom's sector light with distance darkening (deliberately unchanged): far trees
  look darker than the ground.
- Older overworld packages without DDNATURE get woodland ground everywhere (snow in winter, but the wrong set in
  deserts, mountains and swamps). The latest demos' `overworld.pk3` (e.g. `review\ammoname`) lacks DDNATURE;
  rebuild it with `gen_overworld.py --world-dir` pointing at a converter `--world` output.
