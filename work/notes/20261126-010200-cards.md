## Card 1: earn Skill XP from damage dealt to Doom monsters

**Goal.** Every point of damage the player deals to a hostile Doom monster becomes one point of Skill XP in a pool
that Daggerfall keeps and saves.

**Rules from the owner.**
- Monster kills award Skill XP, a separate resource spent in a menu to buy skill levels (2026-11-25).
- About 30 Doom monsters give the XP of one Daggerfall fight of 6 to 7 hits (2026-11-25).
- Owner's suggestion, not a rule: base Skill XP on enemy HP damage and keep the formula simple (2026-11-25). Damage
  as the basis: OWNER to confirm (the research recommends it).
- Rate: OWNER (A: 1 XP per HP everywhere, recommended; B: per-area-level rate; C: A plus an early-game softener).
- Townspeople and guards give XP: OWNER (lean: no).

**What to build.**
- Doom (Doomerfall mod or a small `dd_*.cpp` unit, inactive unless Daggerfall is bridged): count the HP the player
  removes from each hostile monster, capped at that monster's spawn health over its life. Player-caused only.
  Resurrected and summoned monsters count nothing.
- Report to Daggerfall in batches (about once a second and on map exit) as a new bridge kind, split by Doomerfall
  weapon category so card 3 can use it. Follow `DFB_KIND_CLIMB_USES` (`UZDoom/src/dd_climb.cpp`, `DoomerfallClimb.cs`).
- Daggerfall: a Skill XP pool on the player (whole number), saved with Daggerfall's save; older saves load with 0.
  One line on the character sheet.

**What must stay true.** No change to damage, AI, hit detection or monster health; counting only reads what
happened. DOOM.WAD E1M1, stock and with the DRLA set, behaves the same and sends nothing. XP is never lost or doubled
across save, load, death, area change or a map window swap.

**How to prove it.** In a paired game kill 3 zombiemen and an imp (120 XP), check the pool and log. Kill a monster an
Arch-vile then raises: no second award. Save, load, check the pool.

## Card 2: buy skill points with Skill XP (needs card 1)

**Goal.** A Daggerfall menu where the player spends Skill XP to raise skills one point at a time. Buying is the main
way skills rise.

**Rules from the owner.**
- Buying does not spend Daggerfall XP and is the main source of skill leveling. Costs escalate with skill level.
- Which skills can be bought: OWNER (lean: all but Critical Strike).
- Price multiplier: OWNER (lean: a flat 2 for every skill, not Daggerfall's per-skill use multipliers such as Running
  50, Medical 12, languages 15, which measure how often a use happens).
- Where the menu opens: OWNER (lean: anywhere the player could rest).

**What to build.**
- Price of the next point = `FormulaHelper.CalculateSkillUsesForAdvancement(skill, 2, Career.AdvancementMultiplier,
  level)` x 250 Skill XP, the 250 in one named constant.
- A window from the character sheet: skills, value, next price, pool. One point per click; greyed out when short or
  capped.
- A bought point is a normal permanent raise: `SetPermanentSkillValue`, `SetSkillRecentlyIncreased`,
  `SetCurrentLevelUpSkillSum`, `CheckForLevelUp`, as `RaiseSkills` does, with Daggerfall's mastery message at 100.
- Daggerfall's caps stay (100; 95 once a primary is at 100). No 6-hour gate on purchases. Accumulated uses are kept.
- New skills need an advancement multiplier first (an unknown skill returns 0 today).

**What must stay true.** Daggerfall's use-based raises, guild training and level rule are unchanged. 15 bought
counted points raise the level by one.

**How to prove it.** With test-flag XP, buy Long Blade 25 to 26 for a level-1 Warrior (4,000 XP); check pool, sheet,
and the level-up after 15 counted points. Save and load. Check a preset and a custom class.

## Card 3: Daggerfall's use-based gain as a slow side path

**Goal.** Daggerfall's normal skill advancement keeps working beside purchases at about 1/100 of its rate, with kill
skills scaled for Doom's monster counts.

**Rules from the owner.**
- Use-based gain stays where reasonable at about 1/100; kill skills about 1/30 (2026-11-25).
- Reading of "1/30": OWNER. (a) 1 use per 250 HP dealt with the weapon category (as fast as the pool); (b, lean)
  1 use per 25,000 HP (1/100 per Daggerfall-equivalent fight).
- Guild training at full rate: OWNER (lean: yes, it is paid, not use).

**What to build.**
- One scaling point for use tallies, with a fractional accumulator per skill saved with Daggerfall's save.
  `RaiseSkills`, its 6-hour gate and caps stay. Guild training (`DaggerfallGuildServiceTraining.cs:127`) and quest
  `TrainPc` (`TrainPc.cs:86`) also call `TallySkill`; they must bypass the scaling and stay at full rate (subject to
  the owner's training answer). So scale at the use callers (or route them through a scaled helper), not inside
  `TallySkill` itself.
- Non-combat skills x 1/100 (table in the research note), including Climbing, which tallies from Doom today.
- The nine monster language skills get no use-based gain in a bridged game: their only tally is Daggerfall enemy
  pacification (`EnemySenses.cs:504-525`), which no longer runs because Doom owns the monsters. This card does not
  fix that. What they get (buyable, hidden, or Doom telemetry) is OWNER, held in `work/backlog/revisit-languages.md`.
  Say so in the card's report.
- Combat skills: uses from card 1's damage by category, at the chosen rate.

**What must stay true.** Raises still happen only through `RaiseSkills`. Nothing changes in Doom's combat.

**How to prove it.** Log tallies for a lock pick attempt (0.01 use), a climb, and 25,000 HP of shotgun damage (one
Shotguns use under reading b). Train a skill at a guild and check it gets the full 10-20 x multiplier uses, unscaled.
Save and load keeps the fractions.
