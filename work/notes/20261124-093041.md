# Ammo material tiers: items, prices, weights, shops, and drops (run 20261124-093041)

Card `ammotiers/04`. Branch `agent/ammotiers-20261124-093041` (lane-items worktree), on top of cards 01 to 03.

## Plan

1. Daggerfall: one item per ammo type and tier, with the owner's names, prices, weights, stack sizes, tints, and an
   info text that states the bonus. Loaded ammo (card 03's `IsLoaded`) becomes "the type's loaded tier".
2. Doom: every loose ammo pickup on a Daggerfall map becomes one ammo drop of one rolled tier, with the owner's odds
   and jackpots. Touching a tiered pickup puts its rounds into Daggerfall's inventory, not Doom's counter.
3. Shops: weapon smiths (and general stores, Regular only) stock tiered ammo.
4. Prove it with the paired harness, a save and load, and the E1M1 checks.

## What was built

### Daggerfall (`DoomerfallAmmoTiers.cs`, `DoomerfallAmmo.cs`)
- Tier table in the owner's order: Regular (Iron), Steel, Silver, Moonstone (Elven), Dwemer (Dwarven), Adamantium,
  Orichalcum (Orcish), Mithril, Ebony, Daedric, with the owner's damage, price, weight, and stack multipliers.
- A stack's template index names its type and tier, so Daggerfall's own stacking and saving keep tiers apart:
  Regular keeps the plain-ammo templates 9101-9104 (old saves load as Regular), tier k of type t is 9200 + 10t + k.
  The item also sets `nativeMaterialValue` to the Daggerfall material.
- Names: "Steel bullets", "Regular shotgun shells", and so on. Price per round = base x tier multiple (Regular bullets 3,
  Daedric rockets 750). Weight per round = base x tier multiple (Mithril bullets 0.002 kg).
- Stack (rounds per pickup or vendor stack) = base x tier multiple, rounded half up, at least 1. **Base amounts are my
  call:** bullets 40, shells 10, rockets 4, cells 50. So a Regular pickup is 80 bullets, 20 shells, 8 rockets, or 100
  cells (Doom's small pickups are 10, 4, 1, 20), and a Daedric one is 10, 3, 1, or 13.
- Tint: the inventory picture (Doom's sprite) is recoloured along the material's metal dye ramp by brightness, as
  Daggerfall dyes metal items. The ramp is the darkest and lightest colour of `ImageProcessing.GetMetalColorTable`
  for that material in ART_PAL.COL (computed once, hard-coded).
- Info popup: a new `ICustomItemInfo` interface (two lines in `ItemHelper.cs`, marked `[Doomerfall]`). Ammo shows its
  name and count, "Silver bullets: +10% damage with every gun that fires bullets.", the weight and worth per round,
  and the pack size. Silver's creature bonus and the quiet tag are not mentioned (backlog).
- Loaded tier: per ammo type, Regular, not saved. Card 05 decides per gun or per type (open question
  [20261124-090831]) and saves it. `SetLoadedTier` + `Reload` already move the rounds through card 03's delta path.
  `LoadedMaterialBonus` now returns the loaded tier's bonus (Regular 0%, so play is unchanged until card 05).
- `DFB_KIND_AMMO_TAKE` (174): Doom reports a touched tiered pickup; Daggerfall adds the stack. A loaded-tier stack
  then reaches Doom through the existing delta path; other tiers stay only in Daggerfall.
- `DFB_KIND_AREA_LEVEL_REQUEST`/`AREA_LEVEL` (175/176): Doom asks for the area level of a map pixel (plus a dungeon
  flag); Daggerfall answers with the dungeon level inside a dungeon, else the terrain level, or `-dfarealevel`.
- Shop stock (`DaggerfallLoot.StockShopShelf`, one line): see "Shops" below.

### Doom (`mods/doomerfall/src/zscript/DoomerfallAmmo.zs`, `TRNSLATE`, `dd_items.cpp`, `dd_bridge.cpp`)
- `dd_items.cpp` keeps the area level of the player's map pixel (indoors, the door's pixel) and sends it to ZScript as
  `dfa_level:<n>` (-1 while unknown). `dfa_take:` from ZScript becomes `DFB_KIND_AMMO_TAKE`.
  `DoomerfallAmmoPickup` picks up by touch on Daggerfall maps.
- `DoomerfallAmmoTiers` (static event handler): on a Daggerfall map, every unowned stock or DRLA ammo pickup (Clip,
  ClipBox, Shell, ... RLClip, RLCellPack) is judged a tic after it spawns, so ammo given to the player is never
  touched. Once the area level is known it is replaced by one ammo drop:
  - 2% jackpot: 20 pickups (10 for rockets) of one town tier (Steel, Moonstone, Dwemer, Orichalcum, Mithril, equal
    odds) thrown around the spot.
  - else Daedric with the weapon formula, else Regular, Silver, Adamantium, Ebony with the owner's `falloff`.
  - The roll is a hash of the map name, the spot (16-unit grid), and the ammo type, so converter-placed pickups roll
    the same in every game. Monster drops roll from where the monster died.
- Each rolled pickup is its own class, `DFAmmo_<Clip|Shell|Rocket|Cell>_<tier>`, so the treadmill table (which keeps
  only class and amount) and saves keep the tier. Stock sprites (CLIP, SHEL, ROCK, CELL) with a `TRNSLATE` tint per
  tier from the same ramps. Outside a bridge session, touching one gives plain Doom ammo.

### Shops (my numbers)
- Weapon smiths, per ammo type and tier, percent chance at each restock for a quality-10 shop: Regular 85, Steel 50,
  Silver 45, Moonstone 35, Dwemer 35, Adamantium 12, Orichalcum 6 (50 in the Orsinium Area region), Mithril 25,
  Ebony 25, Daedric 10. Shop quality scales the chance by 0.55 (quality 1) to 1.5 (quality 20), at most 95.
  Regular comes in 2 to 4 vendor stacks, other tiers 1 or 2.
- General stores: Regular only, 60% per type. Other shops: none.
- Restocks use Daggerfall's unseeded random, like the rest of shop stock (open question [20261123-233755]).

## Engineering calls
- Daggerfall stays the record of tiers: Doom only rolls drops (with Daggerfall's area level) and forwards touches.
- Plain Doom ammo that still reaches Doom's counter (backpacks, a gun's own ammo, harness `ClipBox` touched in the
  same tic) becomes the loaded tier, as before.
- No touch pickup is refused for weight, as before (open question [20261124-075147]).

### Bugs found and fixed during the runs
- After a load straight into a dungeon, Doom does not know the outdoor map pixel, so the area level stayed unknown
  and new drops would never roll. Doom now asks for "where Daggerfall's player is" (key 2000000) in that case.
- Placed pickups rolled from where they lay when the area level arrived; a DRLA pickup still falling made one roll
  differ between runs. The roll now uses the spot where the pickup spawned.
- Doom item art was uploaded as non-readable textures, so tinting threw inside the loot window and broke card 02's
  take test. The art stays readable now (icons are at most 48 px), and tinting falls back to the plain picture.

Shop stock (`-dfshoptest`, `DoomerfallShopStockTest.cs`, one-line hook in `DoomerfallBridge.cs`, now part of
`ammo.py tiers`): Daggerfall's own `StockShopShelf` on a quality-20 weapon smith gave 19 tiered stacks, for example
`[Regular bullets x240 3 gold] [Silver bullets x80 7 gold] [Daedric bullets x20 75 gold] [Ebony rockets x4 450 gold]
[Mithril energy cells x25 200 gold]`; the general store got only Regular (`bullets x240, shells x80, rockets x16`).
That run's pickups and counts still MATCH.

Not covered by scripts: the tint's look (in Doom and in the inventory), the info popup text on screen, buying from a
shelf by hand, real monster drops, and Orsinium.

## Play-test list

   and Daggerfall's inventory shows separate tinted stacks ("Silver bullets", ...).

   Rarely, a drop bursts into 20 pickups (a jackpot).

7. Do the tints read well on Doom's sprites, especially Regular (iron is dark grey) and Adamantium?
