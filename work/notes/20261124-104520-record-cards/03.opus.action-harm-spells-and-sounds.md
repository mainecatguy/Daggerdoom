# Records 3: trap actions (harm, poison, drain, spells) and action sounds

Read D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall\AGENTS.md first. The survey is `work/notes/20261124-104520-record-types.md`, section 1.1.
This uses the trigger route built in card 2.

## Goal
Dungeon traps work. Action types 9 (cast spell), 21-25 (hurt), 26 (poison), 27 (unknown, 6 flats) and 28 (drain
magicka) apply Daggerfall's effect to the player when Doom triggers them. Today they only log `DFRULE`
(`tools/dungeon_converter/action_objects.zs:283-290`). Movers also play Daggerfall's own action sound.

## Where they are
- Type 9: 28 models and 28 flats, in about 850 dungeons (flats) and 294 (models).
- Types 21-25: 21 models and 33 flats, in up to about 1,000 dungeons.
- Type 26: 1 flat; type 28: 7 models in 336 dungeons.
- `SoundIndex` is set on 813 action models. Movers play one stock Doom sound (`action_objects.zs:269`).

## Approach
Daggerfall owns HP, status effects and spells, so Daggerfall applies the effect: Doom reports the trigger, DFU runs
the record's action against the player, and the HP and effect sync already in place carries the result back. Use
DFU's own damage rules (`level * magnitude` and so on), not new numbers. For sounds, play the Daggerfall sound the
record names; if Doom cannot play Daggerfall sounds yet, report that and stop at a plan.

## What must stay true
- Damage goes through the existing damage and HP path, so death rules still hold.
- A trap fires as often as DFU lets it (once, or on every touch).
- Movers keep their timing.

## How to prove it
- Paired session in The Tower of Caect'kern (it has types 9, 22, 23, 24, 25): trigger each trap, log HP before and
  after on both sides, compare with DFU's formula.
- Type 28 in Ruins of The F'igta Farmstead: magicka drops by the magnitude.
- A type 26 trap applies Daggerfall's poison effect, seen on the character sheet.
- Record which sound plays for three different `SoundIndex` values.
