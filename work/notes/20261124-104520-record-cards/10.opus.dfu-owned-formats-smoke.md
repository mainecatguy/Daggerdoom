# Records 10: smoke check of the formats Daggerfall Unity still reads

Read D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall\AGENTS.md first. The survey is `work/notes/20261124-104520-record-types.md`, section 1.4.

## Goal
Confirm that everything Daggerfall Unity reads itself and shows to the player still reaches the player through the
Doom window. This is a check that writes a table and fixes nothing large.

## What to check
For each, one real use in a paired session, with a log line or screenshot:
- `TEXT.RSC` messages (a rest prompt, a pop-up), books (read one from the inventory), `FLATS.CFG` names (Info on an
  NPC flat).
- `FACTION.TXT`, `SPELLS.STD`, `MAGIC.DEF`, item templates: the character sheet, spellbook and an artifact's info.
- `CLASS*.CFG`, `BIOG*.TXT`, `NAMEGEN.DAT`: one New Game with a custom class and the biography questions.
- `DAGGER.SND`, `MIDI.BSA`: does any Daggerfall sound effect or music play while Doom has the window? Record what is
  heard and from which program.
- `*.VID` and `*.FLC`: the intro video and a Daedra summoning video. Do they show, freeze, or skip?

## What must stay true
Nothing changes in game code unless a fix is one or two lines. Anything larger becomes a proposed card.

## How to prove it
The table itself, with the evidence per row. Sound, music and video results go on the play-test list, because only a
person can judge them.

## Working rules
- New lane branch from `integration` in a named worktree. Commit there and do not merge.
- Private `-config`, about 30-second timeouts, stop on a fatal error.
- Design questions go to the owner through the questions field. Whether Daggerfall's music should play at all in
  Doomerfall is one.

## When you finish
Write `work/notes/<task>.md` with the table, commands, and a play-test list.
