## Goal
Every editor marker kind (archive 199) does in Doomerfall what it does in Daggerfall Unity, or is skipped with a
written reason. Fix the two known bugs.

## Known problems
- **Passive fixed enemies.** 70 fixed-enemy markers (199:16) have `Action == 99`, which DFU reads as "passive"
  (`RDBLayout.cs:1539-1541`). They sit in Scourg Barrow and the Daggerfall, Sentinel and Wayrest castles, and most
  are Knights. The converter spawns a hostile random Doom monster there (`Program.cs:265-273`), and
  `ReadActionRecords` (`ActionExport.cs:402-410`) also exports the marker as a flag 99 action record. Remove the
  stray action record now. What spawns there waits on the owner (see below).
- **Mobile type 99.** One fixed marker in Wayrest has mobile type 99. DFU and the drop code skip it. The converter
  still spawns a monster. Skip it.
- **Quest markers.** 199:11 and 199:18 (284 in dungeons, 4,927 in buildings). Quest foes already use them in
  dungeons (`DoomerfallMonsters.cs:238`). Report, for one real quest of each kind, what happens to a quest person or
  quest item placed at a marker. Fixing it belongs to the quest cards; this card only reports.
- **Enemy marker fields not used:** random slot (`Flags`), underwater table (marker below the start marker's water
  line), and spawn distance (`SoundIndex`). List what DFU does with each and hand the list to the encounter cards.
  Do not build them here.
- **Unknown records** 199:2, 3, 17, 20: confirm DFU ignores them, and that the few 199:20 records carrying actions
  (spell, teleport) behave as DFU does.
