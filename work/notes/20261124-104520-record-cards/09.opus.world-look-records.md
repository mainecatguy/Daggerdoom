## Goal
The outdoors uses Daggerfall's world data for its look: the sky for the location's climate and time, the climate's
ground textures, and the wilderness nature flats Daggerfall scatters.

## What we know
- Sky: 32 `SKY*.DAT` files. Town and overworld maps use Doom's `F_SKY1`. The converter only prints the location's
  `SkyBase` (`CityExport.cs:94`). The converter already compiles DFU's `SkyFile.cs`.
- Climate: `CLIMATE.PAK` (10 values) picks ground and nature archives. Towns use it (`CityExport.cs:361, 682`). No
  use was found in `UZDoom/src/playsim/dd_treadmill.cpp`; check what the overworld ground and flats use.
- Wilderness nature: DFU scatters climate nature flats over terrain. No nature code was found in the treadmill.
  This survey did not look deeper; confirm before building.
- Castle blocks (start marker `Magnitude`, 5 blocks) are unused. DFU uses them for castle rules. Only report what
  DFU uses them for.
