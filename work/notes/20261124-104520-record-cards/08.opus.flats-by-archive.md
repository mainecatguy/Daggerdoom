Read D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall\AGENTS.md first. The survey is `work/notes/20261124-104520-record-types.md`, sections 1.1
and 1.2.

## Goal
Every flat (billboard) archive that appears in block data is drawn, hidden, scaled, animated and sounded as Daggerfall
Unity does, in dungeons, towns and interiors. This is mostly a check, with small fixes.

## What to check
- One table of every archive found in RDB flats, RMB exterior and misc flats, and RMB interior flats, with: what DFU
  does with it (`RDBLayout.AddFlat`, `RMBLayout`, `DaggerfallInterior.AddFlats`) and what each converter does
  (`FlatExport.cs`, `CityExport.cs:677-698`, `SiteExport.cs:140-151`).
- Interior flats with a non-zero `FactionID` are dropped by `SiteExport.cs:147`. Find what DFU does with them.
- NPC flats with an action (all 85 dungeon NPC flats have one, mostly dialogue): drawn once, not twice
  (`ActionExport.cs:330-339` skips the visual; confirm in a map).
- Animal flats (201, 12 in dungeons and 4,340 outdoors) have no sound; DFU gives them one (`RDBLayout.cs:349`).
  Add it if Daggerfall sounds can be played (see card 3), otherwise list it.
- Missing records: the converter prints `missing flat` lines. Collect them over all dungeons.
- Nature archives swapped by climate (`CityExport.cs:682-683`) match DFU's swap rule.

## What must stay true
- Flat counts per converted map change only where a fix says so.
- Billboard size stays as `BillboardScale` computes it (`FlatExport.cs:28-35`), already checked against DFU.

## How to prove it
- The archive table, produced by a converter listing mode, not by hand.
- Convert Direnni Tower (has the cat), Daggerfall city, and a temple interior. Count flats per archive before and
  after. Screenshot the same spots in DFU and Doom.
