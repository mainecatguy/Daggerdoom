# Records 6: lights in dungeons, interiors and towns

Read D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall\AGENTS.md first. The survey is `work/notes/20261124-104520-record-types.md`, sections 1.1
and 1.2.

## Goal
Places are lit where Daggerfall lights them, so the maps look like Daggerfall.

## What we know
- RDB light records: 4,268, a median of 129 per dungeon and a maximum of 319 (Wayrest castle). DFU makes a point
  light of each (`RDBLayout.cs:269-294`, `AddLight` at 1203). The converter never reads them, and every dungeon
  sector gets `lightlevel = 176` (`Program.cs:957, 1043`).
- Light sprites (archive 210): 4,957 in dungeons, 37,249 in interiors, 2,146 outdoors. They are drawn and animated,
  but give no light. DFU adds a point light to interior ones (`DaggerfallInterior.cs:909, 929`) and lights outdoor
  lamps at night. DFU also gives some of them a burning sound (`RDBLayout.cs:1258-1301`).
- The light record's fields other than `Radius` are unknown.

## Approach
Try the cheapest thing that looks right, and measure: GZDoom dynamic point lights from the converter (a GLDEFS or
spawned light actor per record), or baked sector light levels from the same data. Compare frame time against today
in the worst dungeon and a big interior. Torch sounds are a small follow-up in the same pass.

## Ask the owner first
If dynamic lights cost too much and only baked sector light fits, the look changes (no flicker, blocky light). Ask
before choosing baked light.

## What must stay true
- Frame time in Wayrest castle and Orsinium stays acceptable. Report numbers, minimized and normal.
- Outdoor lamps follow Daggerfall's time of day, not Doom's.

## How to prove it
- Screenshots of the same spots in DFU and Doom: Privateer's Hold, Wayrest castle, a tavern at night.
- Frame time before and after in the same spots.
