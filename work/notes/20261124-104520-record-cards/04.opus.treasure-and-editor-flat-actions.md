# Records 4: treasure piles and editor flats that carry actions

Read D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall\AGENTS.md first. The survey is `work/notes/20261124-104520-record-types.md`, section 1.1.
This builds on cards 2 and 3.

## Goal
A treasure pile or editor flat that starts an action chain in Daggerfall starts the same chain in Doom.

## What we know
- 19 random treasure markers (199:19) carry an action: 10 activate, 6 movers, and one each of teleport, poison and
  type 27. Their blocks are used by 867 dungeons. DFU parents the loot to the marker "to use actions on parent"
  (`RDBLayout.cs:392-400`), so the pile moves with the marker and taking it can fire the chain.
- In Doom, Use on a pile goes to `DoomerfallItems` and opens loot. The converter exports the marker's action record
  (`ActionExport.cs:402-410`) but nothing triggers it, and a moving marker's pile does not move in Doom.
- Editor 199:11, 199:17, 199:18 and 199:20 records also carry a few actions (spell, teleport, text, movers).

## Steps
1. Find out exactly what DFU does with these: what triggers the marker's action (opening the loot, clicking,
   walking), and whether the pile follows a moving marker.
2. Do the same in Doom: fire the action record on that trigger, and move the pile with its marker.

## What must stay true
- Opening, taking from and reopening piles works as today, including the item ledger across save and load.
- Piles without actions are untouched.

## How to prove it
- Convert Ruins of The F'igta Farmstead and The Tower of Thuze. List each treasure marker with an action.
- Paired session: take from one such pile, and see the chain fire on both sides' logs.
- A moving one: the pile's position in Doom tracks the marker.
