# Records 7: ladders and bookshelves in buildings

Read D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall\AGENTS.md first. The survey is `work/notes/20261124-104520-record-types.md`, section 1.2.

## Goal
Interior ladders can be climbed, and bookshelves in libraries, guild halls and temples open Daggerfall's book
window, as in Daggerfall Unity.

## What we know
- Ladders: model 41409 appears 1,744 times inside buildings and 12 times outside, with ladder bottom and top markers
  (199:21, 199:22, about 1,720 each). DFU adds `DaggerfallLadder` to the model (`DaggerfallInterior.cs:496`).
  Nothing in Doom names ladders. Doom climbing exists (`UZDoom/src/dd_climb.cpp`); first check whether it already
  gets the player up a ladder, because then only the markers or a hint may be missing.
- Bookshelves: DFU adds `DaggerfallBookshelf` to shelves in those building types
  (`DaggerfallInterior.cs:808-814`). The Info notes list shelves as unsupported
  (`work/notes/20261123-233755-infovanilla.md`). Use should ask Daggerfall to open its window, the way furniture
  containers already do through `DoomerfallItems`.

## What must stay true
- Containers, shop shelves and house containers keep working.
- Climbing rules stay Daggerfall's (skill rolls and fatigue as already built).

## How to prove it
- Find a building with a ladder to an upper floor (the converter can list them). Paired session: climb it.
- Paired session in a library: Use a shelf, the book window opens, reading a book works.
