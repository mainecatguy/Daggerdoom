# Backstabbing: damage to unaware enemies (20261127-183500)

Goal: hits on unaware enemies do more damage, x1 at Backstabbing 0 rising to x5 at 100, multiplying with the rest of
the damage bonus; each such hit tallies 10x the normal per-hit weapon tally of Backstabbing uses. No perks.

## Plan

1. Daggerfall tabulates the backstab element from its live Backstabbing skill and publishes it next to the damage bonus
   (`dfb_df_state.backstab_bonus_bp`). It cannot go into the single `damage_bonus_bp`, because only Doom knows, hit by
   hit, whether the enemy is unaware.
2. Doom decides "unaware" in one function (`DD_BackstabUnaware`, `UZDoom/src/dd_backstab.cpp`) and, in the existing
   damage bonus hook (`DD_BridgeDamageBonus`), multiplies the backstab element in on those hits.
3. Doom reports sneak hits (`DFB_KIND_SNEAK_HITS`, 168); Daggerfall calls its own `TallySkill(Backstabbing, 10 x hits)`.
4. Test component `-dfbackstabtest <file> <skill>`, harness + runner `tools/backstab/`, runs at skill 0 / 50 / 100.
5. Manual, demo, E1M1 checks.

## Design reading and engineering calls

- **Multiplier:** `1 + 4 x skill / 100`, the live skill (Daggerfall's backstab chance also uses the live value)
  clamped to 0..100. x1, x3, x5 at 0, 50, 100. It is an element of `DoomerfallDamageBonus` and multiplies with the
  ammo and weapon material elements (`gamedesign/damage.md`): damage x (1 + damage bonus) x backstab.
- **"Normal" per-hit tally:** Daggerfall Unity's `WeaponManager` tallies the weapon skill 1 use per melee hit that
  lands, so a sneak hit tallies 10 Backstabbing uses. This is Daggerfall's normal rate, used until the use-based rates
  rework (`skillresearch` 02) is approved, as the card says. (For reference, Daggerfall itself tallies Backstabbing 1 use
  per attack from behind in `FormulaHelper.CalculateBackstabChance`, whether or not it hits.)
- **Unaware (one place, `DD_BackstabUnaware`), the card's "no target, not alerted":** a monster (`ISMONSTER`), alive,
  not friendly to the player, whose `target` is empty or dead (a monster targeting another monster has noticed someone),
  and that is not alerted: it has heard nobody (`LastHeard` empty or dead), **except the noise of the attack now hitting
  it.** Why the exception: `PlayerPawn.FireWeapon` calls `SoundAlert` when the trigger is pulled, which sets `LastHeard`
  on every monster in earshot before the weapon's attack frame fires its bullets, so without it nearly every gunshot
  would hit an "alerted" monster. Any noise from the player before the current attack counts as alerted, however long
  ago (an ambush monster that heard earlier gunfire and never saw the player stays alerted).
  How Doom tells the two apart:
  - **An attack** is a span while the player's ready weapon is out of its ready state: from the tic it leaves
    `A_WeaponReady` (to fire, wind up, refire, reload) until it is ready again. Setting any weapon state clears
    `WF_WEAPONREADY` and `A_WeaponReady` sets it again (the same signal `dd_items.cpp` already uses). It holds all the
    attack's own noise: a wind-up's alert and the firing alert (DRLA's Assault Minigun alerts at `FireWindUp` and again
    at `FireStart`, about 20 tics apart), and every refire of a held burst. The weapon is sampled at the start of each
    player tic (`DD_BackstabTic`, called from the existing per-tic `DD_BridgeHpThink` in `dd_bridge.cpp`, no new vendored
    hook) and again live at each noise and each hit, for weapons that fire on their first frame.
  - A one-line hook in `NoiseMarkSector` (`p_enemy.cpp`, `DD_BackstabHeard`, inactive without a bridge session)
    records when each monster *started* hearing the player (its `LastHeard` became the player).
  - A monster that heard the player is still unaware only if it started hearing during the attack whose hit this is,
    i.e. at or after that attack's start. The hit belongs to the attack under way when it left the player: now for an
    instant hit, or the projectile's `SpawnTime` (the inflictor, passed into `DD_BridgeDamageBonus`), so flight time
    does not matter. A hit that comes from no attack (weapon ready) excuses no noise. A monster that heard a monster's
    noise aimed at someone else is alerted.
  (Verifier reviews: the first version ignored `LastHeard`; the second matched noise to attacks by time, taking the
  newest alert within 1 s, which judged a wind-up weapon's first alert as an earlier noise and could excuse an earlier
  shot's noise for a silent attack. Both fixed by the attack spans.)
- **One attack = one hit:** a shotgun's pellets, or several bullets of one burst landing in one tic on the same enemy,
  are one sneak hit. The first pellet makes the enemy target the player (`P_DamageMobj`), so without this only the first
  pellet would get the bonus. Doom remembers every enemy hit unaware in the current tic (a small list cleared when the
  tic changes); later hits in that tic on any of them get the bonus too and are not counted again, in any order across
  enemies (A, B, A). A blast that catches three unaware enemies is three sneak hits. (Verifier review: the first version
  remembered only the last enemy, so pellets landing A, B, A lost the bonus on A's second pellet; fixed and tested.)
- Sneak hits count even at skill 0 (x1), so the skill can start rising from 0.
- Townspeople and guards are Doom actors too; the test is only `ISMONSTER` and not friendly, as for any enemy.

## Known gaps

- A monster that hears your gunshot and runs its next look before the bullet lands takes a target and is aware; with
  slow-firing guns that will happen sometimes.
- A held burst is one attack, so a monster that first heard the burst's first shots and still has no target (it cannot
  see the player) gets the bonus on its first hit, however long the trigger was held. A reload between shots also
  keeps the weapon out of its ready state, so it joins the shots on both sides into one attack.
- Projectiles still flying after the player's attack ended belong to the attack they were fired in (by spawn time),
  but only the last 8 attacks are remembered.
- Hearing is recorded only while a bridge session runs; a monster that heard the player through another route that
  sets `LastHeard` directly (ACS, a few ZScript actors) counts as alerted. The detection bar is meant to refine this,
  in `DD_BackstabUnaware`.
- A sneak hit is counted where the bonus is applied, before the target's own damage modifiers, so a hit the target
  then shrugs off completely still tallies.
- Projectile pellets that land in different tics on the same enemy only get the bonus on the first; hitscan pellets and
  blasts land together.
- Use-based rates rework (`skillresearch` 02) not applied: Daggerfall's 1 use per hit is the "normal" base, as the card
  says.
- No perks, as the card says.

## Verifier round 4 (attack merging, long holds): fix built, real back-to-back test unfinished

- Attack start is now the last moment the weapon was ready at or before the noise or hit. A one-line hook in
  `DPSprite::SetState` (`p_pspr.cpp`, `DD_BackstabWeaponReady`, inactive without a session) notes "ready until now"
  whenever a weapon state change clears a still-set WF_WEAPONREADY. Every way out of the ready state passes there, so a
  shot fired on the very first ready frame, a queued weapon switch, and a reload begun from ready each start a new
  attack. The per-tic sample and the samples at noise and hit remain.
- Only the opening noise is excused: a monster that heard the player is unaware only if it first heard at or after the
  attack's start and last heard no later than 35 tics after it. Noise later in a long burst, or after a mid-burst
  reload, makes it alerted. Manual updated.
- Run at skill 100 after the change: all scripted hearing steps (silentafter, heardtwice, ownnoise, ownflight, windup,
  noattack), spread, the real starting gun, and the DRLA minigun (1 sneak hit from its wind-up burst) pass; 10 sneak hits
