# Backstabbing: damage to unaware enemies (20261127-183500)

Goal: hits on unaware enemies do more damage, x1 at Backstabbing 0 rising to x5 at 100, multiplying with the rest of
the damage bonus; each such hit tallies 10x the normal per-hit weapon tally of Backstabbing uses. No perks.

## Plan

1. Daggerfall tabulates the backstab element from its live Backstabbing skill and publishes it next to the damage bonus
   (`dfb_df_state.backstab_bonus_bp`). It cannot go into the single `damage_bonus_bp`, because only Doom knows, hit by
   hit, whether the enemy is unaware.
2. Doom decides "unaware" in one function (`DD_BackstabUnaware`, `UZDoom/src/dd_backstab.cpp`) and, in the existing
   damage bonus hook (`DD_BridgeDamageBonus`), multiplies the backstab element in on those hits.
3. Doom reports sneak hits (`DFB_KIND_SNEAK_HITS`, 168); Daggerfall calls its own `TallySkill(Backstabbing, 10 x hits)`.
4. Test component `-dfbackstabtest <file> <skill>`, harness + runner `tools/backstab/`, runs at skill 0 / 50 / 100.
5. Manual, demo, E1M1 checks.

## Design reading and engineering calls

- **Multiplier:** `1 + 4 x skill / 100`, the live skill (Daggerfall's backstab chance also uses the live value)
  clamped to 0..100. x1, x3, x5 at 0, 50, 100. It is an element of `DoomerfallDamageBonus` and multiplies with the
  ammo and weapon material elements (`gamedesign/damage.md`): damage x (1 + damage bonus) x backstab.
- **"Normal" per-hit tally:** Daggerfall Unity's `WeaponManager` tallies the weapon skill 1 use per melee hit that
  lands, so a sneak hit tallies 10 Backstabbing uses. This is Daggerfall's normal rate, used until the use-based rates
  rework (`skillresearch` 02) is approved, as the card says. (For reference, Daggerfall itself tallies Backstabbing 1 use
  per attack from behind in `FormulaHelper.CalculateBackstabChance`, whether or not it hits.)
- **Unaware (one place, `DD_BackstabUnaware`), the card's "no target, not alerted":** a monster (`ISMONSTER`), alive,
  not friendly to the player, whose `target` is empty or dead (a monster targeting another monster has noticed someone),
  and that is not alerted: it has heard nobody (`LastHeard` empty or dead), **except the noise of the attack now hitting
  it.** Why the exception: `PlayerPawn.FireWeapon` calls `SoundAlert` when the trigger is pulled, which sets `LastHeard`
  on every monster in earshot before the weapon's attack frame fires its bullets, so without it nearly every gunshot
  would hit an "alerted" monster. Any noise from the player before the current attack counts as alerted, however long
  ago (an ambush monster that heard earlier gunfire and never saw the player stays alerted).
  How Doom tells the two apart: a one-line hook in `NoiseMarkSector` (`p_enemy.cpp`, `DD_BackstabHeard`, inactive
  without a bridge session) records when each monster *started* hearing the player (when its `LastHeard` became the
  player) and the tics of the player's own alerts. The current attack's own noise is the player's newest alert at or
  before the attack began and at most 35 tics (1 s) before it; the attack began now for an instant hit, or at the
  projectile's `SpawnTime` (the inflictor, now passed into `DD_BridgeDamageBonus`). A monster that heard the player is
  still unaware only if it first heard the player at that alert or later. A monster that heard a monster's noise aimed
  at someone else is alerted. (Verifier review: the first version ignored `LastHeard` entirely; fixed.)
- **One attack = one hit:** a shotgun's pellets, or several bullets of one burst landing in one tic on the same enemy,
  are one sneak hit. The first pellet makes the enemy target the player (`P_DamageMobj`), so without this only the first
  pellet would get the bonus. Doom remembers the last enemy hit unaware and the tic; later hits in that tic on that enemy
  get the bonus too and are not counted again. A blast that catches three unaware enemies is three sneak hits.
- Sneak hits count even at skill 0 (x1), so the skill can start rising from 0.
- Townspeople and guards are Doom actors too; the test is only `ISMONSTER` and not friendly, as for any enemy.

## Results

Builds: UZDoom (`D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-build\uzdoom-shoplvl`), bridge DLL (`bridge-shoplvl`), launcher
(`launcher-shoplvl`), Daggerfall Unity (`CliBuild result: Succeeded`). The bridge schema hash changed (new event kind,
new `dfb_df_state` field), so all four must ship together.

Paired runs: `python tools/backstab/backstab.py run 0 50 100` (DRLA, test town DDT07154, private desktop). The harness
spawns a stock ZombieMan (1,000,000 health) 160 units in front of the player, facing away, and hits it as the player
(`DamageMobj`, base 100) one step per 10 tics. Before each "unaware" step it puts the monster back idle on its spot with
no target, nothing heard and no `lastenemy`. Then a real `LineAttack` hitscan (base 20), then the runner holds `+attack`
for 0.4 s with the player's real DRLA gun aimed at it.

```
skill   unaware  alerted  ratio   volley(3 hits)  infight  hitscan  sneak hits sent  Backstabbing uses tallied
0       100      100      x1.000  300             100      x1.000   6                60
50      300      100      x3.000  900             100      x3.000   6                60
100     500      100      x5.000  1500            100      x5.000   6                60
50 + -dfdamagetest 2 (run 50 --bonus 2): 600 / 200 = x3.000, volley 1800, hitscan 120 (20 x 2 x 3), 6 sent, 60 uses

hearing steps (monster held in its idle frame, no target, LastHeard = player; base 100):
skill   heardbefore  heardtwice  ownnoise  ownflight
0       100          100         100       100
50      100          100         300       300
100     100          100         500       500
RESULT PASS (all four)
```

- heardbefore: the player's noise 50 tics before the hit: alerted, no bonus. heardtwice: noise 50 tics before, then the
  attack's own noise in the hit's tic: still alerted. ownnoise: only the attack's own noise, same tic: bonus.
  ownflight: only the attack's own noise, 6 tics before the hit: bonus.

- "alerted" (target = player) and "infight" (target = another monster) get no backstab bonus at any skill.
- The volley (three hits in one tic) gets the bonus on all three and counts as one sneak hit.
- The real gun: its first bullet landed as a sneak hit (uz.log at skill 100: `Zombieman hit by the player, 12 -> 60
  (0 bp, backstab x5)`), the rest of the burst normally (the monster had woken). So 6 sneak hits per run: unaware,
  volley, hitscan, ownnoise, ownflight, and the real gun's first bullet. The gun's bullet goes through the real
  FireWeapon noise path: the monster heard that shot's own alert and still counted as unaware.
- df.log: `damage bonus: publishing backstab 40000 bp (x5 on unaware enemies)` and one `backstab: 1 sneak hits, tallied
  10 Backstabbing uses` per sneak hit; the test log's tally total equals 10 x Doom's sent count exactly.
- With `-dfdamagetest 2` (the rest of the damage bonus forced to x2) the backstab element multiplies with it: 100 x 2 x 3.
- Skill 0 still counts sneak hits (x1 damage, 10 uses each), so the skill can rise from 0. (The first build skipped the
  backstab path when its bp was 0; caught in review before any run and fixed.)

E1M1 (`backstab.py check`, DOOM.WAD, no bridge): stock exit 0, 0 script errors; DRLA exit 0, 3 script errors, the same
3 DRLA-owned errors as the running and skillbasics baselines. Nothing in `dd_backstab.cpp` runs without a bridge session.

Player.log has the known pre-existing `NullReferenceException` in `DaggerfallUI.SetupSingleton` at startup.

### Failed approaches (harness only)

- The first reset only cleared `target`: the monster stayed in its chase state, and a chasing monster with no target
  looks all around at once, so the real-gun phase hit an aware monster. Fixed by putting it back in its spawn state.
- Then it still woke during that spawn-state `A_Look`: the infight step had left `lastenemy` set. The harness now clears
  it. Both were test setup issues; the game code did not change for them.

Demo: `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\review\backstab\play.bat` (Backstabbing set to 100 by the test flag; sneak hits and
uses go to `play\backstab.log` and `play\uz.log`, nothing on screen).

## Known gaps

- A monster that hears your gunshot and runs its next look before the bullet lands takes a target and is aware; with
  slow-firing guns that will happen sometimes. An attack whose hit lands more than 1 s after its own noise (a slow,
  long-range projectile) is judged against no noise of its own, so a monster that heard that shot counts as alerted.
- Hearing is recorded only while a bridge session runs; a monster that heard the player through another route that
  sets `LastHeard` directly (ACS, a few ZScript actors) counts as alerted. The detection bar is meant to refine this,
  in `DD_BackstabUnaware`.
- A sneak hit is counted where the bonus is applied, before the target's own damage modifiers, so a hit the target
  then shrugs off completely still tallies.
- Projectile pellets that land in different tics on the same enemy only get the bonus on the first; hitscan pellets and
  blasts land together.
- Use-based rates rework (`skillresearch` 02) not applied: Daggerfall's 1 use per hit is the "normal" base, as the card
  says.
- No perks, as the card says.
