# Pick up Daggerfall items from inside Doom

## Result

Pressing Interact in Doom on a Daggerfall treasure pile moves its items into the real Daggerfall inventory. Proven with both real games in Privateer's Hold. Demo: `D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall-loop\review\item-pickup\play.bat`. It starts at the dungeon door. Press Use to enter, look at a pile, and press E.

## Design

- Daggerfall is the authority. `DoomerfallItems.cs` lists the real `DaggerfallLoot` piles in the current dungeon or building and sends them over the bridge (kinds 40-45). Doom only draws that list.
- Interact is `netevent df_interact`, a normal bindable entry in Doomerfall Controls. Doom picks the pile under the crosshair within Daggerfall's treasure reach (96 Doom units) and asks. Daggerfall checks distance and weight, moves the items, and answers.
- Pickup follows `DaggerfallInventoryWindow.TransferItem`: summoned items stay, maps are read and consumed, gold goes to the gold count, quest items keep their state, and stacks split at the weight limit.
- A saved ledger (`DoomerfallItems.json`, inside the save bundle) records each touched pile and what is left in it. It is required: Daggerfall Unity rebuilds and rerolls a dungeon on every entry, so without it taken loot comes back.
- Pile identity is the LoadID plus position, because a repeated dungeon block repeats LoadIDs.
- A pile is remembered for the whole visit. Pile objects were seen briefly leaving the dungeon hierarchy while intact, which made them flicker out of the list. I did not find what moves them. The roster makes the list independent of it.

## Item sources

| Source | State |
|---|---|
| Dungeon treasure piles (fixed and random) | Works, proven |
| Tavern, Thieves Guild, Dark Brotherhood treasure piles | Same code path, not run |
| Piles the player drops from the inventory, indoors | Listed by the same code, not run |
| Piles dropped outdoors, any exterior item | Not shown. Items are safe in Daggerfall but cannot be reached from Doom |
| Single placed quest items | Not handled |
| Shop shelves, house containers, corpses | Left to the containers task. Add their types in `DoomerfallItems.Collect` |

Items dropped inside a dungeon still vanish on leaving it, as in Daggerfall Unity. The ledger protects only piles that belong to the dungeon.

## Evidence (final build)

`python tools/item_pickup/run_pickup.py <name> [--load] [--weight]`, both games, minimized:

- Fresh: 8 piles listed, Interact took a pile (52 units, including a map and gold), 7 left, still gone after leaving and re-entering.
- Loaded from the save: ledger restored, pile absent, still gone after another trip out and back.
- Weight: at the limit, status too heavy and nothing moved. With room for 10 of 21 gold, 10 moved and 11 stayed. Gold total conserved, and the 11 were restored after re-entry.
- `item_pickup.py check`: stock E1M1 clean. DRLA E1M1 has the same 3 script errors and 763 unknown-class lines as the previous build. Plain Daggerfall shows no bridge activity and no exceptions.
- Registry hash unchanged across all runs. `playcheck` launches the exact demo setup at the door.

Not checked: the sprite's exact on-screen placement. The screenshot is too small to judge, and the log only confirms the right texture and scale. Save and load were proven with one pile, not with every item type.

The `NullReferenceException` in `Player.log` comes from `DaggerfallUI.SetupSingleton` at startup. It also appears in the earlier inventory demo.

## Failed approaches

- Test harness used names differing only by case (ZScript is case-insensitive).
- Group 9183's dungeon door is not Privateer's Hold. Group 7154's is.
- `-loadgame` at startup froze Doom's map time with Daggerfall outside the dungeon. The supported `-dfloadtest resume:` path works. I did not investigate the `-loadgame` freeze.

## Play-test list

1. Do piles sit on the floor at a believable size and centered?
2. Does the Interact aim (about 25 degrees around the crosshair) feel right, especially with piles close together?
3. Take a pile, open the inventory (I), and confirm the items are there.
4. Drop an item indoors with the inventory, then pick it back up.
5. Try a tavern's treasure pile.
6. Save, take a pile, load, and confirm the pile is back and the items are gone from the inventory.
