# Doors block like Daggerfall doors (20261123-130741)

## How Daggerfall's doors really block

From `DaggerfallActionDoor.cs`, its four prefabs, and `ProjectSettings/DynamicsManager.asset`.

| Door state | Bodies (movement) | Rays and missiles |
|---|---|---|
| Closed | Solid | Hit |
| Opening | Not solid, from the first frame (`Open` calls `MakeTrigger(true)`) | Hit |
| Fully open | Not solid | Hit |
| Closing | Not solid for the whole swing | Hit |
| Finished closing | Solid (`OnCompleteClose` calls `MakeTrigger(false)`) | Hit |

- All four door prefabs set `IsTriggerWhenOpen: 1`, so no stock door differs from the default. A mod's custom prefab could.
- A trigger collider still answers ordinary ray queries because `m_QueriesHitTriggers` is 1. So missiles (`DaggerfallMissile.cs:337-348`) and most melee rays still stop at an open door.
- A door never waits for a body in Daggerfall, because it is not solid while it moves.
- `DaggerfallActionDoorSpecial` follows the same trigger rule.
- **City gates are different.** `DaggerfallCityGate` swaps the whole model and has no trigger logic, so an open gate's leaves stay solid.

## What changed in Doom

- `door_objects.zs`: a door's links stop being solid when it starts opening and turn solid when it finishes closing. They stay shootable, so hitscans and projectiles still stop at the door in every state.
- Doors now swing through bodies instead of waiting for them.
- City gate leaves set `solidWhenOpen` (`CityExport.cs`) and behave exactly as before, including waiting for bodies.
- Appearance, swing path, timing, and sight blocking are unchanged.

**One engineering choice differs from Daggerfall.** If a body is in the doorway when a door finishes closing, Daggerfall makes the door solid and its character controller pushes the body out. Doom has no such push, and a body inside a row of solid links can get stuck. So the door looks closed but stays passable until no player or monster overlaps it, then turns solid.

## The test

- The Daggerfall side of `parity.py` already treated open doors as absent, so it needed no change. The earlier run's solid open-door boxes were not reused.
- `parity.py` now stops unless Doom reports every link solid when closed, and every link moved and non-solid when open.
- `door_check` now tests the new rule on all 58 doors: solid in each of the 5 states, walking into an open panel, swinging through a body, closing on a body, and turning solid after it leaves.
- `action_regress.py` now fails on the real player and Imp results, which it previously did not read.
- The other-floors check moved to the closed state, because an open door can no longer block anything.

|---|---:|---:|

Closed-door rows are identical before and after in every column: standing 1,865 / 569, crouching 1,176 / 427.

Scourg Barrow's closed-door rows are also identical.

### Why false openings rose

On Privateer's Hold no false opening went away, and 40 standing (42 crouching) were added. About half are within 16 units of a door panel. The rest are farther away, up to 658 units. The likely explanation is that Doom's flood can now pass an open door and reach spots that were already wrongly open, but I did not inspect them one by one. These are candidates for the separate collision work, and were not changed here.

## Other checks run

- `action_regress.py`: stock E1M1, E1M1 with DRLA, E1M1 beside the door package, all 58 Privateer's Hold doors, real player and Imp, and Scourg Barrow save and load all pass.
- A door saved while open loads open and non-solid, then closes and turns solid (private harness).
- A real City of Daggerfall export: all 8 gate leaves opened, and all 48 links stayed solid (private harness).
- The packaged demo launches and places the player at door 0. Its input was not played.

## Failed approaches

- The first door check reported a real Imp as not held. The Imp had woken and walked out of the doorway. It is now deactivated for the test.

## What remains

- Monster sight differs. Daggerfall's sight ray stops at an open door's panel, while Doom's open door clears sight. This was left alone because the goal is movement.
- `DaggerfallActionDoorSpecial` objects are not `DFDoor` objects here, so this change does not touch them. I did not audit how the action system handles their collision.
- The 877 standing false walls and 1,263 false openings on Privateer's Hold are not door state problems, and are left for the collision work.
