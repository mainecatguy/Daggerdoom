# Explosives perks (skillperks 03), run 20261127-024745

## Plan
1. Follow the Automatics pattern: Daggerfall decides the rules from the live Explosives skill and sends them in the
   character sheet; Doom applies them to shots from guns whose skill is Explosives.
2. Sheet: `DFB_SHEET_EXPL_*` 162-166 (damage bp, own splash cut bp, direct-hit bp, chain bp, chain radius), sheet 167
   ints, format 8. `DoomerfallExplosivesRules.cs` fills them; `DoomerfallPerks.cs` marks 25/75/100 built.
3. Doom: `mods/doomerfall/src/zscript/DoomerfallExplosives.zs`.
4. Tests: fake-peer (stock and DRLA), paired run with a pack, startup checks. Manual, demo.

## What was built
- **Damage +0-60%** on every hit from the gun's shots (direct and splash) to others.
- **25 / 75: own splash cut 95% / 99%** (passive `ModifyDamage` on the player, `DMG_EXPLOSION` from the player's own
  tagged shot). Fractions round with their chance, and may round to 0.
- **50: direct-hit bonus** (impact damage only, not the splash). The amount is open, so Daggerfall sends 0 and the
  perk window still says it does not work yet. The mechanism is there and tested with a test value (+50%).
- **100: Inpulsa's Broken Heart.** A monster (`bIsMonster`) killed by the gun's shots queues a blast of
  `SpawnHealth * 25% / 2^generation` at its centre; a monster killed by a blast queues the next generation. Blasts use
  `RadiusAttack(player, damage, 128, 'None', 0)`: Doom's normal falloff and sight checks, no `RADF_HURTSOURCE`, so never
  the player; the kill is the player's. At most 16 blasts go off per tic; the rest wait for the next tic. Each blast
  shows a rocket explosion and plays its sound (`DoomerfallChainBlast`). Blasts get no damage bonus.
- Which shots count: a projectile fired next to the player with an Explosives gun held (tagged at
  `WorldThingSpawned`), one that exploded point blank in its own spawn check (recognised at the hit: new, the player's,
  next to them, gun held), a player-owned projectile spawned within 128 of a tagged shot (cluster fragments), and a
  hitscan puff while an Explosives gun is held. Tags survive weapon switches; barrels and other guns get nothing.
- Engineering picks within the owner's design (say if you want otherwise): chain radius 128 (a rocket's); falloff
  like any Doom explosion (25% is the centre value); 16 blasts per tic; blasts not boosted by the +60%; "enemy" read as
  any monster, so townspeople and guards near a chain get hurt too (they are monsters in Doom).

## Failed approaches / problems
- The first paired runs could not attach (bridge -5): the launcher has the bridge schema built in, so the sheet
  change needs a launcher rebuild (done, copied to the demo).
- DRLA hands the player random armor; it absorbed or added to the cut splash. The fake-peer harness strips it.
- DRLA spawns monsters through spawner actors, and the city's ground slopes: the harness collects the real monsters a
  few tics later and puts probes and spawns on the ground.
- Wall time between tics on the private desktop is not real (minimized, Daggerfall running beside it): it showed
  300-900 ms gaps even before any shot, so the frame-rate check uses playsim time per tic (WorldTick to a thinker that
  ticks after all actors) and reports wall time only.
- **Open:** in the last three paired runs UZDoom exited without an error a second or two into the first skill-100 shot
  cycle, right after the three packs (an earlier run with one pack did complete a skill-100 cycle). No fatal error,
  crash file or log line; the last lines are a normal chain blast. So the "cycles at every step" check fails on 100.
  The skill-100 rules were read correctly (`dmg=6000 cut=9900 direct=0 chain=2500 radius=128`). Not found yet: could
  be the harness, Daggerfall's HP (an encounter demon had bitten the player to 18), or a real crash. Worth one look
  before release.
