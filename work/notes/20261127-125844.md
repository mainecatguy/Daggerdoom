  36e6ace4/1fcc4a12) at `Assets/Scripts/Doomerfall/daggerfall-unity-master/Logs/cli-build.log`. The "Succeeded" seen
  was the old log in the real Logs folder. Removed the stray folder; this run builds with absolute paths.
  (36e6ace4 also had a compile error, `items.PlaceKey()` on a static method, fixed in 1fcc4a12 before this run.)
- Fresh build: `CliBuild result: Succeeded` (`daggerfall-unity-master/Logs/cli-build.log`), and the built
  `Assembly-CSharp.dll` contains this run's new log strings, so the runs below used the current code.

## Results (fresh build, DRLA, private desktop)
`python tools/shop_levels/shelf_run.py run ledger --ledger --rates` (log `review/shop-shelf-levels/run/ledger/df.log`).
Simulated clock, 180 days at a level-30, quality-20 weapon smith with 3 shelves (stock target 75):

| Price band | sold off | mean days on shelf | still there at day 180 |

| under 100 gold | 290 | 2.7 | 2 |

| 1000+ gold (not Daedric) | 135 | 24.4 | 20 |

- PASS unsold items persist until sold off (no removal path other than the daily sell-off; nothing is cleared).
- PASS expensive items linger far longer than cheap ones (2.7 days vs 24-37 days).
- PASS player-sold steel longsword (90 gold, 26%/day): on a shelf the day it was sold (day 30), sold off day 34.
- PASS two runs give identical stock (386,537-character per-day signature of every item).
- **Daedric at a top weapon smith** (60 shops, 3 shelves each, approved odds at the shop level):
  - Level 30: Daedric item on first stocking in 73.3% of shops; after 60 days of the ledger, on **100% of shop-days**.
  - Level 20: 43.3% on first stocking; 91.9% of shop-days after 60 days.
  - So finding one at a top smith is more than "rather feasible": Daedric goods linger ~37 days while deliveries keep
    coming. Tuning knobs if too generous: `SellChance` floor/curve or `StockTarget` in `DoomerfallShopLedger.cs`.
- **Fix-first (approved material odds):** weapons on first stocking are 4.78% Daedric at level 30 (approved 5.00%,
  82 of 1716) and 2.76% at level 20 (approved 2.50%, 37 of 1340): matches within sampling. One fresh delivery per
  shelf (`--rates`, 1,000 shelves): level-30 weapon smith shelf has a Daedric item 45.6% of the time (card 02 measured
  50.0%), level 20 27.8%. Armor: Daggerfall's `RandomArmorMaterial` first picks leather 70% / chain 20% / plate 10%,
  and only plate takes a material, so plate is 4.76% Daedric at level 30 (approved 5.00%) but all armor only 0.51%.
  Enemy armor drops go through the same path. Left as is; asked the owner.
- In-game route (`shelf_run.py run sell`, log `review/shop-shelf-levels/run/sell/df.log`): in the test town's general
  store each real shelf restocked through the ledger and was recorded into the Doom item ledger (`shelf 2 of 3
  restocked from 0 to 11 items (recorded)`, likewise shelves 0 and 1); the harness bought one item, then sold one to
  the shopkeeper: `1 sold items put on the shelves; shelf item counts now 11 (recorded) 10 (recorded) 10 (recorded)`.
- Persistence: re-entry, save and load restore shelves from the Doom item ledger entry (`ApplyLedger`), which is the
  same entry `WindowClosed` writes for a looted container. The run shows ledger restocks and sales now write it.
  A leave/re-enter/save/load trip in a shop was **not** run: no harness can walk out of a town building yet. Play-test item.
