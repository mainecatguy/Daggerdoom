## Results (fresh build, DRLA, private desktop)
`python tools/shop_levels/shelf_run.py run ledger --ledger --rates` (log `review/shop-shelf-levels/run/ledger/df.log`).
Simulated clock, 180 days at a level-30, quality-20 weapon smith with 3 shelves (stock target 75):

- PASS unsold items persist until sold off. Added after verifier round 2: before each day's restock the test replays
  that day's seeded sell-off rolls (same seed and roll order as the ledger) on yesterday's shelves and checks the result
  after the restock. Log line: `PASS unsold items persist: 12457 item-days whose replayed sell-off roll failed, 0 missing
  after the restock; 7010 whose roll hit, 0 still there; longest stay of a sold-off item 150 days: Ebony Katana (9600
  gold, days 19-168)`. So every survivor stays on its own shelf, and items leave only by their sell-off roll.
- PASS expensive items linger far longer than cheap ones (2.7 days vs 24-37 days).
- PASS player-sold steel longsword (90 gold, 26%/day): on a shelf the day it was sold (day 30), sold off day 34.
- PASS two runs give identical stock (386,537-character per-day signature of every item).
- **Daedric at a top weapon smith** (60 shops, 3 shelves each, approved odds at the shop level):
  - Level 30: Daedric item on first stocking in 73.3% of shops; after 60 days of the ledger, on **100% of shop-days**.
  - Level 20: 43.3% on first stocking; 91.9% of shop-days after 60 days.
  - So finding one at a top smith is more than "rather feasible": Daedric goods linger ~37 days while deliveries keep
    coming. Tuning knobs if too generous: `SellChance` floor/curve or `StockTarget` in `DoomerfallShopLedger.cs`.
- **Fix-first (approved material odds):** weapons on first stocking are 4.78% Daedric at level 30 (approved 5.00%,
  82 of 1716) and 2.76% at level 20 (approved 2.50%, 37 of 1340): matches within sampling.
  - Per shelf, one fresh delivery (`--rates`, 1,000 unseeded shelves per row; rerun after verifier round 2). The 50.0%
    and 21.1% figures for level 30 are the research notes' **model predictions** for the approved odds, not
    measurements. (Card 02 measured 90.5% for the level-30 weapon smith with Daggerfall's classic curve; see `20261127-103953.md`.) That model
    counts every weapon as rarity 1, so it overstates how many weapons a shelf rolls. The rates test now counts the
    actual material rolls on each shelf (weapons other than arrows, plus plate armor) and prints the exact expected
    rate, mean over shelves of 1 - (1 - p)^rolls:

    | Shop | Level | Rolls per shelf | Measured | Expected from actual rolls | z | Notes' model |
    |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
    | Weapon smith | 15 | 12.94 | 10.9% | 12.2% | -1.3 | 12.8% |
    | Weapon smith | 18 | 12.91 | 22.9% | 20.8% | +1.6 | 21.8% |
    | Weapon smith | 20 | 12.90 | 30.0% | 27.8% | +1.5 | 29.1% |
    | Weapon smith | 30 | 12.92 | 49.8% | 48.2% | +1.0 | 50.0% |
    | Armorer | 15 | 4.45 | 3.7% | 4.4% | -1.1 | 4.6% |
    | Armorer | 18 | 4.49 | 8.4% | 7.7% | +0.8 | 8.1% |
    | Armorer | 20 | 4.45 | 8.1% | 10.6% | -2.6 | 11.1% |
    | Armorer | 30 | 4.47 | 19.7% | 20.1% | -0.3 | 21.1% |

    z = (measured - expected) / sqrt(p(1-p)/1000). The signs are mixed, all within about 2.6 sampling errors. These
    shelves are unseeded, so the numbers vary from run to run: the earlier run this session measured 45.6% for the level-30
    weapon smith (z = -2.3 against 48.2%) and 18.0% for the level-30 armorer (z = -1.7). The "expected" column is about 1
    to 2 points below the notes' model because real shelves roll slightly fewer weapons than the rarity-1 model assumes.
    The rates match the approved odds.
  - Armor: Daggerfall's `RandomArmorMaterial` first picks leather 70% / chain 20% / plate 10%,
  and only plate takes a material, so plate is 4.76% Daedric at level 30 (approved 5.00%) but all armor only 0.51%.
  Enemy armor drops go through the same path. Left as is; asked the owner.
