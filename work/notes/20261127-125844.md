## Verifier round 3 fix: Doom ammo no longer eats the stock share
Before, every shelf's delivery added all its Doom ammo with no cap, and ammo stacks counted toward the shelf's share.
Ammo surviving the 50%/day sell-off could fill the share, and then no weapons or other goods were delivered. The
previous run's level-30 smith ended with 116 items against a target of 75, about 74 of them ammo. Now:
- Only stocked goods count against the share: everything except Doom ammo and the horse and cart
  (`DoomerfallShopLedger.Stocked`).
- Doom ammo sits outside the stock target. A building gets **one** ammo delivery a day, on its first shelf (lowest
  LoadID). The other shelves drop the ammo from their deliveries, so the amount of ammo no longer scales with the
  number of shelves. A general store's one horse and one cart are also kept on the first shelf only (before, each
  shelf rolled its own, so a 3-shelf store had three horses).
- New test `StockSize` (weapon smiths, 180 days, 30-day warm-up). It passes when stocked goods never exceed the
  building's target (rounded up to a whole share per shelf), never fall below 3/4 of it, and new non-ammo goods arrive
  in the last 30 days.

| Level | Quality | Shelves | Target (cap) | Stocked goods per day | Ammo stacks mean / max | New non-ammo items, last 30 days |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 | 3 | 17 (18) | 18-18 | 15.4 / 23 | 116 |
| 5 | 6 | 5 | 25 (25) | 25-25 | 15.5 / 22 | 170 |
| 10 | 8 | 2 | 35 (36) | 36-36 | 16.9 / 24 | 199 |
| 30 | 20 | 3 | 75 (75) | 75-75 | 23.7 / 30 | 179 |
| 30 | 20 | 6 | 75 (78) | 78-78 | 23.5 / 30 | 227 |

All five pass. Ammo is a separate pool of about 15-24 stacks; owner tuning, see questions.
