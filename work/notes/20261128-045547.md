## Which place: the giver's place (quests a giver offers only)
Quest resources (including `Item _gold_ gold` and `Item _reward_ magic_item`) are created when the quest is parsed.
A quest a giver offers is parsed while the player stands in the giver's building, so its reward level is read from
the player's current location at that moment:

- `DoomerfallDrops.QuestRewardLevel` → `QuestRewardLevelAt(location)`: the location's `Level.Place`. (The travel map
  shows no level number for it: it shows only a danger word, and only for dungeons; `DoomerfallTravelHint`.) For a
  town that is the town level, `max(terrain, class level)` with village 5, hamlet 10, city 15,
  capital 20 (`DoomerfallAreaLevels.TownClassLevel`), so **no town is below level 5**. Other locations (temples,
  taverns, homes, dungeons, graveyards) use their area level (dungeon level or terrain), 1 and up. A shop, house or
  guild hall's own (wealth) level never applies, unlike `WorldLevel.Current` inside buildings.
- Away from any location: the danger level where the player is.
- Unknown (table not built): 1. `-dfarealevel N` overrides, as for all area levels. Never the player's level.

**Only giver offers use the place.** The place level applies only inside `WorldLevel.ForQuestGiver()`, a scope added
as one line at each giver offer call site:
- `DaggerfallGuildServicePopupWindow` (guild/temple/knightly order quests, both the random and the chosen-index offer),
- `DaggerfallQuestOfferWindow` (social quests: nobles, merchants, commoners, tavern keepers),
- `DaggerfallWitchesCovenPopupWindow` (coven quests),
- `DaggerfallQuestPopupWindow` (daedra summoning quests, at the summoner's place).

Every other quest — started by another quest's `start quest` / `run quest` (`Questing/Actions/StartQuest.cs`,
`RunQuest.cs`; e.g. main quest steps), by a letter, by a timer, at game start, or the vampire bloodline quest
(`VampirismEffect`) — is parsed wherever the player happens to be, which is neither the giver's nor the target place.
Those keep the previous fixed reward level 20 (`WorldLevel.DoomerfallUnplacedQuestRewardLevel`) until the owner decides
(question raised). The first version of this work let them roll at the player's current place; the verifier rejected
that, and I agree it did not follow the card.

Why not the target place for giver quests: many quests have several places or none (deliveries, escorts, "kill X
anywhere"), and the target is often picked at random, so it is not clearer in general. The giver's place is always
defined when the quest is offered.

Magic item rewards follow the same level (the card is "quest rewards follow area level", and they used the same fixed
20 before). Guild (faction) quest gold still uses guild rank + 1, as before (owner kept it, 2026-11-24).

Outside a Doomerfall session nothing changes: `QuestReward` is the player's level, as in Daggerfall Unity.

## Region price adjustment (a harness artifact in the first measurement)
Quest gold is multiplied by `(PriceAdjustment/2 + 500)/1000` (`Item.cs` CreateGold). A new game rolls each region's
`PriceAdjustment` in 750–1250 (`FormulaHelper` RandomRegionalPriceAdjustment via `InitializeRegionData`), a factor of
0.875–1.125. The `-dfsynctest` harness leaves it 0 (factor 0.5), which halved my first table. The test now sets the
current region to 1000 (factor 1.0) and also rolls at 750 and 1250, then restores the harness value.
