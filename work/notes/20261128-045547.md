## Which place: the giver's town
Quest resources (including `Item _gold_ gold` and `Item _reward_ magic_item`) are created when the quest is parsed and
started, which happens where the quest is given: the player is standing in the giver's building, in the giver's
town. So the reward level is read from the player's current location at that moment:

- `DoomerfallDrops.QuestRewardLevel`: the current location's `Level.Place` — the level the travel map shows (town
  level for a town, else its area level: dungeon level, or terrain). A shop, house or guild hall's own (wealth) level
  never applies, unlike `WorldLevel.Current` inside buildings.
- Away from any location (a quest started by a letter in the wilderness): the danger level where the player is.
- Unknown (table not built): 1. `-dfarealevel N` overrides, as for all area levels. Never the player's level.

Why not the target place: many quests have several places or none (deliveries, escorts, "kill X anywhere"), and the
target is often picked at random far away, so it is not "clearer" in general. The giver's town is always defined and
visible to the player on the travel map before accepting.

Magic item rewards follow the same level (the card is "quest rewards follow area level", and they used the same fixed
20 before). Guild (faction) quest gold still uses guild rank + 1, as before (owner kept it, 2026-11-24).

Outside a Doomerfall session nothing changes: `QuestReward` is the player's level, as in Daggerfall Unity.

## Gold by area level (formula)
Non-guild quest gold in Daggerfall: `playerMod = min(10, level/2 + 1)`, gold = random 150·mod to 200·mod, times
`(regionPriceAdjustment/2 + 500)/1000` times `(50 + 50)/100`. Before region factors:

| Area level | mod | Gold before region factor |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 150–200 |
| 2–3 | 2 | 300–400 |
| 4–5 | 3 | 450–600 |
| 6–7 | 4 | 600–800 |
| 8–9 | 5 | 750–1,000 |
| 10–11 | 6 | 900–1,200 |
| 14–15 | 8 | 1,200–1,600 |
| 18+ | 10 | 1,500–2,000 (the old fixed band) |

Measured in game: see Results.
