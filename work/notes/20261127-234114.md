# DDLOC lines off the screen (run 20261127-234114)

## Change

All 20 `DDLOC` prints (12 in `UZDoom/src/dd_locations.cpp`, 8 in `UZDoom/src/playsim/dd_treadmill.cpp`, including the
orange and red warnings the card's count of 16 left out) are now
`Printf(PrintFlag(PRINT_HIGH | PRINT_NONOTIFY), ...)`, as the card asks. The cast is needed because `Printf` takes a
`PrintFlag`. Text and colours are unchanged.

| | on-screen messages | console | stdout and `+logfile` |
|---|---|---|---|
| before | yes | yes | yes |
| after | no | yes | yes |

My first version (commit 4322ac13) used `DD_DebugPrintf`, which also removed the lines from the console in normal play.
The verifier rejected that as against the card; it is replaced.

## About `uz.log`

`run\uz.log` is the `-dfbridgelog` file. DDLOC lines were never in it, before or after. The lines tools parse are in
UZDoom's `+logfile` log (`run\log-NAME.txt` in the test harnesses) and stdout, and are still there (29 lines in each
first-route run, 12 in each load run, old and new exe alike). `play.bat` passes no `+logfile`.
