   ball's impact and its rays land about 13 tics apart, so a directly hit enemy splashes twice. Energy shotguns (Mk II,
   Tristar) splash once per arrival tic.

     Plasma Shotgun Mk II.

   - (c) Keep per-tic merging as built and reword the rule.

   keep 20%. Should exactly 5 degrees also get 10%? Reading it as the full cone would also give 10% to every Blaster
   pistol, the Laser Shotgun and the BFG10K.
