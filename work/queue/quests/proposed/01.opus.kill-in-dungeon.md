# Quests 1: kill a foe placed in a dungeon

Read D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall\AGENTS.md first. The survey is `work/notes/20261124-104520-quest-types.md`. Read
sections 1 and 2 (type T1, capabilities C2 and C5).

## Goal
A real kill-in-dungeon quest plays from start to finish through Doom: the offer, the trip, the kill, and the reward.
This type covers 58 quests.

## Rules from the owner
- Each Daggerfall quest foe is one counted leader. Killing the leader is the kill that counts ("Quest targets in mook
  packs", 2026-11-23). How the rest of a pack is built is not decided. Until it is, follow the answer to the quest
  foe question in the survey's section 5. Do not invent pack rules.
- Daggerfall owns quests and their state. Doom only reports what happened.

## What to build
- Send each quest foe's Daggerfall type with its row. Doom spawns the monster class that the encounter module's
  monster pool gives that type. Today Doom picks a stock monster from the id (`dd_monsters.cpp:97-108`).
- Report the first hit on a quest foe as `injured`, not only at death. 93 quests use `injured`.
- Remove a quest foe from Doom when Daggerfall hides it (`remove foe`) while the player is there.
- Delete `DoomerfallMonsters.ClosePopups`. Windows Daggerfall opens by itself now show in Doom
  (`DoomerfallUi.AskForOwnWindow`). The old auto-close eats "The _monster_ is dead" and could close a prompt.

## What must stay true
- The questor turn-in runs through Use on the questor (Daggerfall's own `StaticNPCClick`).
- A kill counts once, survives save and load, and a dead foe stays dead on re-entry.
- Non-quest monsters and their drops are unchanged.

## How to prove it
Take `N0C00Y12` (The Loose Pet Imp) from a real Mages Guild questor in a paired game, through Daggerfall's offer
window in Doom. Travel to the quest's real dungeon, hit the imp once and check the `injured` message, kill it, and see
the kill message. Save and load inside the dungeon, then return and turn in. Check Daggerfall's quest log and reward.
If the quest's rolled dungeon cannot be entered in Doom yet, use the `-dfquest` site redirect and say so. Repeat the
kill part with `B0B00Y01` (Retribution) for a second foe type.
