# Quests 8: quest teleports

Read D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall\AGENTS.md first. The survey is `work/notes/20261124-104520-quest-types.md`
(capability C11). This builds on card 2.

## Goal
When a quest moves the player (`teleport pc`, `transfer pc`), Doom goes along to the same place. 6 quests use this,
including two main quest steps.

## What to build
- Daggerfall tells Doom when it moved the player by itself, in the same way fast travel does (`KIND_TRAVEL`), with
  the target space and spot. `TeleportPc.cs:116` calls `PlayerEnterExit.RespawnPlayer`.
- Doom loads that space and puts the player there. Daggerfall's quest foes and items at the target then follow the
  normal lists.
- Check `transfer pc` in `S0000016` too, and say what it needs if it differs.

## What must stay true
- Fast travel, doors, and save and load still work.
- Position sync does not pull the player back to where they were before the teleport.

## How to prove it
Play `A0C00Y06` (An Unexpected Journey): meet the traitor in town, let the timer run, and get teleported to the
dungeon where the traitor waits. Kill the traitor there. Check both engines agree on the place after the teleport,
and after a save and load right after it.
