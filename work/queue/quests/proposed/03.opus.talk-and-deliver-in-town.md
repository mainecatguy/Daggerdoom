# Quests 3: talk to a person or deliver an item in town

Read D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall\AGENTS.md first. The survey is `work/notes/20261124-104520-quest-types.md` (type T7,
capabilities C3 and C4, and section 4 on moving NPCs).

## Goal
Quest people that Daggerfall places at markers inside buildings appear in Doom and can be talked to with Use. This
type covers 23 quests. The same list is the base for 139 quests that place people at their home building and 69
that use `place npc` or `create npc at` in buildings.

## Rules from the owner
- Daggerfall owns who is where. Doom shows each quest person and does nothing on its own (survey section 4).

## What to build
- A quest person list per interior from Daggerfall: id, flat archive and record for the sprite, marker position, and
  facing. Use `DoomerfallNpcs.IdOf`, so `DoomerfallTalk.FindNpc` finds the same `StaticNPC` and Daggerfall's own quest
  click runs. Cover `place npc`, `create npc at`, home people placed on entry (`Person.cs:389-427`), and named NPCs
  moved away from home.
- Doom spawns a talkable NPC actor for each, with the right sprite, and removes it when the list drops it.
- Live changes while the player is inside: a person placed, hidden, restored, or destroyed updates Doom at once.
  Check that hidden layout NPCs also leave Doom (capability C4).

## What must stay true
- Layout NPCs, shops, and closed buildings work as before.
- Quest people do not duplicate after leaving and re-entering, or after save and load.
- A hidden quest person cannot be talked to.

## How to prove it
Play `F0B00Y00` (Desired Artwork) from a temple questor: find the innkeeper contact the quest names, use them to get
the item, and carry it back to the questor. Then play `A0C01Y09` (The Birthday Present). Log each quest person's
appearance and removal, and show a `hide npc` happening while the player is in the same room.
