Read D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall\AGENTS.md first. The survey is `work/notes/20261124-104520-quest-types.md` (type T4,
capability C3, and section 4). This builds on card 3.

## Goal
A quest person placed in a dungeon appears in Doom, can be talked to, and the rescue plays out as in Daggerfall.
This type covers 17 quests, and 18 quests place a person in a dungeon.

## Rules from the owner
- Escort design is an open question (survey section 5). This card keeps Daggerfall's behaviour: the rescued person
  is hidden and shown only as a face. If the owner answers that rescued people follow as bodies, that is a new card.

## What to build
- Card 3's quest person list for dungeons, using dungeon marker positions.
- Show Daggerfall's follower faces (`add face`, `drop face`) in Doom, because the player does not see Daggerfall's
  HUD. A small list on Doom's HUD is enough.

## What must stay true
- Dungeon layout NPCs and their clicks are unchanged.
- The rescued person stays hidden after save and load, and the face list survives too.

## How to prove it
Play `C0B00Y03` (The Missing Scholar): reach the cleric in the quest's dungeon, use them, see them vanish and their
face appear, and turn in at the questor. Then play `A0C00Y16` (Missing Person Case), which starts in town with
several people to ask, to check card 3's town list in a longer quest.
