## Goal
Foes that a quest spawns near the player with `create foe` appear in Doom, fight, and count when killed. This type
covers 36 quests, and 100 quests use `create foe`.

## What to build
- Daggerfall keeps rolling the spawn (`CreateFoe.cs:109-162`) and tells Doom each new foe: its id, type, and that it
  should appear near the player. Doom picks the spots with its own position check, out of the player's sight where
  it can, and reports deaths and first hits as in card 1.
- Cover all four places Daggerfall can spawn them: building interior, dungeon, town, and wilderness. Outdoor actors go
  through the treadmill registry, so an outdoor ambush must follow its rules.
- Daggerfall's own hidden copies must not stay behind as frozen enemies. Check whether they stop resting ("enemies
  nearby") before and after.

## What must stay true
- Ambush foes count once and stay dead after save and load.
- The `send` form spawns only inside a town, as in Daggerfall.
- Non-quest encounters are unchanged.
