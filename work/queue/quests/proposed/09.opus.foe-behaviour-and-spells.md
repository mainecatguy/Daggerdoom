## Goal
Quest commands that change how a foe behaves reach Doom: `restrain foe` (the foe is not hostile), `change foe` team
and infighting, and spells cast on a foe. 29 quests use these. Quests that need the player to cast a named spell
(`cast X spell do`, 5 quests) work once player spells exist.

## Rules from the owner
- A restrained foe follows Daggerfall: it does not attack. Whether it turns hostile when shot follows Daggerfall's
  rules. If Daggerfall does not say, ask the owner.
- Player spellcasting in Doom is not designed. The `cast X spell do` part waits for it. Do that part only if a spells
  card has landed.

  exists yet, log and skip it, and say so.
