# Quests 4: fetch an item from a town building

Read D:\dev\DoomDaggerfall\AGENTS.md first. The survey is `work/notes/20261124-104520-quest-types.md` (type T6,
capabilities C8 and C10). This builds on card 3.

## Goal
Quest items that Daggerfall places at markers inside buildings appear in Doom and can be picked up with Use. This
type covers 12 quests, and 34 quests place an item in a building.

## What to build
- Send quest items in an interior with the item piles: id, world sprite (the item's world texture archive and
  record), and marker position. Daggerfall makes them as billboards with a `QuestResourceBehaviour`
  (`GameObjectHelper.cs:1116-1160`), not `DaggerfallLoot`, so `DoomerfallItems` does not see them today.
- Use on one runs Daggerfall's own quest item click (`TriggerQuestResourceBehaviourClick`), so `clicked item` fires
  and the item goes to Daggerfall's inventory as a quest item.
- The item leaves Doom when Daggerfall hides it.

## What must stay true
- Ordinary piles, containers, and stealing work as before.
- A taken quest item does not come back after re-entry or after save and load.
- `pc at` fires when the player enters the quest building through Doom.

## How to prove it
Play `O0B10Y05` (Valuable Artwork): enter the house, check `pc at` fired, take the painting with Use, and carry it
to the questor. Its guard ambush is `create foe` (card 7), which may not spawn yet. The quest can finish without it.
Say whether the ambush rolled. Then take the item in `O0B20Y02` (A Prized Item).
