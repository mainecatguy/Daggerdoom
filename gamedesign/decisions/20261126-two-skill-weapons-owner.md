## 2026-11-26: A weapon that belongs to two weapon skills

- **Verdict:** Owner decided.
- **Answer:** A weapon that counts as both a Shotgun or Pistol and an Energy Weapon is treated as whichever of those
  skills is higher, never both at once. If that is technically hard for some weapon, its tags may be reassigned
  instead, as long as every weapon's tags are consistent and make sense.
- **Owner's words:** "if a weapon belongs as both a Shotgun or Pistol weapon and an Energy Weapon, it gets to be
  treated as whichever has higher skill, but NOT both at the same time, that would be too strong. ... If this proves
  technically problematic we can assign tags however for weapons which are problematic for example if coding a laser
  pistol to work like a pistol is hard we can change the plan I'm flexible as long as everything gets tags that are
  consistent and make sense."
