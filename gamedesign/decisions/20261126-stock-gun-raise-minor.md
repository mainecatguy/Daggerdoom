## 2026-11-26: After an automatic tier change, should a stock gun come back up if Doom lowered it to fists? (recheck of 2026-11-24) [20261124-101258]

- **Verdict:** Follows clearly (decided as minor).
- **Answer:** Yes. The gun is raised again automatically. Add it to the play-test list so the owner can feel it.
- **Rests on:** no design line names this. The automatic tier change exists so the player's gun keeps firing when one
  tier runs out (`work/notes/20261124-101258.md`); staying on fists would undo that purpose. Raising it returns the
  player to the gun they chose.
- **Options struck:** none by a quotable fact. "Stay on fists" is left but defeats the feature it follows from.
- **Why minor:** both options ship the same system; the only difference is whether one weapon switch happens after a
  side effect the player did not ask for. A reasonable owner would not care which way it goes once the automatic change
  exists, and the owner can overrule it here.
- **Challenge:** A player-visible weapon switch could be a feel question, and an unexpected raise mid-fight could
  surprise the player. But the lowering itself is the unexpected part, caused only by the tier briefly reading empty;
  the raise restores what the player was holding. No new system, no ripple. Kept, upgraded from Leans to decided as
  minor.
