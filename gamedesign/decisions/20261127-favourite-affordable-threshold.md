## 2026-11-27: When does a regional favourite count as "can be purchased"? [encounters 08]

- **Verdict:** Leans (yes, one ordinary encounter).
- **Answer:** The favourite kind is purchasable when one of its usable, uncapped classes costs at most 1 x the area
  level's average points per encounter (`region_afford` = 1). Otherwise it gets the stand-in. In stock this means a
  Mastermind favourite gets a stand-in up to level 21, a Cyberdemon up to 20, an Arachnotron up to 6.
- **Rests on:** owner `20261126-favourite-fallback-owner.md`: "Pick something as a fallback. I don't care too much what
  you decide here". The encounter design defines budgets as an average per encounter (lines 35, 56, 169) and says what
  one average buys (line 186). One average is the natural "an ordinary pack can buy it".
- **Challenge:** Read literally, the owner's "cannot be purchased" means the budget cannot buy it at all, and giant
  rooms and bosses can buy far dearer monsters, so the literal reading is looser. The fallback entry's own note used a
  different figure (above 7 points at level 1, about 2 averages). Thresholds change how often players see stand-ins,
  so this is not "decided as minor". Since the owner handed over the choice, Leans stands. He sees it and can overrule.
