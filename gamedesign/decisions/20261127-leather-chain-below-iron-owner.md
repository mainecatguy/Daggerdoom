## 2026-11-27: Where do leather and chain armor go in the material odds?

- **Verdict:** Owner decided.
- **Answer:** Every armor piece rolls the approved material table. Leather and chain split Iron's slot, so the odds of better materials (Steel and up) do not drop: leather and chain together take about 20% of Iron's share, Iron keeps the rest. Leather and chain on shelves sit outside the shop's stock limit, like ammo, so they don't crowd the lists. They are flavor.
- **Owner's words:** "I guess we could have iron split with leather and chain so we don't further reduce the odds of the player getting a decent item just don't nerf the ability to find decent items into the ground", "Ammo is beyond the stock limit so is leather and chain so it doesn't shit up the lists. Also do less leather and chain than iron as no one cares about it. Like 20%. It's basically just there for flavor".
