## 2026-11-26: Regional favourite that an area cannot afford

- **Verdict:** Owner decided (left the choice to the product owner).
- **Answer:** When the regional favourite kind costs more than the area level can afford, the area picks an affordable
  stand-in that fits its theme and region, preferring the favourite's own colour or DRLA family, then the nearest
  kind. The favourite itself stays any kind.
- **Owner's words:** "If the favorite monster cannot be purchased, then I suppose the area picks one to act as a
  different one based on the theme and area ? Pick something as a fallback. I don't care too much what you decide here
  since this is a rare exception case right?"
- **Note:** it is not very rare at low area levels: a level 1 dungeon's whole army is about 70 points, so any favourite
  above 7 points (a Baron, a Cacodemon, a Cyberdemon) needs the stand-in.
