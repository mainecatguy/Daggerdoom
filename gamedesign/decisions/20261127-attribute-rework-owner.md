## 2026-11-27: Strength, Agility and Luck rework; Personality gun damage withdrawn

- **Verdict:** Owner decided. Replaces the Agility dodge chance, and withdraws "Personality scales gun damage"
  (`20261127-personality-gun-damage-owner.md`), which came from reading PER as Perception.
- **Strength:** melee keeps +1% per point (double at 100). Shotguns, explosives and automatics also get -20% at 0,
  0% at 50, +20% at 100 (two straight lines meeting at 50).
- **Agility:** the dodge chance is removed. All firearms get -10% at 0, 0% at 50, +10% at 100. Rifles and pistols get
  another -10% to +10% on the same curve, so -20% to +20% in all ("more symmetrical with strength"). Faster reload and
  weapon switching stay.
- **Luck:** besides the ammo drop chance, a 5x super crit on player hits: 0% up to 50, +0.1% per point above, 5% at
  100. Hits, not burn ticks. Splash can be lucky; an explosion rolls once per blast, so a lucky blast crits every
  target it hits.
- **Stacking:** these are "increased" bonuses and add into one pool (`gamedesign.md`, "Damage modifiers", owner
  2026-11-27). The Luck super crit is its own 5x.
- **Order:** do this before the in-game attribute help is updated (`attributes` 07).
- **Owner's words:** "Strength, yes, it's a bigger bonus. -20% to 20% with those." "Agility let's do -10% to +10% for
  all firearms, and another -10% to +10% for rifles and pistols." "Explosions are per blast." "Splash damage can be
  lucky." "Hits, not ticks."
- **Open:** Personality in Doom. The orchestrator suggested it does nothing new (Daggerfall's prices and dialogue
  stay), with a possible later social role (guards accepting a fine). Not yet confirmed by the owner.
