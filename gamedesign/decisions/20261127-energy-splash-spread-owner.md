## 2026-11-27: Energy splash share by cone of fire

- **Verdict:** Owner decided (sheet item 85).
- **Answer:** Energy hits that do not explode splash 20%; guns whose cone of fire is wider than 5 degrees (mainly BFG
  tracers and a few energy shotguns) splash 10% per hit. Piercing beams count as single hits (20% per monster). A
  shot's hits on one enemy merge into one splash. `energysplash` 01.
- **Owner's words:** "Single hit on those. Generally the point of this patch is that it should affect highly inaccurate
  weapons." "If the cone of fire on the weapon is greater than 5 degrees ... then it gets 10%."
