## 2026-11-27: "Increased" damage modifiers add; "more" multiplies; INT and PER multiply on gun enchantments

- **Verdict:** Owner decided. Lasting rule, folded into `gamedesign.md` ("Damage modifiers").
- **Answer:** Unless otherwise specified, percentage damage bonuses are "increased" and add together into one pool;
  "more" modifiers multiply separately (as in Path of Exile). Intelligence and Personality on gun enchantments are an
  explicit exception: they multiply with each other.
- **Owner's words:** "yes they are multiplicative, independent modifiers". "well in this one case they're
  multiplicative because that's fun ... but as a general rule many modifiers will not be multiplicative. We should
  probably clarify that unless otherwise specified, pure damage increased % modifiers are additive, and 'more' is
  multiplicative. Increased all adds together into one pool: as path of exile."
