## 2026-11-26: Weapons in two skills use the higher one [owner]

- **Verdict:** Owner decided.
- **Answer:** Some guns fit two weapon skills (for example a laser shotgun is both a Shotgun and an Energy Weapon). Such
  a gun uses whichever of its skills is higher, never both at once.
